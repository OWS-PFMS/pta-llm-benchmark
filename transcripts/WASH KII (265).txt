I1: In your opinion, what do you think has ensured Kamuli has regular running clean water?
R: WAVE’s operational system. It is preventative. The constant supervision and maintenance of the water source ensures limited chances of breakdown. 
I1: Are there hindrances in trying to maintain constant clean water flow in Kamuli despite the system in place? 
R: It is a challenge for the community to pay for these maintenance fees. They are too high and cannot be afforded by regular community members.
I1: What recommendations can you share with us to do away with these problem?
R: Community sensitization to bring about ownership of water sources is important. People will take care of it better that way. Also, it would be good if maintenance fees can be reduced to allow the regular community member to afford it. 
I1: Which of these recommendations do you believe is more important?
R: Sensitization. People need to understand and appreciate their role and responsibility where clean water sources are concerned. 
I1: Do you believe the current maintenance system is effective?
R: It is very effective. We rarely go many days without water unlike before. People do not have to resort to unprotected/untreated water sources.  
I1: What do you think is lacking in this maintenance system though? 
R: I have already mentioned them but yes, sensitization. In the future when the organizations finally move on, our society needs to be in position to own their water source and protect its operation and hygiene. Finally, the issue of reduction in maintenance fees can be forgotten.
