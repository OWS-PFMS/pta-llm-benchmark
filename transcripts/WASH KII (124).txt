1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent 1: There is a serious water supply shortage in Mille woreda. The water supply cannot satisfy the water needs of the community.
Interviewer: What is the reason for water supply shortage in the town?
Respondent 1: This is mainly due the fact that the town population is growing rapidly and the water supply is not growing in line with the population growth. The water service is based on the water schemes that are developed 20 years ago. 
Some years ago, there is no water supply shortage in the town and the community gets water service 24 hours. Now we do not have regular access for water service. Sometimes we do not get water for more than a week.
Interviewer: Are there any other problem related to water service in the town?
Respondent 1: The other problem related to water service is that some parts of the town do not have water supply lines. Only few areas that have proximity to the main road have water supply line infrastructure. The town water utility office do not covered most of the residential areas during construction or installation of water lines.
The existing water lines are also very old and leak in different places and this resulted in wastage of water.
Respondent 2: Frequent power outage is also the other main problem for the water service of the town. There is no standby diesel generator to distribute water when there is no electric service. Due to this fact sometimes, we do not get water for more than a week. 
Interviewer: What are the problems related to sanitation service?
Respondent 1: As to my understanding as such there is no sanitation service problem in the town. Our main is water supply shortage.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: One of the problems you raised related to water service is water supply shortage. What do you recommend to alleviate this problem?
Respondent 1: The town administration should allocate sufficient budget to develop new boreholes to improve the water service. Currently the water service is based on one borehole which is developed long years ago. Thus, to satisfy the growing water demand of the community additional water schemes should be developed as soon as possible. In this regard, NGOs should also collaborate with woreda and regional government bodies.
Old water lines should be replaced by new ones and the water office should construct water lines in those areas that do not have water service infrastructure. As this activity requires huge financial capital, NGOs should cooperate with the woreda by providing financial and technical support.
We should also enhance the awareness of the community on proper use of this scarce water resource.
Interviewer: You have also mentioned that there is frequent power outage and this affects the water service. What do you suggest about solution to this problem? 
Respondent 2: I think there should be a separate electric supply line and transformer that is directly linked with the main power substation. If we can do this, we can minimize the frequency of power outage.  
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent 1: Development of new water schemes should be given prior attention, as there is a serious water supply shortage in the town. Once we developed new boreholes, we can construct water distribution line where there is no water supply infrastructure. Without having sufficient water source, developing water supply lines is meaningless. 
If the problem related to water supply shortage is solved, the urban community can save its money that will be spend to purchase bottled water. On the other hand, in rural areas we can protect the community from using unsafe stagnant surface water and reduce the incidence of water born diseases.
