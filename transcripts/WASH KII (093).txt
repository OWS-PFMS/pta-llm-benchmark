
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Future water supply and demand imbalance
Currently, there is no drinking water supply problem in Debre Birehan town. However, I have a fear that the town will face water supply shortage in the near future due to the growing residential houses construction and the emerging manufacturing industries. Thus, relevant actors should take appropriate actions prior to facing the water supply shortage. That is the water supply should grow in line with the growing water need of the town. To this end some institutions like Debre Birehan University has started development of its water supply source within its compound.  Dashen Brewery has also developed its own water source to minimize the burden on the town water supply source. 
II.Absence of standard waste disposal site
Sanitation is a big problem in our town. There is no standard waste disposal system in the town. Due to the rapid growth of the population and the town large amount of wastes produced daily in the town. However, there limited capacity to dispose and manage the wastes appropriately due to absence of waste disposal site in and outside the town. 
With the objective of acquiring lessons and good experiences, we had a chance to visit the waste collection, disposal and management system of Addis Ababa and Hawassa cities; however, our city administration has no capacity (financial resource) to develop waste management system like the two cities.   
There are few private waste collection service providers that collect and dispose wastes in another town like Sheno (which is 50 km form Debre Birehan), however, it is very costly for the majority to use the service. 
III.Lack of community awareness on waste disposal and management
Based on the town’s health extension package we provide door-to-door awareness enhancement training to the community on proper waste management. We teach and train to prepare temporary septic tanks for liquid waste on the one hand and to collect and burn solid wastes inside their compounds on the other hand. We also provide advice those household that have no space for waste management within their areas to use carthorse that collect and dispose waste in temporary sites. However, it is difficult to reach every community members through door-to-door visit, awareness enhancement campaigns programs should be organized to reach the community at large. 
IV.Lack of law enforcement
There is no appropriate law enforcement mechanism to control those who violates sanitary regulations. The existing penalties on those who violate sanitary regulations are much lower than the cost of disposing wastes through the formal system. For instance, if someone connect his/her toilet wastes to drainage lines, he/she will be penalized only birr 100 to 200. However, if he/she use the service of latrine emptying service providers, he is required to pay more than birr 1000. So he tends to connect his waste to drainage lines and pay the penalty, as it is much lower than the formal service. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
In order to alleviate the problem related to waste disposal site and management we would like to suggest the following:
Waste disposal sites should be prepared and constructed as soon as possible to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. To this end, sufficient budget should be allocated to develop the required infrastructure.
Massive community awareness creation campaign should be organized to change the attitude of the society on waste management and disposal within their locality. 
Laws should be enforced to ensure sustainable sanitation service 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Preparing Damping site/Waste disposal site for both solid and liquid waste is the first priority to sustain the town sanitation. If the dump site/waste disposal site problem for both solid and liquid waste is resolved, the community will benefit the following:
The community health will be improved
The town becomes beautiful
The town image will be build
