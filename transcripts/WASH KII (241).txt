1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Water supply and demand imbalance
The water facilities that are constructed long years ago are unable to satisfy the current water demand of the community. The existing water wells capacity does not satisfy the current need of the community. There are ongoing water projects in the town to enhance the water supply capacity that meet the current clean water demand of the community. However, these projects are delayed for long period due to weak implementation performance of the contractors as well as budget problems. Currently, the main water supply sources of the town are ground water and river. However, farmers also use the river for irrigation and this led to serious water shortage during dry season. In addition, the capacity of the rivers decreases from time to time due to global warming. 

II.Absence of waste disposal place both for liquid and solid wastes
There is no appropriate waste disposal site in the town both for liquid and solid wastes. This problem forced the community to dispose wastes simply anywhere around their residential area and this resulted in environmental, sanitation and other community health problems.  For example, solid wastes are dumped in open public spaces/green areas and rivers due to the absence of waste disposal site in the town. 

There was an attempt to address the waste disposal site problem by the town administration and it identified areas that are suitable for waste disposal outside the town. However, it could not reach an agreement with the landowners or farmers due to compensation issues. We suggest that the town administration should have discussion with the farmers and allocate appropriate compensation for those who will be displaced from the site. To do this it should collaborate with other relevant stakeholders and potential donors to mobilize fund for compensation as well as project implementation.  

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
I.In order to alleviate the problem related to water supply and demand imbalance there should be collaboration between the government, non-governmental organization and the community itself. The government effort alone could not bring sustainable solution for the problem.

Moreover, the ongoing water project that is implemented with the financial support of World Bank should be appropriately managed and the contractors or the project implementers should be strongly monitored to finalize the project in the intended time. The committee established by the town administration to follow up the implementation of the project should strictly monitor the contractor and if there is a clear implementation performance gap, they should look for another competent contractor that has a capacity to deliver the project within short period. 

II.Regarding the problem related to waste disposal site, having appropriate and standard waste disposal site both for liquid and solid wastes has a prime to introduce good waste management practice within the community as well as to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. Thus, the town administration should closely work with relevant stakeholders (both governmental and non-governmental) including the community to develop appropriate waste disposal site as soon as possible. 

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Developing appropriate and standard waste disposal place is the first priority to alleviate sanitation related problems of the town. We are not saying there is enough supply of clean water in the town. Though there is also water supply problem, we prioritize the problem related to the waste disposal site in the first place, as it is a base to alleviate the problems related to water and sanitation service sustainability. If the waste disposal site problem gets a solution:
We can ensure the sanitation and beautification of the town
It improves the community health 
It is important for environmental sanitation,  hygiene and  community health
