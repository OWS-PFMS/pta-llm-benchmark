1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: One of the major problems related to the water service is that the water supply cannot meet the water demand of the community. The existing water facilities are developed long years ago and they were sufficient for the community at that time. However, currently the size of the population grown rapidly and the existing water service could not meet the water needs of the growing population. 
The other problem is the town water utility office has budget constraint to manage the water service. That is it does not have sufficient budget to undertake regular maintenance of the water facilities. Moreover, it does not get technical and financial support from the regional water bureau.
The head of the water utility office is political appointed by the woreda administration without considering his technical capability and educational background. And he does not have water-engineering or hydraulics background. Because of this, he could not properly manage the water service of the town. Moreover, the other technical staffs of the office are not qualified and experienced. They lack technical capability to undertake the required maintenance service.
Interviewer: What are the problems related to sanitation service of the town?
Respondent: The major problem related to sanitation service is that there is no coordination between relevant stakeholders that are expected to work on sanitation. For instance, the woreda water and sewerage utility office and the woreda health office are the two most important actors for sanitation service. However, there is no coordination between these actors. The woreda health office do not provide awareness enhancement training to the community on sanitation. There is no regular monitoring and follow up of the sanitation situation of the woreda. The woreda water and sewerage utility office do not provide liquid waste or pit emptying service for the community.
Interviewer: Before we move to the next question, are there any other problems related to water and sanitation service in the town?
Respondent: The woreda administrative bodies are not committed to ensure sustainable water and sanitation service in the woreda. For instance, there is a Transform WASH program in the woreda and the woreda water office, health office, education office, finance office, woreda administration office, micro and small enterprise development office and women and children affairs office are expected to play significant role for successful implementation of the program. However, representatives of these offices are not committed to engage in the program. Moreover, they do not have coordination for the realization of the program. In general, there is no coordinated planning and activities between relevant actors in WASH sector of the woreda.
 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: You mentioned that there is water supply shortage in the woreda. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solution to this problem? 
Respondent: Currently, the woreda water office in collaboration with the regional water bureau is planning to develop additional water schemes in the town and preliminary assessment is under way to identify areas for digging boreholes. 
However, there are three boreholes developed by NGOs and left ideal without function. Thus, I would like to suggest that instead of developing new boreholes by investing large amount of money it is better to purchase the necessary materials like water pumps and water pipes and make the boreholes functional to the community. 
Interviewer: You have indicated that the town water utility office has a budget, materials and other resource constraints. What do you suggest to alleviate these problems?
Respondent: In principle, the town water utility office is expected to generate its own income by collecting water service charges from the community. However, the office has no appropriate water consumption monitoring and water bill collection system. Because of this, many households utilize the service without paying their monthly water bills. Thus, the office should improve its monitoring and water bill collection system so that it can alleviate its financial constraint at least partially. 
In addition to this, the regional water bureau and other WASH stakeholders should lend their hand to capacitate the office materially, financially and technically.
Interviewer: You have also mentioned that the woreda administration appointed heads of the water utility office without considering their educational background, work experience and their technical knowledge. Moreover, the staffs of the office lack the required qualification. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Respondent: Having solid experience and technical knowledge is necessary to properly lead water utility office. Thus, it should not be a political appointment rather it should be based on educational background and knowledge of the sector. The woreda administration should change their appointment criteria.
On the other hand, continuous professional development training should be given to staffs of the office to enhance their technical capabilities. 
Interviewer: The other problem you raised is that, there is no coordination between actors that work in WASH sector and the woreda administrative bodies are not committed to ensure sustainable water and sanitation service in the woreda. What do you suggest to alleviate these problems?
Respondent: Frist of all, without coordination we cannot ensure sustainable water and sanitation service in the woreda. For instance, without water service we cannot smoothly provide education service. Without water service, we cannot implement health programs and without water service, we cannot run livestock and agricultural production. Thus, to realize appropriate community development program every actor should work in close collaboration.
 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: Our woreda is very arid and have very hot climatic condition. This implies there is huge water demand both for personal hygiene and for drinking purpose. Therefore, capacitating the water utility office in terms of finance, materials and human resources is the most important solution to ensure sustainable water service in the town.
Particularly, there is a serious shortage of water in rural parts of the woreda as a result the people move from place to place in search of water for their consumption as well as for their livestock. Hence, all actors should give immediate attention to strengthen the water supply service of the woreda. 
If the pastoral community have access for water in their locality, they can settle in one area and have better life as well as they will send their children to school.
