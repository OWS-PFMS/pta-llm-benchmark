I1: In your opinion, what do you think we can do ensure our boreholes remain operational?
R: Aggressively sensitize community members on clean water maintenance. In addition, current water focused organizations should increase on the current number of boreholes. 
I1: What is the 1 hinderance to us acquiring regular clean water here in Kamuli?
R: Boreholes are very few in number. We need to increase them because the area I currently live in has 1 borehole.
I1: Any other suggestions?
R: No. That is the only one. 
I2: How would you grade the current maintenance of boreholes in Kamuli?
R: Good. Very good. 
I2: Are there any practices you would like to see changed to improve borehole maintenance in Kamuli?
R: The annual fee required by WAVE to maintain our boreholes should be reduced by a certain percentage but also, community members need to change their outlook and positively pay this money for us to have clean water.
