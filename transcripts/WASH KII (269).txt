I1: In your opinion, what do you think has ensured Kamuli has regular running clean water?
R: Proper and regular supervision and repair of the borehole. 
I1: Are there hindrances in trying to maintain constant clean water flow in Kamuli despite the system in place? 
R: We find an issue with raising this money for the water source maintenance from the community members. 
I1: What recommendations can you share with us to do away with these problem?
R: Invite the Community Development Officer (CDO) to sensitize the community about water source protection and the benefits of clean water. I have worked with this community and I’m sure their main issue is they lack proper sensitization. 
I1: Which of these recommendations do you believe is more important?
R: Sensitization of community members. 
I1: Do you believe the current maintenance system is effective?
R: It is working well. Especially in areas where communities were sensitized well. 
I1: What do you think is lacking in this maintenance system though? 
R: Training of the water user committees so they can train community members better on matters affecting water around them.
