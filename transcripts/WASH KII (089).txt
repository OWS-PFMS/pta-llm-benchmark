1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in Debreberhan? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?

Improper water scheme construction: - Unplanned and inappropriate water point drilling is causing a serious problem on the sustainability of water supply in the city.
Pollution of underground catchment areas:-  The water source of the city is being polluted by different sewages and chemicals which is becoming a threat for the water service sustainability.
Absence of visionary leadership:-  Leadership gap is a reason for poor water service sustainability in this town.
Absence of sewage treatment plant which exacerbates pollution on the main rivers:- in the city there is no sewage treatment plant;  and because of this fact  sewage is being released openly  to the main rivers . 
Absence of proper solid waste disposal system:- Solid waste of the city  is not being disposed in appropriate landfill points; hence,  the city water and hygiene system is being exposed to wastes and pollution.
Week institutional capacity:- Institutions which are  supposed to handle water and hygiene sustainability  are very week and unorganized;  and this premise is also one factor for the deteriorating water system sustainability in the city.
Absence of waste dumping site:-  There is no waste dumping site in the city which is also becoming a problem for water and sanitation sustainability.
Lack of strategic plan:- the city has no strategic plan with respect to the water schemes, so there is no planned way of treating wastes .
Lack of research by the university – problems like this needs the involvement of higher educational institutions, but the university is not contributing its share as a think-tank.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

Capacitating institutions: - building the capacity of institutions is one way to solve problems related with water and hygiene sustainability.  Institutions like the municipality, water and sewerage service and health office should be capacitated to make the water service sustainable.
Restricting inappropriate drilling of bore holes:-  bore holes are being drilled without research, so the city should restrict drilling of bore holes or the drilling should be clearly examined by the appropriate government organ.
Awareness creation:- creating awareness on personal and community hygiene can be one solution in addressing the problem.
Treating water source catchments sites:- treating and protecting water source catchment areas should  be  one way to solve the problem.  This catchments should be treated through biological and physical mechanisms.
Preparing integrated strategic plan:-  integrated strategic plan will help the city to have  a clear road map on water service and waste disposals; so it should be considered while trying to solve the problems.
 Doing correction on failed leadership:- So far, the city has failed in making leadership regarding water service and hygiene sustainability ; hence,  upgrading leadership skills with determination  is one that should be considered.
Preparing landfill sites :- absence of landfill site is the most serious problem. This should be resolved while attempting to solve the problem substantially.






3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Creating determined and visionary leaders should be the first thing in the process of solving the issues and this determination should be accompanied by technical knowledge. Having such kind of leadership is very important because it can synchronize the efforts of the community with stockholders towards the solution.
Also, the city should pay attention to creating strong institutional capacity that can handle the continuing industry flow along with its impact on the environment.
