1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your town? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Absence of sewerage line and/or system

As a resident and expert of this authority, I understand that having strong sewerage and sanitation system is very important to provide better sustainable sanitation services in the town. However, there is no sewerage and strong sanitation service delivery system in Woliso town. Moreover, there is no short run plan to develop sewerage system in the town, as it requires huge budget and technology. 

Due to absence of sewerage system, some institutions that function in the town directly link their liquid wastes to rivers and this resulted in serious health and sanitation problem to the community. For instance, the town’s prison house and abattoirs directly connected their liquid wastes to a river that is used by the community for household consumption and farming purpose. Although we tried to take corrective actions in collaboration with other stakeholders, we could not get any solution for the problem yet.
   
II.Absence of dumping sites for liquid wastes collected from households and hotels toilets as well as communal latrines: 

The other main problem related to sanitation service in the town is that there is no appropriate site to dispose liquid wastes collected for communal latrines, toilets of households and septic tanks of various service providing institutions. Because of this private vacuum truck emptying companies and the town water and sewerage utility could not provide service to the community. Almost all liquid waste collecting vehicles are idle due to absence of appropriate dumping site. Again, even though we have tried to find out a solution to this problem in coordination with other leaning alliance members, the problem is not yet solved, as farmers are not willing to give their plots of land with the proposed compensation by the town’s municipality. 

Similarly, there is no appropriate permanent and temporary landfill site for solid wastes. Mostly the community dispose solid wastes in roadsides and residential areas and this creates contamination and blocks ditches particularly during rainy season.  Moreover, there is no mechanism and logistics to collect solid wastes from difference places of the town. 

Poor awareness of the community on management of liquid and solid wastes, weak commitment of the municipality to establish appropriate waste management system and temporary landfill site and absence of waste collection service providers resulted in serious environmental and sanitation problems in the town. 

III.Water supply lines are very old and exposed for contamination 

One on the major challenge related to water service in the town is that the existing water supply lines are very old and there is water leakage here and there in the ground. In some cases, we observe a situation that water supply lines contaminated by liquid wastes due to carelessness of the community. 

IV.Mismanagement of communal latrines 

Even though there are considerable number of communal latrines in the town, the community do not properly manage the facilities. That is there is no regular cleaning, maintenance and pit empting. In general, the community practice related to use of communal latrines is inappropriate. There is no sufficient water for cleaning, doors are broken, and the status of most the latrines is not in a way to provide sustainable service to the community. Moreover, the communal latrine-managing committees are not active in mobilizing and coordinating the community to ensure sustainability of the facilities.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
I.To alleviate the problems related to sewerage system, both government and non-governmental organizations should exert coordinated effort to establish appropriate sewerage system by allocating budget and mobilizing resources from potential donors. Moreover, solid wastes should be collected, transported and disposed out of the town in appropriate landfill site.

II.Pertinent to the problem related to dumping sites for liquid wastes collected from households and hotels toilets as well as communal latrines, the town administration should put this agenda in its top priority list and engage in preparation of standard dumping site as soon as possible. In addition, massive awareness enhancement trainings should be provided the community on waste management and handling.

There should be a law and enforcement mechanism on those institutions that pollute the environment by connecting their liquid wastes to the nearby rivers. Institutions should be forced to manage their liquid and solid wastes by themselves without affecting the environment. Environmental and sanitary regulatory bodies should have strong monitoring system and take actions or corrective measures on those institution violating the environmental and sanitation regulations.

Until we get sustainable solution to the problem related to permanent liquid waste disposal place, the community should be advised to develop temporary septic tanks to minimize environmental and sanitation problems.


III.To alleviate the problems related to the existing water supply lines households should take care of when they install water lines to their house. That is they should install water lines in a way avoiding contamination by waste lines. Moreover, the recently started project by World Bank fund to replace old water supply lines by new ones  should be finalized as soon as alleviate the water contamination problem sustainably.  

IV.Regarding mismanagement of communal latrines, relevant WASH stakeholders should provide strong and continues awareness enhancement trainings to the community on communal latrine management and use to ensure the sustainability of the service.  

V.Finally, to alleviate problems related community awareness on waste management and disposal the following should be done:
Awareness enhancement training should be given to the community on how to collect, handle and disposed wastes in their locality without polluting the environment; 
Follow-up work should be performed by the concerned authority;
As there are only few waste collection service providers, we should encourage and organize the community to engage in this business. The municipality should also give emphasis to alleviate the logistic problem related waste collection;
Identify and organize the management and controlling structure at lower level and make functional arrangements to implement waste management activities in the town.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
In my opinion, preparing appropriate liquid and solid waste damping site is the most important solution to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town as it is a base to alleviate sanitation and environmental problem of Woliso town. If this problem is resolved and if we have appropriate site to dispose wastes, it creates a good opportunity to improve the town’s sanitation, it minimize the problems of the community related to sanitation and environmental pollution, and it enhance the environmental sanitation, hygiene and community health.
