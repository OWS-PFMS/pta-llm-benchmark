
1. In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation / Water service sustainability in your woreda?  Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation / water service sustainability?


When it comes to service coverage and sanitation, there is often a problem with the management and professional perspective.  The people blame the municipality for the problem. When you ask the municipality, those who are managing the office don’t pay much attention to water and sanitation. Now if you see the human power placement, they assign people who are not well qualified. They don’t have the moral to do their work. The other factor is financial constraint. We have only one tractor/vehicle. No other facility exists.  There is a solid waste disposal site, but it doesn’t have facilities or it is not a standard disposal site.  There is no sewage disposal site at all. 

The main challenge is the problem of attitude and awareness with the community and its leadership.   If we had been focused on this service, we would have matched both the financial and human resources. Beginning from 2010 (E.C), there has been something that is begun to bring change. We outsourced a work to an association that does cleaning solid waste along the way from the university to the town. We financed the budget to this project. We are also preparing a site for liquid disposal. 

The other main challenge is awareness problem about sanitation both by the wereda office and the people. The sanitation sector is not financed by budget sufficiently. There is no sufficient human power, too. The people bring the sanitation problem to the municipality even if the rules and regulations of municipality say that every citizen is supposed to clean at least his or her 20-meter radius of living area. 

With regard to water, there is shortage of supply and water services giving facilities. Community members also demand more water access, but the urban administration couldn't supply the public with sufficient water access due to lack of financial capacity to do what is needed. 

Interviewer: Is it only because of financial constraint? Can't you make coordination to bring philanthropists, community members and Charity organizations that can participate on water service work and make some developmental contributions here? 

There could be the skill of coordination, but there is no commitment on this. People may participate on such charitable activities, but one who takes the initiative and does the coordination is needed. There are various undergoing water projects in Weliso town, but the demand is not met yet since there is an increasing rate of population growth. 

2. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
 
Nowadays, we are somehow doing awareness’s creation work on the issue using various platforms. We are now getting budget from World Bank and the municipality for water and sanitation services. We need to use this budget efficiently. The other solution is allocating budget and preparing waste disposal site. Landfill site should be immediately prepared or given to the water and sanitation office. Urban Sanitation Committee has also been established this year by the federal level. And this committee is led by the mayor in urban administration level. Health, Water and Environment protection offices are included in this committee. They also participate on awareness creation work, budget planning and execution process with the municipality which is a very promising thing that has to be continued. 

Previously, when a project is going on, what we used to expect is only its final stage and utility. But nowadays, a trend of working closely with all the responsible bodies and bring awareness change is being practiced. Instead of blaming each other, every sector in the town needs to give priority to water and sanitation service. More collaboration is also needed to have actual change those giveaways benefits to the community. Learning and experience sharing platforms that are being conducted these days by the urban administration office and towns of other weredas so as to bring change to the community should also be strengthened. Wereda offices and Municipality offices are having discussions on water and sanitation issues which are so valuable in order to make decisions on various activities including allocating budget for disposal site preparations and other tasks. 

3. Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

The most important issue that needs to be solved primarily is liquid disposal problem. So, we need to be given a piece of land by the urban administration office so that we will do the infrastructure. There is sewage dumping cars parked in water and sewerage office. They can start work if the dumping site is allocated. If you go to the condominium site, there is problem of sanitation. When the septic tank gets full, there is lot of sanitation problem and a poisonous smell coming out. The place gets stinky. Dirt matter from toilets is carried to the open ditches which brings contamination. There is sewerage system at all in the town. When road or any other infrastructure is done, sewerage system is not given attention. So, this needs to be given attention in the future when doing any infrastructure .Septic tank and treatment plant needs to be bought in the future. With regard to liquid waste disposal, there is nothing done so far. Urban administration and water and Sewerage offices don't even know whose mandates are the work of sanitation. So, there needs to be a way to give awareness creation lessons to all the management Staffs. A kind of platform should be setup that makes the mayor, managers of municipality and water and sewerage offices to participate and make decisions thorough discussions. Since there is no proper dumping site now, disposal are being thrown here and there which may damage other infrastructures. Some infrastructures are having decay due to waste that is disposed on them. So, disposal site an urgent matter to have in urban administration level.
