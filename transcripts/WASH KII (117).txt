1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation/water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Since the population of our town is increasing, we are having sanitation problem with regard to the water service we are rendering to the residents. The main reason for this is due problems that are occurring on the infrastructure. The wastage that come out of hotels and other structures make the water pipelines contaminated with dirt. 
The fact that these areas of water points or schemes are being used for investment, constructing residential houses and other purposes is also one other reason why drinking water is being contaminated. There are also many other reasons that can be mentioned, but using areas of water points or schemes for  other purposes is one that creates the main problem for the contamination of the water. 
Sewage that comes out of hotels and residential houses is carried out by open ditches and get mixed up with the pipeline water, and this contaminates the tap-water. 
 It is not only the tap water pipeline that is being contaminated, but rivers and other water bodies are also being contaminated. For example, there is a river known as Beresa River where the outlets of the liquid waste sewerage from the university and other factories are directly connected to it. So, the river is being polluted. Especially, there is hide and skin factory that is releasing a chemical known chrome through its 2-km sewerage pipe and   polluting the river. While this wastage is flowing through its sewerage pipe, the acidity increases to chrome6 which makes the river water easily contaminated with it. Since the upper part of the river is normally used for drinking and down to the lower part for irrigation, the existing problem of contamination is a threat to this locality. In my opinion, the availability of the river water is crucial not only for consumption but also for the area’s beauty and biodiversity.
There is also a car-driving training center near an underground water well that is located around St. Arsema church. I personally assume that the vehicles’ oil and all other wastages have side effects to the water points that are available there. 
There is also NOC petrol station whose liquid wastage flows down to the well and creates contamination. Oil and petrol leaking out of vehicles can easily bring pollution to the water well that is available in this area.
As you said, there are problems of sustainability both in community sanitation   and personal hygiene in your town? What are the possible causes of these problems?
There are various causes for the problems of sustainable water and sanitation services we have here. We also raised the various problems in a meeting we conducted last time here. Lack of awareness about sanitation by the people living here is one of the problems or causes for the water pollution. Releasing liquid wastages to the river arises from awareness problem. They don’t know about how to use the liquid wastage in a recycled way. 
The local water and sewerage office doesn’t have the mechanism to protect or filter liquid wastage flowing out from different outlets. There is no treatment plant to do that. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

In order to use the available water infrastructure in accordance with the population size here, there is a need to use our resources appropriately. Well this could be an immediate solution. We need to have ultimate usage of the available water and keep it safe from wastage. For instance, there are people who use the drinking water for construction and other purposes, and that is wastage. This is a short term solution that I suggest. 
 In the long run, what I suggest is that, the city administration needs to launch digging or constructing more water points that will sufficiently serve the whole population for the coming fifty to sixty years. 
Another option is using Beresa River to get it constructed and connected to the main water pipeline. Since the upper part of the river is clean, it can be filtered and used as drinking water. What I know so far is that there are only water points along the river side, but the river itself has not been used or connected with the drinking water pipeline. 
 
                 Haven’t they used Beresa River as source of the tap-water, yet?
Not yet. The tap-water is constructed from the underground water that is dug along the sides of the river, but not from the river itself. That is what I know so far.
You have mentioned that there is a problem of using areas of the water scheme for other purposes like car-driving training center, petrol station, and so on. Who do you think is responsible for this? 
The city administration office is responsible for all these problems. For example, our office in collaboration with Water and Sewerage office stopped a hotel construction that was being done against the rule of sewerage guideline. Geologists usually make such a quick observation and give haste recommendations saying there is no problem if road or any other construction is done. However, the city administration needs to conduct proper study before any construction is done. The city administration has to make the water scheme area free from car driving and training centers, as well as petrol stations. They are very much closer to the water scheme and may have side effects in the long run. 

There are also factories in the western direction about one kilometer away from the water scheme. I can’t forecast its long term side effect since it has to be scientifically studied by experts. It is only after we do something wrong that critics arise
Normally, it is the city administration that gives license to the driving center and petrol station, right? There is also environment department in the city administration that studies impact assessment of such service giving centers before providing this place to them? How did they allow this place for such purpose?
The law of environmental protection doesn’t allow such petrol stations to be built near water schemes, but what we follow as a trend in our country is improper way. The car driving training center belongs to the government, and when this site was allocated, only the short term benefit of the place was considered, and no one thought about its long term effects to the water scheme that is available next to it. Water and Sewerage office didn’t even oppose when the place was allocated for the car-driving and petrol station centers. But we  speculate that the vehicles wastage and oil leakages will be absorbed to the underground water in the long run and may contaminate the underground water  after ten or fifteen years.

In front of it, there was also a space that was reserved for hotel and petrol station construction, but we stopped the construction in collaboration with water and sewerage office and other responsible governmental bodies. There is also a larger  open space about 30 hectares of land  that is fenced and reserved for future use of water schemes construction. People may claim there is wastage of land usage, but what we evaluate is the negative impact it may have once it is given for residential house constructions or any other purpose. Since this open space is located in upper part of the existing water scheme, any wastage that is produced is likely to flow down and pollute the water scheme beneath. When land is given for any purpose, it should be interacted with responsible bodies ahead. It should not be given just to satisfy the needs of those land users only. It needs to have an all-rounded survey with the responsible bodies so as to maintain cleanness of water.  

 You mentioned that people have awareness problem with regard to sanitation and the drainages and ditches are not standardized. What do think are the possible solution for these problems?

Giving continuous awareness building lessons to the public so that they will be well equipped with good understanding to transmit it to the next generation.  Awareness building program should not be a one-time activity, but it should be continuously applied so as to bring change.

The other alternative solution or option is to enable the university and other factories dig and construct their own underground water so that they don’t use up the city’s water consumption. So, it would be nice if solutions are set and implemented like this as short term, medium and long term solutions.

Since universities are sources of knowledge, they need to be able to do research and be able to recycle the liquid wastage or filter and let out the wastage so that it would be harmful. Factories should also do the same thing. 

Water and sewerage office must build liquid wastage filtering scheme around hotels. Since hotel owners don’t want to spend money for such sewerage schemes, they tend to release the waste directly to open fields or rivers. Hence, this has to be regulated lawfully by responsible governmental bodies.

 The responsible governmental bodies need to have ground rules where residents need to know before breaching such laws carelessly. Once lessons are properly given to the people, punishment and fines can be used for trespassers based on the rules that are already adopted. 

There are laws at the federal and regional level about environmental protections. What does its implementation look like here at city administration level?

There is the law; but it is only uplifted on proclamation level.  You can’t apply these laws unless detailed directives or manuals are prepared so that each responsible sector such as environmental protection office, municipality, and water and sewerage office can follow and implement the law. It is only the law that is available, but these offices can’t implement the law since there are no detailed directions or manuals that allow them to apply the law even if rules or laws are breached. The city administration has sanitation and beauty work unit that is responsible to follow up sanitary tasks. Doing follow-up and monitoring of the town’s sewerages and ditches should be the specific tasks of this office. If anything is done against the rules, this office needs to have the mandate to close any service giving houses or structures. Water and sewerage office needs to follow up these closed buildings and make the owners be punished legally.

 Environment protection office has the responsibility of executing the law of environmental protection that is adopted nationwide. The Municipality, health office and all other responsible offices need to carry out their own part individually and implement the law collaboratively when the need arises. 
       Whose responsibility is preparing manuals and directions for implementations?
The office on federal level formulates laws and policies and implements. It has also the power to prepare manuals and directions so that these manuals will go down to zonal and wereda levels and get implemented. Unless the federal law formulator gives delegations to the regional offices to prepare detailed manuals according to its own regional context, the mandate of manual preparations is the federal level office itself. Nevertheless, most of governmental laws are still don’t have manuals, hence their implementation is so poor. For instance, if Forestry and Climate Ministry prepares new proclamations with regard to forest protection, it shall also formulate detailed manuals for the implementation of the proclamation. But the actual practice is quite different from the existing laws. There is a serious problem with respect to implementation. Though there is a possibility that the federal government refers its delegation and power to the regional offices to prepare manual, the Ethiopian Ministry of Land Administration and Environmental protection has given its power or assignment to the regional offices to prepare manual. 

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

The first important thing is to construct liquid waste sewerages and ditches and prepare filtering schemes so that the liquid waste will be filtered before it is released to the ditches. Instead of releasing the liquid waste to rivers and other natural water bodies and polluting them, it would be wise to use proper sewerage system. If this is done, we can protect the rivers and other water bodies from pollution and use the water for drinking and other purposes. 
The second point is building the public’s awareness. There is a need to enrich the people with good understanding so that they won’t dispose of wastes to the water points and rivers.  Even if there is a law, it will be difficult to implement it unless thorough discussions and awareness building lessons are given to the public. 
The other possible solution is to look for other water points and do water scheme construction for the dwellers of the town.
Doing proper maintenance on the water infrastructures. There is usually leakage of water from pipelines and wastage of water everywhere, hence, this should be given consideration and maintenance should be given with good follow-ups. Some of the water pipes that are installed do suit to the pressure of the water. The pipelines maybe either too small or too big so that the water flow won’t be compatible to the wideness of the pipes. There is a lot of breakage or blockage along the pipelines, too. So, this itself has to be carefully identified and properly done. 
There has to be congruency among the installations of water pipes, electric lines, telephone lines and roads. The new land use policy being formulated on federal level is considering all the congruency of these four infrastructural activities. For instance, when road is being built, care has to be taken with regard to the other three infrastructures. The road should not create damage to any water pipeline or any other infrastructure. So, all the four offices need to work collaboratively so that they should not create any negative impact one another.
