1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion, in Woliso Woreda, what are the primary problems that make for a shortage of water? What are the main reasons for this shortage from your vantage point.
Response: In Woliso when the release the water, there was a small population that lived in Woliso. Nowadays, the population is large and growing. So water coverage is very small. They do no consider the growing population and work to provide coverage for everyone. I think this is the main reason.
Interviewer: The main problem of the current water supply and the population is not balancing. As the population grows, there is no infrastructure regarding the water. Is that what you mean?
Response: Yes, that is the problem.
Interviewer: What are the other problems?
Response: I don’t think there is a problem using the water. Maybe there is a problem with the pipes in the ground. If they are damaged, that might be part of it. I can see sometimes when they break, water streams out onto the roads. The only problem is in the city there is a balancing problem. If they make a new design regarding the growing population, the issue will be solved.

2.What ideas or recommendation do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What do you recommend to work on in order to supply enough water for the population.
Response: We should use another resource. We only have one reservoir currently. It was built a long time ago. In that time, the population was very small. When you compare with now, the population is about three times larger. But the water supply has remained the same. So we should work more on enlarging supply.
Interviewer: To build this, who has to contribute? Can this only be built by government? Or can other organizations do so?
Response: Whoever is involved, the government or NGOs, if they have the budget and work at it, it can be solved.
[Time Stamp: 5 minutes]

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? What next steps could happen…?

Interviewer: In addition, you said it will be good if they build more infrastructure. So if this happens, what change will come?
Response: Well, if they work, it will be nice, but in the current situation, people are suffering to get enough drinking water. For example, kebele 02 is getting water maybe once in a whole month. Plus, if people use water regularly, and if there is any standard, such as how many liters each household may use. Maybe they are using water for showers or laundry. If they use properly it might help somewhat. There are a lot of hotels that use a surplus of water for toilets, showers, etc. This thing causes the shortage of drinking water. If this problem is solved, we might get enough water.
Interviewer: So you mean if the population gets enough water, that means they can take care of hygiene and sanitation. Is that what you’re saying?
Response: yes, it is.
Interviewer: So what benefit will they get? Regarding unity and economy, if the service become larger, what change will arrive in the community?
Response: If people are concerned regarding their hygiene, they will improve their lives, make them more healthy. The can grow vegetables in their compound. If they have enough water, we even can use it to have a garden. This makes a city more beautiful.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water services? What could be improved?

Interviewer: My other question is to improve sustainability of the current water supply, what does the current coordination of government offices and non-government offices look like?
Response: The current coordination is not enough. If they collaborate, who will do the design and administrate the projects. If they collaborate, they must do the research. How much water does the city need? If they work on this, give it to the government, I hope things will be solved. Whatever design is created, a responsible leader must be picked from those organizations mentioned. As a project, there are a lot of sectors who control the budget, the design and the quality. If they do not work on this, people might not get water, which is needed urgently. When they work on this, they must assume a future population that is much larger.

5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

Interviewer: To conclude the question, in the current climate for coordination, what do you think must be done? To work in the future on water supply, what must they do to improve?
[Time Stamp: 10 minutes]
Response: The coordination, as I said, First of all they must perform extensive research regarding the expected future population and the current water supply. The other thing is if we know the current supply, we can distribute specified amounts for particular areas. This will tell us how much water we need in the future. In the future, we will create a design that fulfills the expectation for future population growth. For example, if we plan for 10 years, we should expect the precise amount of people the future infrastructure will need to support. After we have done all this, we will make a budget. If they give us the money, we, the sector bureau, can work effectively. The only thing that is needed is collaboration.
Interviewer: My last question is in your opinion, if there is better coordination that you mentioned earlier, the balancing of supply and demand of drinking water will be solved?
Response: The organizational aim is if they get NGOs that help them with the budget, the work will be more effective and move faster. Because they follow up and look at the problems, which tends to reduce the causes of those problems. You know what, if we build a design that incorporates future population growth, it will be of a higher quality. For example, we are working on the inspection. That design, if it doesn’t retain high quality standards, we do not approve it. There is another organization that works with us. Regarding supervision or funding, when we collaborate, everything turns out better.
