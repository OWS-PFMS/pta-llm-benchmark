
In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda?

The main problems to water service sustainability are: [site selection] mostly rural water schemes are being developed without appropriate site selection. That is most of the water schemes constructed in areas where there is poor potential of ground water. Because of this the water schemes dried immediately after the completion of the construction. In some case water schemes are developed in lowlands that are susceptible for flood. Because of this when there is rainfall flood directly enter into the water schemes and the water become contaminated. [Misuse of water schemes] the community mishandle the water schemes and most of the water schemes easily become not functional within short period of time. And they do not be repaired immediately.

What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

Related to site selection, there should be comprehensive study to identify areas that have enormous potential of ground water that can serve the community throughout the year. The study should be conducted by qualified experts that have experience in the sector. Pertinent to misuse of water schemes, there are individuals that are selected in the kebele to monitor and repair the water schemes as well as to treat the water with chlorine. In collaboration with these individuals other stakeholders should engage in awareness creation on proper use of water schemes. And make the community to feel ownership of the water schemes. Moreover, we should aware the community about the possible consequences of staying without clean water. 

Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 

Relatively the problems related to misuse of water schemes is minimal, however, the problem related to site selection is very serious. Most of the water schemes became non-functional due to improper site selection. That means the water schemes are developed in areas where there is poor ground water potential and they became unable to supply water sustainably. Therefore, in my opinion the main solution to ensure sustainable water service is proper site selection for the development of water schemes.  Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? First, the water schemes can serve the community for long period of time. For instance, there are many non-functional water schemes in the woreda, thus, if this solution occurs, we can minimize waste of resources. Second, we can alleviate the community’s problem related to sustainable water supply. Moreover, we can reduce the occurrence of water born diseases.

In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 

At woreda level I can say the existing coordination is good. Particularly the woreda water office is working in coordination with both governmental and non-governmental organizations. The water office provide information to other organizations about the priority areas (kebeles) that require water supply. Moreover, they discuss with the community and kebele administrators about planned project that are going to be implemented in the kebele and arrive on agreement. And cooperate with water project implementing organization on access to raw materials as well as by mobilizing the community. Thus, the existing coordination at woreda level is in a good status and we have no problem in this regard. What could be improved?  Sometimes there is information gap between stakeholders. For example, I have no information in advance about your visit to our office. If we have been informed about the purpose of your visit to our office in advance, we can prepare ourselves to provide responses very well. The other thing is that there is coordination gaps in designing work plan of the sector. Work plans are not be communicated to stakeholders before implementation. However, if we discuss on plans, we can easily identify priority areas and give emphasis to these areas. We should have internal strategies that is communicated to all stakeholders in prioritizing activities. We should discuss on which activity should come first and which activity should come next. Thus, information should be given to stakeholders on priority areas for intervention.

In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

When I say coordination can lead to more sustainable water service in the woreda, for example, in case of site selection, if there is coordination between stakeholders, we can have appropriate information about areas that has potential water resource. Thus, this may enable us to provide sustainable water service to the community. When there is horizontal and vertical coordination between stakeholders, we can ensure sustainable water service.

The coordination can also solve the two problems that I mentioned earlier. First, in case of site selection, if the water experts coordinate with the community, they can grasp the community’s indigenous knowledge. Because the community can identify areas that can give water during dry season. Thus, the community can help the scientific investigation using its traditional knowledge.

Related to misuse of water schemes, coordination with woreda stakeholders and kebele administrators can help in enhancing the community awareness about proper use of water schemes. Moreover, coordination between stakeholders can facilitate access to spare parts and other necessary materials for maintenance. Thus, coordination can lead to more sustainable water service.
