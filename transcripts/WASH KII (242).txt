1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water and sanitation service sustainability in your town? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Water supply and demand imbalance
The existing water facilities are established long years ago and have the capacity to serve smaller segment of the town. On the other hand, the population of the town currently increased tremendously as a result the town’s Water and Sewerage Utility Office encountered a serious problem to supply sufficient water regularly to the community and forced to announce a water rationing system especially during the dry season of the year. Moreover, farmers engaged in small-scale irrigation at the periphery of the town extensively use the river that is used as a main water supply source of the town. This irrigation practice further aggravated the water supply shortage of the town particularly during dry season. As a result, households have access to water only for 2 or 3 days per week.

II.Absence of temporary and permanent landfill site for both solid and liquid wastes
There is no permanent and temporary liquid and solid waste disposal place in Woliso town. This is a serious challenge to provide sustainable sanitation service to the community. For instance, even though the town’s Water and Sewerage Utility Office have a purpose built vehicles to collect liquid wastes, they do not provide service to the community due to absence of appropriate waste disposal place in the town.  
The town municipality did not arrange a temporary waste collection sites and facilities to gather wastes from the community and dispose outside the town in a regular basis. Due to the absence waste disposal places, the community simply dispose its wastes in rivers of the town. This improper waste disposal practice of the community highly contaminated the rivers that are used by the community itself for household consumption as well as for farming of fruits and vegetables. In addition, it exposed the community for various types of health problems. 

III.Improper use of communal latrines
The residents of kebele 03 have no private latrine rather they mainly depend on communal latrines. However, the community does not properly manage these communal latrines. They do not regularly clean them due to shortage of water supply in the area as well as negligence of the users. This created serious sanitation and health problem in the kebele. 

Although the community contributes money for the emptying of the pits or latrines, they could not get the service due to absence of liquid waste disposal place in and around the town. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
I.Pertinent to the problem related to water supply and demand imbalance the short run solution is that the Water Utility Office should introduce a fair water distribution shift system or rationing system. For example, introducing a shift in which a given household can get water supply every other day or every 3 days. Parallel with this the community should be provided with awareness enhancement training on proper and efficient utilization of this scares resource. As a long-term solution, we would like to suggest that the ongoing water project that is under implementation with financial support of World Bank should be finalized as soon as possible and provide service to the community. 

II.Regarding the challenges related to absence of permanent and temporary landfill sites or waste disposal place, we would like to suggest that the town’s municipality should consider the issue as a top priority and work in collaboration with other stakeholders to develop appropriate waste disposal place in the town. In addition, it should prepare temporary waste disposal places until the problem has get long-term sustainable solution. Besides, the community should undertake regular cleaning campaign at least every month to minimize sanitation related problems.

III.Finally, to alleviate the challenges related to use of communal latrines, the town administration should develop appropriate liquid waste disposal sites so that the community can easily empty the latrines when they become full. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Developing appropriate temporary and permanent waste disposal site is the most important solution to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. This is mainly because sanitation is the base to prevent the community from different forms of health problems. If this problem has get a solution:
oThe community can live in a safe and healthy environment;
oThe community will be prevented from various health problems that occurs due to sanitation problems; and
oIt is very essential to minimize environmental, sanitation and hygiene related problems of the town.
