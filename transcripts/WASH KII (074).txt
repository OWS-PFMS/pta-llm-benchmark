2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer:
Response: Fist the industries have standards in how, in what kind and in what condition to dispose wastes which they have presented and approved with annual renewal requirement, so making follow up and the other is giving them places/area to dispose the wastes based on the agreement and giving solutions when there are issues. Some say we don’t have place to dispose our wastes. The government hospital should be inspected based on the standard of waste disposal, and should have place of disposal for the current disposal area is in the city center which is a problem in rainy season the floor will take it and disperse it everywhere in the city areas. So, the hospital should change the area of waste disposal. The wastes will join the rivers and the river is useful for the people down the stream for their animals to drink and themselves, wash their cloths. The chemical used to remove hair in the leather industry has brought problems on the people who use the river water down the stream. Protecting this water bodies have important implication since these river will join the Abay River. We are building dam on Abay river but the treatment should be made on source.

The transportation means made for the solid wastes are being used to transport construction items because; in simple way they move the items and get money. They form a group and ask for the solid waste disposal transportation means but, they won’t do what they have assigned for, following that the city is full of waste we can say.
