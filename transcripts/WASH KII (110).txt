1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Water and Sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?

1.1The first problem is the inability to carry out tasks based on scientific plan and design. While the city administration has been carrying out developmental activities on housing projects, it didn’t give attention to the infrastructural projects. The housing project should not be accomplished without doing prior and proper work on the city’s infrastructure. It is the main cause of the existing sanitary problems in the city.
1.2While housing agency was doing urban expansion work, it hasn’t been doing infrastructural activities properly. Infrastructural activities are not outreached to the new neighborhoods that have been rising in the town. No improvement work has been done on those localities that had some infrastructural structures.
Interviewer: what are the problems related to sanitation? Can you explain it to me?
1.3Debreberhan town has started developing since six or seven years back, or maximum 10 years back. Prior to that, sanitation was not a problem since the level of the developmental activities and migration of people to the town was so limited. So, the flourishing of factories is the main cause of increasing developmental and business activities. Size of population has also increased significantly. Since the activities that have been done to support the infrastructure of the town are so limited, there is a gap between the capacities of the infrastructural structures and the volume of liquid and solid waste byproducts that are being released from residential houses and factories. Since there are no sufficient structures that let sewages get drained out to the proper dumping site out of town, the problem of sanitation and hygiene is evidently seen.

1.4There is no standardized and planned land-fill structure where solid waste can be dumped, recycled and reused for some other use. There is only a place where waste is thrown and stored temporarily as a means to dispose of the waste away from the living areas.


1.5There is lack of complete understanding about sanitation and hygiene by the society. People have come to understand how to keep their own personal hygiene, but most people have lower understanding about the benefit of community sanitation. That is the main problem of most of the people. What they do is just to clean their own houses and themselves and throw the waste out to the road or communal places. They don’t realize that the waste they have thrown would be a problem to them, gradually.

1.6There is no legal framework and practice to maintain laws of environmental protection in the town. So, the public do whatever they think is right for them. Law of environmental protection is not implemented.

1.7The small-scale enterprises that are doing sanitary activities don’t outreach their services to all the areas of the town. The coverage of their services is limited. Because of this, there are hand-pulled carts that are doing this activity on their own way. In fact, people pay them for the service they are rendering to the people. In general, the service that is being given to the public by the water and sewerage office is not sufficient at all. Both liquid waste and solid waste management is so poor. With regard to liquid waste management, our town doesn’t have dumping site at all. Now people have trend of paying money to private agents and getting rid of both solid and liquid waste away from their houses. But no one knows where it is going and dumped. Those private agents illegally dump the wastes somewhere out of the town’s territory. There is no proper and legal place that is allocated for dumping sewages, nowadays.
Interviewer: To what extent do these problems affect the sustainability of sanitation and water service? And why are these activities lacking in your town? What is the reason behind?
People are not aware of the benefits of waste if recycled and reused scientifically. We need to educate people about this. In order to make the people understand about the benefits of waste and show how waste can be source of wealth, practical work and demonstration should be done with the public and small-scale enterprises. If people see this activity, they will have attitudinal change. Well, in order to do this, the responsible governmental body should allocate sufficient place and give supports for its practicability. The master plan of the town didn’t include this idea in its strategic plan previously. It was included in its plan recently. That is why the dumping site is allocated and given outside the town now. Since it is far from the town, it has become difficult to use it. There are no proper infrastructures and vehicles to use easily. Because of this difficulty, we are using smaller hand-pulled carts to collect solid waste and dump in a common place even if it is not appropriate. It is also polluting the environment; hence, sustainability   of the service is negatively affected.
With the fund we are getting from World Bank, we have conducted a study on solid waste dumping project similar to the one in Legedadi area (Addis Ababa). It is a scheme that can be used for 25 years. However, the document is on the shelf since we need the fund to go ahead with the project. If the town wants to use the fund it has received from World Bank, it can run the project and get it ready, but its duration to use the dump will be limited to five years. So, we are still waiting for fund grant in order to implement the project for twenty five years. We can do it in different phases, but we haven’t decided to do like that.

Interviewer: When you prepare the project proposal, didn’t you indicate where you can get the source of the fund? It could be private entities, government offices, World Bank itself, the city administration, zonal office, and so on? Don’t you think the project can even be done with collaborative work?
Dashen Brewery, Habesha brewery and other factories were using the previous land-fill dumping site together with the public, but they stopped when the land was filled. No land rehabilitation is done, so we can’t use it anymore.  So, these two breweries don’t have proper site for solid waste dumping now; therefore, we are doing a collaborative work with them. Recently, we have almost finished preparing a temporary dumping site where we all can dump solid waste. It is a kind of land-fill scheme. Road and other facilities are almost fulfilled; we will start using it soon. But that is a temporary dumping site. We haven’t done any collaborative work with the city administration or any other government offices with regard to this. What we have planned now is to start the grand projects that will serve for 25 years phases by phase next year. We are having such an alternative plan to do some part of the project in the first phase, and then continue doing the rest in next phases.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

2.1Changing attitudes: First of all, we can’t eradicate waste or dirt all in all unless we plan and avoid from its source. The source could be human’s mind. If we don’t bring attitudinal change to people first, trying to eradicate dirt from our surroundings would be an emotional work or a temporary campaign which doesn’t last longer. We need to build people’s awareness about the importance of sanitation and hygiene. The most important thing is to do work on the ‘software’ part of human beings-mind. Just because the people don’t like waste, it can’t bring profound change to our community by itself. 


2.2We need to do on awareness building activities so that the people will be able to sort out and differentiate solid waste that can be decomposed or not, chemicals and non-chemical wastes, and so on before bringing them out to the garbage area. It is good if we educate them how and where they will do the sorting work. In fact, arranging such kind of mechanism needs time, place and financial source. The city administration will be in such a tough situation until this is fulfilled. In order to meet this scheme, we need to have a secured mechanism of doing the tasks. 

2.3This series of activities can be accomplished as follows: creating small-scale enterprises that do the task of collecting the solid waste from the neighborhoods and dump it at a temporary storage center where our office again can outsource the work of taking this garbage to private agents who use their own dump trucks and transport it to some other area where the waste could be dumped in a land-fill center. There is a federal law of environmental protection that allows us to charge individuals for the service they are getting from waste collectors and dumpers. It will be charge upon the volume of the waste they are bringing to dispose of. There is a kind of study conducted on how we can carry out this task. We also took experience sharing from Debrezeit town. We have also reached on a consensus to collect the fees together with water bill through water and sewerage office. In the long run, the income we will get from this service can be invested on the solid waste recycling scheme so that we will get higher amount of wealth from selling the byproduct of the converted waste. This is what we have planned based on the research we have studied so far. 

2.4Infrastructure is the basic issue before launching water and sanitation projects; therefore, the two departments in Urban Development office -Urban Sanitation and Beauty office and Urban Land Bank need to plan feasible plan compatible with their visions they have already put in place. Instead of conducting meetings and blaming each other, all of us need to be responsible for our missions and accomplishments following monitoring and evaluation system. In our office, there is a problem with the administrative team, but not with the technical team. The administrative team usually takes a different view when a certain work process is going on. If a clear mission is not directed to the technical team, there will be confusion and the result won’t be fruitful. So, I recommend that either a middle team is required to connect the two and create a harmonious working atmosphere. Their work performance can also be easily evaluated, eventually. These workers need an atmosphere that let them do upon what they have understood and be given comments when something wrong is done based on monitoring and evaluation system.


Interviewer: With respect to infrastructure, I understand that population growth and capacity of water and sewerage service are not going hand in hand. Why was this not included in the long term plan of the city ahead? Who is also responsible for this? What should be done in the future, too?

2.5Our town is a beneficiary of the World Bank program that supports sustainable water, sanitation and hygiene service. We plan to implement this program every year though we need to wait until we get fund to run the projects. What the world bank wants us in the long run is to run the projects by ourselves using our own income which we can generate from the various projects we have planned so far. We have problem of presenting our projects to the grant providers in a convincing manner.  We have such a serious sanitation and hygiene problem, so we should be able to catch fund granters’ curiosity with this issue instead of prioritizing issues of small scale job creation in most case. 

3.Of the solutions you listed, which one is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

The most important task to get this result is awareness creation work. Training should be given to those who are directly involved in activities related to solid waste collection and dumping instead of focusing on discussions with politicians. Well politicians will help a lot if they are aware of it, but first the emphasis should be to the people or enterprises that work on the solid waste collection work itself. There are enterprises who have bought truck just by doing solid collection work. There are also individuals who earn income by preparing compost from collected waste and selling it. Hence, the awareness program should be strengthened much more than this so that we will make significant result water, sanitation and hygiene, in general.
