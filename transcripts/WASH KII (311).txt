1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation/water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
1.1Increament of population/consumers. Now it has become challengeing to provide the service for all service users at a time. We couldd’t distribute the water fairly which is resulting in public disobeyance everywhere. Though the people are expected to to the service in turn/ shift that is not even scheduled properly. Added to this, our supervision to control wastage is limited which is resulted due to increasing prices to water infrastructures, for example, previously metal pipe were being used but now they are replaced by PPC, pipees made from plastic.  Due to this problem,  the quality water is beoming very low as we observe damaged ppc every where. Not only the quality is deteriorating but there is also high wastage of the available resource.  
1.2The other problem is, lack of alternative sources of water. I personally feel that we are doing well to accommodate the population growth with limited water supply. The number of service benificiaries have been increasing from time to time but the service is still stagnant without any improvement. Limited efforts have been made to look for alternative ways to increase the supply so far. 
1.3Regarding sanitation, the first problem is our attitude. We observe that most people leak their toilets with the sewearage system, dispose liquid and solid waste materials unwisely.
1.4The other problem is lack of liquid waste disposal system. Currently there are adequate vehicles made available for liquid waste disposalin the town, but the problem is there is no damping sites to dispose and the vehicles are stying idle. For the solid waste, both the vehicles and land fill sites are not available as to my knowledge. 
Due to absence of liqude waste disposal places, people are illegally leaking their toilet with the main swarage  system which has long been contaminating the existing water and procuding a bad smell out of it. 
1.5, Some of the households use open defication. Especially households around 03 kebele live in a very congested area and forced to use communal toilets. Due to over population in this area, their toilet is not clean, they do not use it properly, as you know, we all have problem in managing communal resources or assets. 
As the result of this, we can say that absence of private toilet is the main problem of the low level  hygine and sanitation in our community. Due to this I personally can not confidently speak out that our community’s sanitation and hygin is in a good mood. Sometimes, I am ashamed of mentioning it. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
2.1 In order to improve the supply of water, all responsible government offices and in particular water and sanitation office should work reguriously. To make it more specific, they have to work closely together with other non government organizations who are working on WASH. In addition, they have to work on awareness creation for the community on how to utilize the existing water efficiently. 
2.2 My seond recommendation is for the water and sanitation and environmental offices since their regulare job is related with WASH services.  They have to look for alternative sources of water, be it from springs/rivers and plan for additional water infrastructures and piplines for water. This will gradually increase the provision of safe water for the community. 
2.3 The safety of the water that every indivduals consume in their home should also be improved. Pipelines have to be cleaned periodically and it needs a regular follow up from the water and sanitation office. We observe that wastage  of water is a common phenomena every where as the result of very old and damaged piplines, however, concerned officials do not maintain it timely/respond for the request of the community.  

Why do you think that they do not respond timely? 
I think the first thing is lack of commitment from their side, they are not responsible enough. In addition, there is also shortage of human resources/expertise when we compare with the services needed to cover total population in our town. Some times, we see that expertise start maintaining damaged pipelines and other infrastructures but at the same time they stope working before finishing everything. Their argument is that there is limited supply of inputs/ materials for maintenance. 
2.4 Regarding the liquid and solid waste disposal problem:. I think the town administration/municipality should give serious attention and address this problem. I know that preparing a place for liquid and solid waste disposal could not be the final solution by itself. It actually needs intervention in the practical problems happening in the community in collecting and disposing the wastes. As we give greater attention to planting trees, hygine and sanitation has to be addressed in a more organized and committed manner.  Now the good opportunity we are observing in the town is the availablility of development agents that are operating in WASH and environment related projects, so it is the up to the municipality to reach out these groups and find ways to closely work in the sanitation program. Preparing weekly environmental cleaning program could be one strategy which allows the whole community to take part of the mobilization.  At this occation, the Mayor herself  should be part of the program as it encourages popular participation and initiates everyone to give attention to their own matters.  Accordingly, weekly environmental sanitation program initiated by the development agents and regular monthly program incluing  the Mayor will help to intensively mobilize the community on sanitation issues and tackle the problem gradually.
