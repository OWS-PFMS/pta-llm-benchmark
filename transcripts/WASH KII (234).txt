1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: the main problem is lack of damping site. Even if the woreda representatives said we get a place, and they paid around 400,000 birr as a compensation for the land lords, the people are not volunteer to give that place for damping purpose. Because of this, they left it as it was and now decided to change the site to another place. This is becoming a big problem.
In the communal latrine, people are paying their monthly duty. Even if they collect that money, they can’t dare to bring a sewage suction vehicle. Because there is no damping site. Are they going to travel the waste to tulubolo or where? Our people are in a trouble now. 4 of the communal latrine are full now, because they can do anything with their money. The damping site problem has become a main problem for a long time. 
Interviewer: you said that they paid around 400,000 birr for the compensation of that place. May be the reason for the people around are not volunteer is because the place is in the rural area. Can you tell me if there is any commitment or managerial related problems?
Respondent: that place was selected with an agreement of both woreda and city administration persons .they make discussions and convinced the people about it and the people accepted the request first. That was why they start paying. Then some of them rejected to use that place for damping site of the whole city wastes and they changed it to a political case. The government chooses to remain quiet until the case calms down; because they already make it political issue. Now, the zonal, woreda and city administrations are discussing about it to come to a solution.
2What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Respondent: the major solution is we should find a damping site whatever it costs. The city administration and we should focus on it and take immediate action to solve this problem. So, we should discuss with the community to solve that problem or try to get another place if there is.
Interviewer: do you think there can be awareness problem with the people? If so, what can be the solution for it?
Respondent: Our people have enough awareness regarding sanitary issues. If they made mistakes and throw garbage carelessly, they make it intentionally. The people are paying to privately organized youths and avoid the garbage; the problem is with the liquid wastes. There is also water access shortage.
Interviewer: can you tell me if there is any commitment and integration problems among the organizations? If so, what do you think can be the solution?
Respondent: we work in collaborate. But Most of the time, the city administrators and decision makers are not participating in our discussion and meetings. This will held to take a long time for the solution. This year was a year that we give more attention on sanitation and we even discussed with federal persons. Some people also travel to other places to share what others practice looks like. They also raised the gaps. But, people from the city administration didn’t participate regularly in such programs. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: let us say that your main damping site problem is solved. What changes do you think will occur in the city sanitation?
Respondent: our city will be clean and people will be free from diseases and be healthy. For example, children around the bus station will not be healthy. Because it has a bad odder when you pass through that way. So, I can say that the solution for damping site problem will also solve other related problems.
