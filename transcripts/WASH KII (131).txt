1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda? 
Interviewer: What do you think are he challenges of water service sustainability in your woreda?
Respondent: Due to budget issues there are some areas in our town that do not have access to clean drinking water. However, there are sustainability problems even in the areas where water is currently available, including among the WaSHCOs.
Interviewer: What are the problems related to the WaSHCos?
Respondent: There is, for instance, problems related to cash collection from the community. If the water requires generator community members do not continuously pay their share according to the WaSHCO instructions. There is also some disagreement among the community on who should be the members of the WaSHCo. 
Besides, there are occasions that some community members damage the water pipes when some political disputes rise. 
Interviewer: Why do they cut it?
Respondent: They think they hurt the local government officials by doing so. 
Interviewer: What about in the town?
Respondent: There is shortage of clean water supply
Interviewer: What about in the town?
Respondent: There is shortage of clean water supply. The way the water distribution system is installed is not fair that all residents are not having access to potable water not equal. There are neighborhoods that access water twenty four hours while others get once per week. In the rural areas it is much better.


2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What should be done to solve the major issues that you have raised? 
Respondent: Majority of the water projects are built by partners. Therefore encouraging these donors to engage more in water scheme development is vital for increasing the access of potable water. 
Interviewer: The problem could not be addressed only by the partners. Woreda and regional government structures should play their role, right?
Respondent: That is right. Sector office up to the federal government should discharge their responsibility. The woreda administration should address local administration issues. It should make sure that the currently existing water is being utilized properly and reasonably. The woreda administration should also support WaSHCOs at kebele level. If training is needed, training should be given. 
Interviewer: You have said that there is a problem of not listening to one another among members of the WaSHCos. What ought to be done to address this?
Respondent: Community members should be consulted on who should be the members from the beginning and then these members who are selected by the community should be trained. If the members are elected by the community and they are properly trained, the problem will be solved.
Interviewer: The other problem is lack of awareness. Some community members cut water pipelines with the intention to hurt local government cabinet members. What is the solution for this?
Respondent: The solution is to create necessary awareness and sense of ownership that the water infrastructure is the property of the locality not any government official. 
Interviewer: Who is the responsible one to do so?
Respondent:  The water utility office and kebele leadership should take the responsibility. Health office is also discharge its responsibility, as many water problems lead to health problems. 
Interviewer: You have also said that the water distribution in the town is not fair. How could this be addressed?


Respondent: This is a management issue.
Interviewer: What is the reason behind that one neighborhood get water properly and not the other?
Respondent: There are different water pipelines in the town. Pipelines that people with money or power use different line from the rest of the community. This is the reason behind. Authorities beyond woreda administration could solve this issse.
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Out of the solutions you have proposed, what is the most important one to address the problem of water service provision in a sustainable manner?
Respondent: If we solve the problems in the rural areas that would alleviate the problem of majority of the community. The issues in the town are just administrative or managerial issues. 
Interviewer: What do you think would happen if this management problem is addressed? 
Respondent: It might not address all the water problems but, residents would be able to have access fairly. And clean water would not be wasted unnecessarily.
