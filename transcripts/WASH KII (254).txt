1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: For the formality, let me ask you a question. The first question is in your opinion, as a city why can’t we address the society’s full needs of a drinking water supply? What is the main challenge?
Response: The first thing is because farmers are using irrigation, reservoirs are going dry. We only have the well water. The whole population is using the wells instead. This water does not meet the city’s water standards. So most of the time, the reservoir dries up during the summer. 
Interviewer: Do the farmer’s use the reservoir water?
Response: This reservoir is divided into eight irrigation streams. After they diverted it in eight irrigation streams, the leftover water comes to the city users. This allotment is too small for the city. We are having a problem, because most of the water is diverted to the farmers. This problem is causing a huge difficulty for the urban people.
Interviewer: The water bureau guy mentioned this problem to me. The main problem is using the traditional system. Ok, this is the first thing. But what is the second issue.
Response: The second problem is that the city size and water supply is not appropriate. The city is very large, but the water supply only comes from two locations. The contradiction between demand and supply – this is the problem. There are many neighborhoods [indecipherable] with many households that do not have any water. They have a water line, and they pay every month, but they don’t receive any water. They only pay for the water line. Plus we have only one water tanker.
Interviewer: Okay, another thing.
Response: We only see the internal problems. Maybe externally they have a problem. Maybe the city budget is the problem, and this must first be addressed. They have less activity due to these funding issues. For this they should gather the people and make them aware of these problems and shortages. They must be mobilized.
Interviewer: Is this the water office?
Response: Yes, they are providing a service, and they do a good job. They have to involve the community and ask the people what they want to do to solve this issue. The water bureau has said this is all the water we have. They don’t want to work further or investigate. This is very surprising. Woliso is facing an enormous water shortage. It’s very amazing. It only has one soap factory. What if we have a lot more factories in the future? The shortage will become even worse. And people go to the villages (outside the city) and bring well water with the yellow, plastic tanks. The only water supply is for those living in the middle of the city, because that was where the first water pipes were laid. The places where people are living now outside the city center are seeing a heavy shortage. 

2.What ideas or recommendation do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewer: Is that because of the newly-built houses? We mentioned 4 problems. Let’s take them one by one to talk about solutions. We should talk about who is responsible for each problem. Both you and the water bureau mentioned this problem. So what do you think the solutions are?
Response: The solution, hmmmm. At one time there was a water container at the Kebele farmer’s association, and the leader of the community controlled the water distribution from the container. The farms nowadays are used to getting all their water from the irrigation ditches. Again, the government’s aim is to reduce poverty. Currently, the government is giving awards to the best farmers. Because of that we are challenged. On the one hand, policy has been the problem. On the second hand, the farmers have to leave more water for the rest of society, because people are dying from lack of water. [Time stamp: 5 minutes] They farm onions, potatoes and so on. But now they have stopped, because people are dying. A police officer stopped them, because they place a cost on the rest of us. They cause this shortage.
Interview: you are saying there is no solution?
Response: The solution is building an understanding between the farmers and the city population. Last time they talked, the city government and the village collaborated and worked to have water supplied once per month to fill the city’s water tanker. That will be the solution.
Interviewer: Very good.
Response: The second thing is, we forget the responsible person is the city electric bureau. They must be punished. So we are facing a lot of challenges. The finance & economic bureau also has a part to play, just like the electric bureau. 
Interviewer: Because of the electricity is needed for water pump generation?
Response: yes, because the discontinuity of electricity also makes it difficult.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water services? What could be improved?

Interviewer: Our major agenda is supplying drinking water to the whole city. What is the plan for the long term? You mentioned the problem and the solution. From all that you mentioned as solutions, which one is the most important?
Response: The water bureau and all major stakeholders must have a meeting and discuss the problem. Those stakeholders, such as the city administration, the population and the community, as well as us, should discuss and decide the solution. Because of the budget shortage, they don’t cover all the water customers and areas. 
Interviewer: Is there any coordination among organizations currently? Regarding water, what does that look like? That coordination. Do they work together?
Response: This is what is affecting the population. Especially the water bureau, they act as if they are NGOs. They don’t participate in meetings. And they don’t have any contribution to this discussion. There is a huge gap.
Interviewer: How will you work to change this? What do you think?
Response: They must collaborate and create a plan that incorporates all stakeholders. Second, they have to sign an agreement to work together on this. There is a charter, which the government created. They must use this. 
Interviewer: Who are the stakeholders? All these different bureaus, you mean?
Response: All board members. Even if I don’t know when they are having the meeting, the city mayor will announce the meeting time. The health office is also one of the board members. They also must contribute to this plan.
Interviewer: Who are those board members? Is it the city administration, the health bureau, the ministry of education, is that all?
Response: There are five. 
Interviewer: Oh they are the ones in charge? Why is the Kebele not involved? What is the main idea of this board?
Response: Those five board members are leading the water bureau. You know Girma is a manager. They put him on the board of the water bureau. Girma is the one who works on financing and hiring. He processes the information and projects before sending it to the board.
Interviewer: Who is the board chairman?
Response: It’s the mayor.
Interviewer: So how does Girma act? What does he do?
Response: He is on the board and working as the general manager at the water bureau.
Interviewer: So this collaboration needs to create a plan. So what is the experience until now?
Response: I don’t know if they have created a plan or not, because I’m not a board member.
