
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Service and demand mismatch: Water is very important for our day to day activities. It has a lot of importance to our well being, but in this city factors like ever increasing urbanization, increasing number of city dwellers , expansion of residential structures, flood vulnerabilities and expansion of investment activities is becoming a challenge  for sustainable water and sanitation.
Living standard: Another challenge comes with low level of economic wellbeing (living standard):- due to low level of economic status the community is not affording to get basic sanitation items like bathing buckets, piped water basin installed to their kitchen and other important items is compromising the sanitation and hygiene status of the community. The increasing rate of   population growth ,rural-urban migration , lack of awareness about the value of  water  is becoming a serious challenge that is affecting  water & sanitation  sustainability and  un -coordinated effort of stakeholders is also exacerbating the problem.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

Water and Sanitation office: should strive a lot to meet the water supply adequacy and quality which can be done by expanding the necessary water infrastructure. The other way to address the problem is making factories or manufacturing industries have their own treatment plant because untreated wastes of factories are hurting the society in different ways.

Stakeholders such as the health sector, the kebele cabinet and other NGOS working in WASH related activities need to be coordinated more than ever.  

This can also be addressed by giving sanitation kits like bathing buckets; (actually our kebele has done it in alliance with the Deberebrehan university and it was a good experience) and helping vulnerable economic groups to be impacted positively.

In the ever increasing investment inflow, some mechanisms should be developed to encourage investors construct water points using all the seasonal rivers such as ‘’ Bersea river rather than sharing the existing water schemes with the city dwellers .For instance, there is one horticulture farm near Bersea river, but the farm uses spring water which could be utilized for drinking water. They would rather be using other source of water instead of using spring water for plantation. 

The other solution could be using efficient water source utilization and decreasing the pollution inflow to rivers and other water sources in a coordinated manner. There is a need to work hard to have sustainable water and sanitation service through creating awareness on efficient water utilization.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

Controlling water source pollution should come first in mitigating the problem because if we can control the pollution, then it will be easy to control its impact on the community.
Rivers are being polluted and the community is complaining a lot, so it needs a strong monitoring system for the industries to have treatment plant.  If these problems are addressed, we will have very significant differences on water and sanitation sustainability.
