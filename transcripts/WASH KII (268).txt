I1: In your opinion, what benefits have come to your community as result of having a clean water source such as this? 
R: We do not need for water anymore. Most importantly, clean water. So we have clean water all year round and because of that, we don’t fall sick anymore because of water borne diseases. 
I1: Are there hindrances in trying to maintain constant clean water flow in Kamuli despite the system in place? 
R: Children come late to the water source yet we have communicated time and time again what time we close it. Some community members will not stop bringing their cattle by the water source to water which affects the cleanliness of the water. Lastly, members don’t want to pay the maintenance fees. 
I1: What recommendations can you share with us to do away with these problem?
R: Sensitization by district leaders of the community during community meetings about water source maintenance. Community members respect district leaders more and respond better to them when called upon. 
I1: Which of these recommendations do you believe is more important?
R: Sensitization by district leaders of the community members. 
I1: Do you believe the current maintenance system is effective?
R: It is effective. But it is important that both the community and the maintenance teams work together to keep this going and even make water sources better and easier to maintain. 
I1: What do you think is lacking in this maintenance system though? 
R: District engagement and community is really lacking and needs to be more aggressive.
