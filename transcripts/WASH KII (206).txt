
In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in South Omo Zone?

South Omo zone has humid, temperate and arid climatic conditions. Even though the humid part of the zone has good water source, the people in the area has no access to clean water. The people in arid part of the zone also travel long distance to get water from rivers. In general we cannot say our people has access to clean water. The main problems to water service sustainability in South Omo Zone are: First, there is no responsible body that monitor and manage water schemes developed by various organizations. Second, the community do not consider the water schemes as its own property as a result the water schemes become damaged or broken within short period of time. Third, in most cases water schemes are constructed by unskilled professionals and using poor quality materials. Because of this the water schemes become non-functional within few months after inauguration. Thus, organizations should have proper monitoring and evaluation system during implementation of water projects. After completion of the project implementation, responsible bodies should be assigned that regularly manage the water schemes. What other problems are there related to water service sustainability? The other problem is there is no communication between organizations in the water sector. For example, organizations that develop water schemes in the area do not handover their completed projects to responsible bodies that can regularly manage the water schemes. In addition, there is no sufficient budget to rehabilitate the existing non-functional water schemes. Generally budget is the main constraint to ensure water service sustainability in the zone. If we have sufficient budget and qualified manpower, we can regularly monitor the existing water schemes and ensure sustainable water service in the area.

What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

In order to ensure sustainable water service in the area, first there must be proper exist strategy after completing the construction of water schemes. For example, if one water scheme is constructed in a given kebele, all stakeholders at kebele, woreda and zone level including our association should be informed about completed projects. This leads to better monitoring and evaluation of completed projects. Regarding budget constraint we should mobilize NGOs, private individuals and the government to diversify our budget sources. Moreover, we should use our limited budget efficiently. If we diversify our budget sources and efficiently utilized the available budget, we can ensure sustainable water service. Regarding problem of qualified manpower, there should be on job trainings as well as short term and long term trainings for young professional functioning in the zone.

Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 

Even though all the solutions I listed are very important, diversifying budget source and efficient utilization of the available budget should be given top priority. There must be sufficient budget to ensure water service sustainability. If we have sufficient budget, we can have qualified manpower. And these skilled professional can support the community properly. Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? If the problem related to budget is alleviated and if we have organizations responsible to monitor the existing water schemes, we can fully solve the problem related to water service sustainability. Alleviating the budget problem alone cannot bring any result, there must be organizations that monitor and regulate the water schemes in the area.

In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? What could be improved? 

It is better to say there is no coordination between organizations in the zone related to water service. Every organization runs separately on its way. Because of this there is duplication of effort in the sector. Moreover, there is no formal communication regarding ongoing projects in the area. Relevant stakeholders do not invited to contribute their part for the success of projects. There is no information exchange between organizations. For instance, no one comes and ask what we are planning and what we are implementing. However, as we are working for the development of one community, there should be strong coordination between organizations working in the sector. There should be coordinated planning and activity. Uncoordinated effort cannot lead to sustainable water service. Your (IRC WASH) initiative to create coordination in the sector is very welcome and we are willing to cooperate with you in this front. Finally I want to say that there must be an entity that take the responsibility to enhance the coordination between stakeholders in the sector.

In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the zone. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

The reason why I believe improved coordination can lead to more sustainable water service in the zone is that first, through coordination we can identify which organization can collaborate each other as well as we could have clear understanding about the activities of each organization. For example, we could have information about which projects are being implemented by different organizations so that we can contribute for the success of these projects. If there is coordination, we could have regular meetings either monthly or quarterly to discuss on our plans and activities, and this session enable us to identify our gaps, weaknesses and strength. Coordination can also create accountability among organizations. If there is coordination, organizations can share their skilled manpower and other resources. For example, if one organization face a budget shortage to complete its projects, other organization can collaborate by providing materials, logistics and manpower. Therefore, coordination can lead to more sustainable water service.
