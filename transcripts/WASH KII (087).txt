1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: The first question is as in Debrebirhan town what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability, focusing on public latrines, communal latrine, open defecation and waste disposal site?
Respondent: There are many problems. One of the problems the awareness gap in the community. We have not put our effort into raising community awareness. There is also a lack of sense of ownership which resulted in an attitude that the issue of sanitation is left to a certain body. This in turn led to poor waste management at household, neighborhood and town level. This is one problem.
The other problem is the absence of sustainable waste management system. Even though waste is collected, if not disposed of in modern ways that would turn it into a useful product, it is difficult. As the town gets bigger and bigger, the amount of waste is spiking and the landfill is full now, thus, the garbage returning back to the community. For instance, garbage is polluting farmlands in kebele 07. So, the problem is the non-existence of modern and quality waste disposal site.
The third problem is enterprises working on garbage collection do not come on time.  When they come and collect the garbage, they do not dump it on the designated areas. The town does not have either solid or liquid waste disposal site. To your surprise, we do not even have a place to dump sewer waste. Therefore, these are the three problems that the town is facing currently. Besides, we have a limited number of vacuum trucks; one owned by the municipality and two privately owned vacuum trucks. It is not enough for the demand in the town and it is also rather pricey. Hence, people tend to release their liquid waste to the rain water drainage lines. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewer: The first problem you have mentioned is lack of awareness. What would say is the solution for this problem? Whose responsibility is it? How should it be solved?
Respondent: The town health office and sanitation and beauty process in the town administration should be responsible for the issues of awareness creation and attitudinal change. These offices should teach the community, raise awareness of community members, and disseminate information through leaflets frequently. Besides, they should explain the detrimental effects of unsanitary environment and poor waste management every now and then. 
However, if people cannot change their behavior, legal framework should be set up. There should be appropriate legal measures to deter offenders. For instance, people prefer to release their toilet discharge to the street drainage and get punished than emptying it out paying 3,000 for vacuum truck. Because the punishment way less than the fee of the vacuum truck. Therefore, the penalties should be sufficient enough to discourage people from sanitation malpractices. 
Interviewer: The second one is the absence of modern waste disposal site. What would be the solution for this? 
Respondent: It requires budget. It is a crucial issue. Burning and using carts or trucks to dump garbage is not a long term solution.  
Interviewer: How should it be built? How is responsible for its construction?
Respondent: Town municipality is responsible for selecting and preparing the required land. Besides, the mayor and town administration should raise the fund needed for the project. Other institutions could play supportive role but these are the primary ones. You can find more information on the current status and progress from the town administration. The water utility office is also building a liquid waste treatment plant in the outskirts of the town.
Interviewer: You have said that there is shortage of trucks. What should be done to address this matter? What is the solution?
Respondent: Setting up enterprises that use carts to conduct door-to-door garbage collection operations could be one solution. This can also be a good way to create employment opportunity. The town administration is also working on this and there are beginnings in every kebele. So, we should enhance those efforts. The enterprises should be supported in terms of input, such as carts, budget, and credit services.
Interviewer: But, they don’t have a landfill to dump the garbage, they just throw it on open spaces. that is why first modern sanitary landfill should be built.
Respondent: Yes. That is right.  
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Out of the solutions you have listed, which is the best? If implemented, which solution would solve the other issues?
Respondent: The most important one is the construction of waste disposal site. First, waste would not be thrown away all over the places; second job opportunities would be created. 
Interviewer: What concomitant benefits would it bring, if the waste disposal site is built?
Respondent: It has so many benefits. First and foremost, all the waste we produce will be properly dumped in an appropriate place. Secondly, if the system is modern and has the capacity to recycle and reuse waste, it will create employment opportunities and additional income. I think these are the benefits of the waste disposal site.
