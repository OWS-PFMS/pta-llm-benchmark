
Interview 1. 

1.In your opinion, what do you think is working well in sustaining rural water services in Kamuli?
-There is increased awareness on the importance of reliable clean water supply most importantly the fact that this prevents the spread of water borne diseases. This has led to reduced disease burden on the health care system

2.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems in sustaining rural water services in Kamuli? 
-poverty of the masses who are unable to pay for preventive maintenance services of rural water systems. This reality has made some people unable to genuinely access sustainable water supply. There are still some sections of the population who are not taking water sustainable water supply seriously and they end up remaining poor because they are spending more money of treating preventable diseases.
3.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
-increased sensitization of people to understand the linkages between sustainable clean water and income. There is need for extra funding to allow expansion of these services to all sub counties and communities in the district.

4.How has this network changed over the past two years?
-More water sources are receiving Whave services now and the WSTs used to use bicycles to travel to communities for repair but lately they travel with motorbikes and they travel to water sources not only to repair when they are non-functional but also during scheduled maintenance.
