
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion, what are the main challenges of creating sustainable sanitation in the city? 
Response: The first problem is lack of awareness by the society about sanitation. This means the society doesn’t know how to dispose waste material properly. They are disposed on the streets and on infrastructures.
Interviewer:  what do you mean by infrastructure? 
Response:  for example, like drainage systems that are found on the side ways on the street. Different waste materials are thrown in to this kind of infrastructures and this creates problem. 
Interviewer:  you have mentioned two problems. The first one is lack of awareness and the second one is improper use of infrastructure. 
Response: yes. The main problem is lack of awareness. 
Interviewer:  are their any other problems? 
Response: The other problems is, the city doesn’t have modernized and systematic way of disposing solid and liquid type of wastes. We still use traditional way of disposing. This creates a problem on the society. 
Interviewer:   So, you are saying the city doesn’t have systematic way of disposing waste materials. Can you mention another problem?
Response: The other problem is the society doesn’t use the containers that are assigned in different places for collection of waste materials. For example, when someone eats banana or after using soft paper and wants to dispose the waste, he/she just instead of using baskets that are found on the side of the main street they just throw it anywhere. We also don’t have a mechanism to control individuals who dispose their wastes improperly. Everyone has a responsibility to dispose the waste materials properly.
