
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?

Interviewer: 
Response:    You have asked me about sanitation of Debreberhan City. Even though the city is established in highland areas, the cities sanitation is backward and doesn’t have a systematic way of handling. The main problem for this is there is no place for dumping waste materials prepared for the public to use. It is dumped in a anywhere. The society uses farmers land called Gara Demu as dumping area but this is not good for the farmer’s health. So after Sene 21 it going to be prohibited by the city administration even though the administration didn’t prepare another area for replacement. 
We have done a research on how to handle waste material in a sustainable and systematic way but it needed 150 million birr to apply it on the ground. So because of budget problem we didn’t continue. 
Our is has covered a wide area and it generates huge amount of waste material from different sectors hotels and households but because there is no way of disposing the materials they just dump it in a closed ditch. But if there is proper provided place for disposing those materials it will be very good. At the same time awareness should be created inside the society to use the provided system. 
We tried to create job by organizing and giving cart like small vehicles for some individuals. But the traditional dumping site named Gara Demu is very remote and they drop the wastes material inside the city rather than going to this place because the cart is unable to go that far. ALMA has also bought 4 tractors to transport waste material. But only two has driver the rest didn’t start working. They, only the two tractors can’t cover 9 Keble’s alone. So, we try to solve the problems by the above mentioned ways and by trying to create awareness in the society. But it is not sufficient. The government has to take its responsibility and we should solve awareness related problems. But if the City administration prohibited dumping site without providing another area it will create confusion on the society where to dump their waste materials. There should be planned and organized vehicles to pick the waste materials and it should be communicated with the society. 
As you said coordination problem is one of the problem additional to budget. There is no communication. We have given waste material temporary dumping box per Keble one.  But because they are not collected before they got full, it will be difficult to transport with small vehicles. It needs crane to lift it. The main sanitation problem is occurred from service giving sectors like hotels.

Sometimes liquid waste will appear on the street in which the source is unknown. This kind of problem is solved by coordinating the society to identify which service giving sector it was generated. 2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

Interviewer: In general for the problems mentioned above what do you suggest the solution to create sustainable sanitation for the city? 
Response:  First I recommend by organizing NGOs and others there should be modern sanitation system. And the city has to go to the modern way of disposing liquid and solid waste materials. Otherwise as a city without a proper disposing area it will be a problem. So budget should be issued and the job has to be done. Because in the future there will be more service giving sectors in the city. And they will produce more waste material. Liquid waste filtering systems should be implemented in all four sides of the city to get relatively clean water. If this is done the city may be clean and health issues may also improved and it can also generate power. As a city, with the help or advice of Geologist and skill human power must have a modern waste material dumping area as other cities. We can use also the university as an advisor on the planning.

Budget problem must be solved.  Otherwise in Debreberhan city new emerging companies are growing and they need a proper way of disposing their waste.
