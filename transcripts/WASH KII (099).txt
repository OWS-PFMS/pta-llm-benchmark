
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Water supply lines are old and exposed for contamination
Water and sanitation services are the major problems in the town in general and in our kebele in particular. Most of the water lines in our kebele are very old and exposed for contamination by liquid wastes. It resulted in various public health problems like diarrhea. In order to alleviate the problem there is no budget at kebele level. Skill labor is also another challenge to mitigate the problem. Addressing this problem is the responsibility of the town Water and Sewerage Utility Office, the Municipality and the Zonal Administration; however, they did not give attention to manage the problem. Daily we issue at least 15 identification cards for new entrants to our kebele, which indicates how much the rural community is migrating to the town. The number of populations of the town is tremendously increasing due to rural urban migration and creating burden on the water service. 
II.Absence of appropriate waste disposal site
There is no appropriate waste disposal site for both solid and liquid wastes in Debre Birehan town. The community dispose wastes into drainage lines and ditches. We can say the waste disposal system is very problematic to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. Solid and liquid waste trucks are not providing service due to absence of nearby waste disposal site.  There was a temporary waste disposal place in the town however, the surrounding residents complained to the municipality and forced to stop the service due to pollution. 
Due to absence of waste disposal site, the community do not have access for latrine/pit emptying service. During the rainy months households released toilet wastes into drainages and it contaminate the area.  To hire private latrine emptying vehicles that dispose wastes in other nearby towns, it requires about birr 2000 to 3000, which is beyond the capacity of the community. Because of this fact, most households forced to depend on open defecation.  
To provide temporary solution for the problem the town administration arranged a temporary disposal site at “Tikur Gedel” which is very far from the town and truck owners complain as it is costly to transport waste with the existing rate. The road to the area is not convenient for service provision. The community residing around this temporary site are also complaining due to environmental pollution.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
To solve the problems related to water supply lines we would like to recommend that: 
Old water supply lines should be replaced with new once in collaboration with relevant stakeholders and the community itself. Moreover, old metallic pipelines should be replaced with plastic ones to reduce contamination.
We should change community awareness through training to minimize damage on water lines.
Water office should work to address the water supply problem in collaboration with other stakeholders and collaborators.
Pertinent to the problem of waste disposal site we would like to suggest the following:
Waste disposal site should be prepared an constructed 
Household should be strongly advised to develop septic tank within their compound to minimize sanitation problem of the town. 
Community awareness creation campaign should be prepared on waste management and disposal
There should be strong collaboration among stakeholders to solve the problem related to waste disposal site.
Garbage bins or containers should be available in villages and roads sides to collect solid wastes. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Preparing appropriate waste disposal site for both solid and liquid waste is the first priority to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. If the waste disposal site problem for both solid and liquid waste is resolved, the community will benefit the following:
The community health will be improved
The town becomes beautiful
The town image will be build
