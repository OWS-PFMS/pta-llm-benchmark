
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion, what are the main causes for lack of sanitation in Debreberhan City? What are the problems that makes sanitation less sustainable ?
Response: The first problem that I think the main challenge of sustainable sanitation service and a gap in hygiene is lack of awareness in the city Administrations which are responsible for the whole sectors found in the city.  The cabine (Collection of high level government officials) didn’t take in to consideration the sanitation service while planning the city’s infrastructure and to modernize it. The second one is the society lacks awareness and should be created in many ways. The third problem or challenge is in urban areas to facilitate hygiene related tasks it requires resources like budget and skilled man power. The sanitation process from start to the end it needs capital and science research. And the capital needed for this is a lot. So I think the city also faces lack of capital in solving the problem. Those are the main problems.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: You have mentioned lack of awareness problem on the administration, lack of awareness on the society and capital problem. You have mentioned this three core problems on sustainable sanitation. So what do you think the solution for the problems? 
Response: In my opinion country’s urbanization is growing very fast. Following urbanization hygiene problem will be the next issue. And hygiene problem are the main cause of environmental sanitation problem. So the officials should take in to consideration the sanitation service while planning the future infrastructure of the city. They should plan carefully the process of disposing hygiene in the form of liquid and solid forms. If the officials have a better awareness and consciousness and try to coordinate other bodies it may bring a good result. Then creating awareness on the society by different ways. Creating eagerness on the society to clean their area by showing them what it means to be clean and to have a clean area. After this assigning a good capital for this task will be helpful to accomplish the needed change. I know there are some actions that are being taken to solve these problems in policy level by the government but they should be implemented fast and in a proper way. For example City administration has a policy on how the city must grow and the category of cities and how their sanitation service should look like. And World Bank is also working on disposing waste materials in a proper ways to create green and favorable environment.  But it has to be implemented properly like other services. This is also being a challenge until now. Otherwise, other infrastructures will have no long term use. 
Interviewer: What do you propose to solve lack of capital ?
Response: To solve capital related challenges, we created a project that used to help in disposing waste materials. Debreberhan city used to have a small amount of waste and small vehicles were enough to transport these materials to their end. But now this can’t be applicable because the city has grown. So, with the help of ALMA we provided 4 vehicles and they have been helping the waste material transportation from the city. But when they dispose the waste material it is in traditional way in farmers land with no scientific further process. So we studied this problem and finally the capital needed for this scientific solution is estimated to be 135 million birr. This is not affordable with the city’s budget ability and we are trying to coordinate Federal Government and other bodies. And there is also another hope that is given from Sweden Government to involve in disposing solid waste materials. So to get capital to implement the solution different responsible bodies must come together to have a long term solution.               

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have told me different solutions. But to which one do you give more emphasis from all the solutions you mentioned?   
Response: First of all the high level officials must have a good awareness and the society must be aware of the impact of improper disposal of waste materials. But however you create awareness on the society and on the officials if there is no capital it is useless …. (Interruption from other person) …. So the officials must have an ideas and options on how to generate capital. Even though the high level officials must be conscious and be aware of the problem, at the same time capital must be provided to implement and achieve the given solutions. They must be accomplished simultaneously.     
Interviewer: So you think the two must go together, the awareness process and the capital generation. What do you think the result on the society’s health or on the economic and social growth if this two are applied effectively?         
Response: If the problems are solved, it is a fact that solid or liquid waste materials are useful resources, if they are managed properly. The developed countries have changed it to useful resources using scientific process. Even there is one factory called Reby Solid Waste Material Disposal in Addis Ababa that generates 50 Megawatt for the city. So, having even a small waste material recycling process for our city can bring a lot of change. And it will create a habit of recycling waste materials in the society. On the other hand, if liquid form of waste material is processed in a proper swage system and treated in chemicals, at the end it can be used for plants and cleaning purposes. Compost can also be produced as a final result from this process. So, if the above two things are implemented well, the society can get green areas and favorable environment to live and it can also attract new investments and attract tourists as Debreberhan is one of the historical places in the country. It has many stories like the birth place and the palace of Emperor Minilik and others also. So this will make the economy grow.   

      

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable sanitation services? What could be improved?
Interviewer: In your opinion, how is the effectiveness of coordination between different bodies in maintaining sustainable sanitation?
Response: Right now we can’t say there is cooperation at all. We only coordinate with business sectors and services giving sectors when there is emergency and to control peoples and organizations that create hygiene problem. We don’t have a good coordination with the water sector offices and police offices to prevent and forecast the future upcoming challenges and to prepare action plan.  
Interviewer: So what do you suggest to improve coordination? 
Response: As I said before, solid and liquid waste material disposing system must be planned simultaneously with the planning of the infrastructure of the city. So, if different sectors couldn’t come together and plan, one may destroy what other sector built and misuse of resources may occur. But if they plan together and go into action in a planned way like when road is built at the same time sewage system, clean water distribution system, power and other system put together at the same time in a planned way improvement may come. At least having monthly mandatory meeting with the city offices that have relationship with the city hygiene and discussing on how to make the city grow, what is the city’s plan and how can we implement it and other related issues. And also, there must be accountability on each body. This will bring a great improvement.

5.Can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable sanitation services in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before? 

Interviewer: How can coordination create long term and improved environmental sanitation?  
Response: It has a big impact on environmental sanitation. Because there are different and useful knowledge’s and resources on different sectors to create sustainable environmental sanitation. So, when they put together they will have a great impact on the process. But as I said before meeting must be mandatory and it must have accountability. 



Interviewer: What is the Coordination effect on the problems you mentioned above? What effect will it bring on them?
Response: Related with solid and liquid waste material disposal system, coordination brings collective knowledge and resource on how to solve and improve the problem. For example, health sectors can study the impact of improper waste material on the society’s health and water sector can also bring the impact of waste materials on clean water. Different sectors contribute many useful resources. And the other one, coordination has a great role in keeping the constructed infrastructures for long time by using a proper way of disposing liquid and solid wastes and recycling them for plants and for creating green area and favorable environment to live in.
