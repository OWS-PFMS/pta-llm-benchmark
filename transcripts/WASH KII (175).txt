1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: first there is problem in preparing proper project survey.
Interviewer: what does survey problem mean?
Respondent: there are problems before and after the well digging process. Well, the survey made before unearthing the water by itself has its own problemin addition, we found some toxic materialeven after the digging is complete. 
Interviewer: why is that the well is dig up before proper survey? who’s problem is that?  
Respondent: There is shortage of qualified experts. Since theory has its own implication and practice has its own implication they must update themselves and receive trainings based on their skills and professions. I believe it is surveillance problem that we terminatedsome of the developed water facilities. Other than that, we have budget constraint to maintaindamaged pipelinesduring expansion of road and other infrastructural development. As a result, we experience breakups in water supply. In addition to that, when minor maintenance problems occurred the community view cheap replacement spare parts as expensive. There is also limitation on spare part supply and skilled maintenance manpower. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: in your opinion what are the solution to solve the problem related to the water well survey permanently?
Respondent: From the surveillance perspective, due to budget constraints I don’t think they can resolve easily the problems related to skilled man power shortage. However, allowing the experts to take different trainings from several institutions at regular base could be one solution to develop improved water wells. 
Problems related to spare parts could be resolved at woreda level if we give due attention for the maintenance of existing water schemes. To do this we should capacitate community associations by providing training and by suppling spare parts for minor maintenances. We should organize rural community associations that will be responsible for the management and administration of water schemes within their boundary. 
Interviewer: how about the to resolve problems related to water pipelines?
Respondent: ERA is responsible for such problem. However, as a woreda we should communicate and strength our relationships with responsible authorities before any damage occurs on the water facilities. We should also put precaution conditions and guidelines before they start any infrastructural development. To do this there must be coordination and communication between the authorities and regional administration office. 
Interviewer: how about the to resolve problems related to finance?
Respondent: I don’t know why but the region has a problem of capital budgetholdings. The main problem we are facing now is the budget allocated by the government to the woreda cannot meet the entire needs of the woreda. The woreda’s capital budget is not enough to fully address the problem thus, the government should allocate sufficient capital budget to the woreda. Also regarding to this the woredas natural water resources, first we have to systematical document the water resource potential of the woreda and share the information with those organizations that have interest to work with us in the sector.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

I believe the main is to organize enterprises. To do this we should organize rural enterprises responsible for the management, administration and providing spare parts for water schemes. Enterprises can fill the gaps related to skilled manpower and provide continuous training to water management committees as well as to the community at large on proper use of water schemes. We should promote the purpose and benefits of the organized enterprise within the community. This will contribute towards sustainability of water schemes. They can mobilize financial contribution from community, can provide labor and spare parts that will be used for regular maintenance of existing water facilities. If we work on this front, it will be one good solution for sustainability problems of water and sanitation services.
