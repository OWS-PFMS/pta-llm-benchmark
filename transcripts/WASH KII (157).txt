1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: To start with, we have no WASH related activities in Mille woreda. However, we have participated in one of the learning alliance meeting a year ago.
The first challenge related to sustainable water service in the region is that there are no user-friendly water schemes that provide sustainable water service in the region. For instance, most of the water schemes are based on diesel generators and they are easily out of service due to minor technical problems and shortage of fuel. 
In my opinion and experience, using solar-based water schemes is less costly as it is based on solar-energy. I suggest this mainly because the running cost of solar-based water schemes is much cheaper than diesel-based water schemes. Moreover, the climate of the region is arid and easy to collect solar energy. The maintenance requirement for diesel based water schemes is very difficult for local experts and the maintenance cost is much higher than solar-based water schemes.
Interviewer: What are the other main challenges related to water/sanitation service in your project region? 
Respondent: The other problem that affect sustainability of water/sanitation services is that there are no strong WASHcos (rural WASH service monitoring committees) to monitor the existing water schemes in their respective areas.  The WASHcos do not have the required skills at least to provide minor maintenance service in their areas.
Interviewer: Do you have anything to say related to sanitation service of the region?
Respondent: Our organization (AMREF) do not have any intervention in the area of sanitation in the region. I have nothing to tell you about sanitation related problems of the region. I have not observed any sanitation related problems in the region. 

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Respondent: To alleviate the problems related to water service in the region there should be strong coordination between regional and woreda level WASH actors. Without having coordinated planning and activities, we cannot ensure sustainable water service in the areas. Water project proposals should be developed based on preliminary assessment of the potential of the areas. If we can build coordinated planning and activities in the region, as well as if projects are based on practical situational analysis of the context of the region, we can ensure sustainable WASH service in the region.
In addition, we have to strengthen WASHCos through providing technical training on maintenance of water schemes. This is mainly because most of the water schemes in rural areas do not provide service due to minor problems. If the WASHCos have some maintenance skill, we can ensure sustainable water service in remote areas where there are no qualified water technicians.  

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: In my opinion, the most important solution to ensure sustainable water service in the region is that creating strong coordination between various regional and woreda WASH stakeholders. Coordination avoid duplication of efforts, minimize resource wastage and enable to take collective action.
