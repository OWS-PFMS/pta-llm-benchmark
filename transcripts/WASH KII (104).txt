
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: There are various problem related to sustainability of water and sanitation service in the town. There are specific problems related to water services and there is also problem related to sanitation particularly on waste disposal and management. For instance, one of the major challenge is that those organizations or stakeholders that are responsible to provide sanitation and water service in the town do not dispose their duties and responsibilities by involving the community. Their commitment to ensure sustainable water and sanitation service is very weak. There is no coordinated and participatory activity to provide proper service to the community. 
The second main challenge related to water and sanitation service is that there is no coordination between relevant actors, institution (both private and public) and community organizations to ensure sustainable service. The existing water and sanitation service is not in line with the town’s growth and the public demand. That is the water and sanitation services are not growing as required.  
Interviewer: can you explain in detail the coordination problems observed in the town?
Respondent: In principle, the government is the leading entity that is required to play a leading role in coordinating and mobilizing other relevant actors. Even though the government regulatory bodies are responsible to coordinate other institutions including the community, they are not committed to create the required coordination between actors. The government bodies do not work according to their mandates. That is they do not dispose their duties and responsibilities as per their institutional mandates and guidelines. There is huge commitment gap in the government side to create relevant coordination between WAHS stakeholders to ensure sustainable water and sanitation service in the town.
Interviewer: Are there any other problems related to water and sanitation service sustainability in the town?
Respondent: Related to water service of the town I would like to appreciate the initiative and effort of the town’s water utility office in supplying water to the community. However, I observe occasional water service interruption in the town and some remote kebeles do not have access for clean water in the area. Honestly speaking, the water service in the town is good relative to other areas.
Interviewer: Do you have anything to say related to sanitation service of the town?
Respondent: The major problem observed in this town related to sanitation service is that even though there is a sanitation and beatification department within the town municipality, it is not properly providing sanitation service to the community.
As part of our organization’s (Amanuel Development Organization) WASH activities, we have tried to provide training and material support for those sanitation workers that are hired by the town administration. However, in doing this we did not get appropriate support from the town sanitation and beautification department. They do not cooperate with us in selecting trainees and facilitating the training. In general, the town sanitation and beatification department has a serious problem in coordinating other relevant actors and mobilizing the community to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town.   
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Respondent: In order to alleviate the problems related to coordination and weak commitment of the government bodies, first, those government agencies should accept the responsibilities of WASH activities with full commitment and integrity. Moreover, they should create conducive environment for other stakeholders that can contribute for sustainable water and sanitation service.
Second, the government agencies should not work in isolation rather they should integrate with other stakeholders, private institutions (like manufacturing firms, hotels, restaurants) and the community itself. This is mainly due to the fact that, sustainable WASH service can only be realized by the coordinated effort of all relevant actors including the community.  In addition, government agencies should play remarkable leading role and properly dispose their responsibility in coordination with other actors to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town.
They should also mobilize the community; enhance the community awareness in sanitation management; they should also share responsibilities with other actors at different level and create strong coordination among WASH stakeholders. I think this is the right way to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: The problems and your suggested solutions primary focused on having strong coordination between the government agencies and other relevant WASH stakeholders. Moreover, you suggested that the government agencies should have strong commitment in disposing their responsibilities to ensure sustainable water and sanitation service in the town. Can you tell me what next steps would happen if these solutions occur?
Respondent: Previously our organization (Amanuel Development Organization) has been involved in WASH sector platform that was run by different organizations functioning in the town. Moreover, we had regular meeting every three month to improve the WASH related problems of the town. However, currently we have no such regular meeting and coordinated activity in the subject matter due to weak commitment of the leading government agencies.
If there is strong coordination between relevant actors and if the government agencies play significant leading role in coordinating and mobilizing stakeholders, obviously we can see remarkable change related to sanitation service of the town within short period. Moreover, if there is coordination and commitment we can develop a system and mechanism that could enable us to provide sustainable sanitation service in the town. If a system is in place people turn over cannot affect the service provision that is the new comer (the new personnel) can provide the service within the existing system.
