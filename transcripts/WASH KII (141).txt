
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
The main problem to water service sustainability in the woreda is that there is mismatch between the supply capacity of the existing boreholes and the water demand of the town. The water supply is much lower than the water demand of the town. The other problem is that most of water users do not pay their water bills. In this regard the woreda water office and the water board are taking initiative to alleviate this problem. Moreover, the woreda water office in coordination with the regional water bureau is planning to construct new water schemes for the town. What other problems are there related to water service sustainability? There are no other problems related to water service in the area.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
The government in coordination with other partners should dig new boreholes in four zones of the town. Moreover, water tanks should be constructed for each borehole. 
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? 
If this solution is implemented, the community will get sufficient water service. As a result in addition to the household consumption, the community will engage plantation of trees in the town to mitigate the problems of climate change. If we get additional water supply schemes in all the four zones of the town we can plant trees and reduce the aridity of the area. Therefore, the most important solution to ensure sustainable water service in the area is constructing new water schemes and installation of new water lines in the four zone of the town. This will enable the community to keep its personal hygiene and plant trees in the town.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 
At woreda level there is good coordination between woreda water, health and education offices. The woreda education office gets material support from the woreda water office. We have also requested the woreda water office to construct new water line for different schools but because of budget constraint they could not respond to our question. Generally we have good coordination with the woreda water and health offices. What could be improved? Most of the water schemes constructed in the woreda are far from schools. To connect water lines to the schools require huge budget. Therefore, sufficient budget should be allocated to develop water infrastructure around schools and health posts. Water is very basic for schools, health centers and for pastoral development activities. What could be improved related to coordination?  I think the existing coordination between stakeholders is good. Sometimes some offices do not provide information to water office about their plan and activities related to new water projects.  
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
Instead of implementing activities separately, coordinated effort can bring sustainable changes. For instance, if we construct schools without coordinating with health, water and pastoral development offices, we could not find children that enroll in the school. There should be health posts, water schemes and other pastoral development activities around the school. If we coordinate our resources including budget, we can bring sustainable changes.
