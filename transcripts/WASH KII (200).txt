1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda?
The first challenge to water service sustainability is [lack of budget]. In addition to the budget shortage, there is also a problem related to budget utilization. Stakeholders do not use their budget efficiently to repair the existing non-functional water schemes. [Misuse of water schemes by the community] Due to lack of awareness the community mishandle the water schemes. The community do not consider the water schemes as its own property (resources). They don’t have ownership feeling. [Limited community participation] The participation of the community in water projects is very limited. They have no motive to contribute to projects that are being implemented in their areas.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Regarding budget, the government should give priority for the sector and allocate sufficient budget. In addition, other partners (aid organizations) should collaborate to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. We should also repair non-functional water schemes using our limited budget efficiently. We should also mobilize the community to participate in water projects and we should teach them to properly use the existing water schemes. Moreover, we should enhance the participation of the community in terms of repairing those water schemes that require minor maintenance.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 
I can’t say there is sufficient coordination in the woreda. For example, there are many health posts in the woreda without water schemes. To fill this gap there must be coordination with the water office. Moreover, there should be sufficient budget to build the required water schemes. However, the existing coordination with water office is very weak. We are not working together properly to solve the problems.

What could be improved? First, we have to work in coordination to identify the areas (kebeles) that require water supply in the woreda. To this end much emphasis should be given to schools and health posts functioning in the woreda. Once we identify areas that has water service problems, we should plan together to alleviate the problems of the identified areas. We should also work in coordination to find out possible budget sources. We should also have a common meeting sessions to discuss on problems. Coordination is required not only to identify problems but also to make solutions to the problems. For instance, when we build water schemes it should be accessible both for health posts and for the community.
