1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Interviewer: In your opinion what are the main challenges or constraints that affect the sustainability of WASH service in your woreda? When I say WASH service, it means water and sanitation service. What are the main problems observed in this service?
Respondent: Like I told you there is very limited access for clean water in our woreda and shortage of water service provision in most of the kebeles due to government resource restraint. The worda covers vat area with very large kebeles and high number of population however it is not receiving the appropriate attention both at governmental and regional level. The budget allocated by the government to the woreda and the revenue collected in the woreda are very low and insufficient to mitigate the problem. In the past two years, even though the woreda is endowed with water resource which can be developed with small resource amount, not even one water facility was built by government. In this regard, some non-governmental organizations work on development of water facilities in some areas of the woreda. However, their effort is insignificant as far as the size of the woreda is concerned. Besides, the existing water facilities are not providing sustainable service because the community do not properly manage the schemes.
The other problem is the community they lack awareness on proper utilization after construction. In addition to this problem, in areas where there are water facilities, there is difficulty to make the facilities sustainable due to absence of immediate and regular maintenance service. There is no skilled work force in the woreda that provide training to the community on use and management of water facilities.There is no maintenance service even for those water facilities that are not providing service due to minor problems. As a result, minor maintenance problem is expected to be maintained by the government.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have mentioned the basic problems related to water and sanitation. Now let us discuss about the solutions to each problem. For instance, you have said the woreda has a water resource but there is a problem in developing the springs and ground water and supply to the community. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solution to this problem? What do you propose to resolve shortage of water coverage? 
Respondent: Regarding improving the basic needs infrastructural gap and access for water of the community, first the government should allocate budget by considering the size of population in the woreda. Government must assess realities on ground, whether or not the society being the beneficiary from water schemes, and find out a solution in collaboration with relevant stakeholders. The government should strongly work on areas that can be managed in its capacity. But in areas that are beyond its capacity, the government has to provide appropriate information to potential collaborators in scientific way to resolve the problems. To identify the problems on the existing water schemes, to report the problems clearly, to responsible authority, and to find out potential collaborators or donors, there must be qualified personnel at the woreda water office. Both governmental and other non-governmental organizations should also clearly identify the practical problems of the community to ensure sustainable water service. 
The other thing is the woreda should mobilize the society and work on awareness enhancement through training to create sense of belongingness within the community. Through learning and experience sharing the woreda should organize rural community associations that are responsible for the management and administration of water schemes within their boundary. The woreda should also involve all stakeholders together and discuss on how to ensure the sustainability of the existing water facilities. It will be one good solution for sustainability problems of water and sanitation services if the woreda work on this front. 
Interviewer: what must be done to escalate water use associations?
Like I mention it earlier, the woreda should organize water use associations that will be responsible for the management and administration of water schemes within their boundary. This enables the community to handle minor maintenances on their own in their locality. In collaboration with water service governing bodies, the responsible body has to design mechanisms in which the community itself can handle minor maintenances. To do this the woreda water office should capacitate water use associations by providing training and by supplying spare parts for minor maintenances. These associations can mobilize financial contribution from community that will be used for regular maintenance of the schemes. Particularly, the woreda should have strong cooperation with the town water utility office in terms of taking collective actions. In cases that are beyond the capacity of the woreda, it should request technical support from zonal offices and from interested non-governmental organizations. This will contribute towards sustainability of water schemes.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: What are the problems related to sanitation (personal and community hygiene)?
Respondent: To ensure sustainable water service in the woreda, the first is creating awareness and sense of belongingness within the community for existing water facilities.
Interviewer: when we you say creating awareness, is it at the community level or at governmental authority’s level or is it at both level?
Respondent: Well, yes creating awareness starts from the top it is not one side job. As a woreda you don’t just leave the burden only on the water sector it must participate all stakeholders. In collaboration with water service governing bodies and all stakeholders, the responsible body has to create broad awareness within all stakeholders how to use the common resources on permanent way. In addition to that, the woreda should mobilize, participate, and set together the community to discuss and design mechanisms on how to improve the woreda’s water sustainability problem.
Developing new water schemes requires large budget and longer time. Thus, the woreda have to give priority on monitoring, supervision and management of the existing water schemes to ensure their sustainability. Whoever constructs the water schemes, if there is no responsible body that manages the proper function of the schemes, they will not serve the anticipated period service. To this end, we should allocate budget and the required manpower that can monitor and provide technical support on the existing water schemes. There should be sense of belongingness within the community for these resources. If the communities consider these facilities as their own resources, they will contribute labor and money for maintenance. This in turn reduces the burden of maintenance cost from the woreda water office and the office.
