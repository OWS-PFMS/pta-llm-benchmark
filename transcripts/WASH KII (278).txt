I1: In your opinion, what good practices in Kamuli have ensured that our boreholes are consistently providing clean water?
R: The WAVE organization. It maintains boreholes throughout the year with monthly checkups and maintenances when necessary. 
I1: Any other issue you can mention?
R: The organization needs to sensitize people on sanitation and general water hygiene. 
I1: In your opinion, what do you believe is the biggest hindrance to Kamuli people acquiring clean water? 
R: People’s limited income. For example, WAVE requires 400,000 thousand Ugandan shillings annually to maintain our boreholes and we regular people cannot afford it. 
I1: What solutions would you suggest to help solve the issues you have mentioned? 
R: (a), I’m requesting the WAVE organization to greatly reduce the annual fee. We cannot afford that amount of money annually. 
(b), Secondly, the Ugandan Government should contribute to clean water distribution in Kamuli. They should submit a certain income percentage and in doing so, make it easier and more affordable for regular citizens to pay. 
I1: From both your submissions, which one do you believe is the biggest more important that the other? 
R: Government’s contribution to clean water distribution in Kamuli. 
I1: How do you suggest the Government should do this?
R: By coming up with a water district budget. For example, as Kamuli, we should have a direct fund for our boreholes. Annually, this money should be released straight to that account. That way as Kamuli citizens, we do not have to face water issues every year. 
I1: How best do you think Kamuli people can contribute to the maintenance of their own boreholes?
R: I believe monthly savings of small amounts of money for the boreholes should be encouraged and properly executed. This will solve the constant issue of paying a huge wholesome amount at the beginning of every year to WAVE. 
I1: In your opinion however, how is the current borehole maintenance and running by WAVE going? 
R: Good. 
I1: Any other solution your think would help your situation. 
R: Yes. It would be great if WAVE could reduce on the fees required to maintain the boreholes. Secondly, they should make the project 2 years and not one to allow us accumulate the fees. 
I1: Thank you for your time and submission.
