
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
There is serious water supply problem in Mille woreda. In Mille town water is supplied to the community for 20 to 30 minutes within 24 hours. In rural areas there are some good initiatives to improve water supply. For example CARE Ethiopia (NGO working in the area) in collaboration with the government is constructing rural water schemes. However, there is still gaps in the construction of new water schemes in rural areas (i.e. there are only few water schemes in rural areas). Because if this the rural community migrate to areas where there is river water. When we come to Mille town, the town water utility office sale the water to other areas while the community is under serious problems of water supply. 
Can you please list out the main challenges to water service sustainability in the woreda? First, there is frequent power outage in the town. Sometimes the power outage will stay for about a week and until it comes back there will not be water supply in the town. To alleviate the problem related to power, the community has been started to contribute some money for purchase of fuel for diesel generator. But these arrangement could not proceed sustainably. So due to power outage there is a serious problem on the water supply of the town. 
5:00
Even when there is electricity we only get water for 20 to 30 minutes within 24 hours. Why you only get water supply for 20 to 30 minutes per day? This is mainly because there is only one water tank in the town and it is difficult to satisfy the needs of the entire population by this single water tank. This issue is raised in various community meetings but the response from the town administration and water offices is that there is no budget to engage in expansion activities (i.e. no budget to construct new water tanks). Relatively speaking there is better supply of water in rural areas compared to Mille town. The woreda water office is also focused on development of water schemes in rural areas. CARE Ethiopia is also working with water office in the development of water schemes in rural areas. However, the water supply problems of Mille town did not get attention by stakeholders. NGOs do not also engage in the development of water schemes in Mille town.
Even though there is serious water supply problem in the town, the water utility office sale the water to other areas that have no water supply. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
In my opinion, the first solution to solve the water supply problem is changing the awareness of the woreda administrators and other relevant stakeholders. That is the administrators do not care about the problems of the community. During one fasting season of the Muslim community, religious fathers provide strong comment and advice to the woreda administrators and sector office leaders on the problem related to water service. Because of this we have seen improvement on the service during that fasting period. Therefore, we should work to change the awareness of the sector offices leaders. 
9:58
What are the problems related to awareness of these leaders?  The first thing is they do not understand the problems of the community. For example, selling water to other areas while there is serious water supply problem in the town obviously indicates the irresponsibleness of the leaders. 
Regarding problems related to the power outage, Mille town do not have its own electric technicians. When there is power outage Mille town administration report the problem to Electric Power Authority which is based in Semera (a nearby town). If the technicians failed to respond quickly to the question, the town administration take the technicians to a prison before they fix the problem. Surprisingly the community stay without electricity and water until the technicians released from the prison. Once they released from the prison they temporarily fix the problem and interrupt the service immediately. Therefore, there is a conflict between electric technicians and the town administrators. Because of this conflict the community is facing unnecessary problems related to water service. 
11:27
In rural areas we are closely working with water office on water, sanitation and hygiene. Particularly we are working together to make the kebeles free from open defecation. We mobilize the community to construct latrines to make the area free from open defecation. We also teach the community on how to keep water sanitation and how to treat water with chemicals. We implement all these things in coordination with the woreda water office. We also share materials and logistics that are required for water and sanitation work. CARE Ethiopia also works closely with our office and the woreda water office in the development of water schemes around health posts and schools.
Do you have any recommendations for the problems related to water service in the town? In the town sometimes sewerage lines broken and contaminate the water lines. The coordination to alleviate these problems is very weak. We are not working well in urban sanitation and hygiene. We are now trying to coordinate stakeholders to work on urban sanitation. 
15:32
What do you recommend to alleviate the problems related to electricity and the water supply shortage in the town? The ultimate solution for the water supply shortage is expanding the water supply sources. For example, we need to construct new boreholes and water tanks that have sufficient capacity to satisfy the needs of the community. If we solve the problem related to supply shortage, the problem related to power outage does not affect the community as such. The main problem that should be given much emphasis is the supply shortage. 
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
The most important solution to problems related to water service in the area is expanding the water supply source of the town. Moreover, we need to change the awareness of governmental stakeholders functioning in the woreda. That is stakeholders should be responsible for the problems of the community. They should understand the problems that the community is facing. Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? The community will get sufficient amount of water regularly. This implies we will have clean living environment. The sanitation and hygiene of the community will also improve significantly.
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 
We can see the coordination in two categories, these are the coordination for rural WASH service and the coordination for urban WASH service. The coordination for rural WASH service is very strong and we have achieved good results. The main players in this coordination are woreda water office and woreda health office. However, the coordination for urban WASH service is very weak and there is a serious water, sanitation and hygiene problem in the town. (Note: the main players for urban WASH service are town water utility office and woreda health office). The coordination between these two organizations is very weak. As a result there are several problems related to water service in Mille town. 
21:05
Recently, even though there is water shortage we are planning a sanitation and hygiene campaigning in the town. However, pertinent to the water shortage we have no any plan because we have no capacity to alleviate the problem.
What could be improved related to the coordination? In the first place the stakeholders functioning in the woreda should know how to coordinate each other to solve the problems related to water service. Each stakeholders should know its responsibility related to water service because the water office is not the only responsible body to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. Therefore, we should aware each stakeholder about its responsibility related to water service in the area. 
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
For example, to expand the water service, first we should aware both community and stakeholders about the health consequences of water supply shortage and the effects of using contaminated water. This will be the responsibility of the health office. Alleviating problems related to political issues is the responsibility of administration office. Planning budget for water projects and finding fund from regional water bureau and other sources is the responsibility of the woreda water office so if all these organization create strong coordination, they can solve the problem related to water service sustainability.  
The coordination can also solve the problems that I mentioned earlier. For example, instead of taking electric technicians to a prison due to their delayed response, it is better to closely work with Electric Authority to find out sustainable solution for the power outage problem.  If there is good coordination between stakeholders, we can allocate sufficient budget for the water sector. Therefore, I think coordination can bring sustainable water service in the area.
