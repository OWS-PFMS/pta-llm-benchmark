
Question 1: What do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in South Omo Zone?

According to the respondent the main challenges for water service sustainability includes:

The climate of the area: South Omo Zone has a semi-arid climatic condition. Because of this the water wells (boreholes) mostly dry and become unable to supply water to the community.

Stakeholders that work in the water sector do not conduct appropriate assessment to identify potential areas for development or construction of water wells. They simply construct water wells in areas where there is limited water resource or in areas with poor surface water. Because of this the water wells could not provide sustainable water supply to the community.

The third main challenge for water service sustainability is that only few stakeholders engaged in the development of water service delivery. As a result these few stakeholders could not reach the entire community. They only serve a small segment of the community. The interventions or projects implemented by these few stakeholders are very limited and fragmented (i.e. there is no coordination between the stakeholders). Each stakeholder come up with small projects that cannot satisfy the needs of the community.

The stakeholders working in the water sector mostly focus on the construction of new water wells. They overlooked the maintenance of the existing non-functional water wells. They do not give attention for maintenance.

Both government and NGOs allocate limited budget for water sector development in the area.

Electric power outage: Particularly in the zonal town, Jinka there is frequent power outage. Since the supply of water in the town is mainly based on electric power, the power outage interrupt the water supply frequently. Sometimes the community stay without  water for more than a week

Question 2: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Regarding the problems related to the climatic condition, I suggest that water wells should be constructed in areas where there is relatively better forest coverage. Moreover, stakeholders should mobilize the community to plant indigenous trees around water points (water wells). This may lead to sustainable water service.

Pertinent to weak assessment to identify potential areas for construction off water wells, I personally recommend that stakeholders should conduct rigorous assessment on the water potential of the areas before embarking on the construction or development of water wells. To this end, they should involve qualified experts from the region Water, Mine and Energy Department and other consultants.

The government should encourage various stakeholders to engage in the development of water service delivery. The zonal Administration Office should mobilize various actors to work on water wells development in rural areas of the zone. As I mentioned earlier the few stakeholders that work in the water sector only implement small projects independently. Instead of this I recommend that these stakeholders or actors should aggregate their resources to construct long-term/sustainable water projects.

Related to budget, the government and NGOs should allocate reasonable budget for water sector development. In addition, the community should contribute some amount of money for the development of water sector. In other words there must be community participation in the implementation process of water projects.

Regarding maintenance of existing water wells, I suggest that there should be regular monitoring of water wells by stakeholders and maintenance service should be given on a regular basis. To make this service sustainable, training should be provided to selected community members on how to repair water facilities. Moreover, spare parts should be supplied either by concerned stakeholders or by private suppliers. There should also be an awareness creation program on proper utilization of water wells.

Related to power outage, the government should develop a separate electric distribution line for the water service.

Question 3: Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

Of the solutions I mentioned, I give priority for the following solution: Stakeholders instead of engaging in small/short-term water projects independently, they should aggregate or combine their resources and work on long-term/sustainable projects.

If this solution occurs:

The community will not travel long distance to fetch water,

There will be sufficient clean water supply,

We can minimize water born diseases,

Finally, there will be smooth relation between the community and  the government.

Question 4: In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your zone for sustainable water service? What could be improved?

Honestly speaking there is no coordination at all between stakeholders working in the water sector. Everyone runs independently or separately, and there is no coordinated effort for sustainable water service delivery. Therefore the following should be improved to ensure sustainability of water service:

Stakeholders should plan activities together,

Stakeholders should aggregate their efforts and resources so that they can implement sustainable/long-term projects,

There should be a stakeholder platform on which stakeholders can share information.

Question 5: In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the zone? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

In my opinion, coordinated effort can lead to sustainable water service by alleviating the problems which I mentioned earlier. 

Coordination avoids duplication of efforts. 

If there is strong coordination, stakeholders can aggregate their resources and efforts so that they can ensure sustainable water service delivery. 

If there is coordination one actor or stakeholder repair water wells constructed by other stakeholder so that there will be sustainable water service in the area.
