1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent 2: The first challenge related to sanitation service is poor awareness of the community on waste management. That is some community members and service providing institutions like hotels and restaurants directly link their liquid wastes with diches and rivers carelessly without considering the consequences. Instead of emptying their latrines, using liquid waste collection vehicles they simply connect their waste lines to rivers and this malpractice seriously affect those people that use the river water for various purposes.
The other major problem to provide sustainable sanitation service is that there is no appropriate permanent waste disposal/landfill site in and outside the town. Similarly, there are no proper sewerage lines for disposal of liquid wastes in the town. Moreover, there is no place to dispose liquid wastes using liquid waste collection vehicles. There was one liquid waste disposal site that was developed by French government support long years back. However, currently the site is not functional due to accumulation and over flooding of wastes. The waste assimilation mechanism is outdated and non-functional. Now the town administration has identified another place but it does not started the project implementation. 
Respondent 1: Just to add on what has been said by my colleague the community simply throw solid wastes into open ditches due to lack of awareness on solid waste management. If they clear wastes from their houses they do not care about for the environment so they contaminate the area and block ditches by solid wastes carelessly. 
The other major problem related to sanitation is absence of liquid waste disposal place in the town. The problem of liquid waste disposal place is more severe than the solid waste disposal site problem. Now a day, many communal and private latrines are overflowing and contaminating various residential areas. When we ask the community why they do not empty their latrines, they argued that the government owned liquid waste collection vehicles do not provide service due to absence of waste disposal place. On the other hand, the private pit emptying service providers charges large amount of money which we cannot afford. Moreover, we do not know where they dispose the waste.
Respondent 2: The other sanitation related problem of the town is that there are no public latrine/toilets around stadiums, bus stations and other public gathering areas. The existing few public toilets have no access for water and they are not convenient and attractive for use. Because of this, there is a practice of open defecation and urination in different public areas. 
Interviewer: What are the main challenges related to the water service? 
Respondent 2: Currently there is a water supply shortage in the town and not all parts of the town can get water service every day at a time rather we have access for water in shift basis. Particularly new residential expansion areas do not have access for clean water. Even in our office, we cannot get water during office hours so it is difficult for us to use the toilets. I think this supply shortage is the result of the rapid population growth due to rural urban migration.
Respondent 1: To add few points on water service problems, the town hotels and restaurants are under serious sanitation problems due to absence of regular water supply. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Respondent 1: Pertinent to community awareness problem on solid waste disposal and management I would like to suggest that there should be strong coordination between all actors at different level including the community representatives to enhance to the community awareness on waste disposal and management. Sanitation issue should not be left for one institution rather it requires a coordinated planning and effort to ensure sustainable sanitation service. Recently the town municipality is trying to introduce a system in that the community will pay for waste collection service particularly for solid wastes along with their monthly water bill. Therefore, youth associations will be formed that collect solid wastes from the community and these groups will get their fee from the municipality in regular basis. I hope this will improve the sanitation situation of the town in the short run. 
Respondent 2: In addition to the awareness creation activities, there should be sanitary regulations and enforcement mechanisms to control those individuals that carelessly dispose wastes in residential and public areas. The town municipality should set penalty on those that violate sanitary regulations and laws. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned that there is a serious problem related to liquid and solid waste disposal place. What do you suggest to alleviate this problem?
Respondent 2: The town municipality has already identified a waste disposal site but it does not started the project implementation due budget constraint. Therefore, it should push the regional government to allocate the required budget as well as mobilize fund from potential donors for the implementation of the projects.
Interviewer: You have also mentioned the absence of communal latrines/toilet in public areas and slum residential areas as a main challenge for the sanitation of the town. What do you recommend to alleviate this problem? 
Respondent 2: The town administration in collaboration with other stakeholders should construct convenient communal latrines in public and slum residential areas. This obviously improve the sanitation situation of the town and will serve as a means of livelihood for unemployed groups of the society.
Respondent 1: There are only about 3 public latrines in the town and these latrines do not have access for water as a result their situation is not convenient for users. Therefore, we should have to construct considerable number of public latrines in public areas to minimize open defecation and urination. Currently, there are large number of immigrants every day, these people have no access for toilets and the hotels and restaurants allow only their customers to use their sanitation facilities. Thus, developing standard public latrines will remarkably improve the town sanitation condition.  
Interviewer: Finally, you mentioned that there is water supply shortage in the town. What is the cause for the supply shortage and what do you suggest alleviating this problem?
Respondent 2: In my opinion, the main reasons for water supply shortage are the rapid growth of population and the establishment of University as well as the emergence of large number of manufacturing and service providing firms in the town. All this institutions access water from the supply source of the town water utility service. They do not have their own boreholes. This created the supply shortage in the town. In order to alleviate the problem related to supply shortage, the town water utility has started to construct new water wells but the project is not finalized yet. I hope if this project can be finalized on time, it might solve the problem at least partly. 
Respondent 1: The town administration should have strategic plan to improve town water supply service by considering the growing population and the emerging industries’ need.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent 1: Even though it is difficult to prioritize one problem from the others, developing appropriate waste disposal place both for liquid and solid wastes is the most important solution to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. I put this as a top priority because the town has serious sanitation problem due to absence standard waste disposal site. Residential areas are also highly contaminated by solid and liquid wastes.
Respondent 2: I strongly agree with my colleague. For instance, if we construct communal latrines in public and slum areas without having appropriate waste disposal place, it is meaningless because we cannot empty them when they become full.
Respondent 1: If we get a solution to the problem related to waste disposal place, we can significantly minimize the public health problem that arise due to poor sanitation service. For instance, we observe large number of latrines overflowing in different residential area and causing community health problems. Therefore, if there is waste disposal site we can reduce the rate of contamination by regularly emptying the latrine and dispose in the right place.
Respondent 2: Due to absence of waste disposal site in and around the town, the community is required to pay about ETB 3000 for private liquid waste collectors that dispose the waste into waste disposal sites of other town (according to the argument of the service providers) or into unknown areas. Thus, if we have standard waste disposal site within our locality, the community can get the service with reasonable price. Moreover, some institutions like abattoirs cannot provide service to the community due to waste disposal site problem. Therefore, alleviating this problem will improve the service provision capacity and quality of various manufacturing and service organizations.
