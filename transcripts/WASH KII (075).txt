1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion, what are the main problems of lack of sustainable sanitation in the city? 
Response: lack of awareness of the society is one problem. Even though they know how to clean, but they don’t know how to dispose the waste. The other one is after the waste are disposed and populated in one area, there is shortage of vehicles that uses to collect this material. There are only three tractors to collect wastes from the whole city. The third one is the area that uses as a dumping site is not modernized. Solid and liquid wastes are disposed together. We have studied to build a modern dumping site with the city administration but because of lack of budget we couldn’t continue.  The city is growing and more industries are emerging. They amount of waste is also growing. But it doesn’t have a proper and modernized place to collect it.  
Interviewer: is there anything you want to add? [question for the other respondent]
Response: yes. Regarding liquid type of waste, we are not responsible but for solid type of wastes, the society know they need to dispose wastes. But, when we they are asked to pay for the service they are not willing. We have around 14 associations who are responsible for collecting the wastes form different areas. But the society doesn’t have a habit of calling or asking this associations to collect the wastes. They throw it at night in places that should not be used as dumping site. This is a problem.  The second one is, the current dumping site is not good for use. The farmers around the site have complained many times about the site.

It doesn’t’ have fence and children and animal will enter into this area. Currently we have hired a security guard and planned to build a fence around the site.  We have made a study to build a modern dumping site but it needs huge budget to implement it. The city administration doesn’t have that kind of budget. Because of that we couldn’t continue more that its design.

Response: There was a study made in our sector to solve problem related with the system of collecting solid wastes. But it didn’t get approved by Miker bet. The other thing is the industries found in our city doesn’t recycle their waste or treat it with chemicals to make it harmless before releasing it to swage system.
