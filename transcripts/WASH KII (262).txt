1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda?

Interviewer: What are the major challenges of sustainability drinkable water supply in Woliso City?
Response: It is a good question. One of the problems challenging our city’s water supply is the imbalance between the number of population in the city and the available water supply. There is a scarcity in the supply. Second, expansion of construction in the city and the construction sector has its own needs. Third, the distance from the place where the water brought to the city and limited resource has impeded proper distribution of water to the community.
Interviewer: There is also distribution problem.
Response: Right.
Interviewer: First, there is supply problem, an imbalance between the supply and the number of the population. Second is expansion of construction the construction in the city.
Response: In addition to that there is also an increase in educational institutions. For example, Ambo University Woliso Campus’ opening of additional departments also increases its student intake. And the growing number of service and community institutions also increases the demand for water supply. 
Interviewer: However, the water supply is not growing proportionally.
Response: Yes. It is an issue challenging the city. It was built for a population of 30,000 or 40,000; yet about 93,000 people reside in the city according to current estimates. 
Interviewer: So, the current 93,000 people are utilizing the water infrastructure previously built for 40,000 people? 
Response: Yes. Of course there are some expansions, however, even if there are additions, they were assumed to service about 40,000 people.
Interviewer: Ok. Do you have service related problems? What other problems which challenge sustainability of water supply?
Response: The important thing is the means. It is challenging us.
Interviewer: What do you mean by means?
Response: Financial means.  
Interviewer: What about the water lines.
Response: To tell you the truth, our city’s water department is exemplary experience for the whole country in distribution within the available means and promptly maintaining water lines. Often times, because of lapse of information, there may be delay in maintenance. Another thing which must be underscored is, there is lack of coordination with Ethio Telecom. There may be damages on the lines and stall the service.
Interviewer: Ok. Once the water department lays the lines, then the telecom company damages the lines.
Response: Yes. There is a problem of maintain the damages and coordination.
Interviewer: So there is no coordination in infrastructure development.
Response: There is a problem of coordination as each department goes its own convenient way without regard for infrastructure built in that area previously.
Interviewer: Excellent. [05:00] you mentioned problems of supply. The city’s population is massively growing and new educational institutions are being opened driving the demand for water supply. However, the supply is still not enough as it was build for a population of 30,000. The other problem is damages on infrastructures by other institutions such as, Tele. What do you think needs to be done to solve these problems?
Response: First, the two institutions or any other concerned body should work in close coordination. One institution should not damage what is already built by the other. The two institutions should talk about the where each other’s lines are located.  Second, the organizations should avoid carelessness and need to collaborate and communicate on proposed infrastructure works to avoid costs. 
2.What ideas or recommendation do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewer: What needs to be done to solve the supply problem and balance the supply according to the population size?
Response: What I propose as a solution is first; the city’s water and sewerage department should reach out to supporting partners which can support to expand the city’s water supply according to its population size. Second, the water department should also work on awareness creation so that the community could participate in water development and conservation. Third, creating collaboration between international finance institutions, the regional government, federal government and nongovernmental organizations to seek sustainable solution as the city’s population will continue to grow. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? What next steps could happen…?
Interviewer: Which of the solutions you proposed should be given priority to solve the city’s water supply problem?
Response: Given the urgency of the problem, I think it is better to prioritize looking for funding.
Interviewer: If the proposed solutions are implemented, what changes do you think will it bring to the community in Woliso city?
Response: As I said, it is a good question. [10:00] Water is life, so is sanitation. If you solve the basic problems of the community it will also help in bringing about a developmental and health citizenry. This in turn helps the economic growth. Second, given our city’s proximity to the capital city, Addis Ababa the attraction of tourism will also help build its infrastructure and build its capacity and achieve independence by increasing its revenue. Third, we are planning to build city parks which will show case the culture and history and also serve as a recreational space for the youth. This will help not only as a source of income but also to cherish our values and cultural identity. 
Interviewer: Excellent. You have answered my question in a broad manner.
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water services? What could be improved?
Interviewer: Now the forth question is, in your personal opinion from the point of view of  insuring the sustainability of Woliso city water supply, what does the current state of coordination between different  institutions looks like, how effective is the coordination?


Response: Let alone coordination between government entities, even individuals will not be effective without coordination. There are some beginnings in coordination in our city; however it is not satisfactory yet.  There is a room for improvement in the area of coordination. Institution should also consider themselves as stakeholders and part of the solution rather than just calling the water department when there is a breakdown. The water department needs assistance from other institutions, and there are gaps in coordination so as to respond to the needs of the community in a timely manner. As I said earlier, there are gaps in coordination with Ethio Telecom and other entities. The municipal office is a service provider of a city and water supply to the city is part of its business. These are two sides of the same coin. It is difficult to say there is no coordination, yet is lack sorely. For instance, the municipality is the authority which overlooks this city’s infrastructure. The municipal is the authority which concerns the upkeep and maintenance of the city’s infrastructure; however, there are problems in fully taking responsibility and coordination.
Interviewer: There are some gaps in coordination.
Response: Yes there is a gap. Coordination lacks in preventing problems from the very beginning rather than rushing after the fact. We need to widely think about cause of effects of each institution’s action. There are promising beginnings but it is not enough. For instance, installation of a pump requires coordination with road works. They need to draw a common plan to prevent problem from happening again and strengthen check and balance between different entities.
Interviewer: I just needed to clarify this question. What could be improved about coordination? 
Response: 1st we need to plan and act together. Planning alone cannot do the job. [15:00] each bureau should asses about with which service provider entity it need to coordinate with to achieve effectiveness.  Second, a regular dialogue is necessary to appraise its performance to identify weaknesses and build on its success. Not only that, we also need to coordinate to create awareness on the community about the usage and upkeep of the infrastructure built. We need to start from the base starting from the farm to stakeholder institutions such as Tele and Electric utility. They need to create a strong relationship with each other rather than just call each other when they have a problem. 
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

Interviewer: Excellent. My final question is, you have raised earlier problems related with water sustainability. What role do you think coordination plays to solve these problems?
Response: Coordination has a huge role. It is not the same when you act individually and as a team. The capacity and know-how differs. The problems are easier to solve when we coordinate, however, they will get more complicated when we act separately. To solve our city’s water supply problem, even though the task is spear headed by the water bureau, it requires coordination with other stakeholders. If we band together the problems will be easier to solve but if we act separately thing will get complicated. For instance, when our office gives out data on the current state of the city’s population and when the study is provided to us by the concerned and entity and together reach out to donor and financer for support the problems will get easier. Another thing is, we have to cherish the result we find thorough our coordination as a common achievement and involving the community. Starting from community water committees and encouraging the participation of women, as the committee members are usually men when the water is mostly used by women. Especially to solve our city’s water supply problem, the issue of coordination should not be left to for this or that institution. Each bureau should stand and say we need to coordinate. 
Interviewer: Thank you very much. You gave an excellent explanation. I have finished my question. Thank you again. We have your contact and we will reach out to you when the learning alliance is launched.
Response: Thank you.
