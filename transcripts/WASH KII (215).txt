1.What are the main problems of water service sustainability in your town/woreda?
Respondent: most of the problems are primarily linked with quality issues during construction. 
Interviewer: What does quality issue in construction means, can you tell me in brief?
Respondent: For instance, some construction inputs are not provided according to the design at the beginning, so there is a high risk to break down easily. There are also some design issues. Some of the water points do not reach the distribution points due to several reasons and pipes might burst or leak frequently.    
Interviewer: what is the source of all these problems? 
Respondent: we have cash flow problems where we do not carry out payments for contractors on time and up on request. There is also logistics issue to conduct continuous monitoring and evaluation. 
Interviewer: you have illustrated issues related to quality of construction, power etc., what else? 
Respondent: staff turnover is another challenge. 
Respondent: starting from the managers of the sector, there is high staff turnover. This also includes experts engaged in the different institutes, especially water service. Some staff members leave the office before completing the project they designed. 
The other problem is lack sense of ownership from the community to protect water scheme projects. This is due to the fact that implementers were not responsible to create awareness initially. 
Respondent: The existing staff also lacks commitment and courage to accomplish assignments. Since we lack logistics and budget, staff members seem to be less motivated to go to field work.




2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: let’s proceed to the solutions. You have mentioned that construction quality of the water schemes really affects the sustainability of water service and is a contributing factor. So, what would be the solution to this issue?
Respondent:  we should carry out continuous discussions with all stakeholders involved and the community during pre-construction phases on design and standard issues. Besides, efforts should be made to make payments on time to the contractors and conduct thorough and continuous monitoring and evaluation.
Interviewer: you have pointed out that staff turnover is also an important factor. What should be done to tackle the issue?
Respondent:  experts who take trainings should sign agreement that abide them to work for some period of time in that specific area so that it would be able to retain them in the institute. We should also provide them with some incentives or financial rewards to keep them here for as long as we can.
Interviewer: you have stated that there is a gap in community ownership, what should be done to address that?
Respondent:  WASHCO at kebele level should be strengthened either by government or NGOs. Supporting them both in training and financing will help a lot to bring desired changes. 
Interviewer: it is not easy to create sense of ownership in the community, who do you think should do what?
Respondent:  we are discussing to allocate some finance to kebele leaders, as an incentive to help them create community awareness since they are the heads of the WASHCOs. In some kebeles, simple maintenance trainings should be given to the committee members themselves. 
Interviewer: the problem you have mentioned lastly is lack of commitment and coordination. What should be done?
Respondent: starting from allocation of budget and other resources, we should understand the problem as a common issue. And we should coordinate our efforts in the most efficient way. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: you have pointed out a lot of solutions. Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? 
Respondent: If we are able to reduce the turnover rate, and if the expert who designed the project also participates in construction, implementation and monitoring process, it would solve many problems related to service sustainability.
