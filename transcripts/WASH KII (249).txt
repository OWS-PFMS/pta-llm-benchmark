1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: What everybody in our kebele rises as a problem is lack of damping sites for solid and liquid waste? 
Next, the community itself is not accepting sanitation issue as their concern, rather the government’s. Even though they are aware of it, they throw wastages out everywhere; this shows that they are not accepting it as their concern. We, our office does different researches regarding what the problems are and what should be done to solve them and also reported to different stakeholders regularly. This leads us to plan and allocate budget for the next year to buy a sewage suction vehicle. In addition to this, we also plan to discuss with the community regularly in order to let them accept ‘every sanitary problem is my problem’ and to check on the solutions.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Interviewer; what do you think should be done to permanently solve these problems in the damping site?
Respondent: we tried to solve the problem together with the woreda officials. We have information that government authorities are discussing about it with the community members living around the site and they are creating awareness to them. 
The city administration chair persons and the mayor should be involved, create discussion sessions and take the problems as their problem too in order to get a solution. The city administration should give additional replacement locations for the damping sites if needed and solve such problems permanently.
Interviewer; you raised that decision makers are not participating in different discussion programs and learning alliances. What should be done to solve this permanently?
Respondent: I can’t say that they don’t know about the sanitation problems in our city. So, they should meet with different stakeholders to answer the questions raised from peoples. 
The second thing is they should participate when meetings are held and they should try to solve it by discussing about these issues with respected stakeholders.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The main thing is to solve the damping site problem. Second, at least to have two garbage collector vehicles and we should side to the city administration and help them to be involved in the solution.
Interviewer: if the damping site is prepared, what changes do you think will it bring?
Respondent The changes are 
-There will be no visible dry garbage everywhere, but the liquids can flow through the waste water discharge.
- Our city will be as clean as other hygienic cities.
At the last, I would like to thank you and people should be aware and accept that sanitation problem is our personal problem and its consequences, the city administration should try to understand and help the concerns and problems raised from the peoples.
