I1: In your opinion, what good do you think this clean system of maintaining water has brought to your community?
R: People no longer travel long distances to fetch water, children no longer fall sick from drinking dirty water hence, reduced expenses on treatment. 
I1: Are there hindrances in trying to maintain this water source? 
R: Community members do not want to pay maintenance money for the boreholes. 
I1: What recommendations can you share with us to do away with these problem?
R: The Ugandan government and non-governmental organizations should contribute money to community borehole maintenance so people can find it easier to pay their part of the fees. Also, saving money in the bank annually would provide a fall back solution when the borehole needs repair. 
I1: Which of these recommendations do you believe is more important?
R: Government contributing to the maintenance cost. 
I1: If government was to contribute to water maintenance, would that really help the situation?
R: Absolutely. Community members would actually be more excited and manage the borehole better if they didn’t stress over maintenance fees. 
I1: What do you think is lacking in this maintenance system though? 
R: More sensitization of the community is needed for people to understand that their effort is needed to protect clean water sources.
