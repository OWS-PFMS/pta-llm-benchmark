1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
 Respondent 1: In my opinion, the problems related to water service in the town are first, there is water supply shortage in the town. The existing supply source is not sufficient to satisfy the entire needs of the community. Even though the town water utility office discussed with regional water authority, it does not get solution for the problem. 
On the other hand, the existing water lines are very old and sometimes they can be contaminated by liquid wastes. There is no appropriate design for new installation and for maintenance of the existing ones. 
Interviewer: What is the reason for water supply shortage?
Respondent 1: Previously the water schemes were developed to supply water for the existing small number of the town’s population at the time. However, currently the population is growing rapidly and large number of manufacturing and service providing firms and universities are established in the town. Thus, the existing water supply source cannot cover the entire need of the community and the industries. In other words, the growth of the town and the water supply growth are not balanced.
In addition, the existing water supply lines do not reach those residential areas located in higher altitude.
Interviewer: What the main challenges related to sanitation service sustainability? 
Respondent 2: The problems related to sanitation are very broad and multi-dimensional. The problems ranges from infrastructure to use of sanitation service. In the first place, the community lacks awareness on sanitation. The responsible government bodies do not try to improve the community awareness on sanitation. Even those educated individuals that have awareness on sanitation and its related problems do not have courage to keep their environment safe and clean.
Interviewer: You said the community lack awareness on sanitation. Can you explain in detail in what areas they lack awareness?
Respondent 2: When I say the community lacks awareness on sanitation, for instance, they do not have a clear understanding on how to dispose their liquid wastes without affecting the surrounding environment. When they construct septic tanks, they develop it in away the liquid waste is absorbed in the ground instead of making it cemented and empty when it gets full. This practice predispose for contamination of surface and ground water. The community do not have understanding of the negative effects of such improper practice on the environment.
On the other hand, some individuals directly link their liquid wastes into rivers and open ditches. This practice highly contaminate river water that is used by large segment of the population. 
Interviewer: Are there any other problems related to sanitation service in the town?
Respondent 2: The other main challenge related to sanitation service is that the town has no proper landfill site for both liquid and solid wastes. The existing waste disposal site is not up to date and accumulated with excess amount of waste. Now it is not providing service as it is creating serious public health problem on the community that are residing in nearby areas. The community residing in nearby areas of the waste disposal site strongly opposing the disposal of any more wastes in the area due to the associated health problems.
Respondent 1: In addition to what are mentioned by my colleague, I can say the number one reason for public health problem of the town are the prevailing hygiene and sanitation challenges. The town governing bodies lack understanding on the public health consequences of sanitation problem. They do not give priority for sanitation service infrastructure development when they allocate annual budget for various activities. When constructing inside roads they do not take into account future construction of drainage and sewerage lines. In general, the infrastructure development in the town do not consider sewerage line development alongside.
Currently, there is no liquid waste collection service in the town due to absence of waste disposal place. Sometimes some service providing institutions dispose their liquid waste on farmland during nighttime and this malpractice is seriously affecting the life of farmers in the surrounding areas. The wastes released from factories is also another public health challenge in the town.  
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: You mentioned that there is water supply shortage in the town and the existing water supply service is not sufficient to satisfy the needs of the entire community. What do you recommend to alleviate this problem? 
Respondent 2: Currently the town water authority and the municipality are strongly advising and pushing private manufacturing and service providing institutions (big hotels and restaurants) including the university to develop their own shallow wells within their compound. Accordingly, the university is taking the initiative to prepare its own water source. The town water authority is starting development of additional boreholes that will serve the community. I think the water supply shortage will be solved in the near future. I would like to suggest that we should improve our water management (by both the community and firms) practice through various awareness enhancement campaigns. Without introducing efficient water utilization system, we cannot ensure sustainable water service in the town by developing new water sources alone.
However, the problems related to sanitation do not get solution both by the town water authority and the municipality.
Interviewer: You mentioned that there is a design problem and the existing water lines are very old and requires replacement. What do your recommend to alleviate this problem?
Respondent 2: I think this problem is very serious and the town’s governing authorities should give emphasis for the issue and work on replacing the old and damaged water lines by allocating sufficient budget.
Interviewer: You said that lack of awareness on waste management is a serious problem to provide sustainable sanitation service. What has been done so far to alleviate this problem? What should be done by the relevant actors of the sector?
Respondent 1: The problem related to sanitation should not be left for one organization. All actors that are working in WASH sector should also exert their effort to improve the sanitation service of the town. The problems could not be managed through awareness enhancement campaign alone. We have to provide sanitation service (waste collection service) along with the awareness enhancement campaign. For instance, due to absence of waste disposal site in the town, the people are forced to pay about ETB 2000 for private liquid waste collection service providers that dispose the waste in other towns that have waste disposal place. This price is unaffordable for the community members. Previously the community has access for this service with ETB 500, that is provided by the town’s water and sewerage utility, however, due distance and cost they are not using the service.
Respondent 2: To ensure sustainable sanitation service, there should be coordinated planning and activities between sectors. The town administration or municipality should assign task related to sanitation to concerned bodies and follow their implantation status every time. For instance, our office (health office) remarkably engage on activities of awareness enhancement campaign on sanitation and hygiene. Moreover, other WASH sector actors both governmental and non-governmental actors should be given clearly stated mandates and responsibilities to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. I can say, currently each actor is not disposing its responsibilities related to sanitation affairs.  
The government should also allocate appropriate budget for the sector offices that wok in sanitation service.
Interviewer: You mentioned that there are no appropriate waste disposal site both for liquid and solid wastes. What do you recommend to alleviate these problems? 
Respondent 2: The existing landfill sites both for liquid and solid wastes are out of service due to accumulation of large amount of wastes. Moreover, it is now highly surrounded by residents and creating public health problems. The surrounding residents are opposing further disposal of wastes in the area. Therefore, the government should identify appropriate site, allocate sufficient budget and construct standard liquid and solid waste disposal place outside residential areas. Unless we do this, we cannot ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town.
There should also be appropriate incentive mechanism to benefit those farmers residing around the upcoming new waste disposal sites. 
Interviewer: You have said industrial wastes are contaminating or polluting nearby residential and farmlands. What do you suggest to minimize this problem?
Respondent 1: Currently the municipality or the administration is initiating and pushing manufacturing firms to develop their own waste treatment plant within their compound. Consequently, some industries already started construction of these important plants.  Parallel with initiating of the existing firms, we have to set regulations and advise new emerging firms to come up with their own comprehensive waste management plan along with their project proposal. There should also strong monitoring mechanism during implementation of their projects.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent 2: In my opinion, the most important solution to ensure sustainable sanitation service is that developing appropriate waste disposal site both for liquid and solid wastes.  Moreover, there should be sewerage line to directly collect liquid waste and dispose the wastes with the help of the sewerage lines that will be connected to the destination liquid waste disposal place. 
If this serious sanitation service related problems are alleviated, we can remarkably minimize the top ten sanitation related public health problems. This implies we can reduce treatment costs that possibly incurred by the government and the public at large. We can also build healthy and productive society and make our town attractive for tourism.
