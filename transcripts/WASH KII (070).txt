1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer:  In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in Dbreberhan city?
Response: There are many challenges. The first one is when building sewage system for the city the stakeholders who are responsible for this system and who uses this system are not aware of the use. And don’t give attention to the tasks we do. Sewage system building is the hardest job from all the jobs we. 
Interviewer: Ok. the governors don’t have enough awareness and attention.
Response: yes, the second problem is; as a result of lack of awareness there is lack of coordination between stakeholders. For example, coordination with environmental protection and health sectors is very poor. Also, coordination with industries like, Dashen Beer and Habesha Beer and other industries is very poor. The waste from this industry is disposed in improper way. Even though, there is a low that prohibit untreated waste to be released in to the environment. But it is not applied.  
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: The problem you listed are main and critical. What is the solution for the problem you mentioned?
Response: we focus and are accountable for managing domestic type of wastes. We are not responsible for wastes from industries. Because, every industry is supposed to provide EIR. EIR means Environmental Impact Assessment. And there is a responsible sector that evaluates this EIR. So, this sector should work correctly to minimize our challenge. If this sector doesn’t follow and monitor the industries sewage system, they will dispose the liquid waste without treatment. 
[00:04:47]
Environmental Protection Sector is not available in city level in Debreberehan City. But in Oromia region it is available. So, the sector should be available here in our city. The other solution is improving coordination between sectors. Our city is included in World banks project with 22 other cities. There is a stream committee in that project. This kind of stream committee should be formed here to improve the coordination. For example, there are hotels that release their toilet in to the road way sewage system. This must be controlled and controlling must be done with coordination. The committee must be formed from all stakeholders and must be strong. If the city administration and other bodies are involved, I believe it will be successful. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer:  which is most important from the solution you mentioned? And which one should be given the first priority?
Response: I believe awareness should be given the first priority. Like other projects in the city, for example road building and other infrastructure sewage and sanitation jobs should be seen seriously. After working on awareness, coordination will come next. Without awareness coordination will not be effective. 
Interviewer:  If these solutions are implemented properly, what improvement will come?
Response: starting from the households to the big industry’s sewage system will be managed properly. We can identify and act on illegal acts. For example, if there is a hotel that connected its toilet to the road sewerage system, we (Water supply and sewerage Service Office) can’t directly stop the action. We will notify to health sector and health sector will take corrective measures to this hotel. But, if health sector doesn’t take any measure the hotel will continue it improper act. So, when we coordinate will have more power. 
[00:09:57]
We have coordination with health sector even though it isn’t enough. But we have improved this coordination.   

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable sanitation services? What could be improved?
 
Interviewer:  How do you see the current coordination between the sectors? 
Response: It has been said and they will start to work but, there is no continuity. When they start they work well, but, it will be back to the same thing for this reason, by assigning enduring and strong responsible personnel, if they can control correctly very good looking city and sanitation will be beautiful. Otherwise the beginning will never let you finish in the specific place; for example, let me take our condition, they told us to put wastes in this thing like trash paper and so on and when we say take it please, they say pay and go and in addition, we have to treat them nicely so, there is shortage and if there was no shortage competition will be there which will minimize the burden for the service seekers.
