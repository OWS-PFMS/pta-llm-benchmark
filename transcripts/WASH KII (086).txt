1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: What do you think are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in Debrebirhan town? 
Respondent: The main problem in our town regarding sanitation is the absence of treatment plant. There is a sub-standard waste disposal site but it is now getting full. Therefore, there is no proper waste dumping site and many just dump waste in pits that later join the rivers. Liquid waste from public latrines and communal latrines has also been dumped in a pit that is now full. As a result, the community has no place to dump waste at this time. What I think is if we had had a waste treatment plant the waste could have treated there.
The second problem is industrial waste. Even though majority of them treat their waste at least to a certain extent, there are complaints from residents that some factories discharge their waste untreated. This problem has not yet been addressed. Releasing waste without treating it has caused damages to the farmers nearby, including bad smell. This means industries also do not have a place to dump their waste.
The other is related with sewerage lines. There is a lack of waste discharge. The currently built sewerage pipes are clogged with garbage; because, both the solid and liquid wastes are dumped in the sewerage lines. The pipes are then cleaned during the rainy season. Therefore, this is the third problem; the sewerage lines are not enough for the town and the already built ones are of low quality. 
Another problem is lack of public latrines. In locations such as marketplaces there is no public latrine. Consequently, many practice open defecation which negatively affect the environment. If there are many open defecation sites, coupled with other wastes, the town will not be able to be clean and safe. This open defecation practices are common in places such as hotels, restaurants, crowded places, and around marketplaces. This is I think the other problem. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Respondent: In order to address these issues there should be a treatment plant in the town. if we have a waste treatment plan, all the wastes produced would go there and get treated. So, a modern waste treatment plan should be designed and built that can serve the town for a longer time. This is one solution. 
Interviewer: It is true that it would be good to have a modern waste treatment plant. Why have not built yet? Who should build? 
Respondent: It might not be included in the strategic plan of the town. The town is started growing unexpectedly. The town has completed in three years what it planned to achieve in ten years. The town administration could be overwhelmed by the unanticipated population and industrial growth in the town. As a university, we could make the design and assessment in collaboration with other partners. This is I think the solution for the first problem. 
Second is regarding the industrial waste. Whenever any factory is built, its treatment plant should be built along with the main plant. Environmental impact assessment should be conducted before any factor commences production. There are still certain factories that release their discharges their waste to nearby rivers. The factories should be accountable. Such misconducts should be monitored by licensing and regulatory body. Each and every factory should be obliged by the regulatory body to treat its own waste before releasing it to the environment. 
The other thing is drainage. Firstly, the town administration should take the responsibility to build additional wastewater drainages. Secondly, the community should contribute its share, because the community itself is the beneficiary. On top of that, the community should protect the existing drainage system. The community members should not dump things that clog the pipes. 
Interviewer: How such change could occur? Open defecation is common here, right? How could these problems get solved? 
Respondent: The fourth problem is the practice of open defecation. In order to solve this problem, it is required to aware the community. We should employ new approaches. Most of the community members know that throwing garbage on the street is bad, so they do it during the night. Therefore, we should employ simple psychological influence techniques, like the one the health office has been using; for instance, using white and red flags. Besides, rewarding good practices can be useful. Different incentives can be given to communities that have clean environment. Open defecation is common in places where there is a piece of idle land. Therefore, these idle lands should be developed. If a retail shop or coffee house is built on the lands, people will not be encouraged to practice open defecation. In fact, latrines can be built beside the shops that can be a source of income. The latrine should not necessarily require digging a pit; rather portable latrines can be used for an affordable fee. The very community around the open areas that has been suffering from the problem of open defecation should be the beneficiaries of such projects. If we do so, there will be no open defecation and throwing garbage inappropriately. 
The other is garbage from individual household. There is garbage which is not garbage. It should be turned into an opportunity. 
Interviewer: What do you mean garbage which is not garbage?
Respondent: Kitchen garbage is not garbage. Take for example, you might buy a kilo of banana a quarter of it is just the peel. If the peel was garbage we would not have bought it in the first place. It can be turned into organic fertilizer. So, we should think how we can turn such wastes into organic fertilizer. People will start to collect the garbage not throw it away, because it will be a source of income. Then, it is vital use different reward mechanisms to encourage communities that keep their environment clean. Debrebirhan University also has experts in preparation of organic fertilizer, so we can contribute in the process. If green spaces are developed and equipped with fitness gears, people will exercise and keep themselves healthy and fit. This way community members will keep their environment safe while at the same time maintain healthy and strong body. 
The other solution is to build public latrines in populated area. If public latrines are constructed, we could be able to reduce the extent of the issue. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

Interviewer: Of the solutions you have listed, which is the most important? What next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The most pressing issue of the town is the absence of waste treatment plant. So, the first thing should be addressing this problem. If waste treatment plant is built other problems I mentioned in the neighborhoods could be resolved. The existence of treatment plant would create the opportunity to treat whatever waste is generated in the town. The treated waste water can be used to create an artificial lake and greenery area near the treatment plant. The byproduct of the treatment plant can also be used as a fertilizer. So, the treatment plant would have other concomitant benefits on top of its primary function, i.e. treating waste water.
