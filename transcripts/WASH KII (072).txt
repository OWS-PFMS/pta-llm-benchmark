
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: 
Response: The main problems are shortage of sanitary supplies that are needed for the job. For example, clean water, solid and liquid disposing services are not provided with needed quantity and quality. Additionally, there is lack of awareness. We have studied that 80% of our societies problem is lack of personal hygiene. I think this is caused by lack of awareness. The third problem is lack of needed infrastructure to create sustainable sanitation.   The last one is budget deficiency. 
Interviewer: do you think the last problem; budget constraint is related with lack of sanitary supplies?
Response: no, the budget deficiency problem is a specific budget shortage for implanting or creating sustainable sanitation.  
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: in your opinion, what solutions do you suggest? To read the question directly, what ideas or recommendation do you have for the existing problems or to the problem you listed?
Response: If we stand with strong and motivated to the problems faced coordinating the society, NGOs and other responsible sectors, there will be no problem with no solution. So, if we improve the leadership quality and the coordination, we can solve shortage of sanitation supplies. The other solution is we have to create awareness to the society to keep his/her personal sanitation using different methods. We have to aware the society the effect of sanitation, what problems will be caused due to lack of sanitation and what should be done to keep sanitation. So, if we create awareness on the society, the expense that the society spend on keeping their health ok will be minimized. If individuals keep their hygiene, it will bring sustainable sanitation. The problem of lack of infrastructure is mainly a responsibility of government and non-governmental organizations. NGOs mainly focus on capacity building. They focus on creating awareness on the society. Additional to the awareness creation modeling is also addressed. For example, without giving a proper disposing area if we try to create awareness on the society it will not bring change. First, we have to provide dumping site and other infrastructures. NGOs should have role on this kind of activities also.  Also, if the government assign more budget on creating sanitation rather than health, it will bring more change and improvement.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable sanitation services? What could be improved?
Interviewer: In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable sanitation services? What could be improved?
Response: I can’t say there is good coordination between sectors. It is poor and not continuous. Sometimes it will be strong, sometimes it will be weak. This is because there is no strong follow up. To solve this one dedicated committee should be organized and it should make its main task to follow up the coordination. At the same time this committee will take responsibility and accountability for the performance of the coordination. The committee members should be selected based on knowledge, structure of the sectors and from the society that needs the service. For example, out of the vulnerable.
