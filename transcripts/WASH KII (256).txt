1) Interviewer: Okay my first question is based on your opinion; please state the challenges in regards to providing a sustainable water service in this town? I am asking for your personal opinion.
Interviewee: Well, first of all, I can't say that our problem in regards to water service is not even near to what I hear in neighboring towns like Welkite and even in Addis Ababa. But there are some problems that should be mentioned. Like when we have a broken pump, it will take time for them to fix it. They are supposed to fix it so that it can be used by the residents, but maybe they have a problem of budget or... I don't know. It takes a long time. Then the other issue I see in regards to the water service is that sometimes the water tanker needs to be cleaned, and when they want to do it they don't inform the residents. I don't know, maybe it's because we do not have FM station in our town. We are not much into social media. But they can use a traditional way to address the people when such kind of disruption is scheduled. Then another issue to mention is that those people who live on the other side of the tanker, I think it's 04 Kebele, they do not get water service. They rarely get the water service and the reason given to this is that the place is a bit sloppy for the pipeline. So the pump doesn't have enough power to pull the water upwards. You know that area, right? It's past the Teachers area... behind Negash lodge. 
Interviewer: So why is the reason that those parts of the town are not getting adequate water service? Is this because of the pimp...
Interviewer: Yes, mainly it's the pump that doesn't have enough power. [05:00] But they are building another water tank around OP. I have many friends there and even I spend most of my time there. They are told to wait until that water tank is built so that they can connect from that tank. 
Interviewer: So you have mentioned three challenges. the first one is about the delay on fixing broken pumps, then the second one is when they do a clean up on water storages, you are not notified in advance so that you prepare yourselves. Then the third issue is about those parts in 04 Kebele not getting adequate water service. 
Interviewee: Yes, they sometimes get the water at night time because the pressure will be low, but most of the time it's not there at all.
Interviewer: So why is the reason for this delay on fixing broken pipelines? Why are they delaying?
Interviewee: No, the 04 Kebele issue is about the pump not having enough power to pull the water on that sloppy area. The pump does not have enough power. But if you come to 02 Kebele, say sometimes back two pumps were broken in that area and it took them more than 15 days to fix them. So the water service was disrupted for that period of time.
Interviewer: But what do you think is the reason for the delay in repairing the water pumps?
Interviewee: I think they don't have enough budget. That's what they say. Everything here is related to a budget issue like they start putting cobblestone for pavement and suddenly it stops. When you ask why they tell you they don't have enough budget to continue it.
Interviewer: Okay, do you want to add more challenges?
Interviewee: Well, there is also an issue on the payment system. When people go to water authority to pay their bill, the queue is long enough to make you spend the whole day there. Also to connect you to water service, like we connected recently. It took us more than 15 days to finalize it. 

2) Interviewer: Okay, now that you have mentioned challenges on the sustainable water services, how can these challenges be fixed? What is your suggestion on solutions to those challenges?
Interviewee: Well, on the notification issue, the city administration communication office can try traditional way to address the residents on scheduled water service disconnections since we do not have FMs or other media outlets. They can try putting posters on main public places like the Kebele administration. The people can read that and also inform each other to take the necessary steps on storing more water before the service is disrupted. About the delay in repairing pumps, I think the water authority can allocate funds from other projects by giving this kind of issues a priority. If you have a budget for a planned project that will take place after two months and you also have a broken pump which disrupted water service to a certain amount of residents, you can allocate that budget to repairing this pump because you still have two months for the other project. Water is very important to the residents and disruption on services will create another disaster and should be given a priority. About the 04 Kebele issue, I think the issue is about the pump not having enough power. Even the government knows about this, so the best solution is to change the pump with another pump which has enough power to reach the hill. If that's not possible then they should finish that water tank located in OP and should connect those hill areas to that water tank. But if building that water tank will take years, then I think the government should look for temproary solutions to fix the problem because the residents of that Kebele are suffering too much.
3) Interviewer: Okay, so out of these solutions you provided me now, which one should be prioritized? Which solution is more important in your opinion?
Interviewee: I think the easy thing to fix and the most important one for all parts of the town is about the notification issue. This issue is affecting all parts of the town and the government should fix it easily. Last time we had a show in our Kebele's hall... [10:00]
Interviewer: Please make it more clear, I mean are you saying that the government should notify the residents before any kind of schedule water disruption?
Interviewer: yes, that's what I am saying. And since we do not have media to do that, maybe it will be a good idea for the city administration to create FM station to address this and other issues to the public.
Interviewer: Okay, let's say that all the solutions you provided me right now are implemented. So what do we expect to see in the town? I mean what are the changes you expect to see in this town?
Interviewee: Well, we are talking about an important factor to life. So when you improve that factor you should expect the life of our people to be improved. It will also build a connection between the government and the people. When the water service is disrupted without an advance notification, the people starting from those who are in the city administration will have trouble getting water. But if the advance notification is there, the people can save water so that they can use it during the disruption time.

4) Interviewer: Okay in your opinion how do you see the coordination between the local sectors on the sustainable water service issue? Do they communicate well to give adequate water service?
Interviewee: I don't think there is coordination at all. Like now if you pass by Negash Lodge, you can see there is a broken water pipeline right on the middle of the road. If the Roads authority wants to build a road, then they should communicate with water authority service so that the pipelines which obviously will be disconnected for quite some time until the road is constructed. I usually see pipelines break under such conditions. Even when you want to connect a water pipeline to your place, you'll have to damage the already built road because they didn't plan for such incident. If they plan together, before any road is built, they will see how many pipelines and also even telephone wires should be in that section. They can plan together which will help them not to distro one another's property. 
Interviewer: So in related to coordination, what should be improved?
Interviewee: Well, every town has development stages. So when you plan for town development I think those sectors should be included in that developments plan so that they can also plan their way ahead. [15:00] The water authority will come with its own plan as well as the telephone company. So the development will go on much smoother.

5) Interviewer: My last question is for those challenges you have mentioned earlier, what is the role of coordination in finding a solution to those challenges?
Interviewee: Well the first issue is minimizing cost. When a pipeline is broken we are losing clean water. Then when the water authority comes to fix it, they damage the road and it will be another cost for road authority to fix that. So each action is bringing more cost unless those sectors work together and save not only money but time too. There is also a human resource issue. If they coordination, they will not have a human resource problem at all. You will also satisfy the residents when you plan and work together. There will be no complaints from your customers.
