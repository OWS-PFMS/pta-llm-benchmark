Question 1: In your opinion what do you think are the main challenges to water service sustainability in Kitui County? Could you explain in detail how these challenges influence water service sustainability? 
Respondent: One is the knowledge. Most of management committees, when we construct these water structures, we hand over to community and many of them they have no knowledge on water, they need training to sustain the project like money management, O&M (Operation and maintenance) and what a few.
Interviewer: So what other challenges?
Respondent: The other challenge is the funding. The funding we get from National Government, County Government not enough to cover the needy areas.
Interviewer: What other challenge?
Respondent: Other challenges, as an office we lack transport, we have no vehicle to meet the community effectively.
Interviewer: So you mean vehicle to go monitor what the communities are doing?
Respondent: Yeah, what they are doing, we have a challenge on transport.
Interviewer: Any other challenge you think is contributing to lack of sustainability?
Respondent: The main one is knowledge; they are not trained effectively to the fine details about management, money management that is funds and O&M.
Question 2: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?  
Respondent: We are consulting our seniors at County level, our officers so that we can train these communities and have the transport done but we have challenges in funding.
Interviewer: So what are you proposing? What are your ideas to those challenges, you have talked about a number of challenges like the management committee lacking adequate knowledge, funding, you've said funding isn't enough then you've said lack of transport to do monitoring and then the training. So what solutions do you have?
Respondent: If we can get other actors to support us we can train these communities, they can also give us transport if they have that capacity or if the County Government we support trainings maybe County Government can support on transport. When you train them, they will be very effective. They can manage these water projects. When we hand over they operate like one year, they collapse. Most of the machines when they breakdown they have no capacity to maintain them, to do repairs.
Interviewer: Any other solution that you have or recommendation?
Question 3: Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps will happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The main is funding. We have the capacity of training, we can train them, that is our mandate but funding is the main challenge.
Interviewer: So if you can get funding then?
Respondent: We can train them.
Question 4: What kind of interventions are you implementing in Kitui and what influenced/informed the selection of these interventions?
Respondent: Mainly we are doing earth dam construction, sand dams, drilling boreholes, and we equip them, we hand over to the community and then we welcome other actors like yourselves who support us when we site a borehole we get an NGO, they drill, equip and then we hand over to the community.
Interviewer: So what influenced or what informed the selection of those interventions. the boreholes, the earth dams?
Respondent: We go to the community direct then we ask them what they need, then we go for site visits we see what is viable. Some areas they can propose for an earth dam but the soil is not suitable for earth dam construction so we get an option like borehole, sand dam etc. 
Question 5: How has participating in the WASH forum influenced your organization’s interventions?
Interviewer: Have you participated in the WASH Forums as an office?
Respondent: As an office yeah.
Interviewer: So how has that influenced your organization's interventions?
Respondent: When we partner with NGOs like WASH programs, they support us and then we get a big coverage on water.
Interviewer: What else, how else has participating in the WASH Forum influenced your decisions or your interventions?
Respondent: We are doing well and as I said they help us to have the water structures and when they support us we make average area a big one.
