1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your town/woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda?
Respondent: There is a huge water reservoir problem. The one that is currently serving was built fifteen years ago when the number of residents of the town was around three thousand to four thousand. Nowadays there the town’s population is around fifteen thousand. Therefore, the existing water reservoir is not sufficient.  
Interviewer: What about other problems?
Respondent: There is also a problem related to the water pumps. All the pumps cannot function if electric power is off. If the electric power is cut off there will be no water supply. 
Interviewer: Do the pumps work only by electricity? 
Respondent: Yes, if the electric power is cut off there will be no water supply.
Interviewer: What about other additional problems related to budget and human resource? Is there enough manpower capable of providing maintenance services in the rural and urban areas?
Respondent: Even though there is a problem, there is sufficient manpower. The main problem of is lack of pumps. There is imbalance in the number of pumps and the residents of the town. Whenever there is no electricity, the pumps stop functioning. 
Interviewer: There is also an issue of trained manpower, right?
Respondent: Often times we send technician from here. However, I don’t think there is huge problem in the woreda.
Interviewer: How so?
Respondent: You can water anytime the electric power is working. The problem is when there is a power blackout. 
Interviewer: Have not solar power sources installed yet?
Respondent: There are some of them in the rural. The problem is in the urban parts of the woreda.
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Let’s start with the first problem. What is the solution for the problem you raised regarding water reservoir?
Respondent: Budget has been allocated to put a sustainable solution to this issue. We will see whether this will really be implemented or not.
Interviewer: Has it been released yet?
Respondent:  Not yet. There has been nothing implemented yet. 
Interviewer: Have you followed this issue up?
Respondent:  We could not find time to discuss about it. Due to security issues, all responsible bodies are not here. 
Interviewer: If budget is released and water reservoir is built what problems will be solved?
Respondent:  The operation of water supply will be smoother.  
Interviewer: What about the problem of pumps? You have said that water supply interrupts when there is no electric power. 
Respondent:  We have discussed with the regional bureau and they will send us the generator. We are waiting for them. 
Interviewer: Did not the pumps have their own power generator? 
Respondent: It is not functional due to some technical problems. 
Interviewer: Was it that big?
Respondent: Of course, it was a very big one. 
Interviewer: So, the problem will be addressed if the generator is purchased. 

Respondent: Yes. The generator will come soon. 
Interviewer: What about the problem of technicians, especially in the rural. What should be done to address this issue?
Respondent: Technician in the urban areas should go to the rural areas and serve. 
Interviewer: But there is no enough man power even in the towns. 
Respondent: There are enough of them. 
Interviewer: Do you always send technicians from here to the rural areas?
Respondent: Yes, wherever there is a problem, we send maintenance technicians with a transportation facility. 
Interviewer: Do you access all the rural areas using this approach?
Respondent: No
Interviewer: So, why are others telling me there is a transportation, road and technician problems when I ask them about this issue?
Respondent: There may be problems in some kebeles. There are some kebeles that do not have road access. 
Interviewer: So, does it mean the transportation and road access are not issues?
Respondent: There is a vehicle of the water utility office. We also use vehicles from other sectors, including the administration. 
Interviewer: You have told me you are trying to address the issue of generator by replacing it by solar power sources. What steps have you taken yet?
Respondent: We are discussing with the regional water bureau.
Interviewer: When will it be resolved? 
Respondent:  It could be solved soon, but most of the members of the administration are busy by the current security issue. 
Interviewer: Are there many solar panels in the rural areas? 
Respondent: Yes, there are.
Interviewer: So, they are not many in the urban, right?

Respondent:  Yes. The solar panels are available only in the rural areas.
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned many problems and tried to forward solutions to the problems. What would be the solution that should be given priority? 
Respondent: The main thing is working in coordination. 
Interviewer: Who are the ones that have to work in coordination? 
Respondent: We should work with the regional water bureau. 
Interviewer: If there is coordinated effort is exerted, what issues would be addressed? 
Respondent: It will be solve issues related to water reservoir. 
Interviewer: How would coordination help solve the problem of water reservoir? 
Respondent: If budget is released, we will be able to build the reservoir. 
Interviewer: Why is the coordination so loose, and what should be done to solve that?
Respondent:  This is because most of the regional officials are engaged in addressing the current security issue. 
Interviewer: The security issue is recent problem. The lack of coordination among the stakeholders has been a problem even before that. What would be the solution for that?
Respondent: We have not yet discussed about it. 
Interviewer: Do not you have a common plan, even the stakeholders in the woreda?
Respondent: It is hard to discuss about this issue these days.
Interviewer: Do the stakeholders in the woreda meet and discuss?
Respondent: Yes, they do.
