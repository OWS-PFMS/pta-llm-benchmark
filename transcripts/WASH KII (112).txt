1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: The problems related to water and sanitation services in the town are too many but the major problems are: First, there is awareness problem on the long run effects of sanitation problems as well as the economic benefits of recyclable wastes. For instance, various institution release wastes in the town but they do not understand the consequence of improper waste disposal on the community. On the other hand, governmental stakeholders do not consider waste collection service as means of livelihood for micro enterprises. That is they do not organize unemployed youths to engage them in waste collection business. 
Currently, the town’s population is growing rapidly due the expansion of industries and investments in the town. That is large number of people migrating to the town from various rural area. This implies large amount of waste is produced by the community and the industries release wastes to the environment every day. However, there is no improvement in sanitation service that can accommodate the growing sanitation service demand. 
There is a gap in the governmental stakeholders side in addressing the sanitation problems of the town. For instance, in some areas there are cases in which water supply lines contaminated by wastes. Even we are not sure the water utility office properly apply the water purification and chlorination procedures. The community and I myself do not have confidence on the quality of water supplied in the town. 
Interviewer: Are there any other problems related to waste disposal and management including waste disposal place that affect sustainability of sanitation service in the town?
Respondent: Large amount of wastes produced by the community every day and this requires appropriate waste disposal and management system to effectively utilize those valuable wastes and properly dispose those non-recyclable ones. However, the town administration do not prepared appropriate waste disposal site both for liquid and solid wastes. Recently it identified a new waste disposal place outside the town but the site selection is made without conducting appropriate study on the possible effects of the wastes on the farmers residing in nearby areas. Consequently, farmers strongly opposing the development of the waste disposal site in the area.  
Pertinent to waste management, the community are very negligent. That is they do not sort those decomposable wastes from non-decomposable ones. However, if they sort those decomposable wastes like wastes of food staffs (banana, onion, potato, etc.) the can be used as a natural fertilizers by preparing compost. By doing this they can reduce the amount of waste that require waste disposal place. On the other hand, broken glasses, cans, plastic water bottles can be sorted and used as an industry inputs. In this regard, the government can organize youth that collect and sort wastes and deliver recyclable ones to agents of processing industries and properly dispose the others. 
Interviewer: Are there any other problems related to sustainability of water service in the town?
One thing that I observed related to water service sustainability of the town is that the town water utility office do not conduct regular sustainability check of the existing water wells. They do not conduct studies to understand the potential of the existing water facilities. The office mainly focus on daily routine activities like water bill collection, maintenance services and the like. However, as water is a natural resource, it obviously deplete through time. Thus to ensure sustainable water service in the town there should be continuous assessment of the growing demand and the potential of the existing water wells and develop new water facilities that can meet the community need. In addition, large manufacturing and service providing firms should be strongly advised to develop their own boreholes within their compounds to reduce the burden on the town’s water supply.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: you have raised different problems related to sanitation service of the town. What do you recommend to alleviate these problems and ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town?
Respondent: The problems of sanitation cannot be solved by the government effort alone rather it requires remarkable involvement of the community. For instance, in Debre Birehan town there are more than 100, 000 residents. Thus, if the government in coordination with other relevant actors mobilize at least 10% of the community that are willing to engage in sanitation and beatification activities of the town, we can minimize the sanitation problems of the town until we develop sustainable sanitation service system in the town.  
Moreover, we should create a formal structure or platform that can involve all relevant actors (e.g. the town health office, water utility office, town culture and tourism office, town sanitation and beautification department, etc.) and have coordinated planning and activities to ensure sustainable sanitation service. However, if every organization runs independently on its own, it might be wastage of resource as well as it does not enable to achieve the required goals. 
Interviewer: what do you suggest for the problems you raised related to sustainability of water service in the town?
Respondent: In my opinion to ensure sustainable water service in the town, first we have to know the demand and supply of the water service. We have to have a clear information about the water demand of the community, service providing institutions and industries. Moreover, we have to investigate the water supply potential of the existing water wells. This should be done by qualified experts and should be shared with relevant actors. This information can help us to develop a long-term water supply plan and engage in sustainable water service activities. Thus, instead of focusing only on routine activities relevant government agencies and other stakeholders should also focus on long-term plan and activities, as it is very basic to ensure sustainable water service in the town.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: In my opinion, the most important solution to ensure sustainable water and sanitation service in the town is that the government agencies and other relent stakeholders should have a clear and concrete evidence on the existing water demand and supply potential of the existing water system. If we have such information, we can develop long-term plans and can take evidence-based actions. Currently there is a dilemma that some believe the water wells have sufficient potential to provide the required service and others have a fear that we will face supply shortage in the short-run. Thus, to avoid this confusion there should be scientific demand and supply potential assessment study. Ensuring sustainable water service should be a priority are as it is a base to alleviate sanitation and hygiene problems of the community.
