1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town/woreda? 
Interviewer: First, what are the solid and liquid waste management problems?
Respondent: In Debrebirhan town the solid and liquid waste management system is poorly coordinated. There is huge gap regarding working in coordination. 
Interviewer: Explain it to me.
Respondent: Let’s say Debre Birhan town is a large city, and many investors are being attracted to it. The main causes for the challenges related to solid and liquid waste management are, first, we plan and allocate budget every now and then but there is a problem in implementing the plan. We hear budget is being allocated but nothing is on the ground. 
The second basic problem is when there is turnover among the managers; the issue of waste management becomes a new idea to the new-comers. As the management changes we should approach them and create awareness then after a while there will be another new management. Therefore, I consider that frequent reshuffling of management has become a big challenge. 
Aside from that, there is lack of implementing what has planned, particularly the main sectors that are directly responsible in the solid and liquid waste management.
Interviewer: What are the main sectors? 
Respondent: For instance, regarding water and sewage there is water utility and sewage office, likewise there is Debre Birhan town housing and construction office, and we, the town health office, town administration in general should be working together. It doesn’t mean that hygiene and sanitation is not their business other sectors of the town administration. But, these sectors should work together with due attention to the matter, while working individually as a sector. We are noticing lack of implementing such coordinated activities. So, the issues that I have mentioned are the causes of the problems that I told you. 
The other thing that I have not pointed out is that the budget allocated for the sector is inadequate. We could not finish ongoing projects, let alone building modern solid and liquid waste management systems. However, as this problem is not an issue that can be addressed by the town administration alone, regional stakeholders should be involved in it. This is our first problem. 
The second basic matter in our town is, as I have mentioned earlier, experience sharing sessions have been conducted not only the issue of hygiene and sanitation, but also making it attractive and clean. Especially, TETRATEC action has helped us have several experience sharing sessions. Such experience sharing has been done by other partners too. However, we have huge gap in implementing what we have observed during the experience sharing sessions in our town. For instance, we have the opportunity to see experiences from Hawasa city earlier through other partners and recently through TETRATEC. But when we try to synthesize and implement the best practices we saw, we fail. We lack coordination and take advantage of opportunities we have and implement them. This is the second problem in our town. The leadership does not take the responsibility and closely monitor the implementation of best practices gained from other places. This is our main problem. Apart from that, our town has plenty of options; for instance it has a university. It is a huge university. But, the university has not done enough in researching the public health problems of the city and supporting the city. Because if we do not make use of institutions nearby it will be difficult. Debrebirhan University conducts several researches and, it is expected to carry out surveys and researches to identify the problems in the town and portray them to the community. In reality, the university is not supporting us in so far as training the leadership of the town. There may be some initiations recently, but it has been one of our problems as not utilizing the opportunity we have is our gap. 
The other, forth, basic problem is nowadays our town has become an investment location and we have huge gap in utilizing the opportunities the investors bring to our town. We lack to consider them as a stakeholder and use them as an asset and share them some responsibilities.  
Beyond that, the main problem in my opinion is the lack of a modern waste management system. If we build modern waste management systems and use the opportunity we have, we could alleviate the problems mentioned. 
Interviewer: We will come to the solutions.
Respondent: Ok.
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have told me the problems in detail. I will tell you the problems one by one and you will tell me the solutions for the problems. 
Respondent: Very good.
Interviewer: First you have said the basic problem of solid and liquid waste management. As a professional what do you think would be the solution for this specific problem?
Respondent: Well, what I think that it would resolve the issue is that building a modern solid and liquid waste management system that has the capacity to accommodate the ever increasing size of the tow. The town is expanding, its population has been increasing, and investors from other localities are settling here. Who should build it? We will see it later. However, building a modern landfill, incinerator, and waste treatment plan is vital. Who should be participating I can say many things.
Interviewer: The second issue you pointed out is the lack of implementation. Plans have been devised but they are not being implemented and you have also mentioned issues related with leadership turnover.
Respondent: Yes, when I mention these issues I have a reason. Majority of the budget allocated for this matter is ratified through town municipality council or town housing and construction office. There should be strong and capable body that controls and monitors the implementation and utilization of the allocated budget. 
Interviewer: The other, I think, is lack of coordination among sectors
Respondent: You see there are several sectors in our town that work with us. As I have told you earlier, we work well together with water sector, urban development and construction service, trade, education, finance, women and children offices; the health sectors touches many other sectors. So, what I think is that we should start what we should do. There should be a responsible body that coordinates these activities with specific schedule, whether it is monthly, quarterly, or biannually. If there is a continuous report, the progress is monitored, problems are identified, solutions are proposed, and if the solutions are implemented, we could tackle the problems. For instance, we have 15 kebeles in total in our town. If all sectors support their respective kebeles structures and train them on selected and impactful issues, and carry out monitoring activities timely, we can make a difference. Otherwise if we work separately, we will not go far. So, the solution for this is a coordinated work guided by a timetable and plan. And by doing so, we can improve the intersectorial collaboration.  
Interviewer: The other thing you mentioned is budget related. As the budget that is allocated has been insufficient, it has been impossible to run the services. So, what is the solution?
Respondent: As I have told you earlier, it is impossible to build all the necessary things with the capacity of the city administration. For instance, there was a moment when we agreed to build a sanitary landfill and a proposal was developed, which required millions of birr.  Such kind of expense cannot be covered by the city administration alone. But, the administration can start and the partners that I mentioned you should take part. For example, there are many investments in our town. We can utilize these partners. Alongside this, the city administration should allocate budget on purpose and by plan. The administration should consider the issue as a fundamental problem and it should be able to address these challenges.  
Interviewer: The other thing is related to experience sharing. You have had the opportunity to see best practices from different towns and cities but you are having hard time adopting them to your town. What should be done to tackle this problem?
Respondent: We have been to many cities and town to observe best practices, including Hawasa. As a technical coordinator, we tried to put together what we have observed and prepare a plan, but due to COVID-19 and other emergency issues we could not be able to put it to practice. We have also presented our findings in the learning alliance what we had observed in our stay in Hawasa. However, we become less and less motivated later.
Interviewer: Why? What should be done to solve that?
Respondent: A plan should be devised. Once the plan is ready, the town administration should be able to identify what should be implemented and who should take the responsibility for each task. This has not been done yet. If the town administration had been snowed under with emergency issues, it should have delegated others and allocate some budget for its implementation. We should put them to practice what we have observed as soon as possible. To do so, the town leadership should give due political emphasis to the matter and work in coordination with technical professional and managers. And I believe we have the capacity to tackle the issue accordingly. This requires sensitization of the leadership toward this issue. I think this would be the solution. 
Interviewer: The other thing is, you have said that the opportunities in investors and Debrebirhan University have not been fully utilized. What would be the solution to this issue? Especially the town administration has not exploited the potential in the private investors. What would you think is the solution for this issue?
Respondent: This is a critical issue and we should work on. What I think is, as I have told you earlier, I am a hygiene and sanitation officer and my profession is environmental health; I would be happy to work on this issue more. The solution for this is all stakeholders should meet together and discuss about it. They should play their share in the matter. First off, there should be preliminary research for discussion. Presentations should be prepared to these stakeholders based on the research. Once the extent of the problem is made clear, we should ask them what they can do in the process. We can then, collect what they say and turn their idea into action plan. There are many factories in our town it has a positive aspect to us. But they have very poor solid and liquid waste management practices. This will have a detrimental effect on the workers and the community at large. 
We had built a sewerage system before but it couldn’t go beyond one rainy season. So, we should build a system with a capacity to accommodate the demand in the town by utilizing the potential in the private sector too. If we are not able to involve the participation of the investors and partners, the local government will not able to solve the issue by itself. Some of them might not able to support financially, but they can help us through their technical supports. We should be able to organize a forum for the partners and investors separately to engage all stakeholders in this issue.
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned problems and respective solutions to the problems. Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Which one do you think if the solution occurs, other problems would be solved?
Respondent: Building modern solid and liquid waste management system is the most crucial solution. If we do this we can implement health extension package in our town. 
Interviewer: How?
Respondent: Great. For instance, if the solid and liquid waste management system is modern, if it is supported financially, we can timely collect solid and liquid waste from the community. This will increase the awareness of the community to collect and dispose wastes properly. 
Secondly, if liquid wastes are collected timely, the town’s revenue will increase. For instance, water utility office will have vacuum track that enable it to self-sustain if there is a modern liquid waste disposal site for the trucks. If we build the system we will be able to generate income from the service we give to the community. 
Besides, people are suffering from lack of public latrines. If communal latrines are built open defecation will not be an issue. The community will not be affected by many communicable diseases. 
Regarding the solid waste, solid waste is disposed everywhere in the town. Currently, the waste is being dumped in locations close to the town and the wind is bringing it back to the residential areas. However, if the waste is properly disposed in modern landfills, this problem will be resolved. 
On top of that, we should utilize the potential in the stakeholder that I have mentioned earlier. We should discuss with them and invite them to take part in the solution. If we show them the negative aspect of poor solid and liquid waste management and its detrimental health effects on the community, they will contribute to the solution and we will be able to create healthy community. We will be able to reduce the medical expenses that are incurred due to the transmittable diseases resulted because of poor solid and liquid waste management. If we address this we would be able to tackle other issues.
