1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?   
Respondent: We do have lots of problems as woliso city regarding increasing amount of liquid wastes. Because, we even don’t have damping site for liquid and solid waste. This problem makes communal latrine to remain dirty.
The second thing is financial problem. We don’t have enough budget to pay compensations to the people around the damping site. This problem is affecting especially hotels to damp liquid wastes. 
The last thing that should be seen is researches should be done in this city to study about sanitation issues on the street and everywhere.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Respondent: other than the discussions, this problem needs decision. Woliso city administration should decide and start working on this issue although compensation fee and fence expenses have already made.
There is allocated budget, we should search for funds if the budget is not enough and should stand resolutely.
There is an organization which works on sanitation issue. This organization can collect funds from different organizations. For example, there are factories like soap, macaroni and flower producers. They are not willing to support us financially.
The solution for the latrine being full and damping site problem is to make additional latrine until these become sucked and empty.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The temporary solution is to use the budget and sewage vacuum trucker of the city to avoid the waste to other nearby cities like wolkite and tulubolo since they have damping sites.
