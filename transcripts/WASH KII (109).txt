1) In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Water and Sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
1.1There is shortage of water supply in the town. The supply and demand of water in the town is not at equilibrium level given increasing population number. Again, there is no dumping site in the outskirts of the town. The people in the outskirts of the town don't want waste to be dumped in their area since there is no mechanism of treating the waste to avoid poison and bad smell. Especially with regard to liquid waste disposal, there is a serious problem. The sewage coming from the residential houses and hotels is not properly carried out to the drainage. The open ditches are full of sewage and there is   bad smell, and everywhere it stinks.

1.2The installation of the pipelines in the town is not done based on the city's master plan. You will find pipelines openly exposed to road sides, along the sides of institute's fences and walking paths. That is why they get easily damaged.

1.3Debreberhan town was established long back, so the infrastructural design was not prepared considering the future's developmental activities. If you see the other partner service giving sectors like electricity and telephone, the installation of the lines is not also convenient to the other developmental structures. Lots of damages happen to the lines while construction is going on. 

1.4There is no trend of constructing residential houses in accordance with proper drainage system that lets the sewage get connected to the main drainage line. Most of the residents dispose liquid waste to open ditches; there is also a traditional custom of waiting for winter season having the thought that the dirt will get washed away by flood. 

1.5There is also awareness gap in the society living in the city and in rural areas 
2. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

2.1To solve the water shortage problem, additional water schemes need to be built in order to meet the required demand. There is an ongoing water scheme project in which 80% of the work is completed. There are also other water scheme projects that are studied and proposed to be done in the future. Upon the availability of budget, I hope these projects will be implemented gradually.  That is necessary since the population size and its demand is increasing. The project, whose completion is so far 80% , has so far taken one year and four months. It  is being financed by the office itself except the15 million birr that was given as a loan from the regional office. It is planned to cover the loan from the income it is earning from water bill.  Due to this, the office has financial constraint to do the project and finish on time.

2.2With regard to sanitation, Urban Development office is doing a new solid waste dumping site somewhere out of town. Once they finish the work, they will give us to use it. The location is already identified, and it is being dug with a 3-meter depth. I think they are doing in collaboration with Dashen and Habesha Breweries. Even though compensation is already paid to the farmers for the land that was taken, the office is supposed  to do more public relations work since some people still don't want the site to be used for this purpose. I am not sure where the solid waste is dumped since we do only liquid waste disposal. 

Interviewer: With regard to the existing ditches and drainage maintenance, what have been done by your office so far? What have done to upgrade the people's awareness on sanitation? What do you suggest as a solution for the problems? 
2.3It is only when the water pipelines are damaged or inflicted by drainage that we go out for maintenance service. We don't do prevention work ahead of the occurrence of a problem. Health office, municipality and our office need to do collaboratively. But in practice, there is a gap in Debrebirhan town. By the way, we do maintenance work at night, so it needs collaboration with others for security, too.
Interviewer: Is there anything that you as campaign with the public like community work on sanitation? 
Community work is done by kebele   and sub-,kebele levels. We don't do such kind of activity. The new dumping site is being prepared faster and I hope it will start functioning soon. But there should be other dumping sites like in Dashen Brewery area and some other places. We have good work relations with municipality office and the federal environmental protection office, but we are supposed to this type of relations with Health office. So, we need to maintain good relations with them. 
2.4Regarding awareness creation work, it would be good if a scheduled and timely work plan is launched with the community. We need to start this now.

Interviewer: Do you have board of WASH program here in your town? Regional offices have board of directors that work with Water and Sanitation office? 

No, we don't have WASH board of committee like that here. But World Bank   has got a project that works with a committee. This committee is comprised of the steering Committee and technical committee. The stirring committee, being led by the mayor, contains Urban Administration, Women, Children and Youth office, Education Bureau, Water Development office, Land Administration and Use office and Finance office. The technical committee is again comprised of experts from Municipality, Water Development office, Women and Children office and Land Administration and Use office. The technical committee is being led by a project management Unit here in Water and Sewerage office.

Interviewer: How effective do you think is the work of this committee?

That is the main issue here. Most projects are studied by regional office as a package and the study results are delivered here only for implementation. For instance, there is a demand of having a treatment plant here in Debreberhan, but we still don't have. Debreberhan, Dese and Gonder cities are put in place as one package. So, the project management units investigate the feasibility of the project there in the region. They don't deliver it to us, so we don't get the opportunity to have a look at it as a technical team. For instance, World Bank has recently conducted a study in Debreberhan and proposed the constructions of eight communal public latrines. The good thing is that they gave the proposal to us for comments. We evaluated as a technical team and gave our comments to them. They also incorporated our comments and finalized the proposal and made it ready for implementation. 
Interviewer: So, you are suggesting if the mandate of project proposal preparation is given to the zone or Urban Administration in order to o have effective project implementation? 
Yes, that is right. We have also been presenting this comment as a grievance to the regional office. I don't think the existing system of handling project is helpful.
3. Of the solutions you listed, which one is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

The most crucial issue we need to have is financial source, commitment and integration. Once we have these as inputs, what we need next is enforcement and practical implementation.
The second most important matter is awareness problem. So we need to do much on awareness raising program so that the public will improve the trend of waste disposal system. If this is done, the side effects that have been occurring on the ground water will get reduced. Doing more work on sanitation and hygiene awareness in the public is so crucial. Even if we plan to do such activities, what we lack is practical work and commitment. So we need to have determination on that. 

We need to do faster on the dumping scheme to finish the remaining 20% work too. 

It would be nice if the university finishes its underground water construction scheme. I think they lack someone to manage the work. Out of the three Wells they have planned to construct, they have finished one so far. If they finish the overall work, it will bring temporary but positive impact on the current shortage of water in the town.

I wish the Sewerage treatment plant is also completed soon.

Interviewer: You said that you need to have coordination so as to bring change and see the proposed projects come true. Now World Bank has granted the budget for the study though you need the finance for the implementation? Don't you think some of these projects can be done just by collaborative work of municipality, WASH committee, various stakeholders and your own resources? What is the problem behind this? 

We did the previous water scheme that cost about 42 millions birr, we didn't have sufficient financial capacity to do so except taking loan. The loan was given to us since we agreed to repay it from the financial source that we gain from selling water, giving operational services and so on. The city administration gives us only land which we can do sanitation and water service scheme a by ourselves. We get no other support. If you see electricity and telephone infrastructures, they are done by federal government, but we do sanitation and water service with our own budget which is limited. So, even if there is coordination problem among health office, municipality and our office, the most important challenge is lack of financial support.
