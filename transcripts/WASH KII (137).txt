1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
The first problem related to water service is that some water schemes become damaged before their intended lifespan. Second, decline of surface water in the area. That is the ground water potential of the area is gradually declining. There is also leakage on most of the water lines. That is there is wastage of water during supply of water to the community. What other problems are there related to water service in the woreda? The budget allocated to the woreda water office is also very limited. The budget allocated to our office could not enable us to engage in expansion of new water schemes and rehabilitation of the existing non-functional water schemes. A small activity in the water sector require huge budget, however, the government is not allocating sufficient budget for the sector. As the woreda water office is established very recently, it does not get attention from the government bodies.
Sufficient budget should be allocated to the water sector because if there is no water, there could not be education, health and any other activities. Without water we can’t ensure WASH service in schools, health centers and in the entire community. Therefore, budget is the main challenge to ensure sustainable WASH service in the woreda.

In addition to the budget problem, we have a serious problem of logistics. The woreda water office do not have its own vehicle to provide service in different parts of the woreda. In order to transport spare parts to rural areas we usually request a logistic support from other sector offices. But the response of the sector office is much delayed because of this we could not repair water schemes in appropriate time period. 
We have motor-cycle but it can only serve to conduct assessments of water schemes in different kebeles. The motor-cycle cannot carry materials for maintenance of water schemes. Therefore, the water office as one of the sector offices should have its own logistic facility.   
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
To prevent damage of water schemes, we should have regular monitoring of water schemes in rural areas and take appropriate measures before the schemes become seriously damaged. In case the problems are irreversible (e.g. slipping of the walls of boreholes), we should immediately construct standby boreholes.
10:08
Related to problems of water lines, there should be massive rehabilitate of older water lines. 
Particularly the water supply capacity of the town’s borehole is significantly declined. Thus, by conducting hydrogeology study we should construct new borehole in area that has good ground water potential. 
Since water is life and a backbone of any sector, sufficient budget should be allocated for the water office that could enable the office to fully implement its plan. Moreover, NGOs should cooperate with us in implementing our plan particularly in the construction of new water schemes.
Regarding logistics (Vehicle), for instance the government provided vehicle to other sector offices. However, water is the primary issue for the community because if we fail to provide maintenance service due to logistic problem the community will be forced to use river water. This may predispose the community for various diseases. The consequence of water problem is very immense. Thus in allocating resources, priority should be given to water sector. Therefore, in order to achieve the goals of the water sector, the woreda water office should be provided appropriate vehicle that will be used for maintenance and rehabilitation of water schemes.
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
Of the solutions I mentioned, I give priority for the problem related to logistics. If the woreda water office has its own vehicle, it can freely move in the woreda to repair and rehabilitate non-functional water schemes. So in my opinion availability of logistics is an important solution to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda.
In the second place, sufficient budget should be allocated to construct new water schemes to replace totally damaged water schemes. It would also be great if both NGOs and the government provide us financial support that can enable us to fully implement our plan.
Third, we should construct rural water schemes in areas where there is no water supply at all.  
Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? In order to repair rural water schemes currently we are required to wait for about 2 weeks to get logistic support from other sector offices. Because they give priority for their own plan. They only provide us logistic support when they have no schedule. But if we have our own vehicle, we can regularly monitor the water schemes and provide maintenance service immediately up on request. As a result the community will have sustainable water service. This in turn will reduce occurrence of water born diseases.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service?  
In general the existing coordination between stakeholders is in a good state. We are working with various non-governmental organizations in the water sector. For example, we have good coordination with CARE Ethiopia and AMREF. These two organization coordinate with us in the development of new sustainable rural water schemes. They also work with us in the development of water schemes in areas where there is no water supply at all. What about your coordination with governmental organizations functioning in the woreda? We have also good coordination with governmental stakeholders operating in the woreda. For example, we get logistic support from different sector office even if it is not timely.
What could be improved? The woreda sector offices should respond quickly to our logistic support request. This is because water problem do not give time. They should give priority for problems related to water service. To this end, we should enhance the awareness of the sector offices about the problems of water service so that they will be more cooperative in any emergency cases.  

5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
If there is no coordination there will not be sustainable water service. If there is no sustainable water service, women cannot attend school for two reasons. First, they spent much time to fetch water from other places. Second, during menstruation period they can’t keep their hygiene due to lack of water. Because of this they stay at home until the end of their menstruation period. But if there is coordination between stakeholders the community as well as schools can get good WASH service as a result women do not drop out from school.
Health and water service are directly related so the coordination between health office and water office can also bring sustainable change in the water sector.
