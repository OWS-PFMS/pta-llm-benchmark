1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: what are the main problems of sanitation service that affect the sustainability?
Respondent: One of the main problems that have been affecting the sanitation service sustainability of our town is related to budget shortage. Building modern waste management system, liquid waste sewerage in particular, requires huge amount of budget. This is the first problem. Second it topography. 
Interviewer: Explain what you mean by budget more first. Whose problem is the budget issue? It that the community’s, or the town administration’s?
Respondent: The problem related to budget is not the community’s problem; it is the government’s problem. The problem is a lack of focus on this area and allocating the allowed budget to other infrastructures. 
Interviewer: Is it not allocating enough budget?
Respondent: Yes. It is not allotting adequate budget to this area. 
Besides, the landscape of the locality necessitates huge budget to build sewer management infrastructures. It is not possible to function the sewerage system by building one side of the sewer line. This is because of the difficult topography of the town. as a result, it requires large amount of money to construct the system. 
Thirdly, Debrebirhan town is a somewhat cold town, so there is no possibility of reducing liquid waste through evaporation. Hence, the option we have is percolation, but, the current sites are not capable of percolating it quickly. The current locations rather contain it there and the waste overflows instead of percolating. Therefore, appropriate site selection will be one of the sustainable solutions for the problem. 
 Another problem is the leadership of the town administration does not consider sanitation as an important component in the growth of the town. The administration’s main focus is on emergency issues rather than planning and executing long term goals. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have mentioned four basic problems. The first one is related with budget. What do you think should be done to address this issue? And explain it to me if there is anything that has been doing regarding it.
Respondent: It is essential to consider the issue of sanitation as a key area and allocate sufficient budget. Secondly, it is vital to enable stakeholders engage in the issue and encourage them to play their roles by identifying all potential stakeholders. Debrebirhan is an industrial town. There are many industrial establishments in the town which have their own effects. Thus, they are expected to take part in the effort to mitigate the negative effect they pose on the town. The town administration should organize the efforts and encourage them to make material, financial contribution, and capacity building. If we work this way we will be able to resolve the issue. 
Interviewer: Who are the stakeholders?
Respondent: For instance, there are brewery plants, liqueur factories, tanneries and so on. There are many several industries in the town. All of these industries affect the town one way or the other. Therefore, they should participate in such activities that benefit the environment to reduce their effects. 
Interviewer: You have said that the topography of the town is not suitable. What do you think is the solution for this?
Respondent: If the treatment plan is built, vacuum trucks can be employed to collect and dump the waste in certain places of the town. This can be done by increasing the number of vacuum trucks and involving all private and public trucks. 
Besides, it is possible to build low cost treatment plants in two or three locations. 
Interviewer: Low cost treatment plant?
Respondent: Yes. As it may be difficult to collect and dump it on one site, we can build cost effective treatment plants in two or three places.  
Interviewer: Is the treatment plant site selection problem related to that or it is different?
Respondent: The treatment plants that have been built up until now are the underneath soil type is unknown. Thus, it should be based on a research and the soil should be soft or they should be in locations that were quarry sites. Besides, environmental impact assessment should be conducted prior to their construction. The site should also be wide enough i.e. more than five or six hectare.
Aside from that, industrial wastes should be treated separately from domestic wastes. A separate treatment plant should be built for industrial liquid wastes. 
Interviewer: You have also said that the leadership lacks working on long term goals which is a true problem. What should be the solution for this?
Respondent: We have been working with TETRATECH on awareness creation among the town leaders. We have been teaching the impact of the attitude of the leadership on the residents, industries, and the town based on research findings. Other stakeholders also participate in this. By explaining the problem using researches, for instance, around two million m3 waste water is generated in this town; however, majority of it is not being treated. This untreated waste water has been affecting the health of the residents of the town. Therefore, if the administration is well aware of this, it would be able to allocate adequate budget. 
Interviewer: Have not yet created the awareness needed? What is the real problem?
Respondent: The residents demand a road to be constructed. Mainly they focus on urgent priorities and routines.  
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Out of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? If the solution is implemented, what would happen?
Respondent: If awareness is created and there is coordination, it is possible to solve the location, budget, and other problems. Therefore, first is identification of main stakeholders who can take part in this issue. Then, raising awareness of all stakeholders is vital. If we do so, we have the investors, land and other inputs, and we can address the problem. 
Interviewer: So, in your opinion, the major solution is to work with stakeholders. 
Respondent: Yes.
Interviewer: What additional benefits working with stakeholders would bring? If all stakeholders participate in the issue, what changes would occur?
Respondent: If there is a coordinated effort and knowledge, it would be possible to build better disposal site for the solid and liquid wastes. If we create a clean environment, the town would have a positive image and it would be important for the growth of our town.
