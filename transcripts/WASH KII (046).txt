
Interviewer:  My first question to you is, “In your opinion, what aspect of this network is working well in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: Through consulting and coordination.

Interviewer:  My second question to is, “What do you think are the main problems in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: Capacity building, coordination and lack of knowledge.

Interviewer:  On whose part?

Interviewee: Lack of knowledge from the water management committee and the community.

Interviewer:  My third question to is, “What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewee: We need resources at the sub-county level, capacity building, training in short courses and long courses for the staff, senior supporting staff and juniors.

Interviewer:  Of the solutions you have mentioned, which do you consider to be the most important? “What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewee: Resources.  Availability of resources at sub-county. For the learning of the county… operations and maintenance.

Interviewer:  If the solution happens, what would occur next?

Interviewee: My work would be easier, and at least I will achieve my goals… objectives.
