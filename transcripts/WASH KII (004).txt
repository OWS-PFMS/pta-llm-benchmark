1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation/water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
1.1 Shortange of water supply 
From our community’s perspective, it is clear that the number of population has been increasing but not parallel to the the existing water supply. For this reason, the twon is not supplying the water continuously but dewellers and services are receiving the supply in a periodic shift. In addition, there is sanitation problem which I think is as the result of the population growth. 
1.2 Shortage of budget is another main challenge as a result of which no exceptionally reserved place is available to dispose both liquid and solid wasts. Every household tries to collect the waste but due to absence of dest pin/regular waste collectors, they are forced to left the weste on the streets.  In this kebele, 02, there are about 37 000 dewellers but there is no even a single vehicle used to transport liquid and solid waste out of the kebele. There is one truck for the whole community in town, it is impossible to get it easily, may be per week or sometimes after two weeks. These is really aggravating the existing sanitation problems in the community.  
1.3 Indeed population growth is becoming a highranking challenge for this town. You can imagine how much the problem of sanitation could be given alarmingly increasing population is exisiting with out communal wast disposal place. 
1.4 Lack of awareness on sanitation and hygine: All that said are the same and true for me as well (the health extention worker replied), the residents have been facing such a very critical challenge regarding sanitation and hygine. Some of them have already built their house with only 180 m2 including all of its given space but haven’t prepared toilet hoping the fact that there could possibly be a public toilet in the nearby community. We have witnessed such issues repeatdly but nothing has been done so far to improve the situation. 
Response from government officials is relecting that there is shortage of land to build public toilet. In addition, there is also very limited number of human resourecs in  health center, only four or five trained health care service providers are available for 37000 community members. 
It is obvious that personal as well as community levele hygine and sanitation can be improved through education and continuous awareness creation but it is impossible to address this population through the existing trained health care service providers or extenstion workers. We have been trying our level best but still difficult to cover all round services. Here the problem is not only about addressing this population but also the way we teach our community and the real problem on the ground are becoming incompatible. They know the causes and consquences of unprotected environment but you can observe that no one is applying that. 
1.4 The other problem is open defication/absence of public toilet: Most people use OD, they don’t use toilet or latrine instead. This has high negative effect on the low level of personal as well as community levele sanitation problem. Some indivduals practice open defication every where, it may be around the rivers and/or springs and on the otherhnad, people may use the same water for drinking as well as other household purposes. This will extensilvely affect their hygine level and resulting infection by communicable disease. If you trace back the reason why they use OD, the most probable aswer would be because there is no public toilet. 
1.5 Absence of land fill site is also another problem. People usually collect their waste with plastic bags but they leave it anywahere since there is no place to dispose the waste.  The only thing they can do is collecting household wastes from their own home and disposing it everywhere in an open space be it street or road. It costs the community a lot actually. These were the main problems being raised repeatedly in many of our meetings with the community members. 
The other contributing factor for the problem of sanitation is shortage of water, there is limited supply of water. We get it through unpredictable schedule/shift. It should be expected to have water tanker in every toiet but very reare to exisit. 


2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
2.1Intially we need the finance itself to properly plan and implement projects especially regarding increment of water supply. For this, the government, the community and other stakeholders hould work cooperatively. 
2.2The other solution could be teaching the community about personal hygine contineously, on how to take care of themselves, and keep environmental sanitation through contineous education and awarness creation programs. However, all these activites will  be effiective if and only if it is properly supported by adequate budget/finance. As you know, the economic status of the community is very low, and have limited capacity even to keep their personal hygine. Migeration from rural to urban  areas has been alarmingly increased and I think that is beyond our capacity, but what we can do is that we can find ways out to improve the economic and awareness level of the community so that they can adopt it by themselves.  
2.3Improving the supply of water. In order to bring this to real circumstances, the government has been trying to construct 8 deep under ground water projects. The projects are planned to serve not only for the twon but for the rural community as well although the budget is still becoming a bottleneck and not sufficient unless the community and other stakeholders participate on it largely. 
2.4Improving water utilization strategy can also be a solution. This can be done again through continuous awareness creation programs and demonstrations. 
2.5Buying more vehicles to transport solid waste to an open land 
2.6Preparing land fill sites, I think it is not a big deal but officials do not give attention for this issue. They say, there is budget limitation for compensation to be paid for the farmers to access open land but what ever the cases may be, it is still very important to resolve sanitation problems in the town.  We have discussed over it thoughly with the town municipality services and we hope this will come in to effect the coming year, 2020. 

2.7WASH takeholders coordination is needed as well to promote hygine and sanitation and its sustainability. As we know, health sector needs the contribution of many stakeholders from different sectors as well as NGOs. Unfrotunately, in our community health issues are exclusively considered the sole responsibility of health care service professionals. In order to address this knowledge gap, every community member has to take their own responsibility to keep their environment clean and healthy and there comes a need to aware the community about this concern. 


3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
I think preparing land fill site and buying at least two more trucks to transport wates could be the first priority. At this time, the town is disposing its waste using only one truck but if two more are added, then three trucks will be adequate enough  to transposrt wates easily and frequently. If these problems are solved then people will start collecting wates and make ready for the trucks on a specific space provided. This will in turn improve the health conditions gradually. Improving water supply would come after preparing landfill sites and adding the number of trucks in the town.
