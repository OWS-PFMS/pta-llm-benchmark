Respondent Position: Sustainable Livelihood Improvement Program Coordinator
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: One of the problem related to WASH service sustainability is access to water. There is no sufficient water resource in the region. The second problem is improper management of the available limited water facilities. That is no regular monitoring and maintenance service for the existing water facilities. Moreover, there are no active WASH committees in rural areas of the region. Third, some water facilities do not provide service due to minor cases such as problems related to gas oil generators and batteries.  Pertinent to this it would be good if we can on solar energies instead of gas oil generators. We should also equip WASHcos with basic hand tools that can be used for minor maintenance of water schemes in their localities. 
Interviewer: The problems you raised are related to water service. Would you also please mention the problems related to sanitation service (if any)?
Respondent: Relatively the problems related to sanitation are not serious. The sanitation problems are mainly due to water shortage. As the society is a Muslim community, they do not have as such observable sanitation problems. May be there are few instances open defecation in some areas. In general, there is no serious sanitation problem in the region. 
Interviewer: So the main problem related to sanitation is open defecation. Now let us move to the solution to the problems you mentioned.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What ideas or recommendations do you have about the problem the problems related to access to water (this may be due to geography or the content of water itself that is high level of fluoride in the water)?  
Respondent: In my opinion to alleviate the problems related to access to water, we have to undertake rigorous studies prior to engaging in the construction of water facilities. There are many nonfunctional water schemes that are constructed without appropriate studies. Even if the area is arid and has limited ground water resource, we have to undertake proper study to identify areas that have relatively better water resources. By doing this we can alleviate the problem related to access to water to some extent.  
Interviewer: What do you suggest to solve the problem related to improper management and utilization of the existing water facilities? 
Respondent: Pertinent to the problem related to mishandling of water facilities, we have to enhance the awareness of the community on proper use and management of the water schemes and we should create sense of belongingness within the community. In addition, we should strengthen water committees.
Interviewer: can you please elaborate a bit on the roles of water committees and what we should do on them?
Respondent: There are water committees that are responsible to monitor and manage the unitization of water schemes in rural areas. These committees composed of seven members including the chairperson and secretary. Water committees are also responsible for minor maintenance of water schemes. If we strengthen these committees through training and materials, we can ensure sustainability of water facilities. Mostly water schemes become nonfunctional due to minor problems. Therefore, supporting these groups minimize the problems related to management and utilization of the existing water schemes.
The other problem related to water utilization is over exploitation of water schemes. That is some water schemes are shallow well but the community over utilize the schemes beyond their natural water replacement capacity. Thus, we have to introduce balanced utilization system.
The other thing is that some water schemes do not provide service due to lack of gas oil to run generators. To this end, I would like to suggest that we should rely on solar energy instead of gas oil generators to pump the water. It is good to have a standby generator but we have to mainly depend on solar energies as it has very low running cost. Moreover, it do not create environmental pollution.
Interviewer: Related to sanitation and hygiene you mentioned that there are practices of open defecation. What ideas or recommendation do you have about solutions to this problem? 
Respondent: This is obviously the question of awareness. We should aware the community to dig latrines and to use them properly. Thus, we have to undertake awareness enhancement campaigns in this regard. Awareness creation activities should be incorporated within the government and other partners’ sanitation programs. By doing this we can minimize the problems related to sanitation.  
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: My last question is that of the solution you listed, which is the most important and the first priority area? If this solution is implemented, what changes will happen in the areas?
Respondent: In order to ensure sustainable water service the most important solution is undertaking comprehensive study before engaging in new water schemes development. We have to exhaustively identify areas that have water resource potential. In addition, the study should properly address the social and environmental issues. There should also be strong community participation both during the study and implementation period.
On the other hand, we have to engage the community on the management of the existing water facilities to ensure sustainability of the service.   
Interviewer: Can you remind me what your first priority areas was? 
Respondent: The first and most important solution to ensure sustainable water service is to conduct proper study before engaging in development of new water schemes. We should also participate the community in the study to grasp local knowledge. Moreover, the study should also capture social and environmental issues.  
Interviewer: So if this is practically implemented, what changes would happen in the future?
Respondent: When we say sustainability, a give water facility can provide sufficient and quality water for long period. Thus, if we conduct proper study by participating the community including the water committees, we can ensure sustainable water service in the area.
