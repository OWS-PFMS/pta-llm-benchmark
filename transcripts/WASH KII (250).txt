1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: in the mean time liquid sanitary disposal problem is the main concern in our community. I want to say thank you to learning alliance for working with us to solve sanitation problem for a long time. It has been given capacity building through it. Even though in our city, the main problem is lack of damping cite. We have been discussing about this with the municipality and city administration offices to solve this since it is our main concern. We have been raising the issue that the City administration office should try to discuss about it with different stakeholders; woliso woreda, zonal office and neighbor woredas to solve this problem.
The second problem is there is a gap with kebele representatives and city administration to focus and be organized in solving this problem. 
We have a gap on establishing committees, on reporting things have been done to the respected ones on time.
There is an integration and coordination gap among the 4 kebele leadership. There is also a problem in reporting continuously and using the proper reporting format to learning alliance from the city green area side.
From the community side, there is a gap on taking care of communal latrines; on keeping it clean and addressing maintenance issues. There is also a gap on coordination and being responsible in the community too. There is a gap on coordination of kebele representatives with learning alliance returns.
2.  What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Respondent: personally, I recommend the city administration to focus on it and discuss with the concerned bodies to get a proper damping site solution this year, though the process to acquire the site has now been interrupted after they start paying compensation to the land owners.
I have an information that budget is allocated this year to buy one sewage suction vehicle. And I hope every sanitation concern will be solved this year.
For the concern and coordination problem, the solution could be;
1.Let the city administration to solve it by itself. Of no,
2.City administration, kebele representatives, learning alliance committee and municipality should unite and strive to solve this. Because it is not only a personal duty. It concerns health bureau, city administration sanitation office, the community and UAIDP should work together. 
3.Reports of what has been done, trainings we took and every activity  should be reported to a respective bodies on time.
4.We should do our responsibility on applying practically to the community about all the trainings we took.
Interviewer: what do you think should be done to the community in order to improve their level of responsibility and accountability regarding the maintenance issues?
Respondent: We should take the responsibility to give full orientation to the community. The kebele chair persons, those who are assigned on the city beautification position and kebele health experts should be responsible to make sure users keep the latrine clean. In addition to this, learning alliance, city administration and all stakeholders should be responsible if maintenance is needed. I myself and the assigned committee are also responsible to check and send reports in each phase.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: the first thing is to find a damping site. The changes which follow this solution are:- 
a.The community will be aware of handling wastes properly. 
b.City administration will allocate budget to buy trash collection tools near by the community and buy garbage truck to collect. This will help jobless youth to find job opportunities in collecting, separating solid and liquid wastes and damping the trash from waste collection sites to that vehicle.
