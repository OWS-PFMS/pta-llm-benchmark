
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Absence of coordination between WASH stakeholders 
There is no coordinated planning and activities between relevant actors/stakeholders to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. However, some stakeholders only coordinate to shut down those service-providing institutions (hotel and restaurants) that violate sanitary regulations. 
Another problem related to sanitation is that majority of the hotels act against sanitary regulation. For instance, they directly connect their liquid wastes to drainage lines and diches. 
II.Absence of standard waste disposal site
Though the existence of standard waste disposal site is vital to ensure stainable sanitation service, there is no appropriate waste disposal site in Debre Birehan town. There has been a waste disposal place at the periphery of the town that served for decades but currently it is out of service due to accumulation of wastes and associated pollution in the area. I think it is high time to develop appropriate waste disposal site and improve the waste management system of the town (i.e. sorting and properly disposing liquid and solid wastes). 
To provide temporary solution for the problem, the town administration arranged a new waste disposal place outside the town. However, it is very far from the town and the road is not convenient to transport wastes to the area as a result waste collection truck owners complain due to high cost of fuel. The community residing around the temporary waste disposal site also complaining due to environmental pollution. The town is rapidly growing and new manufacturing industries are emerging thus, there should be a standard waste disposal site and system that can accommodate the growing sanitation service need.  

III.Poor community awareness on waste disposal practices
There is noticeable awareness gap among the community on waste disposal and management. Thus, relevant actors should enhance the awareness of the community at least on how to sort wastes (recyclable from non-recyclable ones) and dispose within their locality without affecting the community. Currently, wastes are disposed without sorting and separating relevant ones for recycling purpose. Hence, there should be close collaboration between stakeholders to create community awareness on waste management and disposal. 
IV.There is a fear that water supply shortage will happen in the near future
In my opinion, currently there is no observed water supply and demand imbalance in Debre Birehan town as the area is naturally gifted with water resource. Indeed there are some complains on water supply in kebele 08 though it is not that much serious.  There are also some additional boreholes with water distribution lines to improve the existing water service of the town. However, there is rapid population growth and emergence of new industries and service providing institutions in the town this may affect the sustainability of the existing water service. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
To alleviate the problems I mentioned earlier, I would like to suggest the following: 
There should be a coordinated planning and activity among the learning alliance members. Individual efforts should be converted to collaborative efforts or action. There should be common agenda to ensure sustainable sanitation and water service in the town.
A standard waste disposal place should be developed for both solid and liquid wastes.
Hotels and restaurants should accumulate there liquid wastes in septic tank and properly emptying when it gets full instead of directly connecting to open drainage lines and ditches. Moreover, strong monitoring and enforcement mechanisms should developed to control those institutions that release wastes to drainage lines and ditches and create pollution and contamination.
Community education and training should be provided to enhance the awareness of the community about the risk of inappropriate waste disposal practice on health and environment.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Preparing standard waste disposal site is the first priority to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. If the community has access for appropriate waste disposal place for both solid and liquid wastes, it will have the following positive outcomes:
The town become clean and suitable for life
Farmers will have access to natural fertilizers like composts if we have a good waste management system.
It is important for environmental sanitation,  hygiene and  community health
