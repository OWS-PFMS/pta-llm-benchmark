
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
The problems related to water service sustainability are: First, the existing water schemes are constructed before 17 years by JICA (Japan International Cooperation Agency). And there is only one water tank in lower Mille and one water tank in upper Mille. This water tanks could not satisfy the water demand of the entire population. Because the size of the population significantly increased within the past 17 years. Moreover, new organizations (e.g. schools, health centers etc.) are emerged in the town that require water supply. There is also considerable migration from rural areas in search of education and employment opportunities. All these factors increased the size of the population and the demand for water. Therefore, the water schemes that were constructed 17 years ago could not satisfy the current water demand of the town. Second, the existing water lines are very old and frequently broken out. In this regard with the support of the regional water office we are trying to fix broken water lines in the town. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
First, older water lines should be replaced by new one and expansion of water line should be done in new areas that require water supply. Second, we need to have new water tanks that have better capacity. If possible we should also enhance the capacity of the existing water tanks.
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
Even though both of the solutions are equally important, I give priority for the construction of new water tanks that have better capacity. For example, we need to have water tanks that have the capacity of 500,000 to 1 million liters of water. If we can increase the capacity of the existing water tanks, the community will have sufficient access to clean water. However, currently the community is not getting sufficient water because of low capacity of the water tanks.

Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? If we get new water tanks that have better water carrying capacity as well as if we construct new boreholes, the community will have sufficient access for clean water. And we can solve the problem related to water service sustainably.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 
Regarding water supply, the town is structured into four administrative units (i.e. lower Mille, upper Mille, 3rd Mille and 4th Mille) and we have water committees in these four parts of the town. We discuss with these water committees on the problems related to water service in the town. So we try to find out solutions in group. What about the coordination with other stakeholders working in the water sector in the woreda?  There is good coordination with other stakeholders functioning in the woreda as well. For example, there is a board that is formed by the representatives of different sector offices to govern the water service in the woreda. This board regularly set and discuss on problems related to water service in the woreda and try to find solutions for identified problems. Generally, I can say there is good coordination in the woreda.

What could be improved related to coordination? When we work in coordination, every organization will have information about the existing problems, and solutions will be identified collectively. Therefore, we should strengthen the existing coordination to sustain water service in the woreda.
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
When there is coordination between stakeholders, there will be information exchange on problems related to water service in the area and we will find solutions for the problems collectively. This will lead us to better water service in the area. For instance, the water committees gather the problems from the community and share to relevant stakeholders. And stakeholders discuss on the problems and provide solution to the community. How the coordination could solve some of the problems you mentioned earlier? Coordination is also important to solve the problems I mentioned earlier. For example, above the woreda there is regional water office, and we present problems that are beyond our capacity to the regional office. Accordingly the regional office support us by providing water pumps and materials for the maintenance of water lines. Therefore, coordination can lead to more sustainable water service.
