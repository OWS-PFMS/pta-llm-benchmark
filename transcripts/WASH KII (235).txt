1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Poor awareness of the community on waste disposal and management
Sanitation is the number on challenge for Woliso Town Administration. The major contributing factor for the sanitation problem of the town is the poor awareness of the community on waste disposal and management. The community is not aware of how improper waste disposal and management affect the image of a town. As the town is structured as a zonal town very recently, it has limited capacity to manage the town’s waste disposal and management problems by itself.   Most of the community members are not willing to travel long distance to dispose wastes outside residential areas. The hotels and restaurants of the town have also weakness in managing their wastes and keeping their hotel environment safe and clean.  If this community awareness problem is not solved on time, it will seriously affect the image of the town. In order to alleviate awareness related problems, in collaboration with Ambo University Woliso Campus, we have provided training to restaurant and hotel owners on how to manage wastes and make their surroundings clean and safe. It is obvious that customers need to see clean environment and get better services from the hotels and restaurants. Thus, the hotel and restaurant owners and managements should be aware of this and provide appropriate service to their clients by making their environment clean. 
II.Absence of waste disposal sites both for liquid and solid wastes 
As there is no permanent and temporary waste disposal place in the town the community dispose wastes anywhere in the town and this seriously affected the environmental sanitation, community health and the image of the town. Even though there are liquid waste collection vehicles that are owned by the town’s water and sewerage utility office, they are not providing service to the community due to absence of waste disposal place.     
III.Absence of regular water service 
There is frequent water service interruption in the town. For instance, the hotels and restaurants operating in the town might not have access for water for about three consecutive days or for a week. This seriously affected the hotel and tourism services of the town. The existing water service does not take into account the growing hotel and restaurant services in the town. As a temporary solution, we suggest hotel and restaurant owners to develop shallow well that will be used for sanitary purpose. The water service problem is more severe on the hotels and restaurants than households.  Currently there is an initiative to alleviate the water supply problems but the projects are not finalized in the intended time. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
I.In order to alleviate the problems related to the community awareness on waste disposal and management, there should be awareness enhancement program as it is important input to ensure community development. Without strong awareness enhancement campaign, we cannot improve the prevailing sanitation problems. To this end, the town Culture and Tourism Office, Municipality, Town Health Office and the town Environmental protection authority should have a coordinated planning and activity in changing the community awareness on waste disposal and management. Thus, a coordinated effort should be exerted to undertake massive awareness campaign in each kebele as changing the awareness of the community is the first and important step to get solution for problems related to waste disposal and management. We should advise the community to collect its solid wastes and burn every week or every two weeks instead of disposing in residential and open spaces. 

II.Related to the problem of waste disposal site, the town administration is working to identify appropriate site for waste disposal. Developing waste disposal place is a prime factor for sustainable sanitation service, thus, the town administration should work in collaboration with other relevant stakeholders to implement the project as soon as possible. 

III.Pertinent to the water supply problem I would like to suggest that the ongoing water supply expansion project that is financed by World Bank should be finalized and ready for service within short period.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Sanitation is very important and decisive for public health. When there is sustainable sanitation service, we can enhance the community health condition. Thus, developing appropriate waste disposal site is the first priority that should be addressed in the short run. This because it is a basis to alleviate other sanitation related problems of the town. In addition, designing waste transportation mechanisms is very important to ensure sustainable sanitation service and enhance community health. 
If the suggested solution is realized, the community become happy and healthy, will be motivated to clean its surroundings, we can improve the image of the town, and the tourism industry of the town will be enhanced and contribute for economic transformation.
