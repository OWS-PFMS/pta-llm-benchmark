
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
The main problems related to water service sustainability in the woreda are: Lack of qualified manpower in the water sector; scarcity of materials, parts and equipment for maintenance of water schemes. It takes long time to get spare parts to repair water schemes. Water pumps require regular maintenance but spare parts are not available in the woreda. Are there any other problems related to water service sustainability in the woreda?   There are no other problems.   
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
In the first place, the woreda water office should have required number of qualified manpower that could maintain sustainable water service in the area. Moreover, water pumps should be regularly monitored and repaired on time. To this end, spare parts should be available at least at woreda level.
There is also a shortage of budget in the woreda. Particularly the budget allocated for water office is very limited. The available budget could not enable the water office to purchase spare parts and other required materials to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. Therefore, to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda sufficient budget should be allocated for the water sector.  
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
Of the solutions I listed, the availability of spare parts and materials is a most important solution. This is because sometimes the community stay without water for more than two weeks due to absence of spare parts in the area. 
Woreda water office do not have budget to purchase spare parts on its own. It is the regional water bureau that supply spare parts to woreda. And the regional water bureau do not supply the required spare parts on time. The supply chain is very tedious. Therefore, to alleviate this problem sufficient budget should be allocated to the woreda water office so that the office can purchase spare parts immediately when required.
However, this year we have tried to allocate budget for assessment of areas that has potential ground water sources, for the purchase of spare parts and for maintenance of non-functional water schemes. I hope we will solve the problems to some extent this year.

Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? If these solutions will be implemented in the future, we can improve the water supply in the area. And the community will have sufficient access to clean water.
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service?  
I can say there is good coordination between the woreda water office, the town water utility office and the woreda administration office to solve the problems related to water service in the woreda. If there has not been coordination between stakeholders, the community couldn’t get what it is getting now. What could be improved? Particularly, the woreda administration office should understand the water problems of the area and regularly monitor the activities of the sector. The woreda water office should supply the required materials and spare parts on time for maintenance and rehabilitation of water schemes. The town water utility office also assign water technicians for maintenance of water lines. Thus, if these organizations create good coordination, we can alleviate the problem related to water service in the woreda.
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
Coordination can lead to more sustainable water service in the woreda because if there is coordination among stakeholders, problems can get solution instantly. Coordination can also solve the problems that I mentioned earlier but it cannot give solution for the problem related to budget. Moreover, without qualified manpower in the water sector, the coordination alone cannot bring any change. Older water pumps should also be changed by new ones and the budget problem should also be given much emphasis.
