Question 1: In your opinion what do you think are the main challenges to water service sustainability in Kitui County? Could you explain in detail how these challenges influence water service sustainability? 
Respondent: Thank you. Let me take this opportunity to thank USAID for promoting our partnership. In my capacity as in charge of Mutomo, I have the following, one the key challenges, management of the water schemes, poor management. Number two, ownership, number three, lack of training, that is capacity building. Then, these are now the key challenges. Then intervention, the water management committees must be retrained and check up. That is monitoring and evaluation and be advised accordingly. When we talk of capacity building we must be smart because it is too general. There is no way you can compare a giant water project like Masinga with these community because a donor comes, inject money there, training is being done, there is no follow up then we give the project to the community and they start fighting maybe because of money, and what have you. Basically what I'm saying, for community managed water projects to sustain themselves, the committee must be thoroughly trained on all aspects like financial management, O&M, procurement, and that's why I emphasize, I want to give a very good example of Kisasi. When I was in Kitui Rural, Kisasi used to do okay but when I met with them and I demanded financial report, I was surprised to find that the treasurer is also acting as the chairman, the pipe fitter and all those things so I had to dismantle that. The same applies to Kanyange. 
We had yet another problem. The political element. This also very important, political interference. Here I don't have such but I think Kanyangi it is there. You find these highly powered MCAs, because they want to get political mileage, they end up by saying no to that chairman, but where I am now I don't have this. The political interference it has no any weight as such. It can't be compared to like areas like Kanyangi and other areas, Zombe and all those other areas. You find MCA wants to be the signatory of the withdrawals. MCA must know the total collection in various communal water points. Some MCAs must be told by the end of the day or closure of the business, how much has been sold. And whenever money is going to be withdrawn, he must be notified, in my case here I have said no. Actually when I came here I called them, the operational ones and I told them, there is no way so long as I'm here, you withdraw money without bringing budget here for my approval and above all, VA-Village administrator in that particular locality of that water scheme must be signatory.
