1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town?
Respondent: First, let us see …., and we will come to our main problems. As in Kebele 06, we undergo cleaning services by our youth and women in our kebele. Weekly, every Friday we conduct a cleaning campaign. 
One of the problems regarding waste management is lack of garbage truck. Currently, most of the waste collection trucks are undergoing maintenance, so we cannot dispose the garbage we collected. 
Secondly, there is a liquid waste that comes out of a location in the town called “Kuteba bet”. We have informed the town administration to take care of it. The septic tank has not been emptied and it is posing some issues.
Interviewer: Why the septic tank has not been cleaned/pumped?
Respondent: It is a rental property managed by housing office of the town administration. I do not know what held them back from cleaning the septic tanks up. It is beyond our capacity to resolve the issue, but we have been notifying them that it is creating problems. 
One of the reasons for the problem is that when we collect garbage, we could not get enough garbage trucks timely that we couldn’t dispose the garbage we collected. 
The second reason is septic tanks are not getting pumped in time. The town municipality believes kebele administration should address the issue and vise versa. 
Interviewer: Are there any institutional problems in relation to human resource, technology and there like. Waste management system is a complicated system. If there are any issues related to that.
Respondent: Basically, there are three main waste management strategies. The first one is liquid waste management; liquid waste is being dumped in a dedicated location in kebele 03. But, what is currently happening is that it is polluting the surrounding. And we and residents have been repeatedly requesting, but no one responded to that. This is one issue.
Interviewer: Is this problem due to lack of budget? Is it related to shortage of vacuum trucks? 
Respondent: May be, town municipality and water utility office should work hand in hand. I think they have not been doing so. The water utility office runs two waste management systems, and the municipality coordinates overall. Currently, burning is the primary method of waste disposal in our kebele. But, we do not believe we have done enough. But, we have tried to burn wastes where the problem is worst. 
The third way of waste disposal is burying. We don’t have much to burry. However, we have removed huge waste such as dippers, plastics and bones from butchery are being burned. We have tried to remove a lot of garbage using the winter volunteers and later, we have also get rid of huge waste every Friday through the summer volunteers. But, we could not remove non-combustible waste, dust particles in particular, due to shortage of transportation facilities. Even though we have tried by collecting some cash from our personal pocket and few investors, we failed to sustain because the kebele is not collecting revenue. If the municipality’s trucks are maintained and get back to work, we will be able to find a solution. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned most of the solutions alongside the problems. I will point out the problems and you will suggest the solutions one by one. Garbage truck is a problem. Who should take responsibility to resolve this issue? 
Respondent: The kebele administration takes the responsibility to collect garbage generated within the kebele. We have been able to collect the garbage by involving youth, women, and volunteers in the kebele. However, when the garbage is collected we do not get garbage truck to pick the collected and dispose it. As a result the garbage is blown by the wind and gets back where it was. The first reason for this is due to the size of the town, the administration is having hard time to monitor all the kebeles properly. Secondly, the garbage trucks are not accessible. There are only two garbage trucks in the whole town; however, our kebele alone needs two trucks. 
Interviewer: Is it for the solid or liquid waste?
Respondent: For the solid waste. However, recently, the town administration has assigned town sanitation and beauty experts. If we work with these experts and use the trucks I think there will be a solution. But, there has been no solution until now. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: As TETRATECH is working on sanitation and liquid waste management try to focus on the issues related to that when you propose solutions. Now, you have said that there is lack of vacuum truck. What do you think is the solution for this?
Respondent: Currently the problem is the liquid waste coming out of the rental properties owned by the housing and construction office called ‘kuteba betoch’. Particularly, around kebele 06 is where the main problem is. There is a septic tank down the residential area but when it is full, the water part of the sludge spills over to the surrounding environment. To address this issue, I think, the town administration should work with the water utility office. There are these vacuum trucks; they had been under the town municipality before, but now the water utility office runs the trucks. I think both the town administration and the water utility office should work together. It is beyond the capacity of our kebele administration and we can do nothing about it. This is because the revenue from these rental properties is being collected by the town municipality. When the town administration collects the rental fee, it should take responsibility of the health of the community near the septic tank. The liquid waste from these houses should not put local community at risk, therefore, should be disposed properly. 
Interviewer: The solution for the septic tank is the responsibility of thetown administration, municipality, and water utility office. This is beyond the capacity of the kebele. The other complaint is related to the dump site. In relation to it what about the dumping site? What should be done to solve the problem? 
Respondent: The liquid waste collected from the town has been dumped in a disposal site in 07 kebele. However, it is full now, so liquid waste dumping site should be relocated. I think the town administration has already shifted the waste disposal location. I think it is under construction, and it should continue. There is no other option. I believe if the site is full it will have detrimental health effects on young children, elderly and animals. So we should work on this. 

Interviewer: Last question here is, the town administration and water utility office have coordination gap, I have heard this in other places too. Particularly, the three responsible bodies, town administration, town municipality and water utility office lack coordination. This is the biggest problem that has been pointed out in many places. What do you think would be the solution for this? If these three actors had worked together and there had been a modern sewerage system, no waste would have been disposed outside the dump site. Is it beyond their capacity?
Respondent: I don’t think so.
Interviewer: Yes
Respondent: I don’t think it would be beyond their capacity. I think it is due to lack of paying due attention and time. It is straightforward. If the septic tank gets full, it should be emptied. If it gets emptied the community around the septic tank will have safe and healthy environment. To you surprise, now as we speak, the septic tank is full and the sludge is overflowing. The overflow of the sludge is huge. I have known the place since 2015 and the location has been in problem. This is not because of lack of liquid waste management but lack of attention. We should keep the benefits of both who live in the ‘kuteba betoch’ and those near the location of the septic tank. If necessary I can show you in person. Therefore, it should be considered seriously and it is not out of reach. Public and communal latrines are getting pumped, so as the municipality is collecting rental fees from these houses, it should take care of the waste that comes out of these houses. I believe this is within their reach. The town administration, town municipality, and water utility office did not give it much attention. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Lastly, focusing on the liquid waste management, of the solutions you have listed, which is the most important? The solution that you believe if the solution occurs it would solve other problems too.
Respondent: In our kebele there are health workers, kebele leadership, and community leaders. We have discussed with these bodies that there should not be garbage outside the waste disposal sites. However, there are still some problems such as dumping laundry wastewater on the streets. I think such things are dangerous to the environment. These wastes can be allowed to get infiltrated within a compound. Infiltration is one waste water disposal strategy.  
The second strategy is liquid wastes that should be pumped and dumped immediately when it id full by the town administration, particularly those in the ‘kuteba betoch’. If this is done local community can be safer. 
Thirdly there is waste that is disposed by the community. When individual latrines are full, people can get their latrines emptied. Health workers and kebele administration assigned to the structure under the kebeles –Ketena -should work on the peace, security and health issues, because these issues are inseparable. If we work on these areas we can improve the public health in our kebele. Health extension workers are informing the community members to dig a one meter deep pit inside their compounds for the laundry waste water. We have 3 health extension workers and four ketanas they can create the awareness wherever they go. So, by creating awareness on the agendas that we should work hand in hand with our community and disposing wastes through burning, burying, and vacuuming out, we can create health environment in our locality and our kebele. 
In places where the problem is worse such as in the case of rental properties owned by the town administration called ‘kuteba betoch’, town administration and water utility office should be involved to resolve the issue. I think that if other concerned bodies are involved in the process of addressing the issue, we will have much more cleaner town.
