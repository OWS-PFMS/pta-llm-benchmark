1) Interviewer: Okay, in your opinion, what are the challenges to provide a sustainable water service in the town?
Response: The main challenge is that the supply and demand are not compatible. The reason for this challenge is a shortage of budget. Our limited budget will not allow us to provide a clean water service to the town although we have many resources of water nearby. If we fix our budget issue, then we can use those resources to provide clean water service to the town. 
Interviewer: Okay, I have recorded that challenge. Do you want to mention any other challenge?
Response: We have also this challenge of priority. Most farmers use the water for irrigation and it would affect the clean water we provide in some parts of town like Woliso, Goro, and few others. We have limited capacity for providing clean water for the town and when farmers take much of it for irrigation, shortage of clean water occurs.
Interviewer: Do you want to add more challenge?
Response: We have also a shortage of experts on this water service field within our town's Water Authority office. These are the challenges I see in providing sustainable water service.
Interviewer: You are saying that there is a shortage of experts in the town?
Response: Yes. 


2) Interviewer: Okay, you have mentioned three challenges now. So, you as an expert, what have your office planned to tackle these challenges. Let's start from the beginning. The first challenge you mentioned is that incompatibility of supply and demand. What should be the solution for that?
Response: I think the solution for that is fixing the budget so that it can accommodate all our problems on meeting the demand of the residents of the town. If we have more budgets we can increase our service capacity and reach even remote areas in providing sustainable clean water.
Interviewer: So why didn't this happen already?
Response: We always ask for an increase of budget every year. Like if you go to 01 Kebele, they don't get clean water even once in a year. The population size is increasing on a daily basis, yet our service is limited to old installment and it's not improving. So if the government can focus on this issue and increase our budget, we can improve the service to the town. We can also separate lines for cleaning water and water used for irrigation...
Interviewer: I remember that you raised this issue in the last meeting with Water Authority office. You have mentioned that it's creating a conflict of interest between the farmers and town residents.
Response: You know the problem is that we have a limited supply of clean water. I think the Water Authority is preparing a regulation on how the water for irrigation should be used. If this regulation is implemented, we can share the water without conflict between the farmers and the town residents. We need also to keep training those staff of Water Authority so that they can also be a part of the challenge and can come up with solutions of theirs. 
Interviewer: sorry to interrupt, about the experts, are you saying they are few in numbers or they are not trained well?
Response: I think both are the problem. They are few and also those few are not trained well. So we should give attention to this issue and increase their number and as well as train them well. You know those materials we have in regards to clean water service are imported and once broken or damaged, it will take time to replace them. So also buying those materials in bulk and putting reserves in stores so that when something is broken, we can replace it immediately helps to maintain the water service in Woliso Town. We have water resources but purifying the water so that it can be used for drinking is the challenge now.


3) Interviewer: Okay, from these challenges you have mentioned, which one should be prioritized so that we can see a big difference in water service? 
Response: The main priority should be an increase of budget, because most other solutions can be effective if you have the budget to implement them. An increase in our budget will allow us to improve our service at the same pace the population is growing. Currently, we have 93 thousand residents in the town, yet we are using a system designed to provide water service for 20 to 30 thousand people. The population has grown threefold in last decade, yet our service is not improving. So a budget increase can help us to improve our service so that it can accommodate more residents.
Interviewer: So a budget increase will make a big difference in water service?
Response: Yes.
Interviewer: Can you explain specifically which part of those challenges on maintaining water service will be affected by an increase of budget?
Response: As I said earlier, our population is increasing and we don't have those materials to purify the water from the water resources and provide it to the residences. An increase of budget can buy those materials and also allow us to hire more experts in the field.


4) Interviewer: Okay, I am left with two questions only. My next question is about coordination. We all know that running water service is not a one-party job. It needs coordination between multiple parties. So I would like to ask your suggestion about the current status of coordination between stakeholders on water service. Is it tight or is it something to be improved?
Response: I think stakeholders on this matter should always plan and work together. But in our case, we only meet when there is an emergency situation. We don't plan projects together but that is the one we should do so that we can achieve more goals and can also assess what we have achieved together. 
Interviewer: Okay, so you are telling me that the coordination is not well. Can you tell me which offices are working on the water service issue? Do you have board or committee? 
Response: We have a board of course and the Mayor is the chairman. The Water Authority office reports to this board. So we shall all come together on that board to plan and see our achievements...
Interviewer: What has the board achieved so far? 
Response: The board is not active right now.
Interviewer: So what needs to be improved in regards to the board? 
Response: I think the board should be active and also involve more Non-profit organizations focusing on water services. If the board is active and has a regular meeting then we can tackle more challenges than before.


5) Interviewer: Okay, my last question is what kind of role does coordination of this institutions play in order to solve those challenges you mentioned on my first question? Like the budget issue, the human resource shortage and the demand and supply issue. Do you think it has a role to solve those issues?
Response: Yes I think it has a role.
Interviewer: How?
Response: You see when we plan for budget or try to figure out how many staffs we need on water services we need to plan together so that everyone knows what is expected from their part. We can set our goals together and after we finish the year we can meet to discuss how much of it is achieved. 
(Someone comes at the door asking for the interviewer. The interviewer politely tells him that he is interviewing and will see him later.)
Response: As I was saying the coordination can help us to defeat those challenges...
Interviewer: Let's be specific. How does coordination help on the human resource shortage issue?
Response: If the board meets regularly, everyone will know where we are lacking a human resource on supplying clean water for the residence. They will also know which department should get training and building capacity because they will get a regular report on performance. Involving NGOs will help them to point out on need projects and allocate their budgets to those projects. We will also evaluate how these NGOs are performing and see if they can be given more responsibilities or can be replaced by other NGOs who are working in the same area.
Interviewer: I am done with my questions. Do you have any final thoughts?
Response: My final thought is the organization you are working with...
Interviewer: It's called IRC Wash.
Response: IRC wash is doing a good job by studying the challenges we are facing in providing clean water to our town. I wish we could work together and...
Interviewer: You want us to work with you?
Response: Yes.
