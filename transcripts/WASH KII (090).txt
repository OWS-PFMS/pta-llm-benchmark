1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: The first, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in Debrebirhan town?
 Respondent: Regarding the town’s waste management and sanitation, one of the problems is lack of awareness among the community. The community members are not working to create clean, beautiful and sanitary environment, but to just get the garbage out of their yard. Aside from that there are enterprises that work on environment sanitation and beauty. However, we do not have strong structure that work on liquid waste management. The government body’s monitoring and supervision is also poor. Because of these problems liquid waste disposal is a huge issue in Debrebirhan town.
Currently, we have set one association up in kebele 07 that could help alleviate this problem.
Interviewer: What kind of association?
Respondent: The cleaning service association is made up of ten first degree holder youths. They are transporting wastes to the dumping site using carts. 
The other main problem is lack of waste disposal site for both the liquid and solid wastes. The business plan for the association is not based on complete assessment. Both the solid and liquid waste dumping sites for the entire town is in our kebele. The liquid waste dumped from the industries, hotels, and individual households is destroying the environment and the crop around the area. It is now overflowing and has been a governance issue for us in the surrounding area. We are currently asking the town administration to relocate the dumping site. 
Similarly, solid waste is not also disposed of properly; many are dumping garbage outside the compound of the landfill. Even the licensed enterprises unload their carts when it is full. That is why I believe it has been a massive problem to our town. 

Interviewer: You have mentioned the main problems let me remind you. The first one is lack of awareness among the community. Second is lack of strong waste management structure from the government side. Third is the absence of solid and liquid waste disposal site. Can you elaborate more on the second issue? What is the weakness in this area?
Respondent: There are a limited number of people who provide this service. Acquiring the license to collect waste from individuals has a lot of ups and downs. Further, the water utility office is not conducting appropriate monitoring and supervision. There is only one expert, when the expert is not there waste is being dumped everywhere. 
Interviewer: When you say there are a limited number of institutions, are they private institutions? What are they? 
Respondent: There are some individuals who try to collect garbage from households using carts for a small fee. But, it is a daunting task and they give up after some time. Also because of awareness gap people dump it on the riversides. Government structure at all levels has not created the necessary awareness. Besides, there is integration among kebeles, associations that work on waste collection, the water utility office is rather poor. There are incidences that waste is dumped outside of the landfill inappropriately, even from areas outside the woreda’s catchment. For instance, brewery factories sometimes dispose their waste on farmland. There is lack of coordination.
Interviewer: Is the lack of coordination related to this?
Respondent: Yes. For instance, if kebeles, town administration, and water utility office work in coordination they can do great job for the community.   
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Let’s proceed to the solutions. The first problem, lack of awareness, has been raised by many people and it is a fundamental problem. This can be linked with the problem of open defecation. What should be done to solve this issue? 
Respondent: The main solution is coordination. If the government structure at different levels work in coordination starting from kebele. For instance, in our kebele we have put a lot of effort to legalize the association. Monitoring and supervision should be conducted regarding the waste disposal system, and corrective measures should be taken if there is any defiance. We can make a big difference if we do so.
Apart from that, there are many unemployed youth that can be engaged in this area. They can undergo door-to-door garbage collection by signing agreements with the residents. So, setting up such enterprises and make sure its availability in all kebeles can be one of the solutions. 
Further, a modern waste disposal site for both the solid and liquid wastes should be prepared outside the town residential areas, taking into account the future growth of the town. The current waste dumping sites are close to residential locations, and thus, risking the nearby inhabitants to many communicable diseases. 
We should convince factories to act responsibly and dump their wastes in the landfills only. If these are done, it is possible to make the town attractive, clean, and beautiful. 
Interviewer: The issues regarding to waste disposal site has been raised repeatedly. Is there any initiatives so far? I think the current one is full. Right? 
Respondent: Yes. 
Interviewer: I think there is something that has already been planned
Respondent: In order to relocate the waste dump site, the town administration, engineers from our kebele, concerned bodies from water utility office, and local community have signed petition. Many hectares of land around the liquid dumping site have been devastated because of the waste. As a result, it has been suspended for a while and resumed again recently. I think assessment has been conducted by the city to relocate the disposal site, even though I cannot be accurately know what is currently going on. 
The solid waste has also been disposed of in the current location for a very long time. It has no monitoring and supervision in place. It does not even have a security guard. People dump garbage near the gate. 
Interviewer: Who is responsible for this?
Respondent: The town municipality is responsible for the management of the sites. We have seen some improvements; experts from the municipality undergo supervisions. We, kebele administration, are conducting follow-ups on the local cart owners who collect garbage door-to-door whether they dump the garbage they collected inside the landfill or not. Plus, butchery had been throwing leftovers on the streets, but not we are creating awareness among the community and we are also trying to correct such activities. 
However, the long long-term solution is to build a modern landfill outside the residential area of the town. The current one is already inside the town. The liquid waste disposal site is also currently full. It gets full when few factories dump their wastes there. This is because the site is not big enough and assessment-based. 
Besides, there are farmers who did not get compensation for their land it has been taken for the waste disposal site. Sometimes these farmers block the road to the dumping site, and then many trucks unload the waste just in the open or on the farmlands. 
Therefore, first and foremost the municipality, second, the water utility office, third, other town and kebele leadership on different levels should work together to address these issues. 
Interviewer: What I wanted you to answer for me in relation to that is lack of coordination, which is one big purpose of this assessment. As you have mentioned earlier, the stakeholders have been identified. However, there is lack of coordination in planning, implementation and so on. What solution would you propose for that? Because as you have said, the town’s sanitation and beauty is not the issue of a single sector and it would not succeed that way. So, what is the gap and what should be done?
Respondent: The first reason is that we have not built an institution or a community that takes ownership of the sector. In order to solve this, the institutions that we have mentioned should discuss regularly, it might be quarterly. Secondly, when the management tightens its supervision system on the grass root level workers, they will monitor by whom, where, and how the waste is disposed of. Besides, the lover level workers will create community awareness and legal framework to penalize people who dump garbage on the streets.  
Interviewer: That by itself requires coordination. 
Respondent: Yes. It requires coordination and harmonization. 
Interviewer: Tit required coordination with security and legal bodies right? 
Respondent: Yes. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Of the solutions you have mentioned, which is the most important? What would happen if the solution is implemented?
Respondent: Awareness creation is critical for both the community and leadership. If enough budget is not available, it can be conducted locally. Second, institutions from top to bottom should coordinate their efforts. They should monitor the disposal sites and check if there is any leakage. Aside from that, the existing location is not based on proper assessment, and causing problem on the local community. Better waste disposal should be built. If we do these we will improve the situation. 
Interviewer: Do you believe that the first solution that you mentioned, i.e. awareness creation is the best solution both in the community and institutions.
Respondent: Yes. 
Interviewer: Awareness has been created. If the awareness creation is fully implemented what would happen.  
Respondent: At least it is possible to create a generation that loathes throwing garbage on the street, if awareness is created properly. Besides, community members would be able to separate liquid and solid wastes. If they use different waste disposal mechanism, and hand over garbage to the waste collector enterprises, we will have a clean Debrebirhan that is free of any odor, diseases, and garbage.
