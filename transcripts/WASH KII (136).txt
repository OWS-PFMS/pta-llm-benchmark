1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
The woreda water office in coordination with the regional water bureau and other non-governmental organizations is exerting significant effort to ensure water service suitability in urban and rural areas of the woreda. Water problem primarily affect women because they travel long distance to fetch water. And sometime women face gender violence when they travel long distance to bring water. Because of this facts the issue of sustainable water service is also the concern of our office.
Even though there are considerable efforts to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda, the supply capacity of the existing water sources is far below the population size of the woreda. What is the reason for the supply shortage?  If we take Mille town as an example, in previous years there was 24 hours water supply in the town. However, due to rapid population growth the existing water schemes could not satisfy the water demand of the growing population. 
There are some boreholes that are constructed by the federal government outside the town. Because of their distance from the town, it is very expensive to pump the water to the town’s water reservoirs using diesel generators. Although the town administration has requested Electric Power Authority to install electric transformer that will be used to pump the water to the reservoirs, the authority did not take any action. Since the power problem is beyond the capacity of the woreda, there is a serious problem related to water service.  
What other problems are there related to water service sustainability? In some areas of the woreda absence of ground water is another challenge for water service sustainability. In some areas even if we get ground water, the water is salty and not suitable for household consumption.
There is also problem related to use of water schemes in some areas of the woreda. For example, children play with hand pumps of water schemes and it easily broken out. And spare parts are not available to repair these broken water schemes.

Do you have anything to add on this? Even though I have no detailed information, the woreda water office has limitation of resources to conduct maintenance and expansion works. Most non-governmental organizations only provide technical support and they do not provide material and equipment support. The woreda has no capacity to develop new water schemes using its own resources. Generally there is resource limitation to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. However, if there are sufficient materials and equipment at woreda water office, it is possible to provide sustainable water service in the woreda.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
In this arid area water is very essential so sufficient resource should be allocated to the water office. The other solution is that we should enhance the awareness of the community on proper utilization of the existing water schemes. That is we should teach the rural community about how to keep the water schemes clean and safe.
Relatively there is a serious water service problem in rural areas, hence there should be massive effort to construct new water schemes in rural areas. Particularly there are four kebeles where the government is implementing community settlement program. In these kebeles there is serious water problem. Even if the federal government construct one borehole in the area, it does not have the capacity to supply water to all kebeles. Therefore, emphasis should be given to construct additional water schemes in the areas. 
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
Even though all the solutions I mentioned are very important, I give priority for enhancing the awareness of the community on proper use of the existing water schemes. This awareness enhancement is very important to make the existing water schemes sustainable. This is because if we don’t use the existing water schemes properly, constructing new water schemes is meaningless. Beside the awareness creation, we should also construct new water schemes in areas where there is no water supply.
Moreover, due to misuse of water schemes, some water born disease might happen in the area. Therefore, proper use of water schemes is one of the solution to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda.

Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? If the suggested solutions implemented, we will see significant changes related to water service in the area. What type of changes you will see in the area? For example, if there is clean water in rural areas the community settle in one area. That is the community will not move from place to place in search of water. If there is sufficient water in the area, pastoralist can grow feed for their animals, their animals will have access to clean water, and women do not travel long distance to fetch water. 
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service?  
I think there are gaps and weaknesses related to coordination of stakeholders. Water office, pastoral development office, women and children affairs office and other sector offices only meet when NGOs come and organize workshops. We have weak linkage with sector offices. What could be improved? Currently there is no coordinated planning and activity among sector offices. Therefore, instead of planning and implementing activities separately, it is better to plan and implement activities collectively. This is because a separate effort cannot bring the required change. 
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
The woreda water office alone cannot manage all problems related to water service sustainability. Thus, when the woreda health office visit kebeles, instead of only focusing on health issues it would be great if they also address the problems related to water services. Moreover, if women and children affairs office engage in teaching the community about proper use of water schemes, we can solve the problem related to water service sustainability. Therefore, coordination between sector offices can lead to more sustainable water service.
