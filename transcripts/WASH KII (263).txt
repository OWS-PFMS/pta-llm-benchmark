1) Interviewer: What are the challenges of providing a sustainable water supply to Woliso town? What are the problems you see that affect the water supply in this town?
Female Interviewee: Well, we have a shortage of the supply of water in this town. They don't even tell us when the supply breaks. For example in Addis Ababa and other towns when water supply breaks, you will be told before that happen so that you prepare yourself. But here, we will just wake up and see there is no water on the tap. So we try to fetch water from other areas and we don't even have a clue where water is there and where the water is not there. The second issue is that when a pipeline breaks, they don't come and fix it immediately...
Interviewer: Oh they don't come and fix the pipeline immediately?
Female Interviewee: Yes, they always come late. 
Interviewer: Okay, you have mentioned two challenges so far. The first one is that the residents will not be notified when the water supply on their side breaks and the second one is when a pipeline breaks they don't fix it immediately. What do you think is the reason for them not to come and fix the broken pipeline immediately? What do they say?
Female Interviewee: They say that they don't have enough manpower to response for such emergency. They tell us that all their staffs are engaged and we should have to wait until they finish what they already are doing. 
Interviewer: Oh, they don't have enough human resource?
Female Interviewee: That's what I think.
Interviewer: Okay, do you have any other challenge to add? Sir, do you have anything to add to these challenges?
Male interviewee: Well on my personal matter I have not been troubled in regards to water services, but as my colleague said I see water supply breaks in some parts of this town.
Interviewer: Where exactly have you seen water supply break?
Male Interviewee: It's down from here, still in the same Kebele where we are now. 
Female Interviewee: Here in Kebele 03 and Kebele 04...
Male interviewee: We don't know if the pipeline is the same with us or not. I know we have two pipelines operating around here. The first one is connected to the waterholes and the second one is connected to the rivers. We are using the pipeline from the waterhole resource and we do not experience much trouble on our side but...
Interviewer: So you do not have water supply disconnection frequently?
Male Interviewee: Yes we experience disconnection rarely. 
Interviewer: So the other part you said gets frequent disconnection, their pipeline is connected to the river?
Male Interviewee: I think so. The pipeline connected with the river is used mostly by farmers for irrigation. So they frequently have water disconnection. But for us connected to the waterholes, unless we have a broken pipeline and they delay to fix that we do not get much trouble in regards to the water supply. This is the challenge I see.
Interviewer: So those parts which their pipeline is connected to the river are the one with much trouble.
Male Interviewee: Yes. Ours is related to a broken pipeline issue only. And sometimes even we fix the pipeline by ourselves and the supply continues.

2) Interviewer: Okay, you have said that any pipeline that breaks is not fixed by the office. You have also said that when the water supply interrupts, you would not be notified. You have also mentioned about those parts whom pipeline connected to the river have much trouble in accessing water supply. So what do you suggest to fix those issues? What should be done?
Female Interviewee: Well I think you should talk to the concerned parties in regards to solutions. [05:00] We can only tell you what we are experiencing, but the solution should come from the city administration. The City administration knows we are having those problems. 
Interviewer: I understand, but am asking about your personal suggestion on how the city administration or other concerned parties should solve the issue.
Female Interviewee: Well, at least they can start by awarding the people when there is a discount on the water supply. Then they should also have enough manpower so that they can fix broken pipelines on time.
Interviewer: Okay, Sir do you have anything to add?
Male Interviewee: No, I don't have.

3) Interviewer: Okay, my next question is, in your opinion, which one of the solutions you mentioned should be given priority? 
Female Interviewee: I think the notifications should be given priority. Even if they don't use media, they can use a megaphone to tell the residents that there will be a water supply disconnection in a, b, c, d part of the town and to this date or time. That will allow us to organize ourselves to store enough water and pass that service disruption time with no trouble at all.
Interviewer: So if these solutions you have suggested are implemented, what kind of change do you expect to see in Woliso town?
Female Interviewee: Well this is like creating a bridge between the people and the Water Authority. If the water authority listens to what the people say and implement it, even the people will be obedient and they will take care of the water supply materials as their own. But if there is no bridge between the people and the authority, then the people will be stubborn and that will create more troubles.

4) Okay, my next question is about coordination between those sectors and agencies who are based on water supply. How do you see their coordination? Are they working together to provide a solution for the people?
Female Interviewee: Well I think we are facing troubles because they don't have that coordination. If they had coordination we wouldn't have much trouble. I think they should have coordination so that they can have enough human resource and working plan and implementation. 
Interviewer: So you are saying that there is no coordination between those sectors and the troubles you are experiencing are because of this reason.
Female Interviewee: Yes.
Interviewer: So what's your suggestion to create this coordination? What needs to be improved so that we see this coordination?
Female Interviewee: Well if the society listens to what the authorities are saying and the water authority also plans and set up meetings with other sectors... For example, if the water authority has a Complaint Hearing desk, we can tell them our problems and that will help them to find and solve our real problems.

5) Interviewer: [10:00] Good, Now my last question will be about how this coordination you suggested will have an impact on those challenges you mentioned earlier. How does coordination help on solving those challenges you have mentioned?
Female Interviewee: As I told you earlier, it's like creating a bridge between the people and the sectors. If the people's voice can be heard and get a response, the people will also be responsible for the materials and services they get and that'll solve many problems.
Interviewer: Okay, thank you very much Madam Yirgalem.
