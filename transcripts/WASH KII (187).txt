1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: One of the basic problems related to sustainability of water service is that we do not conduct comprehensive study prior to development of water schemes to identify areas that have potential water resource. Most of the water facilities developed without a clear understanding of the potential of the areas. Because of this, the water schemes do not supply water for long period.  
The materials used for development of water schemes are of poor quality and easily become worn-out. Moreover, the contractors are not qualified and do not have the required technical skill. This obviously affect the sustainability of water service in different parts of the zone.
Most water schemes do not provide service due absence of maintenance materials in rural areas. Even if local community can repair some of the problems, they cannot repair them due to absence of spare parts and tools. Moreover, the zonal and woreda water offices do not have regular monitoring and maintenance schedule to ensure sustainability of the water facilities.
The community also lacks awareness on proper use of water facilities. This mismanagement by the community seriously affect the sustainability of water schemes in various rural areas.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have raised that there is no appropriate study prior to development of water schemes and this affect the sustainability of water service in the area. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solution to this problem?
Respondent: During studies prior to development of water schemes, we have to consider local knowledge. There should be discussion with local communities about areas that have potential water resource. Moreover, qualified local consultants that have solid experience in similar assignments should conduct the study.
Interviewer: You have mentioned that the materials used for construction of water facilities are of poor quality and this has implication on the sustainability of water service in the area. What do you suggest to alleviate this problem? 
Respondent: When we select materials for construction of water facilities, we have to consider their durability and availability of the materials in nearby areas when there is a need for maintenance. Moreover, the regional and zonal water authorities should undertake materials quality check throughout project implementation. Otherwise, contractors use cheap and poor quality materials to maximize their profit.
Interviewer: You have indicated that considerable number of water schemes do not provide service to the community due to absence of regular maintenance service and unavailability of spare parts in local areas. What ideas or recommendations do you have about the solution to this problem?
Respondent: First, we have to aware the community on proper use of water schemes to minimize frequency of damages or breakages. Then we have to provide training on minor maintenance skill for selected groups of the community. Moreover, we have to equip those selected and trained individuals with appropriate maintenance tools. We should also facilitate access for spare parts through organizing groups that supply materials in local areas. In addition, there should be regular follow-up by water offices to identify gaps and provide maintenance for major problems as well as to provide continuous skill development trainings. 
Interviewer: Finally, you have mentioned lack of awareness of the community on proper use of water schemes as a main factor for sustainability of water service in different rural areas. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solution to this problem?
Respondent: There are water users associations or WASHCos in rural areas. Thus, we have to strengthen these committees by providing various trainings on proper use and management of water facilities. Once we make WASHCos well aware, they can provide trainings to the rest of the community members. By doing this we can enhance the awareness of large segment of the community within short period. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: In my opinion, the first and most important solution among the problems and solutions I mentioned earlier is conducting comprehensive study by involving local communities prior to development of any form of water schemes. Currently, in some areas water facilities are developed without prior study and considering local community indigenous knowledge. This obviously has implication on sustainability of water schemes. If we conduct such studies in consultation with local community and by involving qualified experts, we can develop water schemes that have a potential to generate quality and long serving water for the community.
