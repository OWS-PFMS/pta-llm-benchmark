1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Absence of waste disposal site 
Woliso town has no waste disposal place. As there is no a waste disposal site the town sanitation becomes worst. Our Association for the Communal Latrine is facing a problem to empty the latrines due to absence of waste disposal site. The liquid waste collection vehicles owned by water and sewerage utility of the town are let idle due to disposal site problem. Due to this fact currently, our communal latrines are over flowing and difficult to provide service to the community. The problem is very serious and it is beyond our capacity to solve. There are few private pit emptying service providers but their service charge is very expensive as they dispose the waste by traveling to other towns that have waste disposal site. We have discussed with the municipality and the town water and sewerage utility office to get solution for the problem. Even though they promised to provide a solution within short period, the problem is not solved yet. As our kebele is the major open-air market center of the town it is highly affected by solid wastes. In this regard even though the municipality promised to provide temporary waste disposal containers, they did not practically implement the promise.  

II.Misconduct of the community on waste disposal
The misconduct of the community significantly worsen the sanitation problem of the town. They throw wastes into streets, rivers and drainage lines that bring serious sanitation problems. Our kebele (kebele 03) is a market center and accommodates large number of people particularly during market days. This resulted in huge accumulation of solid wastes in the area. The wastes are not collected and disposed properly due to absence of waste disposal place and poor awareness of the community. 
III.Shortage of water supply
Shortage of water supply is the other main challenge for the residents of Woliso town. In some instances, we say without water for about three days or a week. The water office argued that the main reason for the water supply shortage is that farmers for small-scale irrigation also use the river that is used as a major water source of the town. Due to this fact, there is no regular supply of water in our kebele as well as in the town.  Thus, we are forced to travel long distance to fetch water from other places. 
IV.Absence of regular maintenance of communal latrines
The government to serve the community constructs the communal latrines. However, currently due to absence of regular maintenance they are going to be out of use.  The roofs, floors and other parts of the latrines are seriously damaged and risky for use unless they are repaired immediately. Though we tried to repair the latrines by contributing money, we could not solve the problem sustainably. As these latrines serve large considerable number of poor segment of the community, the government should pay attention to repair the latrines. Unless these latrines will be repaired and provide service, the community will be forced to depend on open defecation as they have no other alternatives. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
In order to solve the problems related to sanitation and waste disposal place I would like to suggest that:
The town administration should prepare appropriate waste disposal site for both liquid and solid wastes as soon as possible.
There should be awareness creation training for the community on how to dispose wastes and the consequence of waste disposal malpractices.
The town administration should arrange temporary waste accumulation containers in different areas of the town to minimize the sanitation problems. 
There should be immediate support from the government and other actors to repair the communal latrines. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Preparing waste disposal site is the first priority as it is the basis to solve other sanitation related problems of Woliso town. Thus, preparing temporary and permanent waste disposal site is a burning issue that requires immediate action. If the town gets appropriate waste disposal site, it will have the following positive effects on the community:
The town becomes clean and beautiful
The community will be protected from various forms of health disorders 
The town becomes attractive and eye caching
