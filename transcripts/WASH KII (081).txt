
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion, in Debreberehan City what do you think the main challenges or problems of sustainable sanitation? 
Response: In Debreberehan City the society should get aware of this thing. Until now awareness creation about sanitation is not done. Each waste material should be disposed properly. But there are things that are being done by hiding from the authorized person to control sanitation. That is disposing improperly liquid and solid type of wastes. For example, liquid waste from the hotels are being disposed on the streets this is not allowed but the society is doing this. This indicates there is lack of awareness. If there was awareness, they will know what they do has a direct bad effect on themselves.
Interviewer:  Ok what about other problems? Regarding sectors? 
Response: as I mentioned earlier, sectors push duties to each other. They are not willing to do tasks by themselves. They consider sanitation task is only given to Health and Water sectors. We have to work together. But mostly, this is considered as a responsibility of one or two sectors. 
Interviewer:  are there another problem?
Response: No, there are no problems.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: Ok, now for the problems you mentioned what do you think are the solutions? For example, for lack of awareness of hotels, individuals and organizations? What do you think the solution to create this awareness? 
Response: the first thing is, it is to create awareness. As I said earlier, currently the job is left or considered for one sector. So, by creating awareness we should change this kind of thinking.  Without creating awareness nothing cannot be successful. 
Interviewer: who should do this task? How is it going to be done?
Response: as I said earlier, everyone has its own role. For example, there is a person assigned in sanitation. This person’s first task should not be taking measures in individuals who are out of law. He must have a program to create awareness first. He should create a sense of responsibility. Then everyone will be responsible. Hiding from the authorized person to control will not exist. Everyone will be controller.
