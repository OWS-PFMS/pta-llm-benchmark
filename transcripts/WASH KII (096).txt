
1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Respondent: The first challenge is that public and communal latrines do not have complete sewerage system so they are polluting the water drainage system. Second, we don’t have vacuum truck at kebele level. The existing number of trucks in the town do not match with the number of kebeles in the town. We have to wait for one or two weeks to get the service for our turn. Some public latrines overflow and pollute the environment before our turn to receive vacuum truck. This is because public service institutions are not growing in parallel with the growing population of the town. 
Secondly, there is no liquid waste dumping landfill available in the town. It has been around two years since the construction of the liquid waste dump site has started. Even this year nothing has been done regarding that. It is our big challenge. The growing population needs more services and liquid waste dump site. Assessments have been conducted but due to lack of financing we couldn’t continue. The selected location is rocky area which requires huge investment due to this the project is terminated. As a result, people are dumping waste on farmlands and this is creating more discontents from the neighboring rural family.  
Thirdly, when the town was established, it was not equipped with better sewerage system. Currently, some people are using water drainage pipes which were originally built for discharging rain water in the town to discharge liquid waste. 
Interviewer: When you say sewerage system do you mean it is sub-standard or there is no any sewerage system at all?
Respondent: There is no sewerage system in Debrebirhan town at all. The ones that are currently available in our town are water drainage system not liquid waste drainage system. Water drainage system and liquid waste drainage system are completely different. Liquid waste drainage system required its own dedicated line until it reaches a waste water treatment plant. However, in some places in our town toilet wastes are inappropriately discharged to the water drainage system. These have been huge issues that require leadership commitment and financing. If we don’t act timely there will be replicable and serious challenges in the town. 
Besides, the communal latrine and public latrine in our town are not properly handled by the users. This problem can be easily addressed. This is our responsibility. We can make sure kebeles that manage these latrine services handle them appropriately. The existing communal latrines and public latrines are not providing the services that are expected of them because their doors have been broken; people are defecating outside the latrines.
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Excellent, let’s proceed to the solutions. The first problem you have mentioned is lack of vacuum truck. It is true that given the size of Debrebirhan town, the existing number of vacuum trucks is small. What do you think is the solution for this fundamental problem? Who should do what? 
Respondent: The solution is easy. First of all, a strategic plan should be devised regarding this particular issue. The town administration needs a broad-based strategic plan. Once the plan is prepared, necessary funds to procure the vacuum trucks can be raised from the community, partners and government. The main thing is to plan what is required and then raise the finances needed. It is required that we should integrate these three solutions. If two or three vacuum trucks are purchased there would be an improvement from the status of town sanitation we have now. 
Interviewer: What do you mean when you say integrating the three solutions?
Respondent: The first thing is planning according to the population size. Second, the town administration should split the budget that is needed to collect from the community, investors and merchants. Third, partners should be approached on how to play their role regarding this issue. If we integrate all these, we can change the situation within short period of time. These do not take much time. 
Interviewer: Second is the problem of liquid waste disposal site.
Respondent: In this case, I know that a plan that has been formulated and implementation has already been commenced. The problem here is no one is working intensively to raise finances needed to implement the plan. It is rather difficult for the town administration to build liquid waste dumping site by itself. So, it is important to discuss with the regional and federal governments as well as partners that work on this area such as TETRATECH and other stakeholders. The town administration has completed the assessment and developed a plan for implementation. However, given its size and standard, it is beyond the capacity of the administration to build it alone. The problem is lack of budget. So, fundraising is the critical issue that should be addressed primarily. For instance, TETRATECH has been one of our supporters in such areas, so working in coordination with these partners and convince them to cover half of the required budget could be very helpful; because, the rest can be covered from the government. 
Interviewer: There are other partners such as the World Bank, because TETRATECH works in the areas of research and consultancy as well as technical assistance. Was there any effort to involve other partners with better financial capacity?
Respondent: The World Bank had participated. The World Bank’s budget is distributed for road construction, maintenance, drainage construction, and waste transfer stations. When we say partners it doesn’t necessarily mean finance only. We should involve technical assistances and the town administration should invite partners to discuss on this. The administration can discuss with business companies in the town. Therefore, TETRATECH should help us in coordinating such efforts to mobilize resources, as it has the technical capability to do so. The World Bank’s budget allocated for the town is not enough; the total World Bank’s budget for all sectors is not more than 60 million. However, the waste disposal site requires up to 200 million. Yet, the role of the consultant is vital in effective resource mobilization. 
Interviewer: What about the problem regarding sewerage system. You have mentioned that there is no sewerage system. What is the solution for that? 
Respondent: Building a sewerage system on the currently existing infrastructure in the town is a bit difficult; because, the present sewerage system is not built based on an assessment.  However, from now on, a sewerage system should be built for the hotels that are being built in the future. For the buildings to be constructed in the future, if it continues as before, it would be very difficult. Even if we build a sewerage system, it would be useless. Take for example the condominium apartment in our town. it does not have a liquid waste drainage system, and the waste is either linked to the water drainage pipe or simply on the open areas. On top of septic tank, big hotels and condominium apartment should have liquid waste drainage systems reaching the treatment site. If the liquid waste is treated, it would no longer be harmful. However, it is very difficult to use the vacuum truck and many discharge their liquid waste from toilet to the water drainage system during the night. But, if liquid waste is treated it can be used for other purposes such as irrigation. A consultant plays key role here; water technology experts should participate in the planning process of the town to accommodate its growing population. As the town is expanding and growing in the future, continuing the way that we are handling liquid waste now will have a negative effect on the aesthetic quality and biodiversity of the town. 
Interviewer: Lastly, you have mentioned that the public and communal latrines are of poor quality; people have awareness gaps and are defecating near the toilets. What do you think should be done? In my view it is not a simple issue, because if there is no change in awareness, it is difficult to materialize cleanness in the town. 
Respondent: Regarding the public and communal latrines, we do not need that much man power to solve the problem. It does not necessitate large finances too. We have committees already on the ground and what we need to do is to make sure that they are handling the facilities built properly. If vacuum trucks are available the latrines should be emptied immediately when the septic tank is full. If the responsibility is given to the committees and they are aware of the situation, it is easy. We did not manage it strictly, but if we place strict follow up we could at least address the issue without much effort to keep it clean. It is required to create awareness among the users, and to do so, we can train members of the community and change the surroundings of the public and communal latrines. Nonetheless, it is not an easy task. 
Interviewer: What do you call these committees? 
Respondent: They are called “Environmental sanitation and beauty” committee
Interviewer: Are they under your command?  
Respondent: Yes, kebeles are responsible for setting up the committees. 
Interviewer: If these committees are handled properly, you can get many things done. Right?  
Respondent: Yes. If we place a strict management on the committees we can accomplish a lot of things as this does not require huge budget and complex technical assistance. TETRATECH has trained the committees so what is left is to make them functional. That is why I told you that it is not a big deal.  
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: Out of the solutions that I have proposed earlier, what I think would bring speedy result is involving technical and legal consultant in the design of the town. We should waste no time.
Interviewer: Discussing with stakeholder and working together with these stakeholders?
Respondent: Yes. Coordinating stakeholders is the first solution. There is no solution out of this; because if stakeholders do not have coordinated efforts it is not possible to support one another. Secondly, the town administration should integrate the stakeholders, mobilize resources and discuss and work together. That would be the solution for all the problems.  
Interviewer: As you have stated these are funders and consultants. What changes bringing together these stakeholders could lead to?
Respondent: First off, the consultant would provide us with insight or advice; both the leadership and professionals in the town administration would be benefited. They could get knowledge that can be useful not only for the existing town but for the future expansion of it. 
Secondly, the proposal that could be developed and resources we could mobilize with these consultants would be helpful for future works. That is why these two solutions are vital. 
It has two advantages. For starters, workers who lack technical skill will improve their level of competency, be it in administrative or technical stuff. Secondly, if funds are secured it would create job opportunities; liquid waste will be disposed appropriately. It would also lay a foundation for the ever expanding town. Now we do not have a foundation; but as it is also related with awareness, if we do the underground work, it would be simple to go forward as fast as we want.
