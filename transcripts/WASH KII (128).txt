
1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the main water utility problems that you observe? 
Respondent: The water wells are not getting cleaned when in fact they should have been flushed every now and then. Most of the new wells are also powered by electric power only. It cannot work with fuel which is posing problems on us. 
The other problem is lack of adequate water reservoir. There is only a reservoir that with a capacity of 82,000 liter. 
Interviewer: How many water reservoirs are there in the town?
Respondent: There are three water reservoirs in the town.
Interviewer: Are three of them functional?
Respondent: Water cannot enter into the one reservoir. The other two reservoirs are currently functioning. However, the two reservoirs are not sufficient to serve the residents of the town. In areas where there is no water reservoirs, water is being served directly from the source.
Interviewer: What other problems are there?
Respondent: Many of the water pipelines in the town are too old. The lines were installed many years ago and they have never been renovated ever since. There are some water schemes that are not currently functional because of lack of electric power. The electric utility office is not willing to provide us with power because we have unsettled payment with the office. 
Interviewer: Why don’t you make the payments?
Respondent: The higher officials may know reason whys the payment has not been settled yet. The water board and the town administration has done nothing to solve the issue may be due to budget shortage. 
 Interviewer: Is there any problem that you didn’t mention?
Respondent: Lack of generators and solar panels is also a big problem. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have said that there are only two water wells and two reservoirs. What would be the fundamental solution for this?
Respondent: Additional reservoir should be built. Besides, the electric utility office and water utility office should agree on how to solve the issue of unsettled payments. Besides, if there were generators the water schemes would serve the community even if there is no electric supply. The generators of the two water wells are not functioning. 
Interviewer: So, generators should be bought right? Who should purchase these generators? Whose responsibility is it?
Respondent: The board, regional water, and energy bureau and woreda water utility office are the responsible ones. The woreda board should take the initiative to buy generators or mobilize resources from different stakeholders. 
Interviewer: You have said that the water pipes are too old. What is the solution for this?
Respondent: The old water pipes should be replaced by the woreda water board. If budget is an issue, the board should be able to find additional resources from donors and other stakeholders. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: In your opinion what would be the solution that should get priority and would be helpful in solving many other problems? 
Respondent: The old water pipelines should be replaced with a new one. If the old pipe is changed, there will be much less wastage along the way to the reservoir. This in turn will help to fill the reservoir in a shorter period of time and the residents will get water for longer hours than they are now. Besides, other neighborhoods that are not currently getting water because the reservoir is not getting full will have the chance to access clean water from the reservoir. Further, there should be other options of power sources, such as generators, other than the electric line.
