1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: the first challenge is finance, which affects more about access. Even if there is a need of sanitation in our area, we have financial scarcity and shortage of budget to build more communal and standardized toilet. The second thing is management problem. There is lack of strong management to check the sustainability and effectiveness of these latrine centers which already are built. There is no one who responsibly administers and follows if the latrines are 100% efficient to the users. The third one is lack of damping site. Even if we got a damping site land last year, it is still suspended because of compensation issues.
Interviewer:  In the management problem, can you tell me the public and communal latrine in separate way? Looking at the city administration in general, where do you think is the problem raised from? Is it more about commitment or coordination problem?
Respondent: regarding management there is no one who takes responsibility to follow the neatness of the communal latrine and to collect money from users and to pay monthly wage for the cleaners. The latrine could have enough income to cover its cleaners wage cost by collecting coin from users if there were a strong management system from government. There is also a problem in coordinating government stakeholders in order to bring a tangible change.
2What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Interviewer: You have raised some major challenges earlier, now let us discuss about the solutions to these problems. Let’s start from the finance problem. How do you think can this be solved?
Respondent: the main thing is additional budget should be allocated from the city administration. The second solution is to give more attention to sanitary problems like how it is given to health and education then search for additional aid from different NGOs. The city administration should improve awareness on the necessity of sanitation and to give attention and allocate adequate budget for it. If it doesn’t have enough budget, it should collect funds from the community and federal. There should be inclusive budget allocation plan for latrine to be built in some distances when different infrastructures like road and health centers are made. To improve management problems in general, trainings should be given to managers in improving awareness about the necessity of latrine, then outsourcing sanitary latrine, expand management systems to innovate different jobs like recreational areas around the communal latrine widely and make them financially independent, give them a mandate to check the quality and to cover all about management issues.
Interviewer:  what do you think should be done to solve and assure the sustainability of the damping site problem?
Respondent: the main challenges are in the damping site is sanitary design problem and less awareness on the community about the odder brought by wind. To solve such problem, the design should have an air flash and absorbent tube then give enough awareness about the advantage of using and having it beyond the compensation they get from their farming land. This will be successful through time by showing them clean and green areas and having it a good fence. We should stop rash works rather make it up to end and change the mind of the community.
3Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: every solution starts from awareness. The community should have awareness, and that is the main thing. They pretty know that waste will be disposed. We can see COVID as an example that people wash their hands to prevent it, because they got enough awareness that washing hands prevents them from different diseases. This shows us that giving them enough awareness can change and solves everything. Even if we have enough budgets, without enough awareness there will be no sustainable solutions.
