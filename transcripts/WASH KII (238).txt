1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: The main question raised from our community is damping site problem during summer season when it is needed the most. The municipality should assign specific location for this purpose, but because of lack of budget it has not been solved for many years. In the mean time, the city administration has allocated budget for compensation payments for the land owners and try to use it for damping site. Even though, the community living around is not accepting the idea because the city administration has lack of budget to make the location standardized and ready for use.
Interviewer:  what do you think is the reason for the budget scarcity?
Respondent: As it is known that having planed residential area with full of those facilities needs huge budget. But the allocated budget from the government can’t cover such expenses beyond wage salary of workers. The municipality bureau has limited income compared to the area of the city, infrastructures including ambulance service and demand of the people in the city. That is why it doesn’t give attention to build communal latrine. When we see other kebeles, there are communal latrines, even if they need vacuum truck every now and then. In this kebele, the people have their own toilets. These people have no dedicated damping site at all. Woliso town water and sanitation office has the vacuum truck and they are ready to serve, but they don’t have specific damping site to use. Educated adults don’t recommend and allow them to use the farmers land as a damping site, because they do worry more about its side effect than its advantage to the soil.
2What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Respondent: the city administration should give focus to this problem and try to solve it permanently. We have programs and funds with World Bank to solve such problems, and I think the city administration have it as a plan. But priority should be given to this problem, let people participate in it to solve the problem by themselves since they feel the pain more.
In order to solve the budget problem, 30 of us have dispatched around the community and try to collect money from the people. So, the people (4 kebeles ) are planning to address financial problems by themselves.
The other problem was about lack of access to vacuum truck and damping site. So, we should start to apply the project so fast. And we should find a solution for the damping site place, once we get a vacuum truck. It was used one farmer’s eucalyptus tree as a damping site until he said it has side effect and tell us to stop it. So, we should discuss with different stakeholders and solve it.
3Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: in the last, I can say that we should find different organizations to work with us and solve the problems of our people like how we were doing it before. We should work with governmental and nongovernmental organizations, take trainings and at least to find temporary solutions together. We should discuss with zone, woreda and city administration and with the people about getting temporary solution and this will answer the peoples concern. The community will use toilets other than open defecation. And this will protect them from different diseases. We can see that other associations have their own finance and saving account. They can cover the vacuum truck expense by themselves, but still there is a damping site problem. And this forces people to practice open defecation. This problem is taking them back to the open defecation time and to the time when there was no education and awareness. So, every stakeholder should involve in it and solve the problem together.
