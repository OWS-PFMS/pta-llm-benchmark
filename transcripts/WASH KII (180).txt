
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation / Water service sustainability in your woreda?  Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation / water service sustainability?

I don’t think that there is shortage of underground water in south Ari wereda in general. The main issue is awareness problem related to properly use the services. Since south Ari is sloppy in its landscape, there is difficulty of installing pipelines in all kebeles. There are situations where the people construct their own wells in some kebeles. South Ari wereda water and sanitation office do construction of water schemes in collaboration with WASH project offices, too. The community lacks the culture of using the water infrastructures properly. 

With regard to sanitation and hygiene, we notice that the society is a bit far from the proper ways of practicing sanitation and personal hegiene. This is becoming a major cause for the prevalence of water borne diseases. 

 There has been continuous program to improve the level of awareness everywhere. In fact, one of the problems we noticed in this regard is that other sectors have left this issue to the water and Sanitation office. This is creating a burden to the office as the mission involves integrated activities from all sectors, including our office.    

We are not doing awareness building activity on sanitation and water borne-diseases. Our office is mainly focusing on making children’s rights in such a way that they need to learn on how to protect themselves from abuses. We didn’t go far in teaching the community about sanitation and hygiene problems and that is one of the major gaps we need to improve. This is because women and children are directly connected to the issues we are discussing now. It’s actually our commitment problem. 

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? For instance, what do you think is the solution for awareness problem or gap? 

Water and sanitation office is more responsible for this and health office should be seconded. We are also closely working with them though it is not as expected. Support is needed from each of the other sectors to create awareness on sanitation and hygiene and to develop boreholes. Since the woreda is dominated highland and semi-highland areas, it wouldn’t be as such difficult to develop a number of boreholes as much as we need if we can work together.  
Due to lack of coordination, we can see that some boreholes are left without use. For example, at Sida area, 8 Km away from here, there has been a water point drilled by the water and sanitation office, but it is not well developed. The water is not pure since it is not scientifically treated. This is one of the areas that need our coordination. Thus, we can say that our problem with regard to water is not shortage of the underground water but the techniques of accessing and making readily available for use. And this needs a joint action. It shouldn’t be left to water office only. 

Interviewer: what is the gap you observed in terms of coordination? 

There is a WASH stream committee that is mainly led by the water and sanitation office. If the stream committee doesn’t conduct evaluation every quarter of the year about the contribution of each member, there will be inefficiency. When the committee arranges a meeting regarding WASH, members are not required to present their accomplishments. Performance evaluation systems are not agendas for this meeting, as to my knowledge.  As a principle, it is stated that each of the member offices need to have their own plans and performance reports that would be presented in such meetings but in reality that doesn’t work. 

The major role of implementing all these duties is at the hands of water and sanitation office. That is the main cause for the failure of the water scheme projects in this woreda. Integrated work is vital in order to address problems related water and sanitation. 

3.Of the solutions you listed, which one is the most important?  Can you walk me through what next steps will happen if the solution occurs?

Working together is the best solution.  There are seven sector offices in the WASH committee, including agriculture and natural resource office, Livestock and Fishery office other than the main sectors that I mentioned earlier. WASH problems are diverse and many and they should be solved with the commitment of many sectors and different experts.
