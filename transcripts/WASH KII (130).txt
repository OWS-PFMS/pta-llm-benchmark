1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your town/woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the problems in the water service provision?
Respondent: The government set this enterprise up to create employment and improve the water service. One of the problems related to water service in this woreda is its distribution is not equal. In some localities we only get water for a few minutes a day.
Interviewer: Why is that? Whose problem is it?
Respondent: It is the problem of all the management, experts, and sometimes it is because of simply unfair distribution of the available water. 
Interviewer: What are the problems in the rural area?
Respondent: I don’t have much thought about that.
Interviewer: What is the purpose of your enterprise?
Respondent: The government set this enterprise up to create employment and improve the water service by providing spare parts. There are spare parts supply problem
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: For the problem you mentioned regarding unfair water distribution what would be the solution? 
Respondent: The concerned bodies should set up a monitoring system. It is mostly management issue not lack of water. Therefore, local officials should work on the supervision. Besides, the water utility office can distribute water with the existing capacity by employing shift-wise approach. Besides, few people cut water pipes off because water is not coming to their neighborhood which is very sad. Consequently, significant amount of water is being wasted due to the broken pipeline. This should be monitored properly. 

Interviewer: What should be done to solve the issue of spare parts?
Respondent: We are trying to promote what products and services we provide. And we are working 24/7 to serve the community in terms of providing spare parts and maintenance services. 
Interviewer: Does the government support your efforts?
Respondent: The regional government has supported us when we were established. However, nowadays, the woreda doesn’t create job opportunity for us. Instead many water works are being built by other by contractors not our enterprise.  
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Management issues need to solved at the first place. I say this because there are lots of things to be solved at the management level since this is the main decision making organ. Things that need to be solved includes narrowing the gap among the different stakeholders and working together, fund raising and mobilizing the community and other private stakeholders. If this is solved, then we can say that half of the problems can be addressed
