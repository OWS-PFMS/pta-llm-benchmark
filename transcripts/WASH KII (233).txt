
1) Interviewer: Okay, in your opinion, what are the challenges of providing sustainable water service in this town? Please raise your volume.
Interviewee: Well though we have a shortage on water service, I can say we are doing fine especially this year 2009 (2016-2017) we have been experiencing shortage all over the town.
Interviewer: Why is the shortage happening? 
Interviewee: I think the proportion of the water holes we have in regards to the number of people we have is not balanced. So we have to work on balancing that proportion by expanding our resources. In order to expand our resources, we need to have more budgets. And the budget cannot be covered by the town, so we need the federal government's support on that. Once we are financed, we can balance the proportion and have a sustainable water service in the town.
Interviewer: Okay, do you have more challenges to add, I mean in related to distribution and management?
Interviewee: I think the problem I see is the proportion only. The distribution and management are doing fine.

2) Interviewer: So how do we solve this challenge you mentioned?
Interviewee: We can only solve it by adding more water holes. This can be done when the budget increases. I said earlier, the town's budget is not enough to implement this. So we need the federal government support. So if we get that support, we can add more water holes and can have sustainable water service all over the town.

3) Interviewer: Let's say we implement the solution you have suggested right now. What kind of changes should we expect in the town?
Interviewee: We will see many changes. The town is surrounded by water resources. So if we start using them all, we can attract investment to our town and most unemployed residents of our town can get jobs. So it will boost the economy.
Interviewer: What about in related to health and social life of the people?
Interviewee: I tell you we will see many changes. We have water-borne diseases all over the town. We can prevent such things from happening. We can also make our town very clean if we have adequate water service running.

4) Interviewer: Okay, my next question is about coordination between agencies which are working in regards to water service. How effective relationship do they have? Do they have coordination on working together?
Interviewee: [05:00] Actually, I see only the Water Authority and the City Administration working on this matter. Now we have added sectors and if they work together I think they can bring change to the town.
Interviewer: But on a current situation, is this coordination there between the sectors you have mentioned?
Interviewee: I don't see much. 
Interviewer: What are the reasons for this no coordination issue?
Interviewee: The problem is the budget. We have very little budget and creating a task force together needs the additional budget. If we increase our budget maybe we can see viable coordination between the sectors. 
Interviewer: So what needs to be improved in related to this coordination matter? 
Interviewee: I think to start with the Mayor's office, the city administration and all heads of the sectors should be joined and must have a taskforce to tackle the water service challenges one by one. If we have this kind of taskforce we can also involve the people and can do many productive things.
Interviewer: But this kind of relationship is not there right now.
Interviewee: Yes, it's not there.

5) Interviewer: Okay my last question is, you have mentioned a challenge that has an impact on running sustainable water service. If we create such coordination between those sectors you have mentioned, do you think it will help solve the challenge you mentioned?
Interviewee: Yes it will help.
Interviewer: Please explain it to me. 
Interviewee: You know you cannot clap using only one hand. These sectors, they can succeed only if they work together. The water authority, the city administration and other sectors, if they have coordination, they can plan their budget and also plan projects together and can deliver their service efficiently to the people. Currently, only the Water Authority and City administration are trying to connect. I don't see other sectors joining. 
Interviewer: So if all sectors join, the service can improve...
Interviewee: Yes it will improve.
Interviewer: Okay, that'll be all for now. Thanks!
