1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: What are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town?
Respondent: We have traditional methods of waste management in this town. It is not uncommon in the town to dump solid waste and liquid waste without separating one from the other. Secondly, the community’s awareness to recycle and reuse waste is poor. Enough awareness has not been created among the community members to consider waste as a treasure if properly handled. Therefore, waste is being disposed to everywhere in an open space.  
Another problem is that there is no a scheduled program for cleaning campaigns. The system is just sporadic and non -inclusive.
Interviewer: What do you mean by non -inclusive.
Respondent: The focus of sanitation is mainly in some specific areas and main streets. The other problem is lack of well-designed location for waste disposal site based on the master plan of the town. On the other hand, liquid waste from public latrines release their discharges to the nearby rivers and drainage systems which are flowing all their way around the town and nearby rural areas creating bad smell and other hazards
The town’s sanitation and beautification office does not conduct environmental impact assessment before constructing public or communal latrines. The office should have conducted impact assessment and select locations based on the results of the assessments. 
Another issue is that people just throw it to the streets in a plastic bag rather than disposing garbage in systematic way. Then, the garbage thrown to the streets contaminate the whole town when it moves around through wind and flood during the rainy season. 
In general, due to lack of awareness, people dump waste outside the allocated dumping site in times when they cannot be seen by others/usually during nights. 
The inputs necessary to collect and dump waste such as trucks and dumping sites are not readily available. Thus, people dispose wastes either in their private latrines if available or in an open space 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What recommendations do you have about solutions to the problems you have mentioned?
Respondent: There should be modern and sustainable waste management system. Solid and liquid wastes should be disposed separately. This is in principle the responsibility of the town administration since it is clearly indicated in the town’s strategic plan. Greenery areas should not be waste collection sites. 
We should also have to develop recycling practices such as composing decaying organic wastes.
Our society should develop the attitude to consider waste as a source of wealth. This can be brought when we practice a massive public awareness programs 
We should work strongly to identify problems with public latrines, communal latrines, and even latrines in individual households that release their wastes to the nearby rivers and open space. Then, measures should be taken to force them use their own septic tank and pump it when it is full. If it is dumped into central treatment plan, it can be used as compost. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The most important solution is collecting waste as per the schedule using modern waste collection system and dumping site. Then is should be disposed to the dumping sites on regular basis. Experts and university staff should participate in a research work and investigate menses to turn the waste into income source. 
Interviewer: What would happen if that is implemented?
Respondent: The town waste could have an economic advantage, including attracting many unemployed youths. The community will be able to generate income out of collected waste
Interviewer: How come?
Respondent: For instance, it could be sold to the farmers as a compost fertilizer. Secondly, it can create job opportunities even to the rural people. Local communities can set up enterprises to collect and transport waste. Besides, if our waste management systems are supported by experts we will have sustainable ways of waste handling practices and awareness.
