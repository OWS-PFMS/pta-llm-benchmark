1) Interviewer፡ Thank you. My first question is that, in your opinion here in Woliso town what are main challenges in related to water service sustainability?  
Response: This is a good question. We all know that the population of this town is increasing from time to time. And the storage of water we have in the town, I mean the dam, is not compatible with this growth of population. 
Interviewer: You mean the population growth is not compatible with the water storage?
Response: Yes, it's not compatible. The main problem is this one. The second challenge is that we have a shortage of Water & Drainage Authority staffs. This is also another cause of the problem. So unless we might have an expansion in these areas in the future, we will have big issues in related to water services. We are not providing water coverage in all areas of our town. Many parts of our town have water service shortages.
Interviewer: So you have raised two main issues. The first one is that the water service in the town is very small, which is not compatible with the population growth. The second one is that the number of Water & Drainage Authority staffs is not enough for the town. Do you want to add more challenges?
Response: The other issue is that the main water pipelines are mostly located very close to the road which makes them vulnerable to be broken by cars which are passing by the road.
Interviewer: Oh you are saying the pipelines are broken by cars?
Response: Yes, they often break.
Interviewer: Do you want to add more challenge?
Response: Well these are the main Challenges which we are facing in our town.

2) Interviewer: So what are your recommendations on solving those challenges you have mentioned now?
Response: My solution to those challenges will be like the storage issue can be solved by expanding the dam so that it becomes compatible with the population growth of the town. And the water pipelines alongside the main road should be kept away from the car traffic so that we won't have problems there. The Water & Drainage Authority should add more staffs so that its service can reach to all part of our town.
Interviewer: What about increasing the water services? What should be done about it?
Response: Well we can mobilize the residents of the town and also involve the government to work on the expansion of the dam so that it can store more water...
Interviewer: Are you talking about the water holes?
Response: Yes, we can also dig water holes in some areas and they can support the river. We have some of these already dug up, yet they are not enough. Like for example when it's Summer, we experience a high shortage of water. So this means that they are not enough and we should mobilize the residents and involve the government to improve it.
Interviewer: What about those pipelines you said that they usually break? How do we solve that challenge in a durable way?
Response: The durable solution for this challenge is that we must change the design to install those lines and they should be made up of strong material and also put very far away from the main road. [05:00] They should not interfere with the road whereby cars pass every day. The road is becoming congested even for public use. So the lines should be very far from the road.

3) Interviewer: You have mentioned three problems. The first one is the there is a shortage of water service. The second one is the shortage of staffs from the Water & Drainage Authority. The third one is about the pipelines that are breaking due to car accidents. So out of these three challenges, which one do you think should be prioritized? 
Response: Well I would prioritize the pipelines close to the main road, as the others need long-term commitment and budget. But the pipelines that are breaking due to car accidents can be fixed right now because new pipelines that are fixed alongside the main road are breaking continuously. Say for example we are now building asphalt roads connecting our town with Ambo town. The pipelines alongside that road are fixed very close to the road like measured in inches or millimeters. We are having broken pipelines on that road more often. So the pipelines should be fixed far from the road so that cars will not have access to it even if they have accidents. I also want to emphasize about the manpower issue. The Water Authority should increase their staffs.
Interviewer: Okay, so let say those solutions you mentioned now are implemented. What kind of changes do you expect? What kind of benefits will the town get?
Response: I would say that there will be no water shortage and the residents of our town will be satisfied. You know water is the very important factor on our people's day to day life. If there is no water many sectors will be affected. So we have to solve these issues quickly.
Interviewer: So you are saying if these solutions you mentioned will be implemented, the water shortage problem will be solved and the residents will get adequate and sustainable water service, right?
Response: Yes.
4) Interviewer: Okay, my next question is about coordination. How effective are these sectors in this town working regarding water services when it comes to coordinating? In your opinion are they working together effectively to solve the water shortage issue? Do they have good work relationship?
Response: Well I think every sector in our town should be concerned about this water shortage issue but all I see is that it's left to the Water Authority Department only. In my opinion, all sectors should come together to work on the water shortage issue and look for a durable solution. If we leave the issue to Water Authority only, we might not get any solution in a near future. Every sector should work together to solve this water service problem.
Interviewer: So you are saying that there is no coordination between sectors in regards to this water service issue?
Response: I don't see much effort on the other sectors regarding coordination. [10:00] All I see is Water Authority say for example when pipelines break tries to fix it. I don't see the other sectors trying to do anything. I think they say it's not our problem and that's wrong.
Interviewer: So other sectors are not coordinating with Water Authority.
Response: Yes they are not coordinating.
Interviewer: To finalize this part of my question, what is your suggestion to improve this weak coordination between the sectors working in your town?            
Response: I think it's a matter of creating awareness in those other sectors. We must create awareness to these sectors so that they know that water service issue is everyone's concern including them. If we do that they will start to respond in accordance to create that lost connection between them to come together and work together. Water service is a very important issue to our community and all sectors should work together to address it properly.
Interviewer: You are saying by creating awareness we must increase the participation of all sectors in related to water services.
Response: Yes.    

5) Response: Okay, my last question will be, you have said that the water service issue should be every sector's concern and they should coordinate well and not leave it to one office to handle it, right?

Response: Yes, it helps a lot.

Response: Well I have already mentioned the main important matters. What I forgot to mention is the drainage issue. You know winter is here and our town is near to a river.
