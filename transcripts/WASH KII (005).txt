1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation/water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
1.1 I personally assume that the main problem of shortage of water is incompatability between the demand and supply. The total population of the town and the current supply of water is completely incompatible.   
1.2 The second problem is concerning sanitation issues. This town doesn’t have solid waste and liquid waste disposal system, there is no place prepared to dispose the waste which I hope has been the main challenge regarding sanitation problem of the town. There is no vehicle as well as solid waste disposal container to be used  to transport the waste. The community memmbers could have kept their environment clean if they have got containers in their nearby area to collect the waste and make available for disposal. With the exception of the work being conducted by the small and micro enterprise office which is collecting the waste through home to home visit every week, there is no permanently reserved place to dispose it. 
We see that every household members dispose out the waste unwisely, it is very common to see waste materials and food leftovers every where. The community members would dispose the waste properly if they get appropriate collections places.  
I think area identification for liquid as well as solid waste disposal is already made by experts and offcials, they are on the way to pay compensation for the land. Last time, we were discussing about that in a meeting and they didn’t complete the process yet. As long as the land is still owned by the farmers, and the compensation is not paid yet, we can not say that the place is ours. 


  The community has been highly affected by the sanitation problem for long and they are now being affected, I know that the community members dispose the waste unwisely and they are doing that because they don’t have any options. We can not blam the community. For me, the town municipality should take the responsibility for this problem, they have to develop good waste disposal management and awaress systems so that the community can apply. Preparing container for every villages, and awareness creation for the community  should be made so that the town will be clean enough. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
2.1 The first solution could be increasing water supply to meet the demands of the current population. The current water supply should be made to absorbe the needs of population of the town. Research must be made about the population, available water resources and possible alternative sources of water. When new alternative source is planned, it has to be prepared in a carfeul study and sustainable manner. The water service assumed to be improved should consider the needs of the future generation too. Therefore, the water and sanitation service office by being together with the Mayor have the primary role to improve the supply of water through applying different alternative sources like deep well and others 
2.2 Regarding the financial resources, I think those two offices in particular can initiate a  discussion platform with the community and other stakholders so that additional support can be easily obtained. Hopefully,  the community will be willing to support with their labor and finance if adequate awareness creation platforms are conducted. 
2.3 Regarding personal hygine, I think it is fully the responsibility of individuals themselves. But community’s sanitation problem can be improved through continuous awareness creation program. The center of the awareness creation program rely on changing the attitude of the community to keep their environment clean. In fact we are working on it but it still needs more assignments and commitments. 
The community is ready to change their environment clean but the problem is associated with lack of appropriate waste disposal system. They would have placed all the waste properly if they have got proper places including containers. 
Instead of that what is being conducted now is that they collect the waste and let the human porters to dispose it everywhere, be it infront of other’s hose or in the road, including doing this at night so that they would be saved from no one’s complain.  The porters only care about their money and it is not their business whether they damp it wherever they need.
