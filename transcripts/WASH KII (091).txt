1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in Debreberhan? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Budget shortage: - shortage of budget is restricting the city from building infrastructures necessary for water service sustainability.
Urbanization and increasing population:- the pressure exerted by this phenomena is becoming a challenge for water and hygiene quality .
Outdated infrastructure incapable of entertaining the current demand.
Absence of skilled human resource capable of handling sustainable waters service in the city.
Absence of sewage treatment plants in hotels: most hotels do not have any treatment plant so they simply realize their wastes to the environment which has created a problem on water service sustainability.
Awareness problem on the community on personal and community hygiene.
Water shortage is one reason for water and hygiene service sustainability in the town.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Here are possible solutions that can be implemented to bring change: _
Creating Awareness: - creating awareness on personal and community hygiene can be one solution in addressing the problem.
Preparing landfills: - absence of landfill site is the most significant problem; so this should be solved while attempting to solve the problem substantially.
Resolving water shortage problem: addressing the quantity and quality problem related with water is one way to solve water service sustainability.
Identifying wastes as recyclable and non-recyclable so as to treat them accordingly.
Arranging dustbins at household level.
Enacting a legal frame work on sewage treatment plant: - this frame work should make sewage treatment plant as an obligatory facility for hotels and industries.
Allocating sufficient budget for tasks associated with water and sanitation purpose.
Training sufficient skilled manpower working on water, sanitation and hygiene.
Protecting and maintaining the environment, rivers and other potential pollution destinations.
Working on household level waste management.
Working on awareness building program in the the community, building infrastructure and encouraging participation of stakeholders can be taken as a solution package for attaining water service sustainability.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
The first thing should be preparing and building proper landfill site in order to store wastes and manage them according to their types. This landfill scheme should be scientifically used in order to give solution for the problem in the long run. Solving these problems will decrease environmental pollutions, and addressing the issue of water problem should also be dealt along with various stakeholders by building water scheme infrastructure and using waste dumping trucks that can give affordable service fee.
