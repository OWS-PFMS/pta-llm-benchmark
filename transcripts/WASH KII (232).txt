1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Water supply and demand imbalance
There is a serious shortage of water supply in Woliso town consequently the town water and sewerage utility office is forced to introduce water distribution shift system. That is some parts of the town get water service and others will not have access for water in a given day. We have discussed with the water office and learned that they are working to solve the problem in the short run. On the other hand, the existing water supply lines are very old and needs a complete replacement. 
Some areas that are not included in the town’s master plan there is no water supply infrastructure. This is another challenge on the water service of the town as the demand for water in these areas is too high.    
II.Absence of waste disposal sites
There is no waste disposal place in Woliso town and there is no waste collection and transportation mechanism for solid wastes. However, large amount of waste is produced daily in the town. In contrast, the town administration is not committed to develop appropriate waste disposal place in the town. The weak awareness of the community on waste management and disposal is another challenge for the town sanitation.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
I.To alleviate the problem related to water service, I would like to suggest that the town administration in collaboration with other stakeholders should work to replace the old water lines by new ones. Moreover, it should develop additional water sources to meet the needs of the community.  
Those illegal houses constructed outside the master plan of the town requires water service. Thus, the town administration should include them in the master plan and provide water service for the residents as water is life for all creatures.

II.In order to solve the problem related to waste disposal site the town administration should discuss and work closely with relevant stakeholders and take immediate action as sanitation service is a key issue that require considerable attention. 

We need also to take lesson from other towns that have good experience on waste disposal and management. Having appropriate waste disposal place is vital for growth and development of any town, thus, the town administration and other concerned government and non-governmental bodies should give a prime emphasis to solve the problem of waste disposal site by any means. In addition, continuous awareness enhancement campaign should be implemented to improve the waste disposal and management practice of the community.  

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
All the problems and solutions I raised earlier are very important, however, the problem related to the waste disposal site is a burning issue. The community produce waste daily and dispose it haphazardly in the town. Consequently, various forms of outbreaks that affect community health occurs frequently.  The absence of appropriate waste disposal place is also encourage open defecation, as the community could not get latrine/pit emptying service. Hence, preparing appropriate waste disposal site is the first priority as it is the basis to solve other sanitation related problems of Woliso town. 
If we have access for appropriate waste disposal place both for liquid and solid wastes, it will have the following positive effects on the society: 
The community health condition will improve
We can attract new investments to the town
We can mobilize the community groups that can engage in waste collection and disposal business.
