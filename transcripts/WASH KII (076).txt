
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer:
Response: In Debrebirhan area liquid wastes don’t have area of disposal; the waste is full because it is not much. The Municipal also says this, it is difficult, the city don’t have place for solid and liquid waste. Now we that work here are begging to dispose the liquid waste and the request is much we can’t keep up for we can’t dispose what is in out tank. By working for households/private what we get paid is 700 or 600 birr, previously it was 350 birr and we had to pay for disposal 0.50 cents or 0.25 cents then it became 7 birr. It is Water Minister we have two liquid sucker trucks; one is from the institution and the other from Dashen Bank donation. Now our tank is half way full, we have to make a deal and pay with the disposal approval giver before we start our work and if is full and closed it is closed as I have said earlier, the district urination at night or day, dumping waste at our gate we clear and collect as new but we haven’t benefited anything and the city’s sanitation I don’t know; first collecting the waste from here and taking from every street banana skin, chewed sugarcane and others; there is none that is not there specially if it’s holiday the slaughter byproducts will be in sewerage system.
Interviewer: So, there is no proper waste disposal practice?
There is none, because they don’t have place to take and place which the current wastes are disposed is out of the city and those people who live around the disposal area are complaining, it is more unhealthy.
Interviewer: The absent of disposal place is disturbing the farmers?
Yes, hole was being said but, that will fill quickly after being excavated and in the summer season they take the waste. People’s life is expanding for example if we see in this area, it is bringing more difficulties; the city doesn’t have a land to assign as disposal area; in the vehicles area for collecting liquid waste, the number of people is growing in Debrebirhan city so, even additional trucks for liquid waste disposal can be brought it won’t be enough. There are limited liquid waste trucks; one is Mr Akedom’s, two trucks of Water Minister and another two hospital has its own so, these are the trucks available in numbers; one privately owned totally five trucks are there and if more is added it is not enough. They have made it expensive for us; we get one birr but we have to pay for sucking, water, electricity and for detergent to get rid of the stink are the expenses, we have problem in what we earn.
