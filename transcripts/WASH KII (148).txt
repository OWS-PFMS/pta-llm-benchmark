1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the region?
Repeated occurrence of drought in the region.
Only very few organizations work in the water sector in the region.
Water schemes mostly constructed without sufficient study about the ground water potential of the area. Because of these water schemes dry within short period of time.
There is budget constraint to construct sufficient number of water schemes in the region.
Most water schemes are not constructed based on WASH principles. Because of this there is water sanitation problem in the region. This sanitation problem predispose the community for various health problems.
Finally, there is no regular coordination between stakeholders working in the water sector. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
There should be comprehensive water study that consider the settlement of the community and geographical location of the area to improve the water service of the area. We should also investigate the areas that require construction of new water schemes.
Every stakeholder should follow the principle of WASH in their activities to develop water service.
We should also enhance the awareness of the community related to proper use of water schemes.
We should also build the capacity of water technicians functioning at different level in the region.
Coordination is very essential to ensure sustainable water service in the region, therefore, there should be strong coordination between governmental and non-governmental organizations functioning at different level in the region.
There should be strong monitoring and evaluation of the implementation of water projects in the region. Once the water schemes becomes functional (start to provide water supply), there should be regular supervision of the operation of the schemes. And there should be regular maintenance of water schemes. Moreover, the community should be trained on proper utilization of the schemes.
When there is occurrence of drought in the region, immediate response should be given to the community. We should also form a permanent taskforce that respond to the community question in cases of emergency. When there is emergency, immediate temporary solution should be given for the community.
There should be regular assessment to understand the status of the existing water schemes.
We should also understand the community’s culture and indigenous knowledge, and used as an input for solutions to problems.  
We should also establish early warning system for problems related to water service in the area.   
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
We should conduct water studies based on the needs of the community and construct new water schemes accordingly.
We should create strong coordination between stakeholders working in the water sector and establish monitoring and evaluation system in the sector.
We should also form a taskforce that give immediate solution for emergency water problems.
We should also work on capacity building of the water technicians functioning in the region.
Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
If these suggested solutions will be implemented in the near future, the Afar community will settle in one place and will not migrate from place to place in search of water. Moreover, the health of the rural community will be improved and school enrolment will increase in the region. We will have educated society. We will also reduce poverty in the region. Therefore, working to ensure sustainable water service is very essential. Sustainable water service leads to more productive livestock and agricultural production in the region. This in turn improve the living standard of the community.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your region for sustainable water service?  What could be improved related to the existing coordination? 
To ensure sustainable water service both for human and livestock, the existing coordination between stakeholders is weak. But there is good coordination in cases of emergency (i.e. when there is drought in the region). However, there is no regular coordination between stakeholders during normal times. 
To improve the existing coordination, we should first identify stakeholders in the water sector and we should also know the resources available at the hand of different stakeholders. Moreover, we should understand what can be done by each stakeholders. Once we have information about the available resources and what can be done by each stakeholder, we prepare a kind of memorandum of understanding to work in coordination in the sector. We should also have regular meeting to discuss on the implementation status of projects.

5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the region? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
If we create good coordination in the region, we can efficiently utilize the available resource to ensure sustainable water service in the region.
If there is coordination, we can solve problems on time and provide sustainable service to the community.
Coordination can also avoid duplication of effort in the sector.
Coordination can also improve monitoring and evaluation activities in the sector. As a result we can have information about status of ongoing projects and related problems.
Therefore, improved coordination can lead to more sustainable water service in the region.
