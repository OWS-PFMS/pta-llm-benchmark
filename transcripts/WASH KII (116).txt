1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation/water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Lack of financial resource: 
We get income just by charging people who come and use the public toilet. We have a big water barrel, and we usually fill it with smaller plastic buckets since the pipeline water may not be available regularly. When the latrine gets full, we pay private dump truck owners for discharging the sewage. Once it is empty, we also clean it with water and get it ready for service again. 

Absence of Damping sites: 
There is no nearby place to dump the liquid waste from the latrine. So what the truck owners do is that they transport it away from here and dump somewhere out of the city and on the way to Addis Ababa road. I don’t even know where exactly they dump. I know there is sewage dump site being prepared somewhere out of the town, but it hasn’t started giving service since the farmers did not receive legitimate compensation for the land that was taken from them. Nowadays, the government’s Sewerage dumping service has stopped and their dump trucks are parking. There was also private dumper whose name is Aklil. He has also left since he couldn’t get a sewage dumping site. Currently, there is a private agent whose name is Betru working for us. He charges us 2300 birr just for   the service of their dumpster. 700 birr was an increment he made recently. 

                Interviewer: Why has he made the increment? 
It is only him that is giving this service. He has got many clients and competent, so you can’t negotiate with him regarding payment. Nowadays, the hospital and the prison here in our city are also facing this same problem. 
          How long do you have to wait till it gets full again and how is this affecting sanitation?
It gets full within eight days time. The problem of sanitation is mainly due to shortage of water. When the pipeline water is off, we buy a bucket of water for two birr from some other neighborhoods who get tap-water regularly. Some neighborhoods with the old pipelines get water all the time as they get it from a different reservoir.

 Interviewer: How about problems related to community sanitation? Can you mention them?
With regard to community sanitation in general, there are piles of garbage everywhere. The women who do the cleaning work come and collect the solid waste starting from four o’clock at night till morning, but the vehicle will come so late in the morning, hence the piles of dirt will spoil the area again. Bodies of dead dogs and pets are also thrown to the piles of the garbage and make the area so stinky. 

Poorly constructed drainage system: 

Now the other main cause of the sanitation problem in this area is the fact that the drainage coming from upper area and passing this way spoils our locality and fills our latrine’s holes. It is almost directly connected to our latrine, so the latrine is mainly filled with the sewage being brought here by the drainage. I don’t know where the drainage starting point is, but along its way there is one other latrine which is connected to it. So, that latrine’s sewage is carried to our latrine with this ditch and ends up here. That is our main problem. Even if we have reported it to the water and sewerage office so many times, no one cared about it and gave us feedback. It is affecting us not to use our latrine properly throughout the year. Especially in the fall season, our latrine gets full, almost every week. 

Demand and service incompatibility: 
There was one other latrine over there serving the next neighborhood, but it is not functioning now. So, the people from there come and use here. There are about 200 people who use this latrine here. If the municipality maintains the other toilet in the other neighborhood, the load will get easier here. If it is maintained, some other people would also be organized and get work opportunity just like us. But no one listens to them though they already reported to the municipality many times. Now, many people think that I am the one getting benefits with the income we gain here. But in reality, we spend much of the income for sewage dumping service. It is only 200 birr that we save every month for ourselves. Two of our association members are disabled people. One of them is physically disabled, and the other one is blind and HIV/AIDS positive. And the remaining two people are women working with us. So, we are five totally. We charge 30 birr per month for a household in our neighborhood. We don’t have a big water tanker since it is expensive.

Interviewer: How much do you charge those who come from other areas and use the latrine? How much does the big tanker cost?

 People who come and use this toilet from some other places pay 2 birr at a time. But we don’t charge children, disabled and elderly people. 


The latrine has got only six holes; and we use these several medium size buckets to fill the bigger reservoir. It costs about 3500 birr in the market. We have planned to buy in the future once this fall season gets passed. Some people have problem of using latrine properly. Having used the latrine, some people don’t even pour water to the hole properly. So, we have to wait and check whether water is poured to it or not. We ourselves do the cleaning work if it is not done properly. We buy this chemical known as Dettol to use it as disinfectant and to avoid bad smell. We buy each bottle for 70 birr. We buy six per month and we spend about 420 birr monthly. 

Every month we have to pay for water bill; we also pay about 150 birr for electricity usage that we are hiring privately from a neighboring house. We give this public latrine service starting from 6 a.m. in the morning till 8 p.m. in the evening. We even stay till 9 p.m. when there is greater number of people on holidays. In the dry season, the latrine hasty a bad smell, and people who walk along this way complain that it stinks. We also accept their complaints and we use more water to reduce the smell as much as possible. In the rainy season it is better since the rain washes away some of the waste. 

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

The main solution for our problem is to divert the ditch that brings fluid waste and fills our latrine immediately. I don’t know who is going to solve this problem for us, but that is the main solution we need from any responsible governmental body. 
     Haven't you reported the problem you are having with the ditch to water and sewerage office?
We have reported it to Water and Sewerage office, but no one gave attention to what we said. They told us to report it to the ‘kebele’ or municipality. Going to the municipality is now and then is also hectic, and no one gives us feedbacks. If that is done, we will be able to save the money we are spending for the private sewers every week. 
While me and one of my partners were working in the bus station here in Debreberhan some 35 years back, we used to charge people 10 or 15 cents for the latrine service, but now it is 2 birr because the cost of water and sanitary service is higher. Since there are daily laborers who work around here, there is a demand of bathing service. But we can’t give bathing service since our latrine doesn’t have drainage or ditch where the sewage can easily be disposed of. We would have additional income if we were able to give bathing room.
There should be two to three dumping sites for sewage disposal .There is only one site recently prepared some six kilometers away from here. It hasn’t even officially started giving service. Only some government offices like health office, prison and EBA are dumping their sewage hiding from others. They also allow them to do, but they don’t give regard if we ask them to do. So, the only way we avoid sewage waste is through the private agents paying them money that we collect from this service.
Dashen brewery used to dry, recycle its sewage and sell it to the public so that people used it as cattle forage. I don’t know if they are also doing this nowadays. If this kind of technology is adopted, it will help a lot. Private dump truck owners are there now ready to give service if you pay them, but there is no legal place to dump the solid waste.   

If you see the liquid waste, there is leakage and flood from the drainages along the road to St Gabriel where it ends up in Beressa River. The drainage is directly connected to Beresa River.
Solid waste cleaners do their work properly by collecting the wastes from the neighborhood. But once it is kept somewhere in the middle of our community, it won’t be taken to the dumping site in time. I wish this is properly done by the responsible governmental office. It will help to alleviate the problem.
When we go water and sewerage office, sufficient numbers of workers are there, but the problem is with their administrative body. There is no proper work management. When we complain to them, they will say ‘okay’, but they don’t do it practically. So, I suggest that the management of sewerage office needs to be restructured. The municipality office needs to take care and manages the drainages that leak out and bring sewage all around the residential houses. It should be the duty of the municipality to clean the living neighborhoods. I have written two formal letters and given to kebele 8 Red Cross office and Health office. Though I did this twice, no one cared and acted up on it. If this place is free from the drainage’s spoilage, we can even reconstruct the latrine and give showering service, too. The income that we can get from the bath service can even cover the money we pay the private sewerage dumpers. 

3.Of the solutions you listed, which one is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

Constructing more water schemes: 
Water should be given much more consideration than electricity. Since Debreberhan town has potential underground water, more water schemes should be constructed. There should also be bigger tankers here and there to use them as reservoirs from the existing water scheme. That is the solution I recommend if there is an intention to help us to solve the problem of liquid waste and sanitation problem in the Debreberhan town. 

Regarding solid waste, proper dumping site needs to be prepared closer to the town. We used to burn solid waste in a specific place as a means of avoiding waste in the past. If that practice doesn’t work now, the garbage should be disposed of in an appropriate place that is deliberately prepared only for this purpose. Last time I sent someone from here to Addis Ababa just for experience sharing purpose. He told me he saw people recycling latrine sewage for other construction purposes. So, if there is a mechanism to use this kind of treatment plant here, it would also help us. If people are there to help the poor, I wish they think about it. I don’t think the problem of electric interruption is not due to shortage of power, but it is due to lack of diligent people who can properly handle the management. There used to be a water pipeline that comes from Mendida direction (Mendida is name of neighboring wereda town), but it is not functioning now. 

Regarding liquid waste management, it is almost similar to the solid waste management. The two tasks need to go hand in hand. First of all, a new dumping site need to be built or constructed somewhere not very far from the town. In addition to this, medium reservoirs should also be constructed here and there for temporary liquid waste reservation that will help to facilitate and make the task easier. They can even transfer this sewage to the main dumping site when these reservoirs are full. This will alleviate the sanitation problem that exists in the neighborhoods. That is all I can say about the solutions to solve sanitation problem that exists in Debreberhan town.
