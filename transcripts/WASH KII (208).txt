

In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in South Omo Zone?

In Jinka town the main problems to water service sustainability are: First, we have only limited source of water. We mainly use ground water to supply water to the town. The supply capacity of the ground water declines during dry seasons. Because of this there is mismatch between the supply and demand of water in the town. Moreover, the town is expanding and the population is increasing as a result the existing supply of water could not satisfy the needs of the community. Particularly last year we have faced prolonged dry season and consequently the supply capacity of the ground water sources was very limited. Before we go to the solutions are there any other problems related to finance, input supply and coordination with stakeholders? Related to our coordination with other stakeholders, in the first place our office is governed by a board that is led by the mayor of the town. The board regularly approve our plan and activities, and provide any support as per our request. However, previously we did not get support from stakeholders functioning in the water sector. For instance the zone water, mineral and energy office did not support our office previously but now they are trying to support us. Previously our office did not have coordination with other stakeholders. We were running separately to accomplish our plans. Recently, we are getting technical and material support from the zone water, mineral and energy office.

What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

Pertinent to limitation of water sources, we have discussed with stakeholders to alleviate the problems. For example, we have conducted assessment to identify areas that have good ground water potentials. Based on the findings of the assessment we are trying to develop water schemes in the identified areas. Moreover, in coordination with regional bureaus we have identified potential rivers that can be treated and serve as clean water supply in the town. The work is already approved by the regional water bureau and we have applied for financial support from water fund office. We hope the work will be started in coming September and it will be completed within two years. As far as the population and the size of the town is concerned do you think these solutions will alleviate the water supply problem? Sure it will solve the problem because when we conduct the assessments we also considered the size of the population as well as the size of the town. We believe that these new water projects will satisfy the water demand of the town 100%. The existing boreholes (water sources) and the new water projects collectively can solve the water problem of the town.

Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? 

Of the solutions I mentioned earlier, treating river waters is the most important solution to ensure water service sustainability in the town. Constructing new water tanks as well as rehabilitating water lines are also important solution to improve water service in the town.

In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? What could be improved? 

Previously our office has no coordination with other stakeholders and it was running separately to alleviate the water problems of the town. However, this cannot bring the required change in the town. In order to alleviate the problems sustainably, working in coordination with stakeholders is mandatory. For instance, to successfully implement the river water treatment project and construction of new boreholes we need to get significant support from the zonal water office. There should also be materials and technical support from other stakeholders as well. Since water supply is one of the issues of good governance, there should be support from all stakeholders to answer the community’s question. 

As a procedure our office directly request for support from the zonal water office and the zonal water office present the issue to regional water office if it is beyond its capacity. For instance, previously we have requested support to get water pump but both the zonal water office and regional bureau did not respond to our question. On the other hand we requested support to get generator and spare parts, and we got positive response for our request. Therefore, what I want to say is there should be more coordination between sector offices to solve problems related to water service in the area.

The town administration office should also be cooperative to solve the water problems of the town. For instance, when the town municipality provide land for individuals for house construction in new areas, it should also be responsible to construct water infrastructure in the area. And our office will also cooperate by providing technical support. Thus, there should be coordination between the town administration and water utility office to ensure sustainable water service in new expansion areas. 

In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the zone. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

If there is strong coordination between stakeholders functioning in the zone, we can regularly supply water to the community. Currently we are supplying water in shifts (i.e. the community is getting water in every three days/they get water for one day and stay without water for two days). Thus, if there is coordination with stakeholders in implementing the new water projects, we can supply water to the community regularly.

If there is coordination we can also solve the problems related to the existing water lines. That is if there is coordination, we can rehabilitate the existing water lines and construct new lines in expansion areas. By doing this we can answer the community’s question related to water supply.

In addition, most of the time water pumps burned out and water supply stops in some areas. Since our office do not have reserve pumps in our store, the community will suffer until we buy new pump from the market. And to purchase new pumps it costs us more than 300 thousand Ethiopian birr. Thus if there is coordination, stakeholders can contribute to have standby pumps in our store so that we can instantly solve the problems. 

How is the coordination with NGOs? For example there are NGOs like IRC, Catholic Development, Save the Children etc. what is their contribution in this respect? In 2015, we have conducted studies to identify potential water sources and invited NGOs to intervene in the identified areas but they did not give any response. So far we did not get any support from NGOs. It is only Red Cross that support us by providing spare parts for water schemes. However, even though we have requested support from other organizations, we did not get any response.
