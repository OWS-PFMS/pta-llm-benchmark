1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: In your town administration, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in so far as public latrine, communal latrine and open defecation issues?  
Respondent: In general hygiene and sanitation has a great impact on the town’s development.  One of the main problems in our town is a lack of sense of ownership of communal latrines. There are initiations to utilize the latrines, but the community falls behind when it comes to taking responsibility to clean and empty the latrines when it is full. As a result, many public and communal latrines are becoming out of service way before their project lifespan. The reason for this is a lack of a sense of ownership. Public latrines are not outsourced and thus they are prone to be misused. Local communities are not taking responsibility of communal latrines too.  Both public and communal latrines are effective in solving community sanitation problems. Because of lack of finance, land, and topography of the town is it quite difficult for each and every individual household in Debrebirhan town to have private latrines. 
Interviewer: What about other problems regarding issues such as human resource, sewerage system, and dumping sites.
Respondent: I don’t think there is a problem of resources or budget. The problem is lack of appropriately identify and utilize resources in the town. In this town there are more than forty communal and public latrines. It is expected of us to hand over to people who can utilize it properly. We do not need another additional resource to appropriately utilize the existing ones. 
However, there are problems of lack of dumping site and inadequate waste trucks. The issue of waste truck should be addressed by the town water utility office. But, the short term solutions that have been trying to solve the problem of waste disposal site are not good enough. This issue has to be thoroughly assessed and solutions that can bring long-lasting solutions should be devised. 
Aside from that, there is a problem with the sewerage system. There is a lack of a networked sewerage system in the town. This made the usefulness of the public and communal latrine less effective. What I think is a basic problem is that we are not using the currently existing ones as effectively as we should. For instance, many communal latrines located in kebele 02 are now closed. 
Interviewer: Is it because there is no one who pumps the latrines?
Respondent: Yes. For one, there is no one who empties the latrines when they are full. Secondly, there is a lack of a sense of ownership. Communal latrines are given to some community members to manage them properly. Those community members stop taking care of the latrine. This is due to many reasons, such as relocation from the neighborhood, or having their own private latrine. As a result, many communal latrines are not functional. Besides, there is a site selection issue. So, most of the problems of communal latrine is a lack of management. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Let’s come to the solutions. Lack of ownership is a basic problem, particularly, in the communal latrines. What should be done to solve that? If there are things that has been done you can mentions too. 
Respondent: The solution is to work on creating awareness of the community every now and then. The awareness creation effort should not be something that is done for a limited time and then stop. The process should have continuity until the desires level of awareness is created. This could be implemented by training selected members of the community and create a suitable environment for them to work with the community. In order to improve the sense of ownership, it should be explained to the community members that creating and maintaining clean environment is crucial for personal and communal health. Majority of the diseases that are affecting our community are communicable ones. These diseases can be prevented by practicing hygiene and sanitation. The sense of ownership should not only be to lock the communal latrines but also to keep the environment clean and use it properly. 
The issue of the truck should be addressed by the town administration. The administration should find donors or allocate some budget by itself to solve the problem. In this town, there are private vacuum truck owners, but there is no dumping site. Since the topography is not suitable and building a sewer system is costly I the inner city, vacuum trucks should be purchased by all means necessary. However, in areas where it is sensible to build sewerage systems, we can solve the problem by constructing sewer pipes and temporary septic tank. 
Interviewer: You have told me how the absence of dumping site is a pressing issue. But, what is the solution for the problem. And what has been done yet to address the issue? 
Respondent: Geological survey had not been conducted for the temporary dumping site. So, when the construction started, we found out that it is not possible to go down the depth in the plan. 
Interviewer: Is that the one in kebele 07?
Respondent: Yes. It is a bit far, and the vacuum truck owners charge more due to the distance. We do not have a dedicated site in the town plan for solid waste and liquid waste dumping site. We have selected another temporary site other than that is located in kebele 07, but it is also problematic. Therefore, the long lasting solution is to include modern dumping site when revising the master plan of the town. 
Interviewer: You have said that there are more than 40 public and communal latrines; and the problem is poor utilization of the resources we have. Therefore, what do you think should be done to solve this problem? As you said there are many closed communal latrines. What should be done in order to address this?
Respondent: These latrines are sources of job opportunities. Public latrines should be cleaned first. Second, we can set up enterprises on these public latrines. And additional facilities such as shower, coffee house can be included on top of the latrine services. By doing so, the latrines can be more effective and beneficial to the service providers and local community. 
Interviewer: How is it possible to use properly those that are currently closed?
Respondent: We should examine the reason for their closure. By identifying the idle communal latrines, they should be turned to public latrines so that people can use it.
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have provided interesting solutions. Of the solutions you have listed, which is the most important? What next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: If you ask me to prioritize the solutions, I would say the first solution should be awareness creation. The community should detest garbage. Many community members know that but they get it of their home but throw it in their environment. If we apply modern garbage handling methods right from the household level, we can turn waste into wealth. Similarly, liquid waste not only the excreta from latrines but also other liquid waste can be dumped with little cost and address the issue.  
Secondly, public latrines should be outsourced to enterprises and additional funds should be mobilized to add entertainment services that are provided by these latrines. We have one model facility in our town. Such things should be scaled up. But, awareness creation is the most crucial one.
The other thing is we should have a coordinated effort among sector offices. For instance, health, culture and tourism, municipality, and other sectors should work in collaboration with each other. If we do so, we will be more effective.
