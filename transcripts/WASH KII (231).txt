1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: in our city, we have only two communal latrines. The first one serves only 15 people and the second one is for 6 peoples. The problem is so they were not organized, they were not saving money. This brought them financial problems to avoid or damp the latrine wastage. Then we try to let them discuss about it with the help of the training from your organization including awareness issues. Even if they start to save money and be financially stable for the vacuum truck; here they have a problem of damping site to avoid wastes.
There was no sewage vacuum truck. At this time, the city water and sanitation office is trying to solve it. There is a change on the awareness among the community after all of us, the zonal administrations and team leaders took continuous trainings. 
If you ask hotels, the major problem according to our city is lack of damping cite. Especially if we take melkagudina (03) kebele as an example, it has more latrine facility that other kebeles. But the soil type is full of groundwater and it starts to spring water in summer seasons. This needs frequent suction. This creates a big problem for the people. City administration and kebele leaders can solve it if there is a damping site only. There were some activities in getting the site; they pay money for the amends of the land. But it stops now because of complaints from the community members. I think we will see some changes if this problem is solved.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Interviewer: what do you think should be done to solve the damping site problem permanently?
Respondent: The city administration should try to pay and convince them quickly as needed as possible. If it is needed to change a site, they should try to pay compensation for the land owners and change the site so quickly. This problem should be seen as a main problem; because it may cause health problems to the community.

Respondent: It is obvious that this problem can’t be solved in one moon. Respondent: Once this damping site problem is solved, awareness gap will be filled among the society. They will use to avoid wastes on the recommended places. There should be laws to punish the people who throw dry and liquid wastes on places other than the allowed one. This will help us to get solutions.
