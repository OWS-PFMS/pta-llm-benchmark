I1: In your opinion, what do you think has ensured Kamuli has regular running clean water?
R: WAVE is doing a good job. 
I1: How so?
R: They fix boreholes and regularly carry out supervision and maintenance. 
I1: What hindrances does clean water distribution face in Kamuli?
R: Community members who don’t want to pay the borehole maintenance fee. 
I1: How can we solve this problem? 
R: We recently resorted to locking the borehole (with a pad lock). Anyone who hasn’t paid this fee can’t get water from the borehole. 
I1: How would you grade the current maintenance of the boreholes? 
R: Good. The fact that it is in a better condition has also reduced the fights that used to happen in the past with community members struggling to get water without order. 
I1: Any recommendations you can give to improve this situation more? 
R: Being in the trading center, the line becomes really long because of the population causing fights. I recommend some other water pipes around the trading center are provided to do away with this problem. Simply, more water sources or boreholes should be provided.
