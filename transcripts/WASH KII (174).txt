1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Interviewer: In your opinion what are the main challenges or constraints that affect the sustainability of WASH service in your woreda? When I say WASH service, it means water and sanitation service. What are the main problems observed in this service?
Respondent: First, the main problem to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda is lack of budget. The woreda’s budget is not enough; budget allocated by the government to the woreda cannot meet the entire needs of the woreda. Due to budget constraint there is a serious water supply shortage both in the woreda. The community could not prepare water for hand washing around the latrines. Even in areas with relatively better access for water, they do not put water for hand washing around latrines due to lack of awareness.
Second, is the inability to maintain existing water facilities. There is difficulty to make the facilities sustainable due to absence of immediate and regular maintenance service. We do not have the capacity in managing the existing water facilities to ensure their sustainability due to huge gap in coordination and shortage of spare part supply. Because of this, we cannot mobilize and capacitate the community in terms managing the existing water schemes. 
Third, the community does not properly manage the existing water facilities and they lack sense of belongingness on proper use of water schemes. This is the other main problem for sustainability of the water service in the woreda.
Fourth, some part of the town does not have access for clean water due to road construction in the town. Moreover, the schools operating in different rural kebeles have limited access for clean water. For instance, due to infrastructure problem for 4 kilometer way it takes up to 90 minutes to bring one patient to health facilities.


2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions problem to ensure sustainable water service?
Respondent: The woreda should strongly work on areas that can be managed by the woreda’s capacity. We should allocate the finance resource to the water sector efficiently. But, in areas that are beyond our capacity, we have to involve potential NGOs and collaborators in the development of water schemes. For instance, in some kebeles Action for Development has developed many water scheme. Likewise, Christian Aid, World Vision, and IRC have been working in the area. Therefore, we have to organize the financial source, design various projects and involve potential NGOs and the government itself in the development of water schemes.
Interviewer: you have raised inability to maintain existing water facilities as one of the problems for sustainability, what ideas or recommendations do you have about the solutions?
Respondent: This may be the responsibility of the woreda water and the woreda administration offices. However, we should work in coordination with other sectors in terms of sharing the existing limited spare parts and experts. Particularly we should have strong cooperation with the town water utility office in terms of taking collective actions. The woreda water office should be providing the required support related to skilled manpower, logistics, budgetary constraints and organizing enterprises. If we can create coordination between different sector offices operating in the woreda, we can minimize the effects of lack of spare parts and labor shortage. 
Interviewer: you have raised problem related to awareness creation and lack sense of belongingness on proper use of water schemes. what ideas or recommendations do you have about the solutions?
Respondent: To resolve this problem, whoever construct the water schemes either the government or potential donors, they should create sense of ownership within the community. We should also create sense of belongingness within the community for water schemes through training and awareness enhancement campaigns. The woreda should organize rural community associations that will be responsible for the management and administration of water schemes. These associations can mobilize financial contribution from community that will be used for regular maintenance of the schemes. We have to fill the gaps related to skilled manpower and provide continuous training to water management committees as well as to the community at large on proper use of water schemes. If the communities consider these facilities as their own resources, they will contribute money for maintenance and they will discuss on how to ensure the sustainability of the existing water and sanitation facilities. 
Interviewer: you have raised problem related to constriction basic infrastructure facilities as constraint of sustainability. what ideas or recommendations do you have about the solutions?
Respondent: This issue is also related with our office (South Ari Woreda health office).   To alleviate the problem related to sustainability service all responsible authorities should give due attention. Including the woreda administration, Zone, and regional administration must communicate and coordinate to solve the infrastructure problem permanently. If they work on this front, it will be one good solution for sustainability problems of water and sanitation services.

3.  Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The main problem to ensure sustainable water service in our woreda is lack of budget. The budget is far below the woreda’s budget requirement for the development of new water schemes. The woreda is naturally rich in ground water and surface water resources. However, the woreda lacks capacity in utilizing these potential water resources. The other thing due to water shortage, the community could not prepare water for hand washing around the latrines. Even in areas with relatively better access for water, they do not put water for hand washing around latrines due to lack of awareness. If financial resource is available, other related problems are insignificant. Therefore, if budget constraint is abolished it will be one good solution for sustainability problems of water and sanitation services.
