1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Inappropriate waste disposal practices 
I observed serious malpractices related to waste disposal in Debre Birehan town in general and in our kebele in particular. For instance, hotels and manufacturing industries simply release their liquid wastes into drainage lines and highly contaminate the environment. Especially the liquid wastes released from the manufacturing industries contaminate the environment and expose the community for various health disorders. Moreover, some community members connect their latrines to drainage lines and dispose their solid wastes into ditches during nighttime.
To alleviate this problem at least at household level, we teach the community through house-in-house visit to dispose solid wastes in three ways: 
1.To sort solid wastes that can be decompose within their locality from those that do not decompose 
2.There are microenterprises engaged in collecting and dispose wastes and we inform the community to use these enterprises to collect and dispose their wastes. 
3.The community also informed to use wastes for compost preparation and to use as fertilizers in horticulture. Otherwise, to burn in a way that do not affect the environment and the community.
There is no that much problem related to drinking water. However, occasionally when we visit-house-in-house, for instance, we observe that they store water in narrow container and didn’t see inside which made algae. We inform them to use open container and clean glass as much as possible. There are also places that have no water supply and use public water pipes 



2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
To solve waste disposal problems I would like to suggest the following:
Industries should have septic tanks and treatment plants to minimize environmental pollution.
There should be strong law enforcement mechanism to avoid inappropriate waste disposal practices.
Appropriate waste disposal site should be developed and microenterprises should be encouraged to engage in waste collection and disposal service. 
Individuals should be encouraged and advised to produce composts from wastes and use them as fertilizer for urban agriculture. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
The first burning issue that need immediate action is improving the solid and liquid waste disposal system of the town. If the waste disposal system and site problem for both solid and liquid wastes is resolved, the community will benefit the following:
The community will be prevented from various form of health disorders that arise from sanitation problems.
The town will become model for other towns
