
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer:  I will go to the question directly. In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your town in general? The study focuses specifically in this town. So, what do you think the main problems are for not having sustainable sanitation? For example relating with institutionally or any other kind of problem?  
Response: As you know the city is growing fast. This has its own effect on the city. For example, the amount of waste material grows as a result of growth in populations. Mostly you don’t see the waste materials in the street rather than plastic things. On the other hand, liquid waste material is growing as a result of number of hotels growth in the city. Additionally, because there are no public toilet individuals will cleanliness on the side of the street. I consider this mainly the main problem in the city. The other one is plastic bags that used as a container. This has a big impact on the environment. You will see plastic bags and plastic water container that are called highlands everywhere dropped. I believe this are the problems in sanitation. To prioritize them, the plastic bags used and through by the society will come the big problem. The second one is liquid wastes that comes out of different hotels and factories. The third one is unavailability of public toilet. This are the problems that exist inside the urban city. Around the city there are rivers. The rivers are getting contaminated. To mention a specific river that is being contaminated rapidly called Chacha river. It is found around Baso area. Long before people used to drink from this river. Currently, leave along people animals are not allowed to drink from it. The farmers have told me personally that there is huge amount of contamination that comes out of the factories as form of liquid and it will join the river. This has made the river dangerous and unusable. This shows as a result of growth in number of factories, the waste chemicals that are being disposed are getting increasing too. This is the one that create a problem on water sanitation also.   
Interviewer: Well, before we go to the solutions let me ask you one thing regarding the factories. Before factories are established studies will be made on the impact of the factory on the environment. So, is this the problem of the body who studied the impact? 
Response: Yes, there is a study. But, our countries main problem is mostly you do not get on the ground what is said or planned. There was a seminar recently and in that seminar the zone industry office head was saying factories should be accepted by the society as their factory. Other wise it will not survive.  This means it has to get benefit from the factory and the second thing it should not be hurtled by the existence of the factory. What amuses me is if you go to factories right now they do have treatment plan but they don’t use it. When sanitarian comes they will use it to cover up. 
[00:04:57]
But if you go and see unexpectedly, you will see that they will dispose it to the ground as it is.  This is caused by lack of controlling and negligence. Factories will create unfavorable condition for itself when doing this kind of things. Because if a farmer loses his animal he will start to hate the factory. On the other hand, there are also good factories that use proper way of disposing their wastes. For example, Habesh Bera factory has built treatment plant and uses every time. And farmers will use it for irrigation. This is a good example for factories that dispose harmful chemicals without treating. In the seminar they have said after this forward there will be serious controlling and there will be improvement.  

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: Really thank you. You have mentioned problems and in your opinion what ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to existing problems? For example, to stop wastes from hotels and other sources what should be done? For plastic bags, what should be done? 
Response: there is no better solution than creating awareness on the society. This is also our sectors mandate. It is good to train the society in the environmental protection. The second one is because creating awareness by itself is not enough and I believe if one is not troubled doesn’t create trouble, proving other solutions to change their habit is another solution. For example, when we say don’t use plastic bags or highlands we have to provide other means for replacement. A country in Africa call Kigali was recognized as the cleanest country. This country doesn’t use mostly plastic bags. They use carton bags made from paper. We have to use this kind of systems to replace the existing plastic bags. We have to use paper, carton or another material which doesn’t affect the environment in a bad way. Recycling process must a habit. This will decrease wastes that are disposed anywhere. Because it will be valuable to reconstruct other material. We have also started recycling plastics and for example in you cannot find in Jimma University any waste dropped anywhere. After every meeting there are individuals who collect the empty plastic highlands or water containers.
[00:09:59]
 And they will be recycled to create like fountain tree, chair and other useful and beautiful things. Me also use the plastics to plant flowers and use for different purposes in my home. If individuals who are architect given additional training and recreate these materials they will be reused. 
The other thing is liquid waste materials from hotels and different factories. To solve this problem Trade and Tourism office has to check whether the organizations have toilet and sanitation service before giving license. I know they will check before giving license currently but they organization will close the toilets afterwards. They have their own problem that is, people who are not customers of our service will use the toilet mostly. So, to protect this they will lock the toilet. The government should construct public toilet and make it available for the society. When this public toilet is built it can generate income for some individuals also. For example, disabled individuals can be organized and given the mandate to administer and control this area. There is this kind of request that come to as a proposal. The proposal owners are blind individuals and they requested to have the opportunity to work in group on public toilet. In this kind of system others like youths and other groups can be helped. When someone wants to use the toilet he or she will pay in cents. 
Interviewer: you mean like the system started in Addis Ababa?
Response: yes, by prioritizing the groups who needs the job we can help the society. Otherwise, if the public toilet is built but left for someone known it will not last long. For example, if we build the public toilet for the groups who submitted their proposal, they will willingly control, generate income from it and when it gets full they will call Water and Drainage Office to transport it. They will maintain the cleanness as it is.   
Interviewer: what should be the solution for contamination of rivers and the factories case? Who should be responsible?
Response: the factory it self should be the first responsible body. If one doesn’t protect my safety and works against me, I will definitely start working against it. Legally or in other system.  I heard this directly from the farmers. I really felt their hurt. They have lost oxen and other animals. Even they have been sick. They told me they have gone to the woreda office and zone offices but no one has heard us. We have tried everything but no solution has been given. If we cannot get solution legally we will solve the solution by force. This was what the farmers said informally.  
[00:15:04]
Debreberhan is in a good peaceful condition right now. We are the one who creates peace and destroys it. If you ask the factories who being blamed for this contamination, they will tell you they have built treatment plant. But if you go unexpectedly and see yourself, they will not use it. This problem should be solved first by making the factory responsible for their action. Second, the license giver sector should control tightly for this kind of problems and take measures.
Interviewer: are they breweries or leather kind of factories that create this kind of problem? 
Response: I don’t know but as I heard from the farmers it is the Areke factory (brewery factory) who creates this contamination. There are lots of factories around the Areke factory. So, study should be made to identify exactly which one is creating the contamination. This river is huge are it gives many uses to the society. Even there are societies who uses this river as the only source of water. There for, if they lose this one it will be hard for them to live. This can be seen beyond sanitation, as a security case. 
Interviewer: yes, for example like the case of MEDROIC. It is closed now. We have that kind of power. We have to use it. 
Response: by the way, we have been taking care of our responsibility. We have been meeting with Industry sector to identify problems that needs research for their solution.  
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

Response: the first one is creating awareness on the effect of improper disposal of waste material. For example, one may think it is harmless to burry wastes underground. But the water we drink is came from underground water.  So, if we burry the waste underground it will affect the ground water. So, awareness should be created first. If we know the damage we will not do that. Then the question “I want to dispose where shall I put my waste?” question comes next.  Debreberhan is not that much covered with dirt like other places. In other places dead dog and other dirt’s are found on the street. In the city, we don’t find this kind of things. There are groups that collect wastes every week. I will pay happily for them because they have minimized my burden. And the groups will transport it out side the city in farmers land. This should be solved. It is creating another problem for the farmers. Other place should be given for collecting wastes that comes from the city.

The other one is when license is given for hotels and other service giving sectors, they should be checked every time.
