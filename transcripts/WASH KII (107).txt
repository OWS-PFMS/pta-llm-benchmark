
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: My first question, as we know sanitation services giving institutions are found Debreberhan city but currently sustainable sanitation is facing a problem. The way of keeping hygiene by the society is getting poor every time. What do you think the main problems related with this? What keeps the city from being clean? Can you identify problems related with institutions coordination or problems related with industries?    
Response:  yes there are problems. The city doesn’t have enough toilets and no waste collecting baskets for the public. I think this is destroying the city. When the city was established it didn’t have plan for infrastructures. So this led to unorganized way of disposing waste materials in the city. Now, the government is trying to make this in a planned way but until now it was unplanned and unorganized. The other one even though there are some public toilets in the city, most of the society has no habit of using them. May be in the future I have a hope this will be improved. People are seen doing things on the streets and on road corners that should be done in the toilets. So, because of these two reasons I think there is sanitation problem in the city. I recommend the City Administration take responsibility to assign man power and resources to clean the area. This will help to improve the area sanitation service to the society.  
Interviewer: Does the city have employees who are assigned by the City Administration to keep the city clean? 
Response: We see some workers on cleaning the main road. But on areas that are outside of the main road, we don’t see them. For example we do have public toilet built by the church and the City Administration will assign workers temporarily like two times per year to clean the area. But because the population is growing fast and it is getting harder to manage the hygiene. There has to be another modern waste deposing are and public toilet that support many people at a time. The infrastructures of the city are also going to be destroyed after a while. The other problem that church faces today is there are many beggars that live on the side of the church. They sleep eat and totally live on the street. We have talked with the city administration about it.

It is creating a problem of sanitation for the area. There are around 200 beggars. And not all of them are real poor that needs to be helped. Most of them have money and are wealthy but they adopted a habit of begging and getting things while just sitting. This should be solved and they must learn to work and keep the area clean. The government should take serious measure on this issue.                  
Interviewer: I head there were few public toilets in your area, built by the City Administration but they are closed now. Is it true? What is the reason?
Response: Yes, there is public toilet built by the City Administration. But it is not the only public toilet we have also built another toilet for the people to use. The city administration didn’t assign a worker that is responsible to clean the toilet so most of the time it will hard to use them and even to pass by. So, they are closed and the people use the toilets we built. If the city administration assigns a person the toilets can be used again. This also shows lack of awareness of the society.  
Interviewer: What if you hire a person and assign that person to clean the area rather than closing the public toilets? Have you thought about this?
Response: We have thought about it. The City administration has given some social work tasks to the church. So, we are considering it as one of the serious matters that should be addressed. We are planning to hire a person and give responsibility for that area.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: You have mentioned different problems above. What do you think the solution for the mentioned problems? What should be done to create sustainable sanitation?
Response: The solution is applying what other cities have done to solve this problem. For example to prepare waste material collecting baskets or containers on the side of street and creating awareness on the society by creating a group and by creating a discussion. As we know the city is urbanizing and people come from rural area. They may not know the disadvantages of improper waste material disposal. So as the government created awareness on traffic rules with the church and other bodies. This should be put together gain for sustainable sanitation. If the people are educated in every meeting using different methods and if waste material collecting basks or containers put on the side of the streets, the sanitation for the area and for the city will be sustainable.
