1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Absences of waste disposal place/system for liquid and solid wastes 
The waste disposal system of Woliso town is very problematic due to absence of of standard liquid and solid waste disposal places in and outside the town. It is a big challenge for the town sanitation service. Due to absence of appropriate waste disposal place, the vehicles purchased by our office for liquid waste collection cannot provide service to the community. The commitment of the town’s municipality to find solution to the problem is very weak and they due do not give attention for the problem.  The municipality has no ears to hear our questions and solve problem related to waste disposal sites.   The town administration lately attempts to address the disposal site problem and it identified a site outside the town but did not reach an agreement with the landowners due to compensation issues. 
II.Lack of community awareness on waste disposal and management
In Woliso town most of the members of the community dispose their waste in roadsides and any open spaces. On the other hand, concerned bodies that are responsible on sanitation affairs are committed in enhancing the awareness of the community related to waste disposal and management. Households carelessly dispose solid wastes in open spaces without separating those wastes that can be recycled for other purposes due to lack of awareness. We observed that households, industries and service providing institutions dispose wastes into open areas and rivers of the town without sorting recyclable ones. The other problem related to sanitation is that small-scale dairy farm owners operating in the town dispose animal dungs in residential areas and roadsides due to lack of awareness and weak sanitation regulation enforcement mechanisms. Industry wastes are also disposed in to open areas and seriously affecting animal health and farming practices. 
III.Water supply and demand imbalance
The respondents argued that the water supply shortage of the town aggravated the sanitation related problems of the area. There are only seven water wells, which is found to be inadequate to satisfy the water needs of the growing population of the town. The use of the potable water for traditional irrigation purposes by farmers during the dry season aggravates the water supply shortage in the town. On the other hand, the oldness of the existing water distribution lines creates water wastage due to leakages and even contamination of water by liquid wastes. This further aggravated the water supply shortage of the town. 
In addition, there is frequent power outage in Woliso town. Since the supply of water in the town is mainly based on electric power, the power outage interrupts the water supply frequently. Sometimes the community stay without water from three days to a week as the water service is highly affected by power outage. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
To alleviate the problems related to waste disposal site and system we would like to suggest that: 
Appropriate waste disposal site should be developed by taking all the necessary actions and reaching an agreement with landowners by providing reasonable compensation.
The liquid waste collection vehicles owned by our office provide service to the community by facilitating at least a temporary waste disposal site outside the town. 
Wastes should be collected, sorted (those recyclable ones and those that can be used as natural fertilizer/compost) transported and disposed outside the town in appropriate place instead of disposing into open areas, streets and rivers by enhancing the awareness of the community and industries on waste disposal and management. 
Regarding water supply shortage problem: 
We have to improve our water utilization efficiency by avoiding leakages from the water distribution lines.
The ongoing water supply expansion project which is financed by World Bank should be finalized within short period and provide service to the community. 
Irrigation practice should not be based on potable water rather farmers should rely on rainwater by harvesting water during rainy season and use during dry season.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Developing appropriate waste disposal place is the most important solution to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. If this problem get a solution, obviously, the community health will improve and we can create productive society.
