1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: Let me take you to the first question. What are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in Debrebirhan regarding public latrine, communal latrine, and waste dumping site?
Respondent: The main problem is lack of waste disposal sites. In this town there are some open places though they are not sufficient. Besides, there are no adequate garbage trucks. 
Budget: There is also budget problem. Due to lack of budget, locations for the solid waste and liquid waste dumping sites have not been identified yet. Compensation payments have not been paid to the land owners. But, the first thing is to identify and select locations; only then can a budget be allocated. Our finance management directive also requires that the identified land should be free from third party’s ownership to request budget. 
Interviewer: What do you mean by ‘free of third party’?
Respondent: After the location is identified, waste disposal site and a property ownership certificate need to be issued. Why I mentioned this as an introduction is that after a budget is allocated, usually many projects will fall on privately owned lands. Therefore, projects need to have legal ownership for the sites they are being constructed. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: The first problem you have mentioned is lack of adequate waste disposal site. What would be the solution?
Respondent: In the municipality, there is are departments that is responsible for these issues. They should identify locations that are suitable for waste disposal and request the town administration. The town administration should then prepare the selected sites. 
Interviewer: Why is it that it has not been done yet?
Respondent: I think they should answer by themselves why they have not done it so far.  

Interviewer: What would be the solution for the garbage collection trucks?
Respondent: Yes. The second issue is lack of garbage truck. The town administration only has one truck. Procurement is the mandate of the regional government. We hope additional truck will be purchased and we will be able to solve this problem. If we had two or three, it would be enough for the town. 
Interviewer: When would that happen?
Respondent: It depends on the regional government procurement process. The mayor should take the responsibility to follow the progress. 
Interviewer: What do you think should be done to solve the issue of budget? Who should do what to address the issue? 
Respondent: This should be addressed after locations for solid and liquid wastes are set in place. The town administration should handle this issue primarily. With regard to budgeting, I think the town administration can decide and partially solve this. In addition, it is important to identify partners. when the required budget is beyond the town administration’s capacity, other stakeholders (NGOs) should contribute. Further, the administration could also build disposal sites by allocating a certain amount of budget annually. That way, we can alleviate the problem within few years. 
Interviewer: As finance office, is there any budget that you allocate annually for the purposes of dump site or truck procurement? Have you proposed any budget for such intentions?
Respondent: Some investors have supported us to purchase garbage truck. Other than that we allocate a limited amount of budget for the green development projects. But, we do not think it is enough. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The most important solution is to prepare the locations for solid waste and liquid waste disposal sites. Even to create community awareness, we should first prepare dumping sites. There will be no choice for the community to dispose wastes unless there is proper site for the solid and liquid waste. Therefore, we should get the sites ready for now. 
Interviewer: If the waste disposal sites are prepared, what changes would happen in the town? What problems could it solve?
Respondent: If the dumping sites are prepared, garbage that is being thrown away everywhere will be collected and disposed to proper location. 
Interviewer: The one that has been used is now full right?
Respondent: It is not adequate. There are also some sites that are already full. The reason that I told you as it is not adequate is that when there are many disposal sites, there will be minimal burden on the current few disposal sites.
