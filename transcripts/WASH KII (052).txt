
Interviewer:  My first question to you is, “In your opinion, what aspect of this network is working well in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: From what we’ve done, in my opinion, all the water service providers are supposed to get authorizations and permits from us, but in most cases they don’t do so.  We realize they are carrying out activities when we go to the field.  

Interviewer:  So you feel that that is an issue within the network?

Interviewee: Yes, it is an issue.  

Interviewer:  My second question is, “What do you think are the main problems in sustaining rural water services in the county?

Interviewee: I think one of them is the management of the schemes themselves.  There is a problem with the management and there is also the problem with operation and maintenance.  And the services they offer doesn’t match with the revenue they generate, which means either they have to get additional funding so that they can maybe be able to break even their operations.  Those ones are the major problems.    

Interviewer:  And my third question is “What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?”

Interviewee: Community water projects, the management committees, they should be trained on management of the schemes.  At the same time, we can try to find out the problems as to why the projects - the revenue they generate - does not match with .. cannot sustain the projects themselves.  Maybe the system they’re using is not appropriate.  For example, they may be using electricity and there can be the option of using solar power.  

Interviewer:  Any other recommendations?

I think they should be trained with the simple operation and maintenance.    

Interviewer:  Which do you feel is the most importation recommendation? 

Interviewee: I think investigating, because when you investigate why the projects are failing, the issue of committee members not knowing good management practices can be a problem.  

Interviewer:  If this solution were to occur, what would happen next?

Interviewee: I expect that after the investigations are carried out, problems are found out, recommendations made, and the recommendations are implemented, then the projects should be able to offer appropriate services or the required services.
