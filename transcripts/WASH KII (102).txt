1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: What are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in Debrebirhan?
Respondent: One of the main problems of waste management is the absence of waste disposal site. Open location has been selected for the dumping site by the town administration. Compensation for the land that is selected for the dumping site has been paid already. This year around twenty-six-million-birr annual budget has been allocated. It would be vital to utilize the budget and start its construction.
Secondly, there are very limited number of public latrines. But, nowadays, the town is growing faster and its population is also increasing. As a result, currently existing public latrines are not enough for the number of residents of the town at this time. Therefore, additional public latrines are needed. 
Interviewer: When you say public latrine, do you mean their number is not enough or there is quality problem?
Respondent: Both, I think there is also problem related to quantity. There are locations in the town that are highly populated yet they don’t have public latrine. So, in places where there are plenty of people, construction of additional public latrines is crucial. It is an employment opportunity on top of creating a clean environment. There are many places identified for construction of public latrines. 
Even the existing ones are not being utilized by the community. We have some public latrines around the bus station in our town. Nonetheless, people do not use them wisely. 
Interviewer: Are there any other problem related to communal latrine and related sanitation issues?
Respondent: As to my observation, there is no critical issue in relation to communal latrine. The town’s main problems are related to the two issues I mentioned: disposal site and public latrine.

Interviewer: What about problems related to working in coordination? 
Respondent: Regarding working in coordination, truth to be told, ever since this program started, we have been working together with all concerned bodies. Sectors such as health office, culture and tourism office, trade office, education office, customs and revenue office, and the municipality have had a joint committee. The committee has been working well up until the outbreak of the global pandemic COVID-19. We could not be able to discuss on the issues of the dumping site and public latrines. We had not had any meetings within the last six months except for informal communications whenever emergencies. However, last week we met and decided to prepare a joint plan together.
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Let me take you to the first point. The absence of dumping site has been a problem for a very long time. So, what solution do you propose?
Respondent: The first thing I would say should be done is pay the compensation for the land to the land owners, because, the identified location is still in the hands of the farmers. As far as I am concerned, the assessment is completed but compensation payments have not been made yet. Therefore, the first step should be freeing the selected location from third party. Then, we can discuss what should be done in the sides of the town administration, sector office, and other concerned bodies. Last year, we had decided to pay compensations and put fence around the selected site; nonetheless, because of budget and other issues we didn’t implement it.
Interviewer: Why do you think the compensation for the land to the farmers is not paid? 
Respondent: It could be nothing else but budget shortage; because, it required huge amount of financing. The other reason is that last year, the manual needed to conduct the assessment had not arrived, but now we have the manual in our hands. I have heard informally that budget has also been allocated. The project is big and modern. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned that there is a quantity and quality problem regarding public latrines. What would be the solution for this problem?
Respondent: To improve the quality of the existing public latrines, it is vital to empower and support enterprises who take care of the facilities. If they are supported, their revenue would increase and they would provide quality services. 
For the ones that will be constructed in the future, it would be beneficial to build coffee houses and other entertainment options alongside the latrines, like those in big cities such as Addis Ababa. 
Besides, additional latrines should be constructed. For instance, there is a spot in 07 Kebele where many daily laborers gather to find employers. These laborers defecate and urinate in the open space nearby, because there is no latrine there. Therefore, certain locations should be identified and public latrines should be built there. It would have plenty of benefits. First, the neighborhood will be clean. Secondly, job opportunities would be created for the local residents. In order to do so, the municipality should take the first step to select locations. The health office, finance, and small and medium enterprise office should also contribute their shares. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned that there is lack of coordination among over 20 stakeholders. What would you say is the solution for this? 
Respondent: In this program, there are around 26 stakeholders. Our sector connects with other five or six sectors because of the nature of the tasks. We have better coordination with these sectors so far as with regard to planning together. However, we couldn’t work with all the stakeholders involved in the WASH program.  With government sectors, though, we have good coordination system compared to non-governmental organizations.
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned plenty of solutions. Out of the solutions you have listed, which is the most important? What next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: In my opinion, the dumping site should be built first. Debrebirhan has been growing and in it there is an industry park. In order to cop up with these changes, first and foremost, a modern dumping site should be readily available. If a disposal site is built, many problems will be solved. 
Interviewer: What changes would happen?
Respondent: First, the waste that we see all over the places would be properly disposed and dumped. Secondly, starting from its construction, many job opportunities would be created, even after is it completed. Thirdly, waste management is vital to create beautiful and clean town. Alongside that public latrines should be built on selected locations in the town. If we can do that, we would be able to reduce open defecation and create a healthy community, and we would create new job opportunities for the youth, elderly, and women.
