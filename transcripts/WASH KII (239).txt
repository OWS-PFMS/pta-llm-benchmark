Interviewer: what about you are doing to avoid open defecation? 
Respondent: we have placed a vessel for garbage everywhere then there are people who organized as cooperative for this purpose pick it up via car. And to avoid defecation in an open area we are making the people to arrange an ampoule at the get of their houses which is also important for security issue.
