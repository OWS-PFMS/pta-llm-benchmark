1)In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Water and Sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
The existing water service was launched   20 or 30 years back by considering the number of Households by that time. Since the population has increased almost by 50%, there is shortage of water to meet the demand afterwards. Challenges of water service sustainability on one hand and increase of demand for the service in both institutions and community living in the town on the other hand are creating additional burden to the town municipality.   
For instance, some of the town residents get access to water bi-weekly while others are waiting for three weeks or a month owning to the shortage of the service. What I know is that the town's water service issue has continued to be a serious and critical matter so does the sanitation and hygiene service. Water as one of the basic facilities in urban areas that should have been accessed by everyone freely, but the opposite is happening in Weliso. This is in turn creating another problem: people are not comfortable to the existing administration system in general and the municipality in particular. 
2. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Water well drilling schemes are being conducted in the town in collaboration with Oromia Regional Government, the town Administration and Oromia Water Bureau. The progress of the project will be some 85 % by now and the target is provision of adequate service to the town in a sustainable manner. Though I am not a member of the project staff and didn’t participate in the process, I can proudly speak that it is such a challenging task but it is bringing a fruitful result. It will definitely solve the problems that the community is suffering due to lack of adequate water coverage.  

Interviewer: What about solutions to the problems related to sanitation? 
 Our town’s beauty and landscape is so amazing, leveled and green look. However the society’s awareness problem has been such a growing issue for decades. Waste disposal practice is very poor; people are throwing solid wastes everywhere. At the first place, they shouldn’t be complained the municipality didn’t execute its duties properly. There is no proper and accessible waste disposal and damping site around the town. It seeks special focus. Not only that, we have also shortage of vehicles and waste disposal equipment in the town. The tractor that has been serving for some time has now sat idle due to engine failure. So, it all needs mobilization of all the resources, including more budgets (to pay it for the compensation) and open land fill sites in a strategic way so that it can be used for long period. Though the municipality service is trying to do at its best possible capacity, it is not adequate. We need to ask all the sectors, private actors and non-governmental organizations for their collaboration. 

When we compare utilization of water services and the awareness gap on sanitation and hygiene practices, I can say that people have relatively better know how on the management of water services. However, in terms of hygiene and sanitation, we need to do more awareness creation programs continuously. This also needs proper planning, fund raising and working on capacity development for the technical experts and trainings for the community.  As you can see out, even the asphalt road is full of rubbish, the ditches are not clean and a bad smell comes out sometimes. This wouldn’t be an overnight work, but the task that we should do it in planned and organized manner so that we can see changes in time. 
Interviewer: Do you think that awareness creation programs can bring the desired changes? 
There is no doubt. I hope we can minimize the bad waste disposal culture we have now by at least by 50 % in short time. But for this change to come true, we should do it as a program. I meant it should not be a kind of one time mobilization. We should also follow up the timely changes and the commitment from the community. Coordination is a very good tool here as I said it earlier. Also, awareness creation program should be conducted based on the resources we have, for example, shortage of water is one of the challenges we are facing from the people when we are telling them about sanitation and hygiene. What does this imply? Yes, they are right we need to have water services available all the time if we are training about sanitation that is the point. 
In this regard, although we are doing the borehole project by being together with the Oromiya water Bureau, in the northern part of the town, but I don’t believe that will solve the demand we have recently.  Believe it or not, a person may wait 20 to 30 days to access tap water to his home sometimes. We need to do more than what we have already executed to meet the demands in the short time. 




The master plan of Weliso town was approved and signed last year in 2011 E.C. it has clearly indicated the master plan, where it should be, the area, the benefits to the whole population and to the surrounding farmers.  As per this plan, the site appropriate compensation would be paid to the owners and construction will then follow immediately. The site is located in the eastern direction on the way to Addis Ababa and we have got the opportunity to visit the site with a team of experts. The main challenge would be fund raising to complete compensation payment, once we have that it will not be a problem to prepare it for use in the short time. 
3. Interviewer: Of the solutions you listed, which one is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Waste disposal system comes out of the important and immediate solution. The most import thing to do is fixing the problem of waste disposal system issue. Though it needs coordinated actions from the different actors, the major ones should be: Water and sanitation office, Municipality, urban administration and environmental protection offices. 
The next steps happening will be:
Improved sanitation and hygiene, 
protected health conditions, 
clean environment, good governance, 
increasing investment, industrialization and tourism 
and quality life and standard infrastructures.
