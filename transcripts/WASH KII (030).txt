Question 1: In your opinion what do you think are the main challenges to water service sustainability in Kitui County? Could you explain in detail how these challenges influence water service sustainability? 
Respondent: I think some of the challenges on sustainability I think is low skills amongst community members, that is skills in operating maintenance of water facilities and I would think that is, because those skills have not been impacted to the users. Maybe the other challenge is on management of the facilities with the management itself and also low ownership of the water sources. By this I mean a number of the water sources have been established by donors or the government, but the community users seem not to have owned the sources as their own. They still consider them as owned by whoever established to a great extent.
Interviewer: Which other main challenge?
Respondent: Of course the other main challenge is lack of resources to establish water sources. Like now, even in our case we are not doing as much as we would want to because of lack of resources.
Interviewer: Which other one?
Respondent: I think for now those are the ones I have.
Question 2: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?  
Respondent: On skills, of course solution would be to provide skills on operation and maintenance of facilities through trainings, which should include not only classroom based sessions, but also field demonstrations of operations and maintenance. I know even when there have been trainings, if they are limited to classroom sessions then they might not impact well. So there should be that field work demonstration, I have mentioned management and by this I meant management of the users, mobilizing them to work together, to own the projects and also allude to financial management where there are funds raised in a facility maybe they are selling water, then the management of those finances which again can be traced maybe somehow to lack of skills in financial management.
Interviewer: What other recommendation?
Respondent: Then I have mentioned lack of resources to establish new water facilities. Of course this I would think the recommendation would be for like now the County Government to allocate more funds now that water is a devolved function, that the County Government allocates more funds towards establishment of new points and also encouraging implementing partners. Encouraging them to be in the county or in the sub-county. This is maybe by way of the government officers willing to work closely with the partners.
Interviewer: Any other solution or recommendation?
Respondent: Maybe for now those are the ones I have.
Question 3: Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps will happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: I think the most important would be encouraging ownership of the projects by the community members who are the users and my thinking is that when that feeling of ownership is increased, then these owners would want even now to gain the relevant skills and would even source for them feeling that this is their project, they would even mobilize even for resources themselves and this would ensure sustainability of these facilities. 
Question 4: What kind of interventions are you implementing in Kitui and what influenced/informed the selection of these interventions?
Respondent: We are improving access to clean water by constructing sub surface sand dams, repairing non functional handpump operated boreholes, and also equipping open wells. Previously we have also rehabilitated a number of handpump operated boreholes by installing solar powered pumping systems. We also have school WASH where we are providing water storage tanks and installing guttering system to help in roof water surface collection.
Interviewer: What informed the selection of those interventions?
Respondent: It was that from the sub county water office, we got that information from the sub county water office that there were numerous non functional handpumps in the community so the community members would come to the office and report that their borehole is broken and then there was that list and so when we came in, we came to the sub-county water office and we explained that we were here to do WASH activities so we were told that those were a priority areas instead of doing new facilities, we could repair those, rehabilitate and also on sand dams we were also informed that was a viable interventions, that those were good water sources in this area. Could be good for collection of rain water. Number two, on the sub surface sand dams the water office had also identified potential sites with the community members with the support I think of SEKU University or something and they had come up with a list of potential sites which numbered to more than 200. So we realized that that is a potential area to focus on. So we didn't have to think so much of what to do there was already something to be done.
Question 5: How has participating in the WASH forum influenced your organization’s interventions?
Respondent: Of course we have gained information on issues like water policy. I remember at one point we were also taken through the county budget so we were able to know what the county government is aiming at and we were able to align our activities with the government. I remember there was a time also in April of 2018 while at one of the forums we were informed of a number of facilities that can be destroyed by floods and actually we changed our areas of intervention. I remember we went to rehabilitate one borehole at Kalambani which was not in our original work plans but because of the information that we got from the forum then we were able to change.
Interviewer: How else?
Respondent: We also got to know about the county strategic plan. I don't remember for what exact period but it should be the current. And in that way we are able to align our work to that planning. The water forums have also been good avenues for interaction with other partners, knowing what they are doing and learning from them.
