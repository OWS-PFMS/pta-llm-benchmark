
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
The main challenges to water service in Mille town are: First, the capacity of existing water tank could not satisfy the needs of the entire population. There is very high population growth in the town but there is no change in the supply of water in the town. 
The town water utility office also provide water for other institutions that based in a nearby towns while there is a serious water supply problem in Mille town. Without satisfying the water demand of the community, the water is supplied to other areas.
The government should allocate sufficient budget to construct additional water schemes in the town. That is currently there is serious budget problem to engage in expansion of water schemes in the woreda.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
The woreda administration office, the woreda water office and the regional water bureau should coordinate and find out solution to the problems.
Sufficient budget should be allocated to dig new boreholes and to construct additional water tanks. 
There is one big borehole outside the town but it is non-functional for long period of time. Therefore the government should rehabilitate this borehole to increase the water supply of the town.
Budget is not allocated for the town water utility office. The office covers it costs by collecting water rents from the community. And the revenue generated from water rent is very limited. It cannot enable the office to engage on expansion activities.

The water supply is based on a water tank which is constructed some 25 years ago as a result it could not satisfy the water needs of the current population. In addition, the water is supplied to other places by neglecting the water demand of the town.    
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
The most important solution is to construct new water schemes and to build additional water tanks that have good water carrying capacity. To do this sufficient budget should be allocated to water office of the woreda. Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? First, we can plant trees in the town to minimize the aridity of the area. Recently even if there is initiatives to plant trees in the town, most of them are drying (dying) due to lack of water. We can also get sufficient water to keep our personal hygiene.
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service?  
Honestly speaking there is no coordination between stakeholders in the water sector. If there has been coordination among stakeholders, we wouldn’t face all these challenges. There is no coordinated effort to solve the problems related to water service in the woreda. What could be improved? The woreda water office should have strong link with the regional water bureau and other non-governmental organization to find out solution to the problems which I mentioned earlier.
There should also be coordinated planning and activity between stakeholders working in the water sector. So far there is no visible coordination to solve the town’s water problem. Without coordination we can’t bring sustainable solution.

5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
Without having sufficient budget the coordination alone cannot bring sustainable solution. Sufficient budget should be allocated to construct new boreholes and water tanks that can satisfy the needs of the community. We should also rehabilitate the existing idle (non-functional) boreholes to increase the water supply of the town. Therefore, coordination plus allocation of sufficient budget can lead to more sustainable water service in the woreda.
