
 

In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your town?

[Lack of public awareness] The first thing that I consider a problem to water service sustainability in the town is the lack of awareness within the community about how water is processed and found. Surprisingly, authorities, city house development office and municipality officers do not have enough awareness about such things. They were also being hurdles for the access and smooth distribution of water and they also do not give enough support that is required from them. [Service population incompatibility] We are trying to distribute as much water as we can in corporation with city municipality in order to solve the water problem. Throughout this city water is distributed in a small proportion and on a shift basis this is mainly because the water system was designed to supply water for 2000 people. The current population of the city is 5174 and this increment could be because people come from all places (50 kebels) as the city is the capital of the woreda. However, we do not have supporting resources such as administration, municipality, and house development that would best respond for the water infrastructure development inquiry. With (if) better administration and institutional change, we could have a support for our utility so as to fully address the water problems that we are encountering. [Remoteness/gravity of the sources] Construction is seasonal. Gravity goes 5.5 KMs outside the town and this has hurdles as this kind of construction takes a long period of time and the construction could get demolished by human and non-human factors before it is finished. There is also lack of power (pumping generators) in times of dry or sunny seasons and the power becomes better available during rainy seasons.

What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

5:00

For the awareness issue, I believe that integration and smooth communication with the residents is important. We need transparency and two way communication with the people. So far we have been isolated with others. Why we haven’t had this kind of communication is partly on us. We usually tend to address the problems rather than telling the reasons behind the problems to the people. We also need a lot of help from other stakeholders in terms of budget and materials. 

Regarding the service population mismatch, I have some proposals, including various solutions for the year 2010 EC (or 2018 GC). The first proposal to solve the mismatch problem working on our staff capacity and office issues since we have to start from the bottom. 

We need fifteen people in our structure; currently we only have six people in our staff. Along with this, we are planning to solve problems related to office space, as you can this, this space doesn’t seem an office (Belay: Of course, I have seen it and I am very surprised, frankly speaking the rented house doesn’t seem an office for such important utility, instead it is a storehouse, everything is there, pumps and other equipment, dirty documents, single table and two chairs….). Currently, we are renting from private landlords and paying them four hundred birr per month (which would increase to six hundred per month). I am suggesting construction of our own offices in our own space. This is our plan and will not be achieved without attention from other line office, especially the woreda administration and town municipality. If we are supported financially, we could cover our service costs and work hard for the sustainability of water. Current costs include salary for the staff, stationary costs, wages and allowances for daily laborers, spare parts, motor and fuel for the motor. The total estimated costs for these are about 351,528 Birr. Currently, we have revenue of about 71,980 Birr collected from the service we provide (for 314 domestic meters, 13 public institution meters and 6 private institution meters). As you can see there is an enormous gap between our revenue and the estimated cost which indirectly affects the service we are rendering for the large population in the town. Thus, by solving the basic manpower and office and its equipment issues, we are sure that we could have the capacity to solve the inconsistency between the growing population and the available water services. 

With regarded to the remoteness and difficulty of the water sources, we need help from South Ari administration council and other stalk holders since it is beyond the capacity of the water utility. We want the woreda council to manage the water resources and infrastructure development plans by board. The region has permitted grade four towns’ (Gazer) water infrastructure to be administered by autonomous boards. We had managed to protect our rights with regard to such administration by having our own budget. However, we face administration and execution challenges and so far no one took the responsibility to manage the water sources, and execute these rights. The rest of the support and collaboration we need from the municipality and house development come after someone takes the responsibility to execute our rights to be administered in board system.

Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

10:07

I give priority among these solutions to the effectiveness of our proposals, particularly the office and staffing issues. If we have adequate and skilled staff and better working place, we could potentially change the water service we have now to a better and equitable manner. To do this, however, financial, technical and material aid is very vital from South Ari administration council, woreda water office and other stalk holders.  In fact, we are receiving support from South Ari woreda water, mine and energy office. What would happen? If we get these kinds of support, I expect the following changes in my utility: first, we will be able to change the old infrastructure lines with the new ones, second, we will have more available lines instead of the very few ones we have now, and third, there will be less water intermittency. 

In addition, the seasonal variation of water services will be improved and water will be available regardless of the seasons and all the time. 

In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? What could be improved?

In my opinion, there is a huge gap between the required and the existing coordination among water stalk holders. We need people to be aware about everything in one hand but there is poor coordination among the water stalk holders on the other hand. One of the reasons for this could be because of the lack of central committee/board. South Ari’s water & energy council should take responsibility and intervene in helping us. What could be improved? Look, one of the main complains is water unavailability and intermittence due to the high population increment which couldn’t be solved with the current capacity of our utility. In order to improve this situation we need more and better lines, for instance, water line with one inch width need to be a two inch line to address the problem of water shortage. I think other stakeholders should take part in helping us, so that we’ll have solutions for our problems and we won’t be having a question of good administration. The other related problem is the water intermittence during dry or sunny seasons for instance if it is sunny for two days then there won’t be any water. The water lines originate from 5.5 KMs outside of town and have less gravity in these seasons. I would say, together we can solve the basic water service sustainably problems that I mentioned above, otherwise we will count years without change. 

In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the zone. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?

The main problem is administrative issue and I suggest that the water office is made autonomous and be managed by the board. This board will have all the authority to make better coordination with other stalk holders, including charity organizations. They will be led by the South Ari main administrator and it will have representatives from the residents. I think if we have a board, everything will be solved and better quality works will be done, moreover, there won’t be questions about maladministration. This board will consist of Woreda administration, two representatives from the residents and people from water and energy council. I think the composition of the board will bring better coordination among the concerned parties. However, we only have this ruling on paper for now and it hasn’t been executed. The ruling was passed by the region for grade four towns to manage their water by board. 15:00. If this comes to action, then the Woreda allocates the budget to the board directly and manages the finance according to the finance rules and laws. Additionally, the board will act as any other bureaus of the Woreda. I think this is the main solution. We can’t run in one leg and I can’t emphasize the importance of coordination more. The Woreda administration and other stakeholders need to work in collaboration. Other stakeholders to be included in helping out are NGOs and religious institutions. 

Another remaining point is that authorities need to understand these problems well. No one has well understood these problems despite our reports which we present weekly, monthly and annually. Usually, these reports remain in papers for instance, the Woreda had just started responding for the problem that we presented two years ago. I think people should not be passive about the problems they face. If the residents put all their problems in our shoulder and not actively engage in solving these problems, then it would take another time for the people to get the desired outcome. Blaming our office only won’t solve everything because some of the things are beyond our capacity to be solved on our own. All of the stakeholders need to take part and people should feel ownership of the water and take proper care of it. For instance, they should be concerned when it is unclear, runs improperly (leak out of the lines or pipes) and ask whenever someone do anything with it (such as misuse or even maintenance).
