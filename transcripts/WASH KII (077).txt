
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion, what do you think the main problem of lack of sustainable sanitation Debreberhan city? What do you think the main hygiene related problems in Debrebrehan city?  
Response: In my opinion, first of all the city structure is backward. Doesn’t have a modern system. For example the liquid sanitation system is doesn’t accommodate the city as a whole. The liquid sanitation built currently is not well studied. The place has a hard rock. It should be built on a place that has a soil rather than rocky area. At least the depth should consider the people in the area and while digging the hole for dumping site it should built with proper materials like soil and rock. Right now every individual dig a hole in the ground and use it as a dumping area for not more than a year and after a year prepare another one. This is not good for health. And it is one problem for lack of sustainable sanitation. Because every individual thinks for himself/herself only.    
Interviewer: you mentioned two problems. The type of the area in which the dumping area is built and the second personalized type keeping hygiene.  
Response: Rather than having two or three dumping hole in different area if there is one big hole prepared for the society as a dumping site there might be sustainable sanitation. Otherwise it would be boring for the society. 
Interviewer: Is there another problem as a city? If you can mentions problems as a city wise?
Response: I mentioned problems related with liquid wastes. Now let me mention problems relating with solid wastes. For example solid wastes are dropped everywhere in the city. This solid waste material is collected by group of youth who are grouped together by the Keble in the form of 1 to 5 or Development group. After they collected the waste material from different areas there is no provided place that they will dump all the wastes together. So they will dump it a little bit out of the city. This is also harmful for the rural area society. The other one is there is no parking place for cars in Debreberhan city.  Because there is no parking the cars will park anywhere they like and pea as they park on the streets. and throw chat garbage everywhere. This problem is out of our control. The other problem is big sectors and hotel doesn’t call the water and drainage service to bring a car that suck the liquid wastes and transport it to other area often. For example, in our area kebele 06 there is a compound that has a big population but the drainage system, the pipe is broken and it has been a while. So, the liquid waste is flooding the main road and we couldn’t handle the problem. We have reported to Mazegaja (City administration) and secretary office. But no solution has been given. The society is going to be contaminated. The city Administration should give quick decision on this kind of matters. Right now, there is no responsible or accountable body for this kind of issues. This should be solved.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: In your opinion, what solution do you suggest for the problems you mentioned above? To create sanitation sustainable service in the city what should be done? 
Response: The first thing, solid waste material should have one area to collect and to dispose in it. Waste materials should be collected in that area. Problems related with liquid waste should be solved by changing the health extension package itself.  
Interviewer: What does the current Health extension package do?
Response: To solve the liquid waste problem we try to dig a hole that is used for collecting wastes that comes in the form of liquid. This hole won’t last long because of the type of the land. It is rocky. It doesn’t sink the liquid so it will get full quickly and flood the area. So I recommend that if the Health extension Package is changed it will also change the way we dig hole. It will create a proper way of disposing liquid wastes.   
Interviewer:  Currently what is the Health Extension doing with liquid form of wastes?
Response: It is a study and provides a mechanism on how to dig a hole to collect liquid wastes. It was studied before 8 years and now it cannot handle our city’s problem. It doesn’t go together with the type of land we have. The hole use to collect the liquid waste doesn’t sink it to the ground. So, every year we try to maintain it but it will get full and create flood again. 
Interviewer:  So, having your experience, what do you recommend for disposing liquid type of wastes for the future?   
Response: The area should be studied well and before digging a hole it should be organized and studied well. There are small individuals who has a safety tanker for collecting liquid type of waste from their household or sector. But they don’t call the Water and Drainage service to send a vehicle and take their wastes to a place provided for this kind of wastes unless we give them warning. The safety tanker system is good but it needs capital and the society in most areas of the city doesn’t have that kind of capital. I don’t know may be their will be another system in the future.    
Interviewer:  Is there another solution you recommend to create sustainable sanitation? 
Response: In our case we try to create awareness on the society. I believe if we aware them the advantage and disadvantage then the society itself will do the rest. Additionally, if there is a tested and proven way of solving this problem the society will be very happy to follow. Otherwise, we can’t make the society follow and do the given tasks and they will not believe it will solve their problem. Regarding solid type wastes, I have mentioned already the solutions before.
