1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: One of the main challenges related to water service of the town is that the residential areas are not well planned and do not have proper sewerage line or system. Thus, the water supply lines frequently contaminated by wastes from residential areas. 
The condominium houses constructed by the town administration also do not have proper septic tanks and this create serious contamination of water supply lines.
The other major problem related to the town sanitation and water service is that almost no industries operating in the town have waste treatment plants. They simply release their wastes into rivers and nearby areas without any treatment. This industries’ malpractice is one of the main factors for surface water contamination and other sanitation related problems. For instance, one tannery opersting in the town directly linked its liquid waste with Beresa River without any treatment. However, this river serves as a potential source of water in downstream areas. Moreover, it contaminates the water supply lines that passes through this river. 
Interviewer: what other problems are there related to water service of the town?
In my opinion, currently there is no water supply shortage in the town but we might face supply shortage in the near future due to rapid expansion of residential areas and emergence large number of manufacturing and service providing firms.
Interviewer: what other problems are there related to sanitation service of the town (waste disposal and management)?
Respondent: The problems related to sanitation service are more severe that the problems related to the water service. In the first place, there is no appropriate waste disposal or landfill site in the town for both solid and liquid wastes. To alleviate this problem the town administration already identified a site but seeking for fund from the government as well as other potential donor to implement the project.
We are disposing solid wastes in a temporary and inappropriate place within the town but there is no place for disposal of liquid wastes.
In addition, the community lacks awareness on waste management and disposal. That is they do not sort wastes that can decompose and serve as natural fertilizer and those that can be recycled for other purposes. I believe we can improve the awareness of the community through awareness enhancement campaigns but the problem related to waste disposal place cannot be solved due to financial constraint and the economic situation of the country. 
Interviewer: Do you have anything you want to add related to problems of water and sanitation service of the town? 
Respondent: Water supply lines are not constructed based on prior study to minimize contamination of water. That is the water office develop new water lines without considering the possible water contaminations by drainage lines and ditches.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: You mentioned that the drainage lines are not well planned and frequently contaminate water lines. What do you suggest to alleviate this problem?
Respondent: Even though I mentioned the drainage and/or sewerage lines as a problem, it is difficult to construct sewerage lines in the town without having appropriate liquid waste disposal place. So prior to engaging on the development of sewerage lines, we have to have standard waste disposal site that have waste treatment system. Otherwise, the sewerage lines discharge wastes into the nearby rivers and create pollution on surface water and soils.
As a temporary solution I would like to recommend that service providing and manufacturing firms including well to do households should be advised to construct septic tanks within their compounds until we have access for standard sewerage system and waste disposal place to manage wastes and minimize sanitation associated problems.
Interviewer: You stated that the industries operating in the town are simply releasing their liquid wastes into the environment without any form of treatment. What do you recommend to alleviate this problem?  
Respondent: To alleviate this problem the biggest responsibility should fall on the government shoulder.  This is because to establish a new industry one has to submit its waste management plan along with its project proposal. Therefore, the government should have strong monitoring and regulatory framework to enforce sanitation and environment laws. The government should force the industries to develop appropriate waste treatment plants within each industry. 
On the other hand, the industries owners should not focus only on profit maximization rather they should give proper emphasis for the environment and corporate social responsibilities. Both the government and industry owners are reluctant to protect the poor from pollutions that emanate from industries. Thus, parties should be ultra-conscious in mitigating this environmental problem.
Interviewer: You said that the community lack awareness in waste management and disposal. What do you recommend to enhance the community awareness on waste management? 
Respondent: In order to enhance the community awareness we can do two things. First, the government in collaboration with other stakeholders can develop a waste management demonstration sites in different areas of the town. In these demonstration sites, we can teach the community how to sort or which wastes to be sorted. For instance, we can demonstrate which wastes can be recyclable and which wastes can be used for preparation of composts for urban agriculture. By doing this we can improve the waste management culture of the community as well as we can create income generating activities by engaging unemployed youths in waste collection and management business.
Second, those government personnel working at different administrative hierarchies should be exemplary for the community by properly managing their household wastes within their locality. For instance, if I can demonstrate how to properly manage my solid wastes with the available knowledge and resource for my neighbors, we can change the society’s awareness within short period. 
Interviewer: You mentioned that the town do not have appropriate landfill or waste disposal site both for liquid and solid wastes. What do you suggest to solve this serious sanitation service challenge?  
Respondent: Regarding the waste disposal site problem, the town administration has already identified a site but not engaged on project implementation due to capital budget constraint. It is argued that the project requires more than ETB 160 million to complete the entire project. Therefore, the town administration should mobilized fund from industries, service-providing institutions, potential donors, from the regional and federal governments and the community itself to engage on the project implementation within short period. If we have proper waste disposal site, we can alleviate other sanitation related problems of the town.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The most important solution to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town is to develop standard waste disposal site for both liquid and solid wastes and build appropriate waste management system to effectively utilize wastes as potential resource for agricultural and industrial production. If we can get solution for this problem, it will have the following benefit for the society. (1) we can minimize the health problems related to poor sanitation service; (2) we can also avoid water contaminations; (3) we can minimize soil and surface water pollution; and (4) we can create employment opportunity for those who can work on waste disposal and management and the waste itself can be used as industry and agricultural input.
