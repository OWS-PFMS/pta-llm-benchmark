
Interviewer:  My first question to you is, “In your opinion, what aspect of this network is working well in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: In terms of relationships, the issues on….This network generally is working, but there are weak areas that may need strengthening. For example, if you look at in terms of authority, there are areas that this office does not have authority over, which is good for proper coordination, then it should be enhanced. For instance, if you look at Tanathi, Tanathi are also doing some water resources, but I think, according to me that network is a bit weak, which needs to be strengthened. Uuumh…without pointing some areas, there are those that are working, like Kimwasco, Kitwasco, the network is ok. The WASH coordination is perfectly well, but generally there are some areas of strengthening in terms of the network for proper coordination.

Interviewer:  My second question to is, “What do you think are the main problems in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: One, there is no policy in place, that can hold the rural communities to account in terms of funds management and also that responsibility in terms of operation, you find that is one of the challenges, especially in keeping the finances and also books of accounts. You find that there are no proper records.

The second thing is the issue is the trainings cannot reach everybody. So you find that those who have been trained some of them, because it is on a voluntary basis, some of them exit. And given that they have constitutions, that in most cases they have to do elections after some time, so you see there is that succession, and then there is no continuous training, although we do train them, both informal, but not it…the schemes are so many that some of them cannot reach that continuous training over and again, and resources are also few.

The other thing that is affecting the schemes, the community schemes is the ownership issue. Although, during planning and everything they are given the opportunity to own these schemes, you find that there is also a gap. 

The other thing is, from the County Government side, the resources are centralized, and if these resources are devolved to sub-counties, you find that there may be a quicker response to these breakdowns and people monitoring, because there is an aspect of monitoring but that has not been very effective. 

There is also the issue of mobility; being an expansive county, there are also issues of mobility that is also affecting the way the schemes are operating, and that is tied to monitoring because they are not able to move as required, and to be inspecting the books, to be seeing that the water is flowing and whenever there is a break down, they are able to fix in good time. 

Interviewer:  My third question to is, “What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewee: One, as I have said is to enhance monitoring, which needs to be enhanced. If we devolve a little bit of funds to sub-counties so that we enable them to move, they will keep these communities on toes in terms of management. I believe that is one of the things that can help.

The other one is at least to anchor the water schemes, the community water schemes into a law which has not happened in the past. I believe that once that is done, then I can see them taking up shape in terms of management.

Interviewer:  Of the solutions you have mentioned, which do you consider to be the most important? 

Interviewee:  The most important to me is monitoring. Monitoring is key. Yes. 

Interviewer:  If the solution happens, what would occur next?

Interviewee: Once this is done, at least we can ensure that the water is flowing and communities have water. The break downs would be reduced and distances of course will also….people will not have to go so many kilometers when a system is broken down because if we are able to fix it within 3 days, then we are good to go. 

The other thing I think we need to do is to ensure that stores have some items that can help fix a problem within a few days, which we have actually taken a step towards that, we are trying to put some resources and have reserves within our stores so that we hasten the response.
