
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your woreda? 
The main challenges to water service sustainability in South Ari are: [The budget allocated by the government for the office is very small] the annual budget allocated by the government do not match with our annual work plan. Our annual work plan is vast but the budget allocated is very small. [Problem related to awareness of the community] the community expects everything from the government. Their participation in the development of water service is very minimal. They do not properly use water points constructed by both the government and non-governmental organizations. Their awareness on the use of clean water supply facilities is very limited. The other thing is we recurrently face budget shortage and this affects our ability to supply water in the woreda. [The settlement of the community] some people settled in remote areas where there is no infrastructure like road. It is difficult for aid organizations (NGOs) to transport materials and inputs to construct water schemes in such areas. This problem prevented the communities from getting access to clean water. Mostly aid organization develop water schemes in areas where there is road access.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
In the first place the government should allocated budget that matches with our work plan. The budget allocated should enable our office to accomplish 100% of our plan. For instance, if we plan about ETB 10 million budget to fully implement our work plan, the government only allocate about ETB 1 million. When we receive only 1 million from the total plan of 10 million, we can only engage in some maintenance activities and we cannot engage in expansion works. Thus, we recommend that the budget should be improved. Moreover, if the offices in the water sector utilized their budget efficiently, we can solve the problems related to water service sustainability.
Regarding the problems related to awareness of the community, our office in collaboration with aid organizations (NGOs) is planning to work on enhancement of community’s awareness. To do this we will gather the community in one place and teach on proper use of water facilities. Moreover, we should also mobilize the community to participate in water infrastructure development by aid organization as well as the government. Once the water schemes are developed, we should also create awareness on how the community manage the water schemes properly. We should also initiate the community to save money for the maintenance of water points. Some members of the community even do not aware of the values of clean water and water born diseases. Thus, we should also work to enhance their awareness on this front as well.
Concerning problems related to infrastructure (access to road), South Ari woreda is very vast and it has about 50 kebeles (the lowest administrative structure). In this fiscal year (2017/2018) the government has ambitious plan to develop rural feeder roads in the woreda. When it starts the implementation of this plan, I believe the problem will be solved. In this fiscal year we (the water office) have also planned to implement water service expansion activities. If God says, we will use our budget effectively and coordinate with aid organization to alleviate the problems related to water service sustainability. Currently, the government is alleviating the problems related to infrastructure (access to road). For instance, there was no road to Arke Kebele but very recently the government built a road that connect this kebele with the woreda town. The government also rehabilitate the roads to Wamer and Ida Kebeles. We believe that the infrastructure problem will be solved in this fiscal year (2017/18).

Belay raised additional question on problems related to community awareness as follows: You said that awareness is one of the basic problem in the woreda. Have you worked on awareness creation so far? If not, what is the problem?
I can say that we did not work on awareness creation in the woreda previously. But in areas where there is infrastructure (access to road) the community has better awareness compared to communities in remote areas (areas with no access to road). For instance, in areas where there is road access, the community regularly report to our office when they notice any problem on the water facilities. On the other hand, the community in remote kebeles do not report to water office when they see problems on water facilities. Instead of repairing the water facilities, they tend to use river water. But the problem with the water facility may be very simple, it can be fixed (repaired) by a material that can be purchased for only 80 Ethiopian birr. Recently, we have tried to teach these community to create awareness related to these problems. We have also a plan to work on awareness creation extensively in the future. If we create awareness on the community, the community will ask relevant bodies to solve problems related to water service.

3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
As a water office of the woreda, we give priority for the problem related to the budget. For instance, in the last fiscal year (2016/17) the budget allocated for our office was finished at the end of the first quarter. We had been without budget in the last three quarters of the fiscal year. Thus, sufficient budget should be allocated for the office. Moreover, the office should use the budget according to the plan. For example, the office should use the budget allocated for maintenance only for maintenance purpose, and the budget allocated for expansion should be used only for expansion purpose. Therefore, if we use the budget properly according to the plan, we can solve the problem related to water service. 
Due to the budget problem, there are no sufficient number of qualified personnel in the office to fully implement our plan. We require to have about 41 experts but currently we have only 16 professionals. We have a gap on qualified experts in the water sector. Thus, the office should fulfil the required manpower in order to ensure sustainable water service. If the budget problem is solved, the problem related to manpower will also be solved. In this fiscal year we have planned to hire about 20 professionals. We hope the government will allocate budget that will enable us to hire the intended professionals. We will also expect additional budget from AGP (Agricultural Growth Program). If the budget problem is alleviated, we can properly monitor and support water users associations functioning throughout the woreda. Moreover, the water service delivery will be improved in the woreda.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 
We believe that coordination with aid organization will help to alleviate the aforementioned problems. In the last fiscal year (2016/17) we had good coordination with other organization. For instance, After we started working with IRC Ethiopia, we could able to identify the number of water schemes currently functional in the woreda, the number of water schemes that are not functional, and the number of water schemes that can be easily repaired. IRC enabled us to have better information or data about the water schemes in our woreda. Our communication with other organizations, particularly with IRC gave us good input for our activity. Within the woreda we are closely working with the woreda administration office and agriculture office. This also strengthen our implementation capacity. However, there are still gaps related to coordination in the woreda. There is weak harmony between stakeholders in the woreda. In some cases some organization do not introduce their programs to the woreda administration office and other relevant stakeholders. 
What could be improved?  There should be harmony between organizations functioning in the woreda because all organization are working for one goal (ensuring sustainable water service). For example, each organization should introduce or share its plan and activity with other organizations functioning in the woreda. There should be a platform to evaluate plans of each organization and gather feedbacks on each plan. We should also have sessions to discuss on the implementation performance of activities and gather feedbacks from stakeholders. Previously every organization run on its way separately, there was no coordinated effort.  
How do you see this coordination helping to solve any problems you mentioned before? Coordinated effort can help much! Because if you work in coordination, you can accomplish things instantly. Moreover, tasks will be finalized within the intended time period. However, if you work separately, you may not address all issues. But if you work in coordination the work will be fruitful and the output will meet the target. In the past 4 months we have tried to work in coordination with the woreda stakeholders, and we have seen good results. We have also ambition to strengthen our coordination in this fiscal year (2017/18) with the stakeholders operating in the woreda. We have also a plan to work with non-governmental organizations as well.  
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how you believe this coordination would lead to more sustainable water service in the woreda?
In general working in coordination can save time and effort. If there is coordination, we all have sufficient information about the sector. It will enable us to know who is working with whom. We can also know which task is accomplished by whom. Thus, coordination is very essential. Coordination will strengthen implementation capacity of organizations.
