And I would say that the biggest challenge which our project face in terms of sustainability in our county is the management issues which majority of the projects in Kitui are managed by water user committees of which some do misappropriation of the funds and to that extent they make the projects less sustainable because of the finance issues. So mismanagement in that they have the knowledge through the training they are capacity built on but they misappropriate the funds.

Question 2: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?  
Interviewer: I'm sure you being in the WASH sector for long you have some ideas on how to address some of those challenges.
Respondent: I would start with the first point of mismanagement of the funds by the water user committee or the water committee. During our initial implementation of the project, if we take a committee then we train them, we register them through the culture office, then we sensitize them on the need of having a bank account. So whichever money is gotten from the project it's either stored in the bank and if any money has been used they should have a proper accountability of each and every single cent. So in that way we'll have addressed challenge one. The other issue of the knowledge gap is that for those committees rather than just focusing on the committee, you know we focus so much on the committee, we forget the entire members of the group. If the budget can allow we just capacity build or train every member so that if I exit and there is another person who has come in as the project operator, he or she will have the knowledge on how to do the operations. So instead of training the committee alone, we just train the whole entire group which runs the project.
 Interviewer: Any other?
Respondent: The other issue is just working hand in hand with the other actors within our county, because at Caritas, take an example us Caritas we don't have a budget which runs throughout the year. At some point we do exit. So if we exit and let's take an example the other actors or the other stakeholders they don't know what we were doing, the project will be left there and it will be termed as the Caritas project. But let's say we are working with the County Government, the other NGO's in the area, some have a budget so when we exist, they'll chip in even while doing the post monitoring after Caritas has left out. So close collaboration with other actors I think can address most of the problems facing sustainability of our WASH projects. Not working now independently as Caritas, we work as a team focusing towards achieving the same objective.

Let's say us we do the hardware part of it then the other stakeholders they chip in,  if they have a bigger budget we do now the training of the entire group. I don't know if you have captured the point.

Question 4: What kind of interventions are you implementing in Kitui and what influenced/informed the selection of these interventions?
Respondent: In Kitui Caritas has been implementing WASH infrastructure ranging from water supplies through boreholes, drilling and equipping of boreholes, and for boreholes we normally do solar equipping. We have also been doing sand storage dams whereby we do construction and also for the safety or quality of water we install a hand pump through off take well, we have also been doing shallow wells which are hand dug, and installing them with the hand pumps. We also do pipeline extensions from existing boreholes which were done by other actors or we also sometimes we do our boreholes then we do pipeline extension to other areas, we also do earth dams, which has bigger capacities to areas where we cannot get boreholes, we look for alternative sources of water intervention which can be done, and all those water works have been influenced by the need of the certain area.
Question 5: How has participating in the WASH forum influenced your organization’s interventions?
Respondent: Okay we have been participating in WASH Forums whereby all the stakeholders in the county have been taking place. So basically what we learned from the WASH Forums, we share reports, what other actors are doing and where they are and through that I think it has helped us as Caritas to avoid duplicating what other actors are doing. We also gain knowledge because they have their strategies on how they apply like this, like this so sometimes we not copy but learn from them.
