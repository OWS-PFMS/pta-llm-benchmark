
Interviewer:  My first question to you is, “In your opinion, what aspect of this network is working well in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: In my opinion, what is working well is the aspect of partnership; being able to share plans, which is normally conducted on a quarterly basis so that at least we are able to know what other partners are doing and where and we are able to link up with them, and whenever we realize that there is a project that is going to be done by a partner in a particular area and we are also targeting the same people, then we are able to come together and partner. This has been made possible through the Coordination office which is bringing WASH actors in Kitui together. I am referring to the quarterly WASH Forum.

Interviewer:  My second question to is, “What do you think are the main problems in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: The main problem in sustaining water services in Kitui I think is all about…the local capacity at the project level could be…is still low to the extent that you find at times when the project…let us say a borehole water project…there is a breakdown, let’s say it’s a motor, it’s a pump,…they are not in a position to fix it with the revenue they have been collecting over time. I think this is not a problem of just lacking sufficient funds, but it is a problem in the way the revenue generated from this project are managed to ensure that the water projects are sustained in the long run.

Another problem, I think, the area being an ASAL (Arid and Semi-Arid Land) area, at times like in Kitui South, the groundwater quality is always a challenge because some of the boreholes you will drill and find…even before you drill, when you do the hydrogeological surveys you realize that the groundwater is contaminated to levels that are not recommended for human consumption and this is even why we came up with the mega water project which we are tapping water from River Athi then pumping it to a treatment plant where we are able to treat it and then we target both Kitui Rural and Kitui South sub-counties. It is supposed to serve an estimated number of 100,000 people in the first phase. So I think the groundwater contamination is a challenge. But in areas you find that borehole water would work best but it is not viable because the water quality is not a certain…

I think the services, I think generally the proper management of the small water schemes is also something that is coming into play and if you would have a forum where we would be able to bring all these small water schemes together just like we are seeing…like KITWASCO you know. This is now a company, a water services company which is now able to provide water to people in a big area. If the schemes could be integrated into one company that would oversee all the services to the people, we would not have a situation where a water project has a breakdown and the community cannot get water because of maybe something small that could be easily fixed if the management was ok.

Interviewer:  My third question to is, “What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewee: I think I have already given some. Like the management, coming up with an umbrella body or a company that is able to focus and look at water service provision to the people. And then when we talk of the underground water, I have talked of tapping surface water, and even if we…being able… like we do train the communities on tapping of rainwater…the rooftop catchment rainwater harvesting which they are able to put to good use, like schools they are able to tap water during the rainy season that takes them up to around 3 months when they are hardly hit by the drought. So generally, it is looking for alternative sources in areas where we have groundwater being contaminated. And also capacity building the community to focus further in storing water during the rainy season that will be able to take them long. The other challenge I talked about, the first one, about the capacities. This is also just, going… entrenching ourselves into building the capacity of these communities. Because most of the time it is not that they do not know but when it comes to financial management is something that…I think is a challenge not only in Kitui but in most places. Yes, because there is the lack of transparency in the way…especially the small committees. In the large water projects we do not have a problem, like the companies we do not have a problem with how they are managing themselves. But now the small water projects is all about focusing…If we can work hand in hand with the local administrators and the government through the department of Social Services and…to ensure that the revenues collected from the projects are put to good use. A case in point will be like one water project in Kitui South, this is Kithini Water Project (if you have ever heard of it). This is a project that from their records I could see that 2 years back they could be able to deposit between 7,000 to 9,000 on a daily basis, that is just daily revenue that they are able to collect, and this is taken to a point where they have close to 2,000,000 in their account but then because of change of leadership when the elections come, during the AGM (Annual General Meeting), we find that they bring different people. When these people come with them they focus on whatever is in the account and how they want to use it, but now not forgetting that it is misused then the project comes and stalls. So I think it is just …us as partners getting deeper and making good follow up on these water projects towards how they manage their finances 

Interviewer:  Of the solutions you have mentioned, which do you consider to be the most important? 

Interviewee: I think that all of them are important because each and every solution is addressing a specific problem. Because, like management…like when you talk of capacity building on water management is equally important as looking for alternative sources of water because they are addressing varied challenges. But all in all, I think going forward, partnership is the key thing that I think we should focus on because when we partner we are able to achieve more. Most of the time we realize that when we are not able to carry out some projects because we have limited resources and coming together enables us achieve more and you know, when we work as a team, whatever I am not seeing you are able to identify it and we are able to address.

Interviewer:  So, are you saying that partnership would be more important than the others?

Interviewee: No, I am not saying that the solution on partnerships would be more important, but I am just saying that going forward, to achieve everything that I have just stated, I think we should uphold effective partnership among the partners. We just…from the map we have just drawn, you realize that the partners…the partnership we are having is not very strong.
