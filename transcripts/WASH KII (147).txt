
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the region?
The main problems related to water service sustainability in the region are: First, there is shortage of qualified and skill water technicians to properly administer the existing water schemes. There is also shortage of machineries and equipment to repair water schemes. There are 5 zones and 32 woredas in the region but we have only three cranes and very few equipment for operation and maintenance of water schemes in the region.
For 32 woredas there are only 8 water technician (electromechanical engineers) in the regional bureau. Thus, there is serious shortage of manpower in our bureau.  
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
I recommend the government to purchase additional machineries and equipment that will be used for operation and maintenance of water schemes. Moreover, the government should restructure our bureau and hire additional water technicians. It would also be great if non-governmental organizations support our bureau by providing machineries and equipment.
Even though we have shortage of manpower, and machinery and equipment, we are trying to manage the operation and maintenance of water schemes as much as possible.  
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
Of the solution I listed, I give priority for the problem related to machinery and equipment. That is we need to have sufficient number of machineries and equipment. Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? If the problem related to machinery and equipment will be solved, we will see significant change related to water service in the region. Currently, if there is a maintenance support request from different woredas at a time, we cannot respond to all question simultaneously. Some of the woredas stay without water until we finish the maintenance work in the other woreda. But if we have sufficient machinery and equipment, we can handle problems in different woredas at a time. As a result the community can get regular water supply.    
Moreover, if NGOs cooperate by providing materials and spare parts, we can enhance the water service in the region.

4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your region for sustainable water service?  
We are closely working with different organization functioning in the water sector. I can say there is good coordination between stakeholders in the sector. What could be improved related to the existing coordination? The water technicians at woreda level have serious capacity constraints (they have skill gaps), therefore, all stakeholders should cooperate to enhance the capacity of these woreda technicians.
Most of the technicians at woreda level are fresh graduates and have no experience to manage problems related to water service in their woreda. Therefore, there should be coordination between stakeholders to provide on job training to these young professionals.

5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the region? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
If there is coordination between governmental and non-governmental organizations in the region, we can sustainably alleviate the problems that I mentioned earlier. Coordination is very important to ensure sustainable water service in the area.
