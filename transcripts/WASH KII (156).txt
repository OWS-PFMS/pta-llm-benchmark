
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the Afar Region?
Challenging issues of water in my opinion are many and the first one is [there is no ownership over the boreholes]. It is common to observe that the community doesn’t feel responsible over the existing boreholes. This is indirectly attributed to lack of appropriate supervision and monitoring from the stakeholders’ side. The second one is that immediately after boreholes are dug, they would be directly launched for use without ensuring that every accessory is fulfilled. On the other hand, there is a situation when the community wouldn’t get water service because the boreholes are kept as they are founded for some time and the service will be started after a year or more. Thus, the government should make sure that power is shared fairly among the WASH stakeholders so that everyone could take responsibility in the water sector. Let us take the Semera – Logia case as an example, in this area water service bureau is found under the mayor’s office. Since this office is not supervised by any of the regional bureaus directly, and Semera gets water in shift, only 2 or 3 time a week. 
Therefore, [government’s lack of inspection and supervision, lack of timely supply of materials needed on the right time, and absence of strong managers and negligent supervision and monitoring system by the region] are all the major problems that causes unsustainable water service. The region will celebrate Nation and Nationalities day in the coming few months in Semera city, I wonder how could that be effective without adequate water service. The absence of water service is because of lack of government’s supervision and monitoring system coupled with lack of needed equipment provision at the right time. When one of the boreholes fails, there is a chance not to get water service. The second one is that the community [doesn’t know that they own the asset] because the community has awareness gaps. Since the community is the owner, it would have been good for members to ask why problems are happening and follow on the procedures. There are clear rights and obligations to the beneficiary but the community doesn’t know that this is a right to them to ask. 
The other challenge is lack of reserve pumps. Reserve pumps and spare parts should be kept as a reserve everywhere. There are consumable materials that should not be looked for when they are finished, there should be reserve of materials in the store. It should be kept carefully because there is no fast system that materials could be looked for during emergencies. There should be a system of supervision and monitoring for this too since there is nothing without water service. Water is life, with the right direction, what should be done should be done; what should be reserved should be reserved.

Activities should be done prudently and the materials needed should be kept always. The community should feel a sense of ownership and ask for their rights. This is true for zone and regional administrators. [The government doesn’t give attention, inspection, and supervision]. Also, the government doesn’t provide materials needed at the right time. This is the problem.
