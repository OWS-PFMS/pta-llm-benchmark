I1: In your opinion, what do you think has ensured Kamuli has regular running clean water?
R: The “Tibo group” was the first to bring us reasonably clean water. Here in Kisege however, we don’t yet have a borehole provided by the government. 
I1: What hindrances stop Kamuli from having clean running water?
R: I am not sure but I think our district leadership has not done a good job. I believe our leadership doesn’t care about the citizens of Kamuli. 
I1: What recommendations can you say will help this situation?
R: Asking for help from you professionals and organizations who reach out to us like this. Currently, our whole area has 1 borehole. Our children sometimes come back at 9PM because of the line and the wait it takes to get water. Sometimes other alternatives have clay in the water which is not healthy. The district leadership and the government should provide more clean water sources. 
I1: Which is of these recommendations do you believe is the most important? 
R: Provision of boreholes by the government and respective organizations.
I2: How do you find the current maintenance of boreholes?
R: Reasonably good. Since the existence of WAVE in Kamuli, we have had minor borehole problems relating to maintenance. 
I2: Any recommendations to help strengthen this good trend? 
R: We have been with WAVE for only 4 months, I don’t know how the future is going to work out. The fees we paid were for a year. It is therefore too soon to tell how the project is working fully or to make any recommendations.
