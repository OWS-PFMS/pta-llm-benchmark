R: We community members do not take good care of the borehole. But now that the WAVE organization is here, all we have to do is make a phone call and they promised to check on it and correct whatever issue the borehole is facing. 
I2: What solutions can we use to make sure our boreholes continue to provide us with clean water?

I2: And what are these teachings?

I2: Is the current maintenance of boreholes in your area well done?
