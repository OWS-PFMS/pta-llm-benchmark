1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent 1: The town water supply is based on electric power (based on the main grid). And the town face frequent power outage. Sometimes we stayed for long time without electricity. This implies when there is power outage there is no water service in the town.
Respondent 2: The water facilities do not be repaired on time when they get problem due to shortage of maintenance materials or spare parts. The regional water office do not respond timely to maintenance and materials support request of the woreda water office. Due to absence of regular maintenance service, some water schemes cannot provide service to the community. Moreover, there is no regular maintenance and follow up of water distribution lines. In some areas, water lines are broken and no one repair them timely.
Interviewer: So far, you raised problems related to water service. Do you observe problems related to sanitation service in your woreda?
Respondent 1: The town is established without appropriate master plan and it does not have sewerage or drainage lines. Because of this, there is serious sanitation problem in the town. Sometimes liquid wastes flood into residential houses. In some areas, there is incidence of contamination of water supply lines by liquid wastes.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: You mentioned that power outage is a serious challenge for water service of the town. What ideas or recommendation do you have about solutions to this problem?
Respondent 1: The existing water storage tanks have limited capacity compared to the size of the town’s population. Thus, to reduce the frequency of water service interruption due to power outage the town administration should construct additional water storage tanks.
In addition, there should be standby diesel generator to avoid water service interruption due to power outage. Sometimes we stay for more than a week without water and electricity services.
Interviewer: You have indicated that there is shortage of spare parts or maintenance materials for the water facilities as a result; the facilities could not get regular maintenance service. What do you suggest to alleviate this problem?
Respondent 2: The woreda water office has very limited budget for regular maintenance of the water facilities. Thus, the regional water bureau and other NGOs that work in water and sanitation sectors should cooperate with the woreda water office to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. The maintenance requirements of the water schemes is beyond the capacity of the woreda. For instance, water pumps may burn out and become out of function. Replacing these pumps is beyond the financial capability of the woreda water office.
Interviewer: You have mentioned that the town has no appropriate master plan and there are no any drainage or sewerage lines in the town. This created serious sanitation problem. What do recommend at least minimizing the associated problems?
Respondent 1: It is very difficult and costly to revise the existing master plan of the town. However, from now on residential houses and business centers should be constructed by following proper master plan and liquid waste disposal or sewerage system should be taken into consideration prior to embarking on construction. Sufficient space should be left for drainage lines both at the back and at front side of the houses. 
The other short run solution to this problem is enhancing the community awareness on liquid and solid waste management with the available resource and space. There should be some legal enforcement mechanisms on those individuals that dispose wastes in a way that contaminate the area. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent 2: The problems related to water service should get prior attention by all stakeholders. That is maintenance materials should be supplied timely and water schemes should be monitored and repaired regularly. If there is sustainable water service in the woreda, the rural community will not migrate from place to place in search of water. Thus, they can settle in one place, send their children to schools, and access other services like heath and adult education. This led them to better life.
