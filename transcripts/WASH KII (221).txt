1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation/water service sustainability in your town/woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the major problems related to water service sustainability in your town?
Respondent: in Gazer town there are certain challenges affecting water service sustainability. One of those problems is lack of adequate financing. This is because Gazer town doesn’t own a water source by itself since water comes from long distance (6km), crossing a river. The present water pipe is built 30 years ago and it has 2 inch diameter pipes, which doesn’t fulfill the current demand and incapable to serve the current population in the town. This hinders us a lot in providing sustainable water service to the dwellers of the town. Installation of new water pipe requires millions of Birr as it requires 4 or 5 inch diameter pipes. Besides, it requires additional tanker where we have only 3 small tankers currently. Therefore, installation of new pipes and construction of additional tanker have not yet started due to budget shortage. 
Interviewer: What about the second one?
Respondent: The second problem is the water service provision had not been managed by the board of directors as per the established principle found in the region and zone. As stated in the directive, the board has the right to collect cash from water service, subsidy from woreda council, and town council directly without interference. 
We had been in a difficult situation to even hire additional staff to run office activities properly and to buy some basic office level equipment, including at least one computer. However, starting the 20th of November 2019 onwards, the board has started to run independently, thanks to the advocacy work of IRC WASH in creating awareness among stakeholders in different meetings. We have made contract staff members permanent because of that and there are also a few improvements.


Interviewer: And the third one?
Respondent: The third problem is related to community attitude. Community members don’t have expected awareness level about their contributions and the requests they are asking.  It is not common to see popular participation in maintenance and management of the water schemes. Normally, the community should be able to notify us if there are any damages in the water supply system but at the same time, they have to take some risks in delivering service properly. There is also a gap to do at least a very small activity, i.e.  to construct fence around communal water points. To alleviate this issue, we have started discussions with different stakeholders. 
Another major problem is that we don’t have adequate trained man power and even on-job training is not given to existing technicians. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Let’s proceed to the solutions then; what do you think are the solution for the problem related to lack of finance?
Respondent: First, it is important to create sense of ownership among community members. The local community should do its share, be it finance or labor contribution. This is because owners and beneficiaries of the water service is after all the local community itself not someone else. For instance, the community has supported one spring development by cash recently. 
Secondly, regarding finance issues, I have written a letter for the different institutions located in the town to collect service fees since payments are not being made by public offices such as health, agriculture offices. A recent figure shows there are more than ETB 21,700 receivables from these institutions. Moreover, this year we have obtained ETB 100,000 subsidy from woreda administration though this has not been utilized in the first quarter yet. 
Another solution I propose is that we don’t have any computers or printers, even copy machine to modernize our day to day operations. We are writing proposals to request support from donors. If possible, it would be beneficial if we can get provision of loan to facilitate our daily activities so that we can pay back in certain time intervals. 
Stakeholders should support us in providing refresher trainings, as we have serious shortage of trained professionals.

Interviewer: How do you think the problems related to community awareness should be solved?
Respondent: Recently, we have seen some promising initiatives from few community members, but we are planning to call a meeting with top management, municipality, city housing management, spokes persons, kebele leaders, and CEOs to discuss in depth. I believe the directive should come into an effect. Despite it is stated in directive, it hasn’t started to take effect. There was no responsible body who implements the directive; consequently, we are still in trouble. 
Interviewer: You have addressed the issues related with commitment and communication, what should be done to address sense of ownership and awareness from the community side?
Respondent: This woreda town is an exemplary once for the rest of towns in the woreda; hence, it is impossible to address the problems in others unless they are properly solved here. Therefore, the management staff and the community should know what the directive says, and start to implement, at least to some extent. For instance, woreda administration has allocated ETB 100,000, but we have made this happen with a lot of struggle.  The board has already been established but it didn’t go through a full-fledged activity. Therefore, next year we will be able to work on other issues, and we are planning to discuss with the respective leaders. Town municipality should have allocated some budget for water supply but nothing is done so far so we will discuss with the municipality to address such issues.

3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned a number of solutions earlier, which is the most important, the one that potentially can resolve the rest of the issues?
Respondent: What I think to be the best solution is that all concerned bodies starting from the community to the different management levels should work in collaboration and develop the attitude “my contribution is vital”. If we initiate the community, we can do a lot of things. This is the first solution. We would have definitely increased water service coverage if can manage the habit of working together and the government took the initiative to lead this plat form.
