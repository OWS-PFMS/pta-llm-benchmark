
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer: In your opinion what are the main sanitation problem? What do you think the main challenges of the city to create sustainable sanitation? 
Response: There are many problems that can be mentioned. I will try to mention some of them. The first problem is lack of systematized ways of disposing waste materials. When we go to everywhere in the city, we can find waste materials here and there. Individuals clean their house and drop the collected waste in the main road. So, this problem i.e., having no coordinated system to dispose waste materials, becomes the main problem. There are associations or groups that aimed to collect solid waste materials and liquid type of wastes in the city. But the society doesn’t organize the solid waste by itself and the liquid waste on the other container. They just collect everything together. This is also a problem. The other problem is the drainage system doesn’t have a well-organized structure. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned important problems. But if you can add additional problem causes for example in relation to sectors or in relation to awareness or any other problem causes?
Response: I was giving you problem causes in highlight but when we go deeper the are different bodies that creates problem and that should be accountable. The first one is to make the city clean and create favorable environment there should a well-studied and well recognized plan by the city. Currently we don’t have that plan. The plans we currently have doesn’t contain all the city. It only considers the main road or a place that is well know only. And for that also there is no follow up.  The second one is the society in that area have no awareness on how to dispose a waste material properly. Even the educated person doesn’t have a knowledge how to keep hygiene. 

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer: Very nice. You have mentioned a number of problems and can you suggest what solution do you think can be applied for those problems?
 Response: If the problem is known the solution can be found easily. The main thing is to identify the problem. The first solution is to educate the society that how it is useful to clean and create sustainable sanitation is for the society itself. 
[00:05:03]
About environmental protection and sanitation, about the beauty of the city and other kinds of useful knowledge should be learned by the society. Without educating the society if the government create different policy and other bodies try different mechanisms, without the society it will not be successful. So, the first thing should education. The second solution is the responsible sector or body should do what is needed from it. To mention what type of tasks should be done by the responsible bodies, waste material collecting bins should be available in every corner, side ways of the road and in every needed places. The other task is to separate the bins in two categories for liquid type of waste collecting and for solid type of waste collecting. The other thing is there should be a mechanism to recycle and use those collected wastes for different purposes. For example, for generating power. We hear different countries use these wastes for different purposes. So, we also have to use them by recycling mechanism. This are the solutions I recommend. 
Interviewer: Can you say something on which body should take which task? What the government is responsible for and what the society and other bodies assignment? 
Response: There are different sectors inside the City Administration. For example, Mazegaja is one sector inside the city Administration. This sector should take responsibility on making the city green and attractive. To do this there has to be a plan and organized system.  The society should also take responsibility. The other important sector is Educational sectors. Their main duty to is to do research and study on different problems and create projects and provide community service. So, while doing these tasks, the sector should study what could be the main problem of lack of sustainable sanitation. There are many civil engineers, researchers and experts on how to create projects. These experts should be involved on the process of solution finding.

5.Can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable sanitation services in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before? 

Interviewer: What change could come if there is a coordination inside the city? If the sectors mentioned above come together and create a coordination on the implementation of the solution, what improvement will come? 
Response: This is simple. If there is a good coordination between sectors we will get good result. For example, Mazegaja or The City Administration by itself want to plant tree, prepare a mechanism for disposing waste material it will be very hard. But, if NGOs and other private sectors and other bodies are involved in those tasks it will be easy. It will also solve the problem that is created because of misunderstanding between the sectors. For example, because there is no communication the drainage built by Water and Drainage service sector will be damaged by Telecommunication Service sector, i.e., to install telecommunication line. But, if there is coordination, this will be solved and every infrastructure in the city will be built with planned drainage system.  
Interviewer: Do you think it is possible to solve all the problem mentioned above by having a good coordination? 

Response: Yes, most of the problems are related with lack of good coordination. So, I believe most of the problems will be solved as a result of having good coordination. If awareness is created on the society, if all responsible government and private sectors complete their duty we can create a beautiful city.   
Interviewer: you have mentioned earlier that one problem is having no plan to create sustainable sanitation. How do you think coordination will solve this problem? 

Response: The main thing, for example education sectors have a good experience in preparing strategic plan or short-term plan or urban plan. So, they could help the City Administration in this area. They could share time and other resources.
