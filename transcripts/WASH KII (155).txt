
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the Afar Region?
The first challenge for sustainable water problem is that the [underground water is salty]. This is why the water couldn’t be directly used for drinking purpose. Besides, as it needs deep excavation, affording the required heavy machineries require is the other challenge. As you know, the community settles sparsely and hence delivering the water to them would be another challenge. Though boreholes and other water projects are being constructed, they will not be used efficiently because they have to leave the area to look for grazing land for their cattle. But there are changes from the government side as it is facilitating the situation to make the community settle in areas where there is adequate pasture and water sources. Therefore, [the life style of the people] in the region is another major factor challenging the sustainability of water services. But for the future to give sustainable solution the community have to in some place and boreholes should be dug. Unless situations are facilitated to help the community settles there would not be sustainable solution. Would it be possible for the governmental and non-governmental institutions to work on systemic approaches to enable the community settle in certain places for relatively longer time that could bring remarkable changes to the current problems. 
What other problems are challenging sustainability? [Water should not be motorized] because it’s costly. Motorized systems should be changed with solar technologies. For example AMREF Ethiopia have been working with motorized schemes in several woredas and zones, like Abibara, Awash, Dulecha, Orgoba, Mille and Chirra located in zone 3. It would be nice if you have the time to visit Mille which is 8Km far. We are discussing with the woreda agriculture bureau to make the community able to use dug boreholes for the nomadic community, for their cattle and for their farms. We have started this. I think changing the motors in to solar system would be very useful and cost effective. This would help in sustaining water services in the area through taking the advantage of tropical nature of region. Individual experts should be trained in handling this system and if this is applied, the current situations would be improved because most of the time, we observe that the motorized systems are not functioning well. The other thing challenging water sustainability in the region is [Drought]. There was wide spread drought in 2015 in many parts of the region resulting in the sharp decrease of the volume of water schemes. 
This requires the need to focus on large and deep excavations as it is recently started by the UNICEF using GPS for their research. If this kind of system is applied by other stakeholders, works can be done in collaboration with UNICEF. [Lack of division of labor] among the WASH stakeholders is the other gap. Look, the budget needed for excavation might be too much and after the boreholes are ready, there may be scarcity in constructing extensions. In this case, it would be better to take the excavation activity by some of the stakeholders and the extension or the distribution task by others which are not involved in the excavation process. If this is not working for the remaining institutions which might have smaller budget for water works, they can involve themselves on community mobilization. Truly speaking, it is when the work is done by this procedure that we would have sustainable water service. Side by side, campaigns should be launched to aware the community so that they can feel ownership and be responsible to handle the water services. One of the reasons for the existence of unresolved WASH problems in the area is because the community here is uneducated; many of them can’t read and write. Above all, women and children are suffering from shortage of water services. They usually fetch water from very far places. 
2.You have told me the problem with their solutions. Of the solutions you have listed, which is the most important?
07:20
In my opinion the first thing that needs to be implemented is creating favorable conditions to settle the nomadic community in clusters/villages. Attention should be given to settling communities where partners need to work with the government in this process. It all needs the following preconditions: grazing facilitated for the cattle; well-structured living conditions, health and educational institutions are facilitated and availability of adequate water service. This would be help in sustaining water service. Changing motors with solar system would also help. Generators usually fail before the service period because the region is too hot and continuous effect of wind and dust. The community should settle and boreholes need to be dug. Attentions should be given to mobilize the community. If you ask why, for instance there are communities who live around rivers and use the river water for everything. For this, though it is costly, water purifying technologies should be adopted because you can’t dig boreholes everywhere. Along with the adoption of technologies, water harvesting methods should be introduced. It is pumping from the river and reserving it in reservoir so that the community could use for different purposes in a more sustainable manner, including irrigation. In other countries irrigations are done even from far areas. But there is no such trend. There is also the side effect of water harvesting for irrigation; it may cause malaria disease and for this too, community awareness should be practiced.  I think these things can improve the problems of sustainability we have now. 




3.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your region for sustainable water service? What could be improved?
10:14

We are in good situation related to this. There is monthly forum in water bureau where partners submit and discuss their annual plans and quarter reports at this meeting. All stake holders who are working on WASH meet on the forum, share plans and discuss promptly. But the problem now is capacity since there is no adequate finance; improvements are not coming as planned. The water bureau is executing its role of coordination except minor gaps. I can say that the strongest coordination forum in this region is the water bureau. The water bureau identifies the needs and priorities including where more drinking water is needed. One of the successful achievements by the water bureau is the work done for water harvesting and activities on shallow boreholes which would have significant roles in times of drought. This will help in assuring sustainable water services. Leading the coordination process, the water bureau provides the program, direction and support to all stakeholders and it consults partners on what, where and how to work on. 
What could be improved? 
12:58

The first thing that should be improved is the current state of coordination. There should be updated data and well organized coordination system. Adequate information and up to date data would help in settling the water services in specific woreda or kebele.  Well organized coordination and data system would help in Identifying priorities like which woreda or sector has more problems and which NGOs are performing on what specific activities. If problems are identified more private investors might be attracted to water projects. The way information is used must be improved. Also we need researches and data on the location and depth of underground water which would be realized with improved coordination. 

5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the zone. For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
14:18

Unless there is improved coordination, all water works will have no or little value. It’s just a waste. If all stakeholders work individually, it will be waste of resources. But, when there is effective coordination system on water projects, we can meet the community needs. I can surely say that working separately can’t bring sustainable water service. Coordination results the community to feel and develop a sense of ownership. One thing that should be coupled with strong coordination is regular monitoring and follow up of the existing water projects.
