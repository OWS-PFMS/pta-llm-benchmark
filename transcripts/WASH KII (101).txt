
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
I.Lack of community awareness on sanitation related problems
The main sanitation problem especially in our town is lack of community awareness. The community is not well aware of sanitation related problems and its consequences. On the other hand, there is limited activity by stakeholders to improve the awareness of the society on sanitation affairs. In my opinion, unlike other big towns, there are no strong institutions that work on sanitation in Debre Birehan town. There is no responsible body at the town level to create community awareness and to control those that release wastes negligently in the community. Recently we have started discussion with different stakeholders to improve the sanitation situation of the town, as the issue should not be left for one institution. However, there is no resource or budget as well as program dedicated to improve the sanitation service of the town. 
II.Absence of standard waste disposal site and system
Generally speaking, there is no appropriate waste disposal site for both liquid and solid wastes in and outside the town. This makes the sanitation service very challenging thus it requires considerable attention and immediate action.  
Related to water services as such there is no major problems in this respect. Moreover, there is an ongoing water service expansion project, and it is expected that it will satisfy the needs of new villages and emerging industries. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
To solve waste disposal site and system problems I would like to recommend that: 
Standard waste disposal site should be constructed/prepared
Awareness enhancement training should be provided to community and service providing institutions (like hotels and restaurants) and there should be law enforcement mechanism to control those that violate sanitary regulations.
Community education and training should be provided to aware the society about the risk of inappropriate waste disposal practice on health and environment.
3. Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Preparing waste disposal site is the major burning issue that should get priority to ensure sustainable sanitation service in the town. If the waste disposal site problem is resolved, the community will benefit the following:
The town become clean and comfortable 
The town will have good image and be a destination for tourists 
Ensue the community’s all rounded  benefit
