Question 1: In your opinion what do you think are the main challenges to water service sustainability in Kitui County? Could you explain in detail how these challenges influence water service sustainability? 
Respondent: Okay, we have several challenges which some of them we can be able to tackle and others maybe it needs other stakeholders to help in tackling them. First of all as I highlight what is Tanathi. Tanathi is an agency which formed through the Water Act. Since it started in Water Act 2002, we have Water Act 2016 which reinforced it and aligned it with the constitution 2010. So currently in the Constitution 2010, we are an agency of the National Government, whereby now we used to be Water Services Board, whereby we were handling the water works, and the water services. Those are two activities; water works and water services. That time we could implement and manage a water facility. Currently our work is infrastructure development. We only lay down the infrastructure of the water systems and hand them over to the County Government of the four counties we have, the Kitui, Machakos, Makueni and Kajiado. So us we don't do service, we do infrastructure whereby we implement, then we hand it over to the County Government, the County Government hands it over to the water providers where those are the water companies. Others are handed over to the community where we don't have a water provider nearby.
 So we have two managements of the water facilities. We have water providers and community water. So you can see us we are not entering anywhere in the service. We are not in the water service we are in the water works whereby it's that development of the infrastructure. So it is hard for us to start managing the water on the ground. That's why maybe we have a challenge of sustainability of the water facilities. But in that what we do, we do a baseline training of the community. That's just a one day training where this is what you are supposed to do. We just give them a way how to start up. So the county should train beyond there and should continuously train until the community comes up with the knowledge of managing the water which is rarely done because of lack of resources. Currently Kenya we have a high hope that our people are more educated of which is not. And if it is education, I think our education it is not rhyming with the things on the ground, let me say so.
