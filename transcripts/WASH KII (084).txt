1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: In your opinion, what are the most prevalent problems in sustainability of sanitation services in Debrebirhan town?
Respondent: There are many problems that hinder the sustainability of sanitation services. One of the problems is the lack of awareness. 
Interviewer: What does lack of awareness mean? 
Respondent: There is a lack of awareness among the town administration and community on the significance of the issue of sanitation. This pose to plenty of challenges on the issues related to coordination, management, and budget for the programs. The reason that made the community have a low sense of ownership is the lack of awareness on the subject of sanitation and hygiene. 
Secondly, there is poor coordination among stakeholders. All concerned bodies are members of the learning alliance. However, due to lack of coordination, the learning alliance members could not fully support the improvement of the sanitation situations of the town.
Interviewer: You have said there is lack of coordination. In what areas is this poor coordination is affecting the sanitation conditions of the town? 
Respondent: When I say lack of coordination, there are offices and stakeholders that are engaged in the sanitation area. These stakeholders have not prioritized the issue and act accordingly. For instance, we have not discussed the sustainability of public latrines in our town. So, poor coordination has been one of our problems.
Third, we have a problem in the structure in our office. We have a very few manpower compared to what the structure of the office requires. For instance, vacuum truck coordination is one process, but we have no expert in that process. So, we have the problem to fill the office positions as per the requirement of the structure. We have a human resource gap.
The other problem is that the currently existing liquid waste dumping site is already full. We are trying to prepare another disposal site. But, the process has been very slow because our coordination and awareness is poor. 
Likewise, we only have one government owned vacuum truck. This led to shortage of vacuum truck in the town. This has been a huge problem on top of the absence of proper disposal site. 
The sanitation section of the water utility office is not getting equal attention as the water utility section in terms of budget and other things. 
The sixth problem is lack of budget. 
Interviewer: When you say lack of budget, is it the budget that is allocated by the town administration, zonal administration, or regional administration? Where is the problem?   
Respondent: It is the problem of the town administration. Any town needs a sewerage line. However, Debrebirhan town does not even have the plan of a sewerage system. Sanitation is not getting the attention it deserves. The problem is beyond the capacity of the town administration. Most sewer pipes get clogged during the rainy season and then people dump wastes in the open. This should have been accomplished by the town administration, an individual sector could not allocate budget and do the work by itself. 
The last problem is related to management.
Interviewer: Is it different from the first problem related to awareness? Can we see it together with that?
Respondent: Yes, if you can. In my opinion, the lack of properly utilizing the already existing resources is a management problem. these are the problems that I found to be the major ones. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: So, let’s proceed to the solutions. You have said there is lack of awareness both in the community and the administration. What would be the solution for this solution? 
Respondent: What I consider as a solution is continuous awareness creation. There have been attempts by different partners. I have been participating in some awareness creation efforts organized by TETRATECH. Such awareness creation efforts should be intensified; because there is significant turnover among management and experts. Therefore, NGOs should be actively working on the matter. For example TETRATECH has been training management, experts, and community members separately to raise their awareness. If they have the awareness they will be able to implement their tasks more effectively. The awareness creation process should be all-rounded and have continuity and schedule. This helps the target community and management understand and implement the desires practices to the level that the practices become a culture. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned about lack of coordination as a second problem.
Respondent: First, as I have said there is a big turn over. Besides, when some managers are appointed, there is a mismatch of their expertise and the management position they are assigned to. Such management will definitely have a lack of awareness to perform its tasks fully, let alone creating a coordinated effort. For instance, before selecting landfill sites, environmental impact assessment experts should participate in the process. Lack of coordination also affects the timeline of projects. Many projects have been delayed because respective experts did not put in their efforts in a coordinated way within the time limit set. 
Interviewer: What gap should be addressed?  
Respondent: First off, there should be a focal person in every office that works in the sanitation issue. Besides, a steering committee could be formulated to coordinate the efforts. Each focal person would bring plan of action and integrate the activities and schedule. After that they can evaluate their progress and solve issues together. 
Interviewer: Has it not been assigned yet? 
Respondent:  Even if there are focal persons, we have never met because we do not have the awareness. 
Interviewer: The third problem is a lack of human resource  
Respondent:  Lack of human resource is comes from not paying due attention to the area. If there is not enough human resource it is difficult to bring the desired results. Therefore, adequate manpower should be assigned as much as possible. 
Interviewer: Fourth problem is lack of liquid waste disposal site.  
Respondent:  Debrebirhan has been growing and is almost becoming a small city. There is huge number of immigrants and investors coming to the town. The capacity of the social services that we provide does not match with growth of the town. Therefore, new plan should be devised and implemented to cop up with the situation. There should be adequate sewerage system, public latrines, and waste disposal site. Town plans should be revised every ten years. 
The other thing is about vacuum truck. There is a project called second urban water supply and sanitation project. I have information that one vacuum truck will be purchased. Therefore, we should push the project managers to facilitate its procurement on time. We can also buy additional trucks to provide more broad services to the community. There are around six privately-owned vacuum trucks also, but they are a bit pricey. There are two government-owned vacuum trucks; only one is functional. 
The final one is management. If there is poor management nothing will succeed. If there is no proper management of the landfill, waste treatment plan, and public latrines and communal latrines we will not be successful. 
Interviewer: What do you think should be changed?    
Respondent:  The problem of awareness should be addressed. 
Interviewer: What do you mean by that?   
Respondent:  There is open defecation practice in our town. Let me give you some examples. Our university has a secondary level treatment plant, which should have been a tertiary level treatment plant. However, untreated wastewater is being released to the nearby rivers There are latrines built by the government and NGOs. But, almost all of them have some problems; it might be that they do not have a door, electric system, water so on. This is because they do not have much attention to the community and the residents of the town. Assigning a focal person and mandate the focal person on key issues is vital. Then, there should be accountability and administrative corrective measures should be taken if necessary. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned the last solution. And the last question says of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? What next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: Committed leadership is crucial. This helps to create a strong system that allows the achievement of major sanitation goals. Universities could also play vital role in the process. 
Interviewer: So, in summary, if awareness, system, and managerial commitment are improved all other problems will be resolved.
Respondent: The awareness I have obtained from efforts from partners such as TETRATECH made me committed and changed me this far. So, if other concerned bodies also have the chance to raise their awareness they will be better committed and accomplish more towards achieving sanitation goals.
