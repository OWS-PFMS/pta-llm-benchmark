1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda? 
Interviewer: What do you think are the main problems that hinder the sustainable accessibility of water services in your woreda? 
Respondent: There is water service access to a certain extent. However, the water distribution system has served many years. The pipes are too old that we have observed frequent breakdown in the distribution system. 
Another problem is related with lack of manpower. The absence of adequate technicians has prevented us from providing better services. 
There are a few water wells developed in the town but, we couldn’t make them accessible to the residents due to lack of resources. Besides, there are hand-dug wells and solar panels in the rural areas, however, they encounter frequent breakdowns. Since there is lack of technicians, it has been difficult to conduct maintenance timely. These are the problems that are hampering water accessibility in the woreda. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned three main problems. What other problems are there, in case you have forgotten any before we proceed to the solutions?
Respondent: The town’s water utility office is currently running only with the revenue it collects. There is no financial support from the government side. Due to this, the office has budget and resource constraint issues. These problems made the town administration unable to install water distribution systems even when water schemes are already developed. As a result, even the water that we currently have is not accessible to the community. This is because of lack of sufficient resources. The water utility office cannot self-sustain with its current revenue. The income being collected from water service provision is not just enough. Therefore, the woreda and regional water bureau should support the town water utility office at least to some extent.  
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: let’s walk through the solutions one by one. The first problem you mentioned is the water pipes are too old. What should be done to address this problem?
Respondent: The water utility office should get sufficient resources and materials. Besides, capacity building efforts should be made to create adequate trained manpower. There should be enough technicians who can undertake maintenance services whenever there is a problem. There are only two plumbing technicians in the town.  
Interviewer: Tell me for the urban and rural separately.
Respondent: The biggest challenge is in the town. The woreda’s water utility office has experts and whenever there is a problem in the rural kebeles, the office can send those technicians using the office’s vehicles. The office normally responds to the technical issues within few days. The hardest issue is in the town, regarding lack of trained man power. Therefore, the town water utility office should obtain assistance in so far as budget, resources, and manpower capacity building. 
Interviewer: Who should be working on this? Whose responsibility is this? 
Respondent: I think the woreda’s water utility office focuses on the rural kebeles and doesn’t put much emphasis on the town’s water provision problems. Thus, the woreda’s water utility office should support the town. 
Interviewer: Does the woreda water utility office have the capacity to do so?
Respondent: Despite the woreda is not capable of supporting the town budget-wise, it can provide technical support in terms of expertise, materials, and other things. I don’t see that the woreda’s water utility office and town’s water utility office are working together; therefore, they should work in coordination. Apart from that the woreda administration should work on this with great emphasis. 




Interviewer: In the rural areas, you have mentioned earlier that there are hand-dug wells. These water wells are experiencing frequent breakdown which affects the sustainability of the water service provision of the woreda. What should be done to address the problem?
Respondent: There are WaSHCOs at kebele level. Capacity building efforts should be put on the committees. They should be capacitated enough to discharge their responsibilities; because there has been some gaps in terms of sense of ownership. This might be due to lack of awareness to protect the solar panels and water schemes. Therefore, kebele WaSHCO should be able to guard the water points and solar panels and awareness should be created to do so. 
Interviewer: Who will be responsible for this? 
Respondent: Water utility office will take the responsibility.  The water utility office is the one who organized the kebele WaSHCO and it should take the responsibility. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned that many of the problems are in the town. They also told me that they have budget and manpower shortages. What do you think should be done to address these issues? 
Respondent: Some NGOs are working in our woreda in the lowland WaSH project. However, much of their emphasis is on the rural kebeles. There has been no support from these NGOs to the town’s water service utility in terms of technical, financial and material assistance. However, the woreda has undertaken a study to solve the problem. Based on that study the woreda administration should identify the main problems and put solutions to address them. 
Interviewer: Has the study been already completed?
Respondent: The study has already been completed and the findings have been communicated with the regional water utility office. 
Interviewer: Is the study specific to the town or the whole woreda?
Respondent: I think it is specific to the town. 




3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned many problems and solutions. Out of them which one is the best solution? 
Respondent: There are some water points that have already developed. What they need is just installation of the distribution system. The installation work is much simpler than scheme development. Therefore, the woreda water utility office should be able to complete the installation of the already developed water schemes. If the problem in the town is solved, many neighboring kebeles could use the water points in the town easily.
