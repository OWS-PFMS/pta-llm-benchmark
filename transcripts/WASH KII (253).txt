
1) Interviewer: Okay, my first question is what are the main challenges of providing a sustainable water service in this town? I just want your opinion.

Response: In my opinion, I think there are some problems on getting sustainable water service in Woliso town. You know we have some waterholes around the town, but these waterholes were dug many years back and are incompatible with the current population of Woliso. The population has doubled up since the waterholes were created and that has brought the water service shortages we see today. Like you see nowadays the water service has to be rationed...

Interviewer: You mean there is water service disruption? 

Response: Yes there is disruption sometimes. And maybe you have noticed that the surrounding of our town is held by farmers who use irrigation system. So when it's summer and the farmers are using the water service for irrigation, the town experience disruption of service. So in the daytime, the farmers use the service and when night comes it will be our turn to get water. So this shows that we have a shortage of water because we are using the water service both for the farmers...

Interviewer: It will be used for irrigation and also for the community use.

Response: Yes.

Interviewer: So since the farmers irrigate on daytime, the community gets the water service at night time only.

Response: Exactly. And the other issue is that I think the water service is related with electricity.

Interviewer: Oh so you say the water service need electricity to run.

Response: I think so because when electricity service, especially on the hillside, disrupts the water service also disrupt.

Interviewer: The water is not pumped up...

Response: Yes that is the case. Since the population is growing up fast in the past two years, we see some part of the town experience a shortage of water service for like whole fifteen days. 

Interviewer: Are you saying the electricity service disruption is the reason?

Response: That's the main reason. Of course, the other water service related challenges also play a role here. I know the Water & Drainage Authority is doing its part. But this is about capability. It is to my knowledge that last year they have planned a project to emphasize capacity and they have given tender to consultants and put aside a budget for it. We need to boost the water service so that it can fit the current population. The waterholes, pipelines and related materials are designed for much less population than the current population we have.

Interviewer: Good, do you want to add more challenges?

Response: [05:00] In related to the water service issues, sometimes I see a problem of planning. You know we have pipelines dug up everywhere. And with the current growth of the population roads are built everywhere. Now with building roads, they break the pipelines on the way and you see a part of the town getting a water service disruption. This problem occurs when those sectors building infrastructures do not have coordination and well-organized plan. So one sector will dig and while waiting for the other sector to implement its materials the hole will attract flood water and bring disease. Even after that hole is fixed, another sector will dig again to put its materials and all problems will rise again. 

Interviewer: Coordination...

Response: Yes, if they have coordination, they can plan to dig together and put their materials together so that there will be no disruption in the meantime and afterwhile. And also when they plan to implement a project, they should consider the growth of our town population. We have so many resources of water in accordance to what our experts tell us. 

Interviewer: So now you have stated three challenges. The first one is about the ration system of water, the second one is about the water service not fitting the population growth and the third one is about planning. You said the sectors are not planning together to get more work done, instead of when one is building...

Response: …the other one delays.


2) Interviewer: Okay. So in your opinion what should be done to solve those problems you mentioned?

Response: First, I think we cannot say that the farmers should not use water services for irrigation because they have to produce and that's what feeds the town community. So this rationing water between the farmers' irrigation system and for the residents can be the only option for now. But in the future, we should add more waterholes and those waterholes should be specifically used for irrigation only. They have to budget for this issue and plan it well so that it fits with the population growth. That will be a good solution.

Interviewer: You mean digging up new waterholes so that the farmers can use that for irrigation and the current water service will fully service the residents?

Response: Yes. And these coordination issues with all sectors in regarding infrastructure development should have a task force which includes representatives from all sectors that will plan how to dig and put their materials together without disrupting each other's services.  

3) Interviewer: You have stated two main solutions. The first one is to increase the water resources so that the growing population can be accommodated to water services. And the second one is about coordination between sectors that are involved in infrastructure development. Now in your opinion which one should be prioritized? Which one is more burden issue?

Response: The burden issue is the first one. Since the population growth is continuous [10:00] and we are far behind on fitting our services to the residents, it should be priority number one and should be started immediately. Then while we are solving this issue, we can plan on how these three sectors should come together and work together...

Interviewer: Who are you referring to by saying "these three sectors'?

Response: Electric Power Authority, Water & Drainage Authority and Road & Transport Management Authority. These three sectors with the City Council should come together on solving service issues by planning and implementing together.

Interviewer: In related to this question, what do you expect to see in the town if the solutions you recommend are implemented? What kind of change do you see in the community?

Response: The first one will be that the community will start to get adequate water service. When there is adequate water in the community, the lifestyle of the people will start to change. People are wasting time looking for water all over the town. If the service improves and they get it direct from their tap, then they will have enough time to work and improve their lifestyle. Second, it will minimize cost very much. You can guess the cost of fixing broken pipelines and other water service related materials. I can say also the beauty of our town will flourish. When every infrastructural development is done according to a plan and people are less fixing things or looking for water and more earning income, our town will flourish with many kinds of developments.


4) My next question is about coordination between the sectors in order to sustain water service to the town. Tell me your opinion on the coordination issue.

Response: Well I say the water service we currently have is good in related to our capacity. I am saying the service should improve by increasing capacity...

Interviewer: I was trying to ask about the working relationship between the sectors. How is it? Is it good?

Response: The coordination is okay. I admire how the Water & Drainage Authority tries to create awareness in the community and also to the city administration. The Authority explains how shortages happen and let the community know about the updates. So once the problem is identified we report it to the head office in the State and we also ask budget like currently, we are requesting a budget of 150 Million Birr for various projects. We have already supplied tender to respective consultants and also finished designing. So this shows that the respective representatives have given it focus to solve the water shortage issue for once and for all. I was just mentioning challenges we were facing on the matter, but now I think it's been given priority and every sector is working on the issue. [15:00]

Interviewer: Okay, so there is coordination. So the problem is...

Response: It's the planning. The problem is planning it on the ground. When we facilitate the project on the ground, every sector should come and do its part so that the project should work flawlessly. If the Electric Power Authority digs a hole to put the wires the water Authority and The Road and Transport management Authority should work on it together so that tomorrow when we plan to install pipelines in that area we will not damage the pole. These sectors should plan together such activities to minimize cost and not to make further damages. 

Interviewer: So these are the coordination mistakes you mentioned earlier. 


5) Response: My last question How is this coordination will help to implement the solutions you have mentioned earlier.

Response: It's cost-effective. For example when a road is built, if there is no coordination with Water & Drainage Authority, they might break the pipelines. The water leakage will bring damages as well as people will have disruption of service. After a while, another cost will fix that broken pipeline. But if there is organized planning, such cost will never occur and the main thing is that there will be no disruption of services at all. 
Interviewer: I would like to thank you very much.     

[end time]
