
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in Debreberhan ? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
In our city there are a lot of bottlenecks but the following can be mentioned as front- runners:
Not Paying a Significant Attention:  Our church firmly believes making  the community be aware of sanitation and creating an environment for   every stakeholder  to participate on the issue is an ultimate solution to make water service sustainable; however,   proper attention has not been paid in this town , and sanitation problem is becoming the main issue. 
Lack of sufficient dustbins in the city: Dustbins in this city are hardly available which is one reason why water is being contaminated by hazardous wastes. Due to unavailability of dustbins, waste is being thrown into open ditches which are becoming a challenge for water service sustainability.
Inadequate Water Supply:  Due to the increasing urbanization and industrialization, the water sources of the city is becoming short of the increasing demand. This problem during the last 3 to 4 years is becoming more pervasive, and some areas are even not getting water for long period of time.  Besides the increasing urbanization the encroachment of the water sources by universities, industries and other big plants is causing severe challenge water service sustainability in this city. This shortage is also causing a problem on the public health because the pipes are being exposed to rust on water shortage seasons which is becoming a source of epidemics.
 Lack of  sewage treatment plants( STP):-  the city has no sewage or effluent treatment plant since it is becoming impossible for the community to get rid of its household wastes. The only way so dispose waste is to pay more than 2000 birr for a single service of dumpster truck and it is  very expensive for the community.
 Shortage of sewage dumping truck:  There used to  trucks  with the municipality giving dumping service but currently those vehicles. The reason they put in place is the vehicle need spare and maintenance. So, this problem is  a big challenge and cause for water service sustainability.
Availability of open defecation in some areas  which include misusing the toilet improperly is also  among the factors that compromise the sustainability of  water service.

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Putting dustbins on waste -prone  areas
Allotting sufficient and  affordable sewage dumping trucks  
Making the community aware of   solid waste and sewage nature & management. 
Giving attention on the severity of the issue or making water service sustainability a major development agenda.
Coordinating the community to participate on fund-raising  resources for water service sustainability.
Meaningful stakeholders’ collaboration. For instance, our church is very welcoming and can contribute a lot in this regard.
Managing road-trade wastes by assigning responsibility to those traders.
Developing legal framework for water service sustainability and waste management.
Developing landfill sites( dumping sites)
Managing mendicant food thrashes appropriately without spoiling living areas
Developing latrine management system: _the main challenge in our church related to water service sustainability is latrine management;  so getting proper latrine management can be taken a significant step in solving the probem.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Most problems related to water service sustainability is related with water shortage and quality, so solving water problem is the ultimate way to solve water service sustainability.  In mitigating the problem, the following should be part of the problem:
oParticipating the community in raising the necessary financial resource or working capital 
oTaking proactive measures like predicting additional water demand that comes along with urbanization and industrialization.
oInitiating institutions to have their own water point.
