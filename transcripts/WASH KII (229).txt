
1. In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation / Water service sustainability in your woreda?  Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation / water service sustainability?
I can say that internal and external challenges are affecting WASH service sustainability in the town.  We have been using water for the last seventeen years from a single water source which was built on a river, this is part of the external challenge.  The same source is being used for the current population. Our internal problem is mainly the technical inability and lack of coordinated plans and actions to act on a problem that involves larger budget and resources. 
Interviewer: What is the reason behind your inability and lack of coordination? 
 Basically, there is shortage of human power with technical capacity in water and sanitation office.  On the other hand, due to lack of water treatment plants in and around the water source, the underground water gets contaminated easily and we are suffering from lack of technical advisory to treat this water. Of course, the problems are all related to the limited budget we have from the government. 
Interviewer: What does the sanitation problem in the town look like? 
Yes, there is a serious solid and liquid waste disposal problem in the town. In the town’s master plan, new and old land fill sites are clearly indicated though appropriate compensation payments are not yet completed. We could have removed all the wastes thrown surrounding the town if we had payed land compensation to the neighboring farmers. This could potentially solve damping problems for our liquid waste. For the solid waste removal and management, we should continuously teach the community so that everyone can collect wastes in allotted places. 
Water and sanitation office can have the capacity to buy and install pipelines everywhere and basically that will not be a major problem. The core problem is that they are left empty for months due to shortage of water in the town. 
2. What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 

There are about nine water scheme projects that are being drilled currently all of which are funded by the World Bank. When the projects are completed, hopefully the water coverage of the woreda will increase.  Next to this, we also need popular mobilization in WASH activities so that everyone will be informed of the sanitation and hygiene services. Awareness programs could also help the community to properly manage the water resource and to be able to care to the infrastructures. 




Interviewer: How about the issue of landfill site? Do you think that it is related to financial problems solely?
Yes, the problem is not only financial issue. There are other issues that need prior attention from the local government and the community. Discussions about the landfill site have already been conducted by town municipality service and the communities surrounding the town. These discussions have always been fruitful with a consensus conclusion. However, the problem arises when the municipality tries to turn those discussions in to ground. In that case, we have serious problems. One of the reasons for this inefficiency could be the high human power turnover, including the Mayor. It is becoming a challenge for a new staff to turn those ideas in to the beneficiaries given the limited experience and exposure. That is in fact the main challenge affecting sustainability in weliso town.  A new staff usually starts everything from zero. Hence, the urban administrator needs to take measure and begin the implementation so as to bring real solution.  
Interviewer: I heard that there is no good relation between extension workers and the community, is this true? 
That is absolutely right. Health extension workers are losing acceptance with in the community. This is due to the steady change of the service itself resulting in lack of trust. This is a series problem that need immediate solution. The municipality, for instance, have the mandate to mobilize people and discuss on this critical issue.  WASH should be a continued agenda for the town municipality and other sectors involved in similar tasks. 
3. Of the solutions you listed, which one is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?

Sanitation is one of the criteria for a place to be considered as an urban. There are towns smaller than Weliso that have already developed waste disposal site in a very good standard. We should take lessons from these towns. Those piles of waste garbage and dirt that are affecting the beauty of the town and the health of the people will be removed in the long run. The nine-water projects that are under the process of construction should be finalized soon.
