1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation/water service sustainability in your town? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability? 

I.Water supply and demand imbalance
It is argued that the town water supply service was constructed for 10,000 population of the town in 1974. The number of public and private institutions, hotels and restaurants were much smaller than the currently existing ones by type and number. The peoples were not give that much attention for water services at that time. Now the population increased tremendously and it is about 120,000. Construction also increased in the town. But, the water supply remains as it was in 1974. 

To alleviate this problem there is an initiative by the town’s municipality in collaboration with World Bank Group to develop boreholes that have a potential to serve the community for the next 20 years. However, the construction is not yet completed and the installation of the water distribution lines is not started. 

II.Inappropriate usage of water
There is inappropriate practice of water usage by the community. Constructions in the town and small scale irrigations also use chlorinated water which aggravates the shortage of the water in town. Using chlorinated water for construction is wastage.  Due to this mismanagement, mostly the water supply is interrupted for more than a week. 

III.Absence of appropriate solid and liquid waste disposal place: Currently there is no waste disposal place in the town. Because of this, households dispose wastes simply in any open areas and this results in serious sanitation problem in the town as well as in adjacent rural areas. We are now on the way to identify the waste disposal site and the town administration tried to address the problem by negotiating with farmers and paying compensation to land owners.  The large amount of wastes generated everyday coupled with the absence of waste disposal place significantly affected the sanitation and beatification service of the town. 
 
Due to the absence of liquid waste disposal place, some hotels and community members connect their liquid wastes with the towns open drainage lines. This created serious sanitation problem particularly during dry season.

IV.Lack of community awareness on waste management: the community has poor knowledge of solid and liquid waste management as a result they predisposed for various health hazards and sanitation related problems. Even if there is a sanitation and beautification core process under the town’s municipality, it has limited role in enhancing the awareness of the community. 
The respondent argued that if the community clean the area one day per month and dispose wastes throughout the month, it is difficult to ensure sustainable sanitation service. Thus, to ensure sustainable sanitation in the town, there should be continuous sanitation campaigns by the community. 

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
To alleviate water supply and demand imbalance as well as inappropriate water usage, the respondent provided the following recommendations:
Provide awareness training on how to use small water to perform more tasks (how to efficiently use water). Our populations need wide land and more water, but it is hard to get sustainable water service under the current situation. If it is not appropriately utilized having more number of boreholes will not add value as the key is to efficiently use the available water sources. For example, the water used to wash our hands should be recycled and used for another purpose. We have to have awareness campaign in this regard.
The community should be informed about the impact of  global warming on ground and surface water resources and remedial actions should be performed
Those who cultivated vegetables should use river water that passed in the town (Egersa river) rather than using chlorinated water. Similarly, constructions should be based on river water rather than chlorinated drinking water. 
To improve the water supply, the ongoing water supply expansion project which is implemented by the World Bank Fund should be completed as soon as possible and start providing services.
Shallow well should be developed in those areas that are not reached by the town water utility service.
Regarding waste disposal place problem the respondent forwarded the following suggestions:
Appropriate permanent waste disposal place both for liquid and solid wastes should be prepared as soon as possible by dealing with landowners and by paying the required compensations for those farmers that are going to be displaced from the area.
Awareness creation campaign should takes place to enhance the awareness of the community on how to manage solid and liquid wastes in their locality.
The monthly sanitation or cleaning campaign that is initiated by our Prime Minister should be regularly implemented in all areas of the twon.

3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Preparing appropriate waste disposal site for both solid and liquid wastes is the most important solution to alleviate sanitation related problems of Woliso town. If appropriate permanent waste disposal place is constructed for the town, the community will benefit the following:
The town will become clean and beautiful
The community health will become improved by preventing diseases transmission
The image of the town will be built
The hotel and tourism service of the town will imrove.
The community will lead comfortable life
