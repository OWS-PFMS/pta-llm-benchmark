1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Interviewer: In your opinion, what are the main challenges related to the sustainability of WASH service (water, sanitation and hygiene services) at region level in general and at the town level in particular? We are talking about sustainability of WASH, what are the major problems related to this? The second question is what are the solutions for the problems you mentioned? Then, of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? These are the questions we are going to discuss about. Now let us start with first question. 
Respondent: In our region, the One-WASH program has been implemented for the last five years and now we are on the way to start a new phase of the program. When we look at the implementation performance of the last five years, it does not achieve the desired goals. One of the main reasons or challenges for this is that the procurement process. There are procurement teams at federal and regional governments (i.e. a team that facilitate purchase of inputs and materials for the development of WASH facilities as well as bids for construction of water schemes).  
Interviewer: What is the problem in the procurement process?
Respondent: The procurement process is very tedious and takes long period. For instance, some contractors compete for water drilling bids without having a clear knowledge and understanding of the situation and context of the region. By proposing lower price these contractors win the bid but when they come to implementation, they could not manage the work with the proposed price. On top of this, as the area is very arid and dry land their machineries frequently broken out during drilling. Therefore, the Ministry of Water and Mine should give the procurement mandate to the region and the bid should be facilitated within the region. Moreover, bidders should provide their offer after having a clear understanding of the areas by making site visit. Because of this, the number of the One-WASH beneficiaries during the past five years is very low compared to the target number.  
The other thing is if we look at education and health services, they are required to be implemented in coordination with the WASH program. The program requires one plan, one budget and one report. The water sector is expected to engage in water service, the health sector in sanitation and hygiene and the education program is expected to focus on awareness enhancement activities. While the health and education sectors properly disposing their responsibility, the water sector failed to provide the required service for the two sectors. The schools and health facilities are unable to ensure proper institutional WASH service due to absence of water service. The latrines and other WASH facilities in schools and health facilities could not provide service due to absence of water service. Rather they become source of contaminants.
The other problem is the regional government do not pay attention for the program (One-WASH program). They mainly focused on political affairs and did not give much attention to solve the implementation problems of the program. In the region, water is a burning issue but the regional governing bodies do not give emphasis for the problems. The regional steering committee did not properly follow the implementation of the program. These are the basic problems observed in the region related to WASH service.
The problems related to coordination are manageable but still they have implication on the sustainability of WASH service in the region.
When we look at the human resource, there are no sufficient qualified personnel to properly implement the One-WASH program at woreda level. This also create dalliance in financial reports. There are no responsible staffs that regularly prepare the program’s financial reports at woreda level. Afar region is known by delaying One-WASH financial report which should be submitted to the federal government. Since the regional office could not get the report timely from the implementing woredas, it could not compile and submit to the federal government on time.  
 



2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have mentioned basic things or problems. Now let us come to the solutions to the problems. First, what ideas or recommendations do you have about the solution to the problem related to the procurement process of the One-WASH program?  
Respondent: As a solution to this problem, what I want to suggest is that we have to simplify the procurement process. This means the process should be handled within the region. For instance, last year the Federal Ministry of Water and Mine come to the region managed the process here in the region. If we would have been done this from the beginning of the program implementation, we do not raise the procurement issue as a problem. This is one solution to the problem.
Second, to prevent those contractors that offer lower price without understanding the intensity of the work and context of the region, we should allow the regional water design bureau to manage such projects as it has knowledge of the areas that has water resource potential. This bureau knows which types of machineries should be used for drilling in different areas. The region lately understand the possibility of engaging the region’s water design bureau in handling such project under the procurement policy.  If these responsibilities have been given to the region’s water design bureau instead of private contractors, we do not face such project implementation problems.   
Interviewer: Do the other regions of the country handle the procurement process on their own?
Respondent: Yes, the big regions like Amhara, South, Tigray and Oromia manage the procurement process by themselves. I think either we should allow the region to manage the process by itself or the procurement process should be held within the region by the federal and regional program implementing bodies.   
Interviewer: The other problem you raised is absence of integrated activities among the education, health and water sectors under One-WASH program. You said there should be one plan, one budget and one report in the program. What is the solution to this integration problem in the program?
Respondent: As I said, the education and health sectors do not have problems because they are not affected by the procurement process related problems. Their budget directly transfer to their respective woredas and manage the budget by themselves. As a result, they almost dispose their responsibilities properly. However, most of them do not have access to water service. Thus, if we can facilitate the procurement process of the water projects from the very beginning, the institutions would have access to water service and the sectors can go together by supporting each other. Hence, the failure in the water sector affected the performance of the other two sectors.
Interviewer: You also mentioned that the regional government including the water bureau do not give attention for the One-WASH program while WASH is the burning issue in the region. What ideas or recommendations do you have about the solutions to this problem?
Respondent: I have raised my suggestion to the World Bank Group who finance the One-WASH program that they should give strong advice to the regional governing bodies including the president to give appropriate emphasis for the program. But I do not know whether the regional authorities have been advised or not.
What I want to say is when the water bureau fail to dispose its responsibility; the regional council should monitor and evaluate the bureau and give directions to alleviate the weaknesses. 
Interviewer: What is the reason for the weakness of the water bureau? Is it due to budget or human resource constraints?
Respondent: The first reason is the high turnover of the heads of the office. One director of the office will not serve for more than 6 months. As they are politically appointed, for some period they assigned to lead education bureau another time they shifted to water bureau. Generally, they do not stay longer in one sector. Hence, it requires more than six months in order to aware the newly appointed head about the program (One-WASH). When he/she become aware of the program he/she immediately replaced by other head due to political reason.
Interviewer: You have mentioned that due to lack of human resource at woreda level there is dalliance of financial reports of the One-WASH program. What do you think or suggest alleviating this problem?  
Respondent: Pertinent to this, similar to the regional finance bureau we have to allocate at least some amount of budget to the woreda finance offices for the recruitment of qualified personnel. If they hire diploma level personnel, it does not require significant budget. Thus, we can solve the problem by allocating small amount of budget to the woredas. We forward this recommendation federal Ministry of Finance and Economic Development. In big regions, they hire staffs that manage the reporting tasks at zonal level and the zonal staffs compile the woredas reports and submit to the region. However, in our region, there is no zonal administrative structure. It is the regional bureau that is responsible to collect the reports from woredas and submit the complied report to the federal government. As we have about 13 One-WASH implementation woredas in the region, it is difficult to collect and compile their reports timely. If we do not go to the woredas, they do not bring the report to the bureau, as they are distantly located.
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: We are talking about sustainability of WASH. Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important to ensure sustainable WASH service in the area?
Respondent: In Afar region, there is no budget constraint. Especially last year we had large budget for the development of WASH facilities. However, the major problem is the procurement process. As the One-WASH program is a package of education, health and water sectors, if one of the sector lags in program implementation, the other sectors would not perform well. Hence, if we can alleviate the problems related to the procurement process, all the three sectors perform well.
Interviewer: Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution to the procurement process occurs?
Respondent: If we can ease the problems related to the procurement process, we can easily facilitate access to water for the schools and health facilities and improve the institutional WASH service. That is the society can get access to water in these institutions and can properly utilize the other WASH facilities. Moreover, we can reach more beneficiaries and achieve the program target.
In addition, there should be proper follow-up of the program implementation by the Ministry of Water particularly in our region.
