1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the water service provision challenges in the woreda? What are the factors that hinder the improvement of the currently existing clean water coverage? 
Respondent: The water infrastructure in the town is built fifteen years ago and there is only one water reservoir. This was build considering the population of the town at that time. After that a couple of water points have been built but they have not been made accessible to the community yet. This is because pipe installation has not yet undertaken. 
There are also some administrative issues. 
Interviewer: What do you mean by administrative issues?
Respondent: What I mean by administrative issues is that there is lack of supervision and monitoring of the water distribution system. There are water points that are just running to the open and get wasted. Besides, while there are some residents that do not pay for the water utility whereas others are being forced to overpay.
In the rural areas when the water pumps encounter technical issues they are not getting maintenance services on time.  Besides, WaSHCOs are not strong enough to work effectively, for instance, collecting fees, buy spare parts and fuel. The hand pumps should be changed to solar panel to improve the accessibility of the water service to the community.
There is also a lack of trained manpower to conduct proper maintenance when there is a technical issue. The water that comes out of broken pipes is creating stagnant water which in turn is causing the local community contract diseases. The currently existing water reservoir is also inadequate to serve the current population.  


2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have mentioned that the pipes are too old so what should be done to address this problem? 
Respondent: We have heard that there is a plan to build new pipelines. If the infrastructure is upgraded there will be a significant improvement.  Besides, people are fetching water from inside water wells using buckets which contaminates the water and cause damage to the pumps because the dirty water get back to the well and the pump draws in the dirt water. Therefore, strong follow-up should be conducted continuously. 
Interviewer: You have mentioned the management problem repeatedly and yet there is this traditional system in place. What do you think should be done to address this problem sustainably, may be computerize it?
Respondent: It would be better if the management is taken over by the government sector not by the board. If the board is the responsible body, the government structure would only be able to make some financial support not have the power to monitor. The board by itself is weak and unable to monitor the water provision service. In order to solve the problem of manpower, the management should be run under government sector office.  
Interviewer: The other problem is related to the lack of solar panels and generators. What should be done to solve this issue? 
Respondent: The woreda water utility office should mobilize resources from different stakeholders to replace the hand-dug wells to motorized ones and the generators with solar panels. 
Besides, water pipes burst because of the pressure of the water that is released. Also, because some of the pipes are buried so shallow the pipe bursts due to different accidents. 
Interviewer: There is also a lack of trained manpower, so what should be done to solve this problem in sustainable manner?
Respondent: Training should be given to the workers and make them act responsibly. 
Interviewer: How should this is done? That is the biggest problem.
Respondent: It is their professional responsibility to serve the community with what they have learnt. If the water gets contaminated there would be a health crisis. 
Interviewer: There is also a problem of insufficient water reservoir. What about that?
Respondent: Additional water reservoirs should be built.
Interviewer: Who should do that? When asked, the town administration says they have limited capacity. Whose responsibility is this?
Respondent: It is the responsibility of all of us. It is also the responsibility of the community. The water board, the community, and the administration have to discharge their responsibility. Besides, if the federal and regional governments work in coordination, the problem will be solved. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: Out of the solutions you proposed, which one would you think should be a priority to solve the problem in a sustainable manner?
Respondent: Awareness creation is the key thing.
Interviewer: Who should be the awareness creation work on?
Respondent: The awareness creation should be done on the experts. It is also important to create awareness among the community members. In some neighborhoods there is excess water and it is just being wasted. However, others are suffering from water shortage. If there were awareness those who have excess water would not waste it so that the communities lacking drinking water would have gotten enough water. Therefore, awareness creation is key. 
We have water sources even though we lack adequate reservoir.  There are residents who get water from the pipes before the water reaches the reservoir. Consequently, the time for the reservoir to get full is getting longer. Hence, the reservoir should be filled first to serve the whole community not just few ones. This could be a temporary solution. Besides, it requires improving the management of the system to introduce new approaches of water distribution.
