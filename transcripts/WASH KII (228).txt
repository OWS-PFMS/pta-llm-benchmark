1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent: the main problem is lack of budget, because the managers did not give focus to these problems.
The second thing is awareness gap with in the community about sanitation. The community members and investors have less participation in such issues.
Lack of coordination among the stake holders is also one of the problems.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems
Interviewer: can you please tell me the solutions you think about the problems you raised above?
Respondent: letting the investors and the society by itself contribute some money can solve the budget problem. Because this problem knocks each door. And I am sure all are volunteer to contribute what they can. We should motivate people to contribute through researches or other ways.
Everybody should hold his/her responsibility. They should not push each problem to the government. Municipality office was not in charge of the issue.. But now, water and sanitation office is responsible. But All Stake holders have their own responsibility too. They should come closer and assign who is responsible for what and they should surmount their responsibility. They should openly tell the problem for example if a city administration makes problems. 




3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
The main one from the solutions is to improve coordination among stakeholders and to discharge their responsibilities. This solution may lead to a change in collaboration works. This will help offices to support each other and every one will try to work and grow together.
