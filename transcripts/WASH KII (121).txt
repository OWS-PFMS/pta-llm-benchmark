1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Respondent 1: There are 11 kebeles in the woreda and there are about 34 water schemes or water points in the entire woreda. The existing water schemes in Mille town are not sufficient to satisfy the water demand of the community. Similarly, the water supply in rural kebeles of the woreda is not sufficient for the community. This is mainly due to shortage of capital budget for the development of additional water schemes.
Interviewer: Are there any other problems related to water and sanitation services?
Respondent 1: In rural areas where there is no access for clean water, the community use Awash river which is highly contaminated by industrial wastes and household wastes. Due to lack of awareness, the community directly use the water without any treatment. This led to occurrence of cholera and other water borne diseases.
In addition, there are no water treatment materials or chemicals that can be distributed to the rural community.
There is also logistic problem to regularly monitor the water schemes and provide maintenance service timely. We have motorbikes but they are not convenient to transport maintenance materials and equipment. 
 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Respondent 1: To alleviate the problem related to water supply shortage the regional government should allocate sufficient capital budget for the woreda. In addition, the regional government should mobilize NGOs to the woreda that have water and sanitation program.
The Learning Alliance is also very important to strengthen the coordination between actors that have direct and indirect role in water and sanitation sector of the woreda.
Respondent 1: To minimize the incidence of water borne diseases we should provide awareness enhancement trainings for the rural community. Water treatment materials or chemicals should be available and regularly distributed to the community prior to occurrence of water borne diseases. 
Moreover, capacity building trainings should be provided to sanitation and hygiene experts of the woreda health and water offices. This is because these experts are required to change the traditional practice and attitudes of the community related to sanitation. 
Interviewer: You have also mentioned that there is shortage of water treatment materials. What do you recommend to alleviate this problem?
Respondent 2: The woreda has no budget to supply the required amount of these materials. Thus, the regional government should either allocate budget or supply the materials to the woreda. NGOs that work in WASH sector should support the woreda by providing the materials. 
 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent 1: The solution to the problem related to water supply shortage is the most important and should get immediate response. Next to this, we should have vehicle to undertake maintenance service regularly and timely. 
If these two main problems get solution, we can provide instant maintenance service for water schemes located in remote areas. The community would get sustainable water service and led stable life.
