1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the woreda?
There are water projects that are being implemented in different parts of the woreda. However, there is no activity to rehabilitate existing non-functional rural water schemes. For example, there are water schemes that filled with sediments of flood. There are also boreholes without water pumps. In general there is no regular maintenance of the existing water schemes constructed in different kebeles of the woreda. I think this is mainly due to resource and capacity limitation. 
In some areas there are motor pumps to supply water to the community but the community could not afford to buy diesel for the pumps. Because of this the community is not getting regular supply of water.
When we come to Mille town, previously there was small number of population in the town. But recently the population is growing at alarming rate. Even though there is an increase in population of the town, there is no effort to expand the water supply. The water supply is based on the previous small water tanks. Because of this there is mismatch between the supply and demand for water. We only get water for about 1 hour per day.
The water lines in Mille town are also very old and there is no initiative to replace this old water line with new ones. Therefore, there is a serious water supply problem in the town.
To alleviate the water supply problems of the town the community discussed with the woreda water office and administration office. A temporary solution is made by supplying water in shift basis (i.e. supplying to some part of the town at a time and supplying to other part of the town at another time). Since the capacity of the water tank is very limited it difficult to supply water to the town regularly.

The other problem related to water service sustainability in rural part of the woreda is even if there are water pumps to supply water, there are no technicians that repair these pumps when they fail to function. Moreover, the community cannot afford the running and maintenance cost of the water pumps. I suggest the water office to introduce water pumps that function using solar energy. There are some solar energy based water pumps that are introduced by NGOs in some areas and they are cost effective.  As our region is very arid there is sufficient source of solar energy. Therefore, if we introduce solar system to supply water, we can ensure sustainable water service in the woreda.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
When I start from the rural areas, there are efforts to dig boreholes both by the government and non-governmental organizations. However, the community do not provide appropriate care for the constructed water schemes. Thus, we should enhance the awareness of the community on proper utilization of the water schemes. We should teach the community to consider the water schemes as its own property (i.e. we should create sense of belongingness).
For example, children mishandle hand pumps and they become broken within short period of time. Until the woreda water office fix the problem the community as well as livestock will suffer for long period of time. Because of this the community is forced to use river water and they become predisposed for water born diseases. Therefore, there should be massive awareness creation program on proper operation and management of water schemes in the woreda.
Moreover, the woreda water office should be equipped with all necessary materials including logistics required for maintenance of water schemes in rural areas of the woreda. The office always be ready to respond to the community’s question.
There are many water schemes that stayed without function for a long period of time, thus the woreda water office in coordination with non-governmental organizations should rehabilitate these water schemes. 
Regarding the water supply problem of Mille town, there are ideal boreholes that were constructed by World Bank. Thus, to ensure water service sustainability, we should first bring these boreholes to function. Second, we should construct water tanks for these boreholes. If we don’t construct additional new water tanks in the town, the community could not get sustainable water service.

The water utility office should also distribute the water uniformly to all parts of the town. Currently we observe some irregularity in the distribution of the water in the town.
There should also be good coordination between the woreda water office, the town water utility office and the woreda administration office to ensure sustainable water service in the town.  
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
Of the solutions I mentioned earlier, I give priority for the maintenance of the existing rural water schemes. The community should get quick response for its request for maintenance support. Currently the community wait for about one month to get response for its request for maintenance support. Since water is life the community should get quick response to its question.
The awareness creation is a long term solution that should come next to maintenance and rehabilitation of the existing water schemes.
Due to delayed response of the water office for maintenance questions, the community is forced to relocate to other place where it can get river water. This predispose to community for various health problems.  
Pertinent to the urban water problem, additional water tanks should be constructed that can satisfy the needs of the urban community. This should be done by assessing the current demand for water in the town.  
Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs? If the solutions I mentioned will be implemented, first, in rural areas water born diseases will be reduced significantly. For example, diseases like diarrhea that caused by use of contaminated water will be reduced. As a result we can also reduce the cost of medication. Generally we can prevent the community from diseases that can be caused by use of contaminated water. This implies mortality rate will be reduced in the area.

In urban areas (particularly in Mille town) if the suggested solution will be implemented, there will be sustainable water service in the town. As a result we can make the town green (by planting trees). For example, currently there are some initiatives to plant trees in the middle of the streets and roads sides but because of lack of water most of the trees are drying (dying).
Moreover, if there is sufficient water supply, we can grow vegetables at backyards. Sustainable water supply can also attract investors to invest in the town. We can also avoid delay of constructions due to lack of water supply. If there is sufficient water supply, the hygiene of the community will also be improved. 
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service?  What could be improved? 
In the presence of all these problems, it is difficult to say there satisfactory coordination among stakeholders in the woreda. But there is coordination to some extent. For example, the woreda water office, Mille town water utility office and the regional water bureau to some extent works in coordination to solve problems related to water service in the woreda. Even though we could not get sustainable solutions, the woreda administration office also coordinate with the community to find out solution to the problems. The pastoralist development office also cooperate with the woreda water office by providing information about problems related to water service in pastoralist areas. Our office regularly report to woreda water office when there is problem in pastoralist areas. It also cooperate with the water office in treating water schemes with chemicals. Therefore, there is coordination between stakeholders in the woreda but it is not at the required level (i.e. the coordination is not sufficient in terms of solving the problems related to water service).

What could be improved related to the existing coordination? There many things that should be improved related to coordination. First, for example the water office should regularly monitor the operation as well as cleanliness of the water schemes. On the other hand the woreda health office should supply medications when there is incidence of outbreaks of diseases due to use of contaminated water. For instance, in areas that are located close to rivers, water schemes easily contaminated by flood and the community become infected by different diseases. In such case the woreda health office should play important role by delivering medicines to the area. The water office should also supply chemicals that will be used for water treatment.
The woreda administration office, pastoralist development office, women and children affairs office and other relevant stakeholders should also work in coordination to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. If there is coordination among stakeholders, the water service problem both in rural and urban areas will be solved.  
5.In your opinion, can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable water service in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before?
The reason why I believe improved coordination can lead to more sustainable water service is that, if every stakeholder concerned about the problems related to water service and coordinate to find out solutions, we can ensure sustainable water service in the woreda. However, if we leave the issue of water for the water office, the water office alone cannot bring sustainable solution for the problems. But if Pastoralist development office, health office and the woreda administration office work in coordination with the woreda water office, we can ensure sustainable water service in the woreda.
