1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in your Zone?

[Lack of community awareness] I think there is lack of awareness of clean drinking water access and water access in our society in general. For instance there are lots of springs and ponds in South Ari but these are being used without treatment since the community doesn’t concern about the purity. The commitment to teach community by NGOs is also lower as compared to our expectation. There is lack of sense of ownership after an NGO built and hand over it to the community. Nowadays, there are guidelines to be used for the formation of water associations and WASH committee to manage these infrastructures. However, after these infrastructures were built and faced with technical difficulties, no one will fix them and they get damaged easily. [Low water coverage in the zone] adding to the low level of awareness is the limited water coverage in the zone which is about 39%. Climate change is the main issue for the low level of water coverage and there is drought in our zone especially Bena Tsemay and Mali woredas were in a ‘red’ rank, where the government has been reporting the drought cases to different NGOs, including IRC WASH, which has showed up through emergency project. Water was brought by trucks without considering the health issues since the time was drought. One of the contributing issues for this is not being able to conserve the environment. There is lack of awareness on how to manage and conserve the environment. If people start conserving the environment even by planting trees that would be better. These woredas were considered as temperate climate, but drastically then they are changed to desert climate. Climate change could also be attributed to increase in population number.
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Regarding the community awareness, the government has a 1:5 community structures at kebelle level (woreda cells) and it can use this as a good opportunity to create awareness. 
5:00. 
This structure is good for creating awareness about environment conservation and rehabilitation process. Moreover, making seedlings to be planted using this community structural template is important. There is lack of fuel wood around here and one stick of fuel wood costs up to 120 Birr because there is no reforestation culture and the number of consumers/ population has increased from time to time, creating high demand of fuel woods. There is also high deforestation in this area.

People also need financial aid to conserve the soil using different techniques including soil beds and prevent them from flood. So we need awareness and finance to be coupled at the same time.
Regarding the low level of water coverage, the community should work on protecting the new infrastructures by building fences and keep the area protected together. The NGOs and other stalk holders need to work together on the community wellbeing by solving the problems on existing infrastructure and maintenance) though providing small trainings on small scheme maintenance for minor technical difficulties. If the technical difficulties in the water infrastructures are major, we should have standby Woreda technicians to handle it. 
We should work on the agricultural sector too; take precautions in conserving water in rainy times and learn how to use water schemes in different seasons. Forecasting may include, planting trees, planting edible foods that ripe in short period of time and giving meteorological speculation for the society on time. This could help the community to manage the changes a head and plant food staffs that can be adaptable to the natural shocks in weather and water supply, is important.
3.Of the solution you listed, which is the most important? 
10:27
Working on the community at large and WASH cos in particular is the vital element in water service sustainability in the zone. In addition to that, we should categorize our water options and check the capacity of our resources before launching of building the infrastructures. There were occasions where projects started digging wells and gradually terminated because there was no enough underground water in the targeted areas. Hence, we must study where our water infrastructures need to be located before building any water scheme, this is very important. We need to work on the community and WASH committee in order for them to have a sense of ownership and protect the infrastructure.
4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable water service? 
Although the Water Bureau is in charge of managing and leading all water issues, water is a problem of everyone and every sector. If you go to health center, you need water, the agriculture bureau needs water for everything … Coordination is needed because water bureau has all the engineers and water experts need and know where water is available. As I said, if health clinic is opened, it will be soon closed if there is no water, if schools are opened students won’t come because of lack of water as many of them have to travel a long distance and it is hard for them to walk this distance without getting water. Hence, there is a need of cooperation for these offices: water, education, health and agriculture regarding water service suitability, they should take the value of working together seriously and plan and work in an organized way accordingly. 


What should be improved? Yes, as I told you before, activities are becoming sector specific. Water shortage is the reason for students’ drop out (education) and extra burden for women (women’s affairs). I have been to desert areas last time and I saw that women have to walk long distances for hours to get water before starting another domestic work. All the institutions plan and wonder by themselves without coordination and if there are improved coordination, I think there will be a change regarding the water services in the area
