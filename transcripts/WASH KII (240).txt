
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to Sanitation/water service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
1.1.Lack of liquid as well as solid waste disposal place is the major problem. Waste is left everywhere and is being wasted off because there is no place to collect and dispose.  When the toilet is full individuals as well as enterprises have no option to sack and dispose it, instead either they use another place or leak the toilet with the main canal along the main road which will drastically affect the health of the community 
1.2.Limited supply of water is also another problem. The current water facility was constructed many years back considering coverage of about 15,000 people during that period. Currently, the number of population is increasing alarmingly which is expected to be more than 100,000. Thus, it has created imbalance between the supply and demand of water services in the woreda. Now, new construction is under way which is assumed to serve more than 20 years in the future if it is successfully completed. 
1.3.Limited awareness of the community members: Some of them are negligent whereby they intentionally dispose wastes unwisely while others have limited awareness about the consequences of it. Waste materials are disposed here and there even in the asphalt road, they know about the effect but nobody gives attention to dispose in the proper way. Lack of commitment to keep their environment clean is by far the main challenge that we observe especially from those individuals who are addicted in chewing khat. 
2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
2.1. The local government office should work in close relation with other stakeholders like local and international NGOs in the process of designing, budgeting so as to improve water supply for the increasing population and services as well as prepare land fill sites that will be used to dispose solid as well as liquid wastes. 
2.2. Mobilizing the community to contribute for the existing solution could also be another option. For example, if the government covers may be about 70% of the cost, then the community members may also support and cover the remaining 30 %. This could be possible if and only if continuous awareness creation works are done either by the local government offices or other partner organizations. 
2.3.  Interims of water supply, there is ongoing project designed to improve the water supply of the current and the future population using underground sources but it has to be completed on time through coordinated actions from the community, the government and NGOs. The fact that the problem is more severe and demanding, the community is expecting the project enthusiastically. Therefore, government’s follow up and strong monitoring system is important to effectively implement and complete the project on the intended time. 
2.4. Continuous awareness creation about hygiene and sanitation for the community is also very essential to change the current attitudes of the largest section of the urban and rural dwellers 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
3.1. I think awareness creation should come first and considered to be important. Once the awareness creation is done on sanitation and water service sustainability, everyone will have a good understanding of personal and community level sanitation and its negative consequences if not properly managed. This will gradually solve all the problems we discussed regarding water and sanitation services sustainability.
