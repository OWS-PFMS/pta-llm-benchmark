
Interviewer:  My first question to you is, “In your opinion, what aspect of this network is working well in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: First is sharing of information and the other one is about skills, sharing of the skills by both actors. 

Interviewer:  What example would you give?

Interviewee: Like, FundiFix, if they are sharing information with us about what is happening in the schemes, we can advise and if it necessitates, we also visit the areas and we work together with them, and discussions are done at site.

Interviewer:  My second question to you is, “What do you think are the main problems in sustaining water services in the county?

Interviewee: Ok, the biggest challenge is transport. Yes, transport is a major challenge nearly to all sub-counties. The other one is the personnel we have; few officers in the office manning a very big area like a sub-county. Like in Mwingi North you can understand that we are two technical officers. The area is big, no vehicle, no motorbike, we just manoeuver our ways to reach the scheme.

Interviewer:  So when you say transport, you mean mobility around your area? Is there anything else?

Interviewee: Ok, the other challenge is, let’s say, funds. As you interact more with the communities, like maybe you want to train them, they are expecting something back. Having them for the long hours then you leave them like that. As you impart the skills, they also need some cash.

Interviewer:  My third question to is, “What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewee: Ok. Solutions. If we can have the sub-county water offices being funded, we can be having some money then in our capacity now we will be free and mobile in the areas . Where there is like an emergency we go there we address the problem. Where the schemes need training we go there we address them, even where the partners are doing some work, we reach there, discuss issues with them, consult each other and our work will be good. Yes. 

Interviewer:  Does that cover the three challenges you mentioned? 

Interviewee: Ok, as you look at the challenges, if we have got funds, now transport is sorted.

Interviewer:  Is there any other recommendation you have other than funding? 

Interviewee: Ok, we can have regular trainings towards the officers. These regular trainings will act like refresher courses to us and also for the new technologies that are coming up. As you look at the water schemes, new equipment with new techniques are being used. So if we are going to have regular trainings, necessary knowledge will be added to us.

Interviewer:  Of the solutions you have mentioned, which do you consider to be the most important? 

Interviewee: Ok, we can have the trainings to get the new technologies which will now enable us to address the major challenges that we have in Mwingi North now specifically. The area is big, water scarcity is high, communities are growing. So we have many people, with many livestock going to compete for the few water sources. Now, if we have got new technologies, we, have got a major river there like River Tana, which has water flowing all year round; it is permanent. So if have got these new skills, we can develop other structures in the river, we abstract water, pump it to the interior, people will get water. We have got also rivers. With the new technologies, we can develop structures like more sand dams and sump wells which will enable access to clean water for the communities; somehow potable. 

Interviewer:  If the solution happens, what would occur next?

Interviewee: One, the water table will be raised, so we will not have dangers whereby deep wells are sunk by people. We will also have increased access to potable water by the community which will now lead to reduced disease outbreaks. Since we will be having more water sources, the walking distance to the water sources will be reduced, and also waiting time at the sources we have. So since the sources are many, people will be distributed, so waiting time will be reduced. Ok, now, people will be having more time for more economic activities.
