1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water/sanitation service sustainability in your woreda? Can you please explain in detail how these problems influence sanitation/water service sustainability?
Interviewer: In your opinion what are the main challenges or constraints that affect the sustainability of WASH service in your woreda? When I say WASH service, it means water and sanitation service. What are the main problems observed in this service?
Respondent: 
 The main problem to ensure sustainable water service in the woreda is budget. Though the woreda is endowed with water resource due to budget constraint there is a serious water supply shortage
2nd the community do not properly manage the existing water facilities and they lack sense of belongingness on proper use of water schemes. This is the other main problem for sustainability of the water service in the woreda. 
 Particularly the community do not have sense of belongingness for the water schemes developed in their area. That is they do not properly care and manage the facilities.
3rd There are spring water and ground water resource in the woreda but there is a problem of coordination and cooperation with the town water utility office in terms of taking collective actions to treat and develop the springs water and supply to the community. 

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?

Interviewer: What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions for budget problem to ensure sustainable water service?
Respondent: the government should allocate sufficient capital budget to the woreda. The main problem we are facing now is the budget allocated by the government to the woreda cannot meet the entire needs of the woreda.
The government should allocate sufficient capital budget to the woreda and should strongly work on areas that can be managed by its capacity. But in areas that are beyond its capacity, the government have to find out potential collaborators or donors and provide appropriate information resolve the problems.
Interviewer: What ideas or recommendations do you have to resolve problems regarding to public awareness 
Respondent: Whoever construct the water schemes either the government or potential donors, they should create sense of belongingness within the community so they be able to properly care and manage the facilities. If there no responsible body that manage the proper function of the schemes, they will not serve the anticipated period service. we have to organize rural community associations that will be responsible for the management and administration of water schemes. 

3.  Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: we should allocate sufficient resource either in terms of financial or nonfinancial. In my understanding first we have to give priority to solve the budget problems. Solving the budget problem is also solving problems related to monitoring, supervision and management of the existing water schemes to ensure their sustainability. If we do this, we can create sense of belongingness within the community to properly manage, monitor and provide technical support on the existing water schemes. Therefore, we should give due attention for resource allocation, particularly on budget allocation.
