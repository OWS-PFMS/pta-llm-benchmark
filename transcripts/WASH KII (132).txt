1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the problems affecting water service sustainability? You can tell me what problems are there in the rural and urban areas. 
Respondent: The problem is the quality of the materials. Many water projects encounter frequent breakdown due to poor quality of the materials. 
Interviewer: Which material has poor quality?
Respondent: The motor pumps frequently stop functioning. The pumps are rather pricey that we couldn’t afford to buy one.
Interviewer: Is the purchase being made according to the government procurement procedure?
Respondent: I recently came to this office I don’t have much idea about it. I have proposed 2013 EFY budget for the water sector. There is an assessment conducted in the woreda which might help to improve the attitude of the woreda cabinet members towards the water sector. Even though we agreed on idea level we have not yet done anything tangible.  
Interviewer: Therefore, a lack of capital budget is a contributing factor.
Respondent: Yes. It is one of the contributing factors.
Interviewer: What about other problems?
Respondent: The main issue is the lack of budget. If there is sufficient budget recruiting trained manpower would not be a problem. 
Interviewer: DO you have enough manpower in the rural areas and have you observed any problem related to community attitude?
Respondent: As I am a new comer to the office, I have no idea on that.
Interviewer: Have you allocated necessary budget to repair the water pumps?
Respondent: An assessment has been conducted by the woreda’s initiation. And the assessment’s findings have been communicated to the regional water and energy bureau. If they accept the assessment there will be opportunities to tackle the problems we have mentioned. 
However, the main problem is lack of budget. The budget allocation is completely unacceptable. 
Interviewer: Are you talking as a region or as the woreda?
Respondent: The budget required for the assessment was covered by the woreda. The maintenance budget, however, is allocated by the region and federal governments. But, there is nothing implemented yet.
Interviewer: I have heard that water distribution in the town is not fair.
Respondent: Yes. There are localities in the town that get water easily whereas some localities need to wait for days to get clean water. This is the problem of the water pipeline. To solve the pipeline issue additional budget is required.  
Interviewer: Couldn’t that be because of lack of proper management? 
Respondent: If we want to solve the issue we should secure adequate budget. The problem is with the old water pipes. The pipes frequently burst and they should be replaced. 
Interviewer: What problems have you observed in the town’s water service provision?
Respondent: We have sufficient water but the problem is poor installation of the distribution system. It is very hard to fill the water reservoir because of the old pipelines. The pipeline has leakages in many different places. The main problem is the distribution system. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: What do you think is the solution for the issues you have pointed out?
Respondent: The solution is to replace the old pipes with new ones. The water distribution system was built in 2001. The population of the town then and now is entirely different. The water reservoir does not have the capacity to serve the current population. The water pump we have is also of limited capacity to fill the reservoir and we use electric power most of the time. As a result, the reservoir needs longer time to get full. Therefore, new pipe installation is critical in addition to building other reservoirs. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: As I have understood from your solutions, addressing the issue of budget is the most critical solution. What problems would tackle adequate budget?
Respondent: If sufficient budget is allocated additional reservoir will be built and new water distribution line will be installed. Besides, expansion of the water distribution system will be implemented to increase access of the community to clean water. 
Interviewer: Don’t the WaSHCos generate their own income for maintenance purposes? 
Respondent: No. they don’t have. 
Interviewer: According to the assessment how much money is required to address the water service provision of the woreda? 
Respondent: It is around 1.8 million. 
Interviewer: If the solutions in the assessment are implemented do you think the problems will be solved?
Respondent: The assessment’s purpose is to communicate the problem and mobilize resources from different stakeholder. The woreda is not capable of covering the budget needed to address the issues.
