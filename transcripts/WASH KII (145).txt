
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to water service sustainability in the Afar Region?
I want this to be considered as to my personal opinion. The region has tropical climate and there is no adequate surface water. Getting underground water may need deep excavation. Sometimes, we might get underground water without deep excavation. In this case, we can get surface, but still accessing the service to the community water easily or transporting water service from place to place would be difficult because there are no road and other necessary water infrastructures. So, is there [ground water shortage?] Yes, there isn’t adequate underground water because of recurrent natural disasters and the dry climate in the region. The surface water could get contaminated because the evaporation rate is very high, the surface water would easily get dry. Less surface water availability and the contamination of the water could also be the problems to the water service sustainability in the region. If we can’t restore the underground water it could be minimized, it is like a fuel. So, we should also consider the restoration process through applying both natural and community mobilization techniques.
