1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of sanitation service sustainability in your town? 
Interviewer: What are the main problems that affect the sustainability of sanitation service in your town, public latrines, communal latrines, and open defecation?
Respondent: … the reason why I said that is the community is not protecting and using the public latrines and communal latrines with a sense of ownership. So, lack of proper utilization is one problem. People defecate on the door steps of the latrines and there no one to clean that up. This is because the community is not using bylaws to protect these latrines. When we come to the care takers of the latrines, there are some latrines that are clean, lockable, and properly handled. I believe there is an awareness gap in this area. 
Interviewer: Who has the lack of awareness? 
Respondent: It is the user community that lacks the awareness. Frequent awareness creation and strengthening local bylaws are important. It has not been worked with the community to pass local bylaws and manage the facilities according to them. 
The other problem is lack of sense of ownership. When you go around the town you see people defecating in open spaces and throwing garbage all over the places. We all lack a sense of ownership, including myself. 
Interviewer: If there is any problem that you have forgotten if there are any open defecation sites in the town. You have mentioned basic problems: one is lack of sense of ownership, not applying bylaws, and lack of awareness. Are there any problems related to institutions, human resource, and so on. 
Respondent: I don’t think there is human resource problem in the town administration. I believe there is a lack of initiative to work. 
Interviewer: What do you mean by that?
Respondent: Experts in the sanitation and beauty department of the city administration are not leading the sector by plan and great focus. If activities are planned and all stakeholders are properly identified and involved we can create a clean town. Environmental protection is our duty, and we have an environmental team. We also determine the suitability of land use.
Another problem is the ties among stakeholders are poor. 
Interviewer: Do you mean by lack of coordination?
Respondent: Yes.  There is a lack of strong organization that integrates the stakeholder’s efforts for a common objective. There are some organizations but they are poor.
Interviewer: What stakeholders are there? 
Respondent: One that takes the ownership is the municipality. Besides, sanitation and beautification, water utility office, health office, environmental protection, land administration and use, labor and social affairs are the major stakeholders.   
Interviewer: So, the problem is the coordination among these actors is not strong.
Respondent: Yes. These stakeholders meet when something strange happens. 
Interviewer: However, they do not have common planning or common activities.
Respondent: There is a plan, but there is huge gap in implementing, monitoring, and feedback. 
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: Let’s proceed to the solutions you have mentioned there is a gap in the community. What is the solution?
Respondent: It is important to raise the awareness of the community. It is vital to give responsibility to take care of its public properties. This can be achieved by improving the understanding of the community. 
Interviewer: Is it easy to raise social awareness? How can it make happen?
Respondent: Members of the community think the sanitation facilities are not their own. It is vital to help them understand that they are the users of the facilities, and no one else. Second, it is crucial to use the cash generated from these facilities to keep the clean. Instead of making the latrines free of charge, they can charge users a fair amount of money. The revenue can be used for water and cleaning services; or, if the community members have a sense of ownership, each latrine user can clean up right after they use. 
Similarly, public latrines could be source of employment. For instance, there people around the bus station. They collect an income from the latrines there by they provide cleaning services. This would also solve the problems low sense of ownership and lack of awareness at the same time.
Interviewer: You have mentioned there is lack of motivation among experts in the government structure. What would be the solution for this? 
Respondent: The one who coordinate the activities should take the responsibility to solve this problem. The coordinator should monitor each and every activity. For example, the town sanitation and beauty is a legal entity of the government. However, it does not accomplish its regular job except cleaning the streets occasionally. Cleaning the street only does not make any difference. In order to effectively address the issue we should work on teaching, raising community awareness. By doing so, people can possess their own waste collection mechanisms and notify concerned bodies if they see offenders who dump garbage inappropriately. It is possible to improve initiation to work by creating such awareness. 
Being responsible is also very important. For instance, if a bylaw is passes, it should be effective and each offender should be punished with no exception. 
Besides, if it is necessary to incentivize experts, it should be implemented. Further, improving the infrastructure is important. This is because many workers may have difficulty in having transportation access. All these can help improve the motivation of the workers. 
Interviewer: Lastly, you have said poor coordination of stakeholders. What would be the solution for this?
Respondent: The stakeholders should have the sense of ownership. Every one of them should work according to a program and plan. If, for instance, the water utility office is the organizer, it should coordinate all the activities and work to strengthen the bond between them. All the stakeholders should meet together on a regular basis to evaluate performance, give feedback, and assign a responsible body. Then, performance should be presented to and discuss with the community. 
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: You have mentioned many solutions. Out of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? What would happen if the solution occurs?
Respondent: The solutions I have mentioned are linked to one another.  But, the first is raising the awareness of the community and developing sense of ownership. Then, reducing the lack of coordination among stakeholders is important. In this regard, strengthening the bond among stakeholders is vital and it can solve many problems. 
Interviewer: Out of these three which one is the most important one? If implemented what differences would it make? Can you prioritize? 
Respondent: Raising the awareness of the community should be priority number one.
Interviewer: What would happen if the awareness of the community is raised? 
Respondent: If the awareness of the community is raised, a sense of ownership will be created within the community. Besides, the ties among stakeholders will be strengthened; the stakeholders will be motivated to work more because of the change they see. If we are able to manage the garbage, we will be able to create clean environment. So, the first thing is raising the awareness of the community.  
Interviewer: If that solution is implemented it would solve the other problems. 
Respondent: Yes. That is what I think.
