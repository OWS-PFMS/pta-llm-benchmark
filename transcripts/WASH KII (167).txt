1)In your opinion, what are the main problems of water service sustainability in your woreda? 
Interviewer: What are the challenges that affect the sustainability of water service provision that you know?
Respondent: As a region the first challenge is related to lack of budget and capacity. When we come to maintenance issues, we have a lack of crane; because we only have three cranes. Besides, we have lack of trained manpower. 
Interviewer: Are the problems in the rural and urban areas similar?
Respondent: In the rural areas there are problems related to road access. This is not a problem in the towns. Other than that the problems are almost similar.
2)What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems?
Interviewer: You have mentioned there are budget and capacity problems what do you think are the solutions for these issues?
Respondent: It is a bit difficult. It starts from the federal government. The issue of water is related with the heath and other aspects of the population. Therefore, government structures at all levels should pay due attention to the sector.
Interviewer: What about the capacity issue?
Respondent: Some NGOs have started to deploy experts from the federal office and set up a 3 expert teams in every woreda. Such kind of efforts should be encouraged. Woredas should also work with regional water and energy bureau to identify and address the capacity problems they have. It could be providing trainings. 



Interviewer: The other issue you mentioned in delayed maintenance services.
Respondent: We have the necessary maintenance tools only at regional level. In order to provide speedy maintenance service, maintenance crew should be set up at zonal level with adequate equipments such as cranes. There are five zones and five teams should be established. 
Interviewer: Why have not you done so yet? 
Respondent: I can’t answer that question.
Interviewer: How could the road accessibility problem be addressed? 
Respondent: Roads that connect rural kebeles to rural kebeles and kebeles to the woreda should be built as much as possible. PCPD are some of the programs that could be beneficial in this regard.
3)Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer: From the solutions you have proposed which one is the most important one?
Respondent: There are areas in the region that do not have any clean water access. It is imperative to build new water schemes to increase the potable water access of the people in the region.  Besides, maintenance of the currently existing water points is also vital.
Interviewer: What contribution would have maintaining the existing water schemes to the sustainability of water service?
Respondent: In order to improve on what we have we should keep the existing water schemes functional. Otherwise there would be no improvement at all. To keep these schemes functional proper maintenance is a very important factor. As you may have known most of our water schemes operate with pumps and power generators. These equipments demand high level maintenance operations.
