
1.In your opinion, what do you think are the main problems to sanitation service sustainability in your woreda?
Interviewer:
Response: The first and the big problem is there is no system at institutional level. When you go in every corner of the city you see that the way disposing waste materials is rampant and irresponsible. From individuals to companies they dispose anywhere any without thinking the outcome in society’s health and environmental protection. 
What I mean is, the first problem is the body who took the responsibility is not doing well. This may have different reasons like lack of resource and facility and the like. The second one is the body didn’t try to include the society to participate. This is also related with lack of awareness in the society. This takes a huge portion of the problem. As I said before from individuals to big companies taking responsibility in creating beautiful city and keeping the city clean is very low. So the problem is going wider and wider also, as a result of urbanization modernization.    

2.What ideas or recommendations do you have about solutions to these problems? 
Interviewer:
Response:  As I said before, the stakeholders who are involved in this problem must come together and have a discussion and above the discussion reminding all the stakeholders that they have responsibility to take action plan and project a system that has transparency and accountability. And every time they must be evaluated updated. So coordination is every important and integration is also a crucial thing to create awareness and transferring the full package to the society. There is a modern disposing area (dumping site) but they are not used effectively. So there is a big problem in every area and the duty bearers must be collected together and solved now.

If they are not solved now I don’t expect they will be solved in the future. Before resource, logistics and awareness this must be solved first. Every one that is involved in this process must take a serious responsibility and get into action. And there must be report and evaluation and full accountability to make the system successful.

Interviewer: What solutions do you recommend to improve society’s awareness?  
It can be created in a different ways. Using health organizations and municipal administrations can create coordination and create awareness. We have this program called urban youth extension program. It is decentralization of health services. It uses health workers or professionals go individually to every household and create awareness. This must be supported to have a big impact. Awareness comes first from other solutions. And public health sectors, NGOs, private sectors, different companies and research sectors must also be involved in this. The current environmental protection process is thought to be behind the door. It must be beyond the doors and must include all places around. 
3.Of the solutions you listed, which is the most important? Can you walk me through what next steps would happen if the solution occurs?
Interviewer:
Response: Change doesn’t come easy. The first thing is proper implementation of the system. Even having the resources and the awareness if the system is not implemented in a proper way it will not be successful.  The second one is, no problem can be overcome by being decentralized system. After creating awareness proper report must be generated and evaluated by responsible bodies centrally. This shouldn’t be one time task. It should be continuous and taken too seriously.  We have WASH platform in our organization. We will meet every three month. When the meeting day reaches we call all the stakeholders and discuss every problem that should be solved put a solution for them. But when we meet on our next meeting the topics will be same again. No one will be responsible to take action and apply the solution to the problems. So, awareness and the proper implementation of the given solutions with assumption that the problems are my problems and it is my duty to apply the solutions, it will create sustainable sanitation service. Everyone must take their responsibilities. The WASH platform is lead by the Cantiba and every organization from small to big is a member of this platform. The process should be participatory. I think this will solve the problem.

Interviewer: What if the solutions you mentioned above the coordination and each one take their responsibility, what improvement will come?  
Response: If solutions are implemented properly many changes will come eventually. First the city will be clean. Second, the society’s health condition will also be improved. Third, individuals will be responsible for their own and for other action. If one comes to try destroy the cleaned area the other will be conscious and give him lesson that it is illegal to dispose waste materials improperly. Even report to the responsible legal bodies. This will grow from individuals to organizations and factories that currently dispose poisons chemicals. So, problems will be solved if everyone took their part. 







4.In your opinion, how effective is coordination in your woreda for sustainable sanitation services? What could be improved?
Interviewer:
Response: The coordination between institutions and stakeholders can be said it exists but not functional. It is only talking and having a plan and bring problem to the table. No actions are taken and no responsibility is assigned to each bodies. Even most of the time the meetings are initiated by NGOs. It is not done in a well organized and coordinated way. This is the main problem.
To improve this we can learn methods from beautiful cities like Hawassa, Bahirdar who experience in solving this problem and who has relatively clean area. This experience sharing is done few times before in our city but the city administration or other responsible bodies didn’t take the experience and synchronize it to the current situation of our city and try to implement it. If creating sustainable sanitation is taken seriously, it can also decrease unemployment. Because youth many come in group and make it a business to create sustainable sanitation. We have seen this in other cities. To create this it needs well organized, transparent and accountable system. Otherwise, it will not create sustainability. The main thing is it to create the awareness in the society that this could be a way of income generation. If we do this the society will accept it as it is one way of making income and make a living from it.

5.Can you walk me through how do you see improved coordination leading to more sustainable sanitation services in the woreda? For example, how could it solve some of the problems you mentioned before? 

Interviewer:
Response: First there should be to have permanent time for discussion. Then go to action. In this city the main problem is, there is no action only discussion. It is considered as a job of one organization. So, beyond discussing there should be evaluation and must be considered as every ones duty. It must be seen if there are also challenges. For example if one Kebele didn’t complete their payment after their waste material is transported to the disposal area. This is one challenge and this should be solved immediately. Each one should have a clear responsibility starting from Kebele. Tasks must be given and evaluated every time. And the system should be participatory.  We have checked this by testing in some areas to transport their waste materials to disposal area and make clean there area. People has said we will pay more than the asked payment price if you make our area clean. This should be implemented in all areas of the city. If we don’t talk to the people and try to address wider area, with having poor system of waste material transportation, creating sustainable sanitation will not be successful.     

Interviewer: If the system is participatory and include coordinated activities, WASH sanitation services will be sustainable….
Response: The area will be beautiful and the problems I mention earlier will also be minimized. But while doing tasks with decentralized way and with only having a discussion and giving the job to one or two organization it will not produce the needed output. So more than what should be done or given tasks it must be participatory, transparent and involve the society and take in to consideration the societies need. This will make it successful.
